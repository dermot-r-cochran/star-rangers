- **Character pages list their viewpoint chapters** (`lib/viewpoint-chapters.js`, `src/_includes/character.njk`, `test/viewpoint-chapters.test.js`, 2026-11-24, Opus, Dermot's *"Yes, as recommended"* to his own question *"Should each character page list their episodes and chapters?"*). A character page now ends with the chapters told partly from that character's point of view, grouped by season in reading order, each shown with its season, episode and chapter code. The list is derived from each chapter's `povs:`, so it never needs maintaining. It shows only chapters the edition carries and respects tier-hidden viewpoints. Lists longer than twelve fold by season. Mentions are left out, because nothing marks them reliably.
