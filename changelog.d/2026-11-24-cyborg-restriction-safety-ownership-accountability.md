- **Why cyborgs are restricted: safety, ownership and accountability** (`src/glossary/cyborg.md`, 2026-11-24, Opus, Dermot's *"It is rooted in safety concerns as well as blurred lines of ownership and accountability"* and *"The political ban or tight restrictions on cyborgs rather than on AI in general"*). An extension of the entry's account of the tightening. Two further causes now stand beside the welfare literature and the political reaction to the cyber-revenants. A made part inside a body cannot be stood apart from the person it serves, and the AI Safety Kernel's constraints bind the part and, by extension, the whole person who carries it. A made part is also both part of a person and somebody's built thing, so the line between what the person did and what the part did is hard to draw. The entry now says the restriction falls on modifying the body, not on artificial intelligence in general.
