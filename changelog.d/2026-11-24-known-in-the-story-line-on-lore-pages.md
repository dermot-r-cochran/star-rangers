- **"In the story" line on lore pages** (`lib/known-in-story.js`, `story-bible/known-in-story.md`, `src/_includes/lore-entry.njk`, `scripts/check-known-in-story.js`, 2026-11-24, Opus for the line and Fable for the classification, Dermot's *"Yes those two optional offers please"*). Each lore page may now say how widely its subject is known among the people of the story, judged by the latest events the page records: common knowledge, known to anyone who has read up, or held in practice by specialists. There is no "recent" value, because the story's present depends on the season and seasons can span decades and overlap; a note gives the year where knowledge changed. It shows in the *In short* box under the plain summary, with an optional one-sentence note. There is no "nobody knows" value, because the Archive holds only what the record's own institutions know. `npm test` refuses an unknown value.
- **Held-back canon is background, shown not told** (`story-bible/intake-2026-11-24.md`, 2026-11-24, Dermot's ruling). Facts the story bible keeps off the pages, such as what the Noögenic Protouniverse is, are fine as background knowledge and may shape a chapter subtly without ever being stated. The repository stays public.
