- **New Cotswolds: a contradiction in its founding corrected** (`src/lore/new-cotswolds.md`, 2026-11-24, Opus, Dermot's *"fix New Cotswolds"*, under his rule of 13 November that a contradiction no in-story reading can make sense of is fixed). The page said New London had spent *five centuries* at L5 when the founding party left. New London was begun in the 2380s UCSD, so that would date the founding to about 2880, after the present. But the same page has the last of four hulls returning ninety-one years ago, about 2735, and fold transit only became practical in the 2710s. The sentence now reads *some three and a half centuries*, which fits both. Nothing else on the page changed.
