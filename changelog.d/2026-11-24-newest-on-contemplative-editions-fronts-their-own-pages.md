### Changed

- **"Newest" on the contemplative editions fronts their own pages** (`lib/newest-lore.js`, `src/_data/latestLore.js`, `lib/editions.js`, `test/newest-lore.test.js`, 2026-11-24, Opus, Dermot's *"Yes, build it"*). The homepage's second button and Lore card showed the newest lore entry the build carries, and a contemplative build carries every tier below it, so the first reader walk found church-space.site offering *Rank and the Chain of Command*. An edition may now name `newestTopics`: the newest entry tagged with one of them, or the newest overall when none is. Church-space names the Communion's topics and its thread; fellowshipoflight.org names the order's. Every other edition is unchanged. `validateEditions` refuses a tag the edition's own filter does not carry.
