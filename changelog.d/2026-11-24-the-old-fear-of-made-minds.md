- **The old fear of made minds and cyborgs** (`src/lore/made-minds-and-the-ai-safety-archetype.md`, `src/glossary/cyborg.md`, 2026-11-24, Opus, Dermot's *"The risk is in historical fear of cyborgs or unregulated AI in general even through this is through dytopian story telling rather than actual incidents"*). An extension of both pages. The long-feared catastrophe of made minds came down the centuries chiefly in dystopian stories, not in events, as the *Veritas Report*'s finding of obedient machines already implied. It is still a risk, because the fear is real in the people who hold it, and some of the caution around unregulated minds and cyborgs is that inheritance. For cyborgs, the Imperium's programmes were its one real chapter.
