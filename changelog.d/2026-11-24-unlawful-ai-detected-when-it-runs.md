- **Unlawful AI is caught when it runs, and found minds are detained** (`src/lore/ai-safety-kernel.md`, `src/characters/rook-7.md`, 2026-11-24, Opus, Dermot's rulings and his choice *"Detected when it runs"*). Lawful systems detect an unlawful one by what it does near them, not by its papers. An AI police assistant then acts at once; the only other case in which it acts without being asked is to stop a fight in progress. An unlawful mind survives by staying dark, or by faking its provenance and doing good until its mask slips. Orbital Five-O keeps the hidden manufacture and supply. A found uncommissioned mind is not always malicious or dangerous, but it is detained; who keeps it stays open. Rook-7's page names the two exceptions to police robots only noticing and flagging.
