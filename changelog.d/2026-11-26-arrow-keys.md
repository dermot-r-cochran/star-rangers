### Added

- **Left and Right arrow keys follow Previous and Next** (`src/js/key-nav.js`, `src/_includes/base.njk`, `chapter-pagination.njk`, `episode-nav.njk`, `scene-pov.njk`, `journal-entry.njk`, 2026-11-26, Opus, at Dermot's *"Now do item 5, the arrow keys"*). On a chapter, an episode page, a scene page (following the character) and a journal entry, Left and Right go to the previous and next page. The keys stand aside in a text field and with Alt, Ctrl, Meta or Shift held, so the browser's own Back and text selection are untouched; without JavaScript nothing changes.
