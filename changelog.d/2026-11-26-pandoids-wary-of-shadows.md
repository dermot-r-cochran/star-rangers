### Added

- **Pandoids are wary of shadows, most of all Membrane Shadows** (`src/lore/pandoids.md`, *Shadows*, 2026-11-26, at Dermot's direction). An extension drawn from four landed chapters: the adults drawing back from the party's moving lamp-shadow (S11E02C02), the people's word *a shadow* (S12E01C01), and the Membrane Shadow on the line beside Stone-First (S12E01C06, S12E02C03). It states wariness and nothing more: no panic, no hostility, and no way of turning a shadow aside.
