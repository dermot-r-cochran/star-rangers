### Changed

- **A Smart Pet's collar is not a succession** (`src/glossary/smart-pet.md`, `story-bible/story-bible-summary.md`, 2026-11-28, Opus, Dermot's *"As recommended"*). The records a collar holds can pass to another animal; the pet does not, since recognition, temperament and memory as an animal were never in the collar. A second animal under a re-certified collar is a new Smart Pet carrying an old file. Whether a subsystem may be reissued at all stays not established.
