Ansel Thale works on scaffolds for the [Engineering Corps](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-command-hierarchy/) at the Earth–Sun L5 fold-staging zone, where the [Lagrange page](/star-rangers/lore/lagrange-fold-points/) says the densest concentration of fold-transit infrastructure grew up during the Expansion period. His working paper, [Coherence Debt as Quantum Interest](/star-rangers/codex/coherence-debt-as-quantum-interest/), is a model of where a fold meets ordinary space, and he would be the first to add that it is a model of the aperture and not of the fold.

## Known History

- On the fold-scaffold specialization of the Engineering Corps at the L5 staging zone in 2826 UCSD, when the paper was circulated for comment and a copy lodged with the Survey Archive.
- The record gives no birthplace, no age, no rank and no earlier post.

## Character Notes

The paper is built to be cited, and that is its author's habit rather than its subject's. It is numbered by proposition. It marks which results come from the old literature and which are its own reading, and asks every later author to keep the two apart. And it gives its thesis in a single sentence, for anyone who needs to quote it: *every fold is held open on borrowed energy, and every rule the Corps keeps about folds is a schedule of repayment.*

What it declines matters as much. It does not explain the fold itself, which is a fact of the Common Manifold. It has no constants, does not account for natural conduits, and excludes every Etheric term. It also adds nothing to the Institute's account of Dock Seven beyond a name for what that account already describes. Thale states each of these limits at the length of a finding.

The record places him at the L5 staging zone in 2826 UCSD and nowhere else.
