A face can be come by honestly, and it still cannot make a claim for the person wearing it.

**Asteria** is a member of [Tissadelle Shepherd](/star-rangers/characters/tissadelle-shepherd/)'s [plural](/star-rangers/glossary/plural-minds/) system: a distinct person sharing the system's one body, on the terms Concord personhood standards have recognised for well over a century. She is not [Asteria Wessex](/star-rangers/characters/asteria-the-sage/), the retired Star Captain, and this page is not that one. Shepherd's page carries the system as a whole. This page carries what the record holds of her, all of it on the system's own account, which is the only authority there has ever been on the subject.

## The face and the name

She has the face of Asteria Wessex as a younger woman, a woman the system never met. [Karla Wender](/star-rangers/characters/karla-wender/) was Wessex's pupil and became Shepherd's mentor, and described her own teacher in the telling. The image formed from that telling is the form the member took, and the name came with it. On her own account she has never answered to the other woman's surname, and has never let it be supposed that she has the other woman's years ([None Admitted (S15E04C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s15/e04/s15e04c01/)).

When the system speaks of her as Asteria the Sage it is repeating Wender. *The Sage* is an honorific of the [Fellowship of Light](/star-rangers/lore/fellowship-of-light/), in which the system has no standing, and it is not hers to hold.

## On the watch

In a later year that the record does not number, she comes down on some nights, unasked, to the watch [Tobble](/star-rangers/characters/tobble/) keeps below the kept hill, and sits the end of it with him. The two of them disagree there about what Shepherd is owed, each on stated grounds, and neither moves the other. The chapter is hers and his, and this page does not retell it. Her own ground is stated there. She holds that Shepherd is owed the knowledge of what comes to the foot of her house, and she does not carry it up the hill herself: her face came into this mind by a telling, and to be believed she would have to describe what stands there. She does not claim that she can support that. She says only that she will not be the one who finds out ([None Admitted (S15E04C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s15/e04/s15e04c01/)).

She came down again on the third night after Shepherd went through. One of the things that come to the watch has Shepherd's voice, and of that one she judged that it was the only thing in the house that had not altered since. Tobble did not ask what she had said to Shepherd at her leave-taking, and she did not tell him. At first light that morning she saw somebody sitting on the unsettled ground past the low wall, who did not turn round. She knew whom she took her for and did not put it into words, and she told nobody, on the ground she had given before: she would not be the road a face came into the house by. The record does not say who was there ([None Seated (S15E06C02)](/star-rangers/seasons/s15/e06/s15e06c02/)).

## What the record does not hold

The record does not say when she came, or why a mind would take a mentor's mentor for a member, and has not been asked to. She says herself that she does not know the whole of it. There is something she says at first light that came to her with the hill; what it is belongs to her, and the record does not have it.

It holds no photograph of her, and never will: a member of a system has no body of her own for a camera to take. A painter who never saw her made a picture from the system's description, filed in the Codex as [an artist's impression](/star-rangers/codex/asteria-the-sage-impression/). It asserts nothing about her face.
