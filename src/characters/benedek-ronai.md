Benedek Rónai is a Field Officer of the [Survey Corps'](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-survey-corps/) Archive Branch, the branch that keeps the record itself, at the Survey Archive on [Threshold Station](/star-rangers/lore/threshold-station/). Since the start of 2826 UCSD he has sat the scale desk of the 2826 archive pass: every sheet in the Archive that wrote *membrane* where it meant the zone, or the zone where it meant the [membrane](/star-rangers/lore/concordant-membranes/), or carried one line because the sheet of its day had only one. Most of the work is reading, and he has initialled a great many sheets since the year began and altered very few. The record meets him once, on the afternoon the [Deadwater](/star-rangers/lore/universes/deadwater/) returns came to his desk as a run of twenty-one, the last of them with a line nobody had filled and a Section Lead's reason on the back. He filled it with one word and a mark, took it up one desk, filed it, and then drew the home stack toward him. The record renders him as *he*.

## Known History

- Field Officer of the Archive Branch at the Survey Archive in 2826 UCSD, trained on the Archive's doctrine for correctly filed blanks, and long enough in the service to have read the head of a home return ten thousand times. Where he was born, where he trained, when he joined the Corps and what desks he sat before this one, the record does not say.
- On the 2826 pass from the start of the year. The pass runs on the Archive's own authority, desk by desk, through every filing the correction of scale touches, and a platform on a membrane is every filing a platform has ever sent. A filing that used one word for twenty years had, almost always, used it correctly for twenty years, since the only membranes anybody had filed from were the three or four with names and the name was always the membrane's; the correction was to the sheet and not to the record.
- Spring 2826 UCSD: the Deadwater run, correct in every line it had. Twenty returns with *Deadwater* at the head, the crews' word kept by the registry as the registry keeps what it is sent; the twenty-first with the new line unfilled and the remark on the back, *The platform has no name for it and has not met its edge. Left for the Archive.* He read it twice, the second time for the shape of it. He sat with the line for the better part of an hour. A name was out, because the registry names what it meets and no crew had sent one. A blank was out, because on a home return both lines are blank and the blank means *here*, and the two sheets side by side would have read as the same word. He wrote *Deadwater's*. He took it to the Principal with the home stack in his other hand, set the stack beside the sheet with its blank head uppermost, and said that a blank would say they were here. She initialled under his initials. He filed the run and turned up the first home return, from a Tier 1 staging site the record does not name: *Membrane*, blank, *Zone*, blank. He did not write in them. See [Left Blank (S01E00C12)](/star-rangers/seasons/s01/e00/s01e00c12/).

## The Work

The desk is scale, and scale is the commonest correction the Corps makes to its own older filings. The Archive now carries two lines where there was one, and the circular that went out with the new sheet says where a finding's scope is the zone, write the zone. His part is to read each sheet the correction touches, initial it where it was right, alter it where it was wrong, and fill what a filing office has left for the Archive to fill. A line filled at a desk and not from a return goes up one desk before it goes into the file.

The doctrine he was trained on is the registry's: it names what it meets, and a name goes into a file when a crew carries one to it. Twenty years of the Deadwater membrane line are that doctrine in practice, the crews' sailor's word kept, not improved on and not asked to be. What the Deadwater zone line needed, as he reasoned it, was a word that was not a name and was not nothing, and the file had one word in it. The membrane's own word with the mark after it claims the zone that universe holds as far as anyone has stood in it, which is one, and names nothing the platform has not met. If a second zone is ever met in that membrane the line will need a second word, and somebody will have met it, and they can send one. The Principal's reading of it, when she had looked at the two sheets for as long as he had: *When they meet a second one, they can tell us what to call it.*

## Character Notes

He reads a remark twice when once would do, and the second reading is for the shape: whether the officer declined to supply a word, said why, and said whose the line was. That is a correctly filed blank, and he knows one when he sees it. He gives a line the time the file deserves rather than the time the line does, and he knows the difference, and says so. He makes his case to the Principal with two sheets rather than an argument, the one with the word and the one with the space, and answers her two objections in two sentences.

What the afternoon changed in him is the one thing the record has him notice about himself. He had read the blank head of a home return ten thousand times as two lines nobody had needed, the way a form carries a line for a middle name, and had thought, until that day, that not writing in a line and nobody's ever having needed to were the same thing. He did not write in them. Nobody ever had. The record has him in one posture: at the desk with the home stack drawn toward him, reading the head of a sheet he has read ten thousand times.

## What the Record Does Not Hold

His age, his birth year and what he looks like. Where he was born, and his kin. Where he trained, when he joined the Archive Branch, how many years *ten thousand times* comes to, and what desks he sat before the scale desk. The Principal's name. Which sheets the pass altered in his hand, and how many. Whether he knows the Section Lead's name beyond her hand and her initials, or she his beyond the initials she did not know. Whether the home-side lines will ever carry a word, and who would send it. Whether the sheet went further up than the Principal's desk. Any chapter after the afternoon he turned up the home stack.
