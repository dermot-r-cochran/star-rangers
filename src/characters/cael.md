*It is tidy* is not praise when Cael says it.

**Cael** is one of the three people of the [plural](/star-rangers/glossary/plural-minds/) system that calls itself the Archivist and goes by [Sen Lovax](/star-rangers/characters/sen/) outside it, with Wyn and Sen, sharing one body and one desk at the Survey Archive on Threshold Station. Cael is a person on the Concord's ordinary terms, not a mood of the archivist's and not a method with a name. Sen's page carries the system and the desk as a whole. This page carries what the record holds of Cael.

## The verifier

Cael is the one who checks. A record that closes cleanly is what a closed file looks like from outside, and Cael has reopened enough of them to know the shape, so *tidy* is the word Cael keeps for the things Cael trusts least. Cael cross-checks, re-derives, and asks the question underneath the question. On the desk's four-way sorting of every sentence it files, Cael's question is a test of what the shelf has verified, never a theory about what a reading expects ([Sen Lovax](/star-rangers/characters/sen/#what-the-desk-does-not-do)).

Cael goes first. On the Shepherd submission to the inquiry, in Late Autumn 2831 UCSD, Cael read every citation against its source, found every one of them sound, and said so, with the reluctance of somebody handing over a finding they had hoped to keep. Cael then pointed out that the submission quoted three lines of a four-line flag and that a discrepancy between a document and its source is exactly what a note is for. Sen declined to add the note, the first time in sixteen years the desk had done so ([On the Record First (S06E02C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s06/e02/s06e02c01/)).

In Deep Winter 2831 UCSD, when the signal at Dock Seven was set beside the record, Cael knew the Union land-survey frame on sight, because the desk had set it beside a Ranger marker once before and Cael does not forget an entry, and then searched the whole shelf for three lines it did not hold. Cael checked the absence twice. The second answer did not change ([Enough Is Enough (S07E01C03)](/star-rangers/seasons/s07/e01/s07e01c03/)).

## In Cael's own words

Where the record gives Cael's words, they come as short declaratives, one finding to each. The same day, Cael dictated the first half of the desk's note on the account, ending it *End.*, and Sen's hand kept up ([Not Under a Name (S07E01C04)](/star-rangers/seasons/s07/e01/s07e01c04/)).

On whether the desk should sign its notes, Cael holds that a name moves no standing: signed or unsigned, a note holds what it holds. *I want the line left because a name is not evidence.* Wyn says Cael wants the line left because a blank line is tidy. The difference is theirs and is settled by none of the three.

Cael's section of [Shepherd Among the Comparanda](/star-rangers/codex/shepherd-among-the-comparanda/), the comparative-archive submission the desk prepared and withdrew, argues from the evidence: the pattern's fourth element is not satisfied but assumed, and filing the cross-reference would not record a match but complete one. *I am not willing to be the desk that closed a file sideways.*

## What the record does not hold

The record holds no account of when Cael came, of how Cael came to share Sen's desk, or of anything Cael has not said at it. It has no picture of Cael, and a member of a system has no body of their own for a camera to take.
