Dorrit Hale is a technician of the Maintenance Division at [Threshold Station](/star-rangers/lore/threshold-station/). The division is one of the station's several chains, and its concern is plant: what is fitted, whether it runs, and what was done to it.

The record holds her for one work order. In the weeks after [Dock Seven](/star-rangers/seasons/s05/e06/s05e06c03/) the crews on the dock's outer gallery reported that it was warm, and the division filed the complaint as a fault in the heat exchange. The order was closed four times under that cause and came back four times. On the fifty-seventh day it reached Hale, who had not had it before.

## Known History

- **2831 UCSD, Late Autumn, the fifty-seventh day after Dock Seven:** tested the gallery's heat exchanger and found it in order. With two hand thermometers she then walked the gallery, and found the air over its last few metres between one and two degrees above setpoint, with no draught, the glass beside it colder than the air, and no source. The two instruments did not put the warmth in quite the same place or at quite the same figure.
- **The same day:** declined to enter one of the form's eight causes, and filed the order open: *no mechanical cause identified*, the exchanger to be left alone. Her section lead let it stand on the section's weekly sheet, and asked for readings morning and evening for a week and a tag on the panel ([No Mechanical Cause (S06E04C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s06/e04/s06e04c01/)).

## Character Notes

Hale does the work on the order before she does anything else, and writes nothing about a thing until she has tested it. She carries a second thermometer, older than the first and scratched, because she trusts it. Asked on the gallery what was warm, she said that she did not know. Asked by a dock hand what the morning of Dock Seven had meant, she said that she did heat exchangers, and then that she did not know that either.

She did not say what the warmth was. The work order does not say, and nothing she filed offers a guess.

The record holds nothing of Hale beyond that week: no birthplace, no age, no earlier posting, and no later filing. It does not say what became of the work order.
