Elsha Rootkin is a [Verdani](/star-rangers/lore/verdani/) and a [Star Ranger](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-command-hierarchy/): a Deputy Officer of the [Survey Corps](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-survey-corps/)' Ecology Branch, posted in the record's present, 2826 UCSD, to the planetary survey crew of a Survey vessel the record does not name, on the survey of a watered world it does not name either. The people's page says that a Verdani on an extended off-world posting needs reliable full-spectrum light and nothing else a humanoid crew does not already have, and that most Federation and Star Ranger vessels carry it for unrelated reasons; the Survey Corps' page says that the Ecology Branch counts who lives there, and that its files say *habitable* least and *habitable for* most, because its whole subject is the fit between a life and a place. Elsha Rootkin is where those two sentences meet. The record has named no Verdani Ranger before her, and has no reason to call her the first of anything else: the people's page gives the Verdani four governments, orbital trade platforms and a Federation seat, and says nothing about who among them has worn a tab.

The name is given as the Federation record gives Verdani names: a personal name and a kinstrain name, in that order, the second naming the thallenwort lineage she was bonded into and not a place. What *Elsha* means, and when a Verdani is given it, the record does not state. She is *she* in the record's translation register, as [Isren Farrowkin](/star-rangers/characters/isren-farrowkin/) is and on the same default; the people's page declares no pronoun system, and this page asserts no sex.

## Known History

- Bonded into the **Rootkin** lineage in the Understory Assembly's territory of the [Canopy Concord](/star-rangers/lore/planets/verdance/#four-countries), on [Verdance](/star-rangers/lore/planets/verdance/) in the Calyx system, in a grove the record does not name and a year it does not hold. The people's page lists Rootkin among the kinstrain names an outside observer notices first, beside Farrowkin and Duskkin, and the [taxonomy file](/star-rangers/lore/cross-biosphere-taxonomy/) names it again as a lineage Survey files by; neither gives the name a meaning or a country, and the Understory is this page's reading. Whether her bonding fell before or after the Assembly restricted the practice to registered grove-elders, the record does not say. Her kinbeds run the deep chlorophyll green of a canopy-belt strain adapted to filtered forest light, which the people's page gives as a reasonably reliable indicator of kinstrain and home biome and of nothing about her beyond that.
- Entered the Corps by the ordinary road: Novice, Cadet, Deputy, each rank held in turn and none skipped, through the Survey Corps' college at the [Academy](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-academy/), where the college and not the command chain confirmed her Cadet standing. Who referred her into cadet intake, and which of Verdance's four countries the Corps was asked in by, the record does not say; the Academy's page says only that intake draws on candidates identified locally. The record does not fix the year, and does not say what the service ages come to for a Verdani, those being set per people by the Medical Corps and never by the command ladder.
- Deputy Officer, Survey Corps, Ecology Branch, in 2826 UCSD, on the planetary survey crew of a Survey vessel, under a Field Officer, on the survey of a watered world; none of the three has a name anywhere in the record. It places her nowhere else, and gives her no earlier posting.

## The Work

A Deputy works under a Field Officer's supervision by definition. The ladder's page says that a Deputy's three bars tell a reader at arm's length what a Deputy is, trusted to work, under supervision, and not yet to act alone, and on a survey crew that is a place and not a waiting room. The Ecology Branch's census of a world is taken on the ground, plot by plot and season by season, under protocol, logged with provenance, claiming no more than the instrument showed, and most of it is done by the officers the Field Officer brought down. Elsha Rootkin brings down the branch's instruments and sets them where she is told to, reads them when they are due, and enters what they read in the record's own terms. The file goes up under the Field Officer's name and her own, and is dated the day it is drawn, as every planetology and ecology file is, because the Corps' doctrine is that a place is rates in the costume of a place and a census taken carelessly becomes the only record of what was lost.

What she asks first is her own. The branch's question of any ground is *habitable for*, and a canopy people's first measure of a place is the light that reaches the floor under its roof, in which season, for how long. That is the number the people's page says Verdance's four countries were drawn by, and the one her own lineage cannot live outside. The record is careful here, as the branch is. Her kinbeds are not an instrument of the branch's and are certified by nobody; what they take of a world's light is hers and goes into no file. What goes into the file is the branch's reading, taken by the branch's instrument, stamped with the ruler that took it. What she has is the habit of asking for that reading before any other, and the crew's files now carry the light at the floor first on every shaded plot, which the record notes without saying whose habit it was.

The world's light is not Verdance's. The people's page gives the mechanism and not the figure: a strain is adapted to one biome's light quality, humidity and canopy density, and a Verdani who carries it into another light is carrying a partner that will do less well there, permanently. The vessel's light is the standard provision and is enough, since the page says it is; the world's own sun is whatever it is. What a canopy-belt strain takes of a sky it was not bred for, the record has not measured, and this page does not. It notes only that a survey day on the ground is budgeted twice, by the crew in hours and by her in light, and that the Field Officer's schedule has learned to carry both.

## What She Cannot Do

Hurry, as the people's page says of every Verdani: she cannot decide to have a harder afternoon than the light allows, and a Verdani's day is paid for in metabolism before it is spent. Work folded for long in poor light without cost; her vanes opening is not display or mood but a meal, and a compartment lit for a human crew is, to a kinbed, worth very little. Present a face, or hold still to be read: the people's page gives no single face, sensory structures distributed along the leading margins where the vanes meet, and a body that orients continuously toward the light the way a person aboard a ship holds their balance, which colleagues of other peoples eventually stop noticing and never entirely stop finding strange. Leave her partner behind on the vessel. Act alone; a Deputy does not. Speak for the Concord, which its Assembly and its Compact do; for Verdance, which the Fourfold Accord does; or for the Corps, which no Deputy does.

## The Uniform

On duty Elsha Rootkin wears the Corps' [modular field shell](/star-rangers/lore/rank-insignia-and-uniform/) as the quartermaster record fits it to a Verdani body plan: the Survey Corps' deep olive, in a few small fitted sections seated on the hide of the low body between the roots of the vanes, and nothing on any vane, because the kinbeds ride on the vanes and a shell that covered one would shade the metabolism it was issued to keep alive. The people's own answer to a mark is nothing worn and nothing grown, for the same reason, and the people's page gives belonging to the kinstrain name instead; the Corps' shell does not compete with that and does not replace it. The rank tab, three bars on the pale grey-green insert of the survey track, is seated on the upper hide between two vane roots, on no vane, which is the one place on this body plan where a mark shades nothing and can still be read at arm's length by anyone who shares the air; the corps badge, the open-based triangle, sits on another section and never where the tab sits, as the standard requires of every body it is seated on. Strain colour is biology and not a mark, and the deep green of her kinbeds says her lineage and her home biome and nothing about her corps, her rank or her work; a colleague who reads it as any of those has misread the one thing the people's page asks outsiders not to misread.

The liner under the shell is fitted to the body and leaves the vanes bare, and the uniform page's rule that in vacuum or a foul atmosphere a Ranger's face is covered, or whatever a body plan carries in its place, is met for her as it is met for anyone, by the quartermaster record. What a pressure suit comes to for a body that eats by its surface, that record holds and the Archive does not: the record has not put her in one, and a Verdani in a pressure suit would be a Verdani eating nothing for as long as it was sealed, which is a cost the people's page would count and this page does not guess at.

## Character Notes

The Verdani are the record's clearest case of a people for whom patience is not a virtue but an accounting fact, and the Survey Corps is the body in the record whose entire discipline is to observe under protocol and claim no more than the instrument showed. The record sets the two beside each other on this page and draws no line between them. Her colleagues on the crew are recorded as finding her slow at the start of a day and exact at the end of it, and as learning, as colleagues of Verdani apparently always learn, that the pace is not a manner. She does not file what she has not read off an instrument. She does not stay out past the light to finish a plot, and has not been asked to twice.

The same summer she was on the ground, in a kept room a long way from it, Verdance's seat put to the Federation assembly a proposal that the Corps be formally asked for its survey data of each member world's own approaches, on the ground that her people have an unusually direct stake in knowing which light falls on which ground; how the session decided is not on the record ([Nobody Present](/star-rangers/seasons/s01/e00/s01e00c08/)). Elsha Rootkin is a Ranger of the Corps whose record that is, in the branch whose files say *habitable for*, and her service is individual and apolitical, as every Ranger's is. The record places her at no table, does not say whether she has read the proposal, and does not say what she would think of it; it notes only that the record's present now holds one Verdani asking the Corps for what it holds and one keeping, in a Deputy's small share, what it holds, and that neither is on the other's page.

With the humans on the crew she keeps the courtesy the people's page gives every Verdani, which is to be attentive from every direction and to make nobody choose where to look. A colleague speaking to her addresses a vane's edge, or the air above the body, or the tab, and she is recorded as answering all three the same; the record has no account of her correcting anyone's aim. On the ground, when the crew stops, she opens to whatever the sky gives, as a body that is eating does, and the Field Officer's plot sheet has come to mark the stops where there is sky worth taking. Nobody has written it into a procedure. The record notes that nobody has needed to.

The record gives her no age, no kin by name beyond the lineage, no grove, no year of entry and no history before the Corps beyond Verdance itself. It places her on the ground of a watered world without a name on the record, in 2826 UCSD, in uniform, with her vanes open to a sun that is not her own and the branch's instrument set where she was told to set it, and nowhere else.

## What the Record Does Not Carry

Her age, and what the three service ages come to for a Verdani, neither stated anywhere; the record holds no span for a Verdani life and no conversion of Verdance's local years. The grove she was bonded in, and whether her bonding fell before or after the Understory Assembly restricted the practice to registered grove-elders. What *Elsha* means, when a personal name is given, and whether a Ranger of her people is addressed by it or by the lineage. Who referred her into cadet intake, which country the Corps was asked in by, and whether the Corps has a recruiter on Verdance at all, which the record does not say. The year she entered the Corps, the Field Officer she works under, the vessel, the world under survey and its system. What a canopy-belt strain takes of a sun it was not bred for, and whether her kinbeds have done less well since she left Verdance, which the people's page says is the cost and no page has measured. Whether any other Verdani is on the roster of any Corps posting, which the record does not say and this page does not settle; the people's page gives no Verdani in any uniform, and one case is not a pattern. Which corps the Verdani in Corps service mostly join: hers is Survey, and the record draws nothing from one case. Whether she has met Isren Farrowkin or Meril Duskkin, or knows of either, since the record brings none of the three into a room with another. Whether she has ever worn a pressure suit, and what one costs a body that eats by its surface. How Verdani speak, since the record has described no channel of their language; what *Rootkin* says in it. Any chapter. The body, beyond what the people's page gives every Verdani and the canopy-belt strain's colour: broad extensible vanes carrying the kinbeds, folded when the light is poor and opened when there is sky worth taking; a low-slung, multiply supported body; no single face, the sensory structures along the leading margins where the vanes meet; continuous orientation toward the light. Nothing on record tells one Verdani from another by sight but the strain's colour, and the page does not try. The portrait on this page is an artist's impression, filed 29 October 2026; the record holds no photograph of Elsha Rootkin.
