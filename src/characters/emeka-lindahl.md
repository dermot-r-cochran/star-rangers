Emeka Lindahl is a [Chief](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-command-hierarchy/) on the general operations track and carries the working title Chief Ranger, as a Chief who runs a general operations department does. His department is the watch at a fold-approach post on the outward frontier, which the record does not name: six instruments, a crew of twenty, and a line office nine hours away by relay. He had been its Chief for nine years by the late autumn of 2829 UCSD.

When a post's Starwarden goes up, the post passes to its Chief Ranger pending the next one. Lindahl had held his post that way three times before 2829, and held it a fourth time for thirty-three days that autumn. On each of those days he stood the Starwarden's middle watch as well as his own forenoon watch, and the state sheet he wrote at the end had no line for that.

## Known History

- **2829 UCSD, Late Autumn:** handed the post to Starwarden [Tissadelle Shepherd](/star-rangers/characters/tissadelle-shepherd/), her first command. She assumed it by one line in the log and did not sign *received as stated* under his initial on the state sheet. He told her how the line office would read the blank, sent the sheet up as she asked, and had it back in eighteen hours with the Line Captain's initial and no query. She took the middle watch from the first night and changed nothing on his bill ([On Assumption (S03E03C02)](/star-rangers/seasons/s03/e03/s03e03c02/)).

## Character Notes

Lindahl runs the watch and does not want the post. He has never much liked the fit of answering for it in the gaps, and has never said so, since no form asks. He states a position once, to the officer whose decision it is, and does not argue it a second time. When something looks wrong he works it through twice, as he would a watch bill.

He does not tell his watch what to make of a new Starwarden.

The record holds nothing of Lindahl before the post: no birthplace, no earlier postings, and no age.
