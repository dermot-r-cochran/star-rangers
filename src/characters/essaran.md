Essaran is a [Cerebraun](/star-rangers/lore/cerebraun/) of the [Cerebraun Hegemony](/star-rangers/lore/cerebraun-hegemony/), an officer of the Prime Ganglion's office, and the author of one document in the record, [What the Limbs Already Were](/star-rangers/codex/what-the-limbs-already-were/): the office's answer to the Survey Archive's request for a history of the Cerebraun by one of them. The answer is a filing, sent under the office's seal through the standing data-exchange arrangement, set out in items, each answered in three parts, with a line set apart beneath some of them. The office's filings go out under nobody's name. This one carries Essaran's, because the desk asked for a hand to file it under, and the office gave the name of the officer who drafted it and formally noted the giving.

The record renders Essaran as *they*, as it renders every Cerebraun person: the nine centres are one person, and the pronoun counts the person. *Essaran* is the name as the office gave it, in the exchange's translation register, and the record asserts nothing by its form.

## Known History

- An officer of the Prime Ganglion's office who drafts the office's filings to parties outside the Hegemony, by the filing's own description ([What the Limbs Already Were](/star-rangers/codex/what-the-limbs-already-were/)). How long they have held the work, and what they did before it, the filing does not say.
- Drafted the office's history of the Cerebraun for the Survey Archive, after the office reserved the question of [the Sentinel](/star-rangers/lore/planets/sentinel/)'s authorship to itself in the late spring of 2828 UCSD, which the filing gives as done. When the filing was drafted, in any reckoning, is not on it.
- Was named to the Archive as the filing's drafting officer, at the desk's request; the one time, by the filing's own account, that a filing of the office has gone out under a name as well as its seal.

## Character Notes

The filing is the office's, and its three parts and its reservations are the office's register: *formally noted*, *reserved*, *not supplied*. What the page can say of Essaran is what the drafting shows. It is exact, and it is courteous to a reader it expects to misread it: the acknowledgement tells the Archive that a singly-brained reader takes the third part of an answer for the true one, and that the error is common and not the reader's fault. It keeps to doctrine, filing the Hegemony's order of authority as stated, and it files beside the doctrine that the limbs are not the voice's equals and are not silent either. It reads the Hegemony's own history the way the Hegemony reads every history, as a nervous system settling, and gives the period before the voice one sentence, because limbs do not file.

Beneath three of the items, and beneath the close, there is a line of the kind the record already knows from [Sohrel](/star-rangers/characters/sohrel/): a second line written to read as routine caution. *Objections lodged through an Administrator-Voice are recorded in full. A filing closed is a filing, and is not to be read as a finding.* The last is in the drafting officer's own name, and advises that the filing's evenness is not the whole of it. The Archive reads each line as what it says. Whether any was written over a dissent among Essaran's centres is on the channel the exchange does not carry, and in three places the filing marks that passage as also given in display. None of it was sent.

On the Sentinel, Essaran's filing holds the office's reservation and supplies nothing past it: the reason is on the Sentinel file, and the question is the office's. On [Meridian](/star-rangers/lore/planets/meridian/) it files the two surveys that took the reef-cities for rock as the limbs' and the correction as the voice's, and faults neither.

## What the Record Does Not Carry

Essaran's age, home world and length of service; as for every Cerebraun, the record names no world of the home cluster and holds no Cerebraun lifespan. Where the Prime Ganglion's office sits, how it is composed, and what rank, if any, a drafting officer holds in it; the record holds the office and its register and nothing of its staff. Whether Essaran has dealt with [Sohrel](/star-rangers/characters/sohrel/), drafted any of the office's notices to the Halyx administration, or read [Tavesh](/star-rangers/characters/tavesh/)'s channel or [Vaurel](/star-rangers/characters/vaurel/)'s filings; the account names none of them. Essaran's own view of the Sentinel's authorship, of Meridian's objections, or of the reason recorded against the Sentinel file, none of which the filing gives. Anything the filing reserves: the Hegemony's dating, its count of worlds and persons, the home cluster's worlds by name, how the Prime Ganglion's capacity is shown, the arrangements with worlds of the Celtic Union, defence posture. What Essaran's display showed while the filing was drafted, which the exchange does not carry. Their body, which no page describes beyond the people's. No portrait is on file.
