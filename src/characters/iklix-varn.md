Iklix Varn holds the rank most Deputies hold for exactly as long as it takes to stop being new at it, and was, in the spring of 2828 UCSD, still new at it. Their first field posting was the fold-approach watch on [Eden Space Habitat](/star-rangers/lore/eden-space-habitat/) — a compartment on the fold-approach side of the ring, behind a hatch the habitat's residents walk past without seeing, where six cross-correlated instruments file their agreement with each other every ninety seconds and the nearest qualified officer stands a shift in case the event comes. It has not come in [Zoe Smith](/star-rangers/characters/zoe-smith/)'s tenure. She said so on Varn's first day, as a fact about the posting and not as a comfort, and Varn understood that it was neither. Varn stood the watch from the early spring of that year to the early winter, when the Corps posted the Deputy on, still a Deputy Officer, to a watch that is not Eden's ([The Same Day (S09E08C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s09/e08/s09e08c01/)).

Varn is young, in the way the [rank](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-command-hierarchy/) implies and the record does not further fix: old enough to have finished a Cadet year and been trusted with a posting under an officer the Corps hands its rawest Deputies to first; young enough to be standing, most days, one step further from the desk than they need to be. The record declines to attach a number to it. What it does carry is three words, which is more than it carries for most Deputies: lean in build, dark-haired, and of a quiet bearing — the last being the quality Smith's assessments will eventually have to find a longer word for.

## The Posting

A Deputy works under a Field Officer's supervision by definition, and Smith supervises the way her assessments say she does: with exactly what can be checked and nothing that cannot. The posting's first lesson, which Varn took on [the first day](/star-rangers/seasons/s09/e01/s09e01c01/), is that the watch does not watch anything — the instruments watch themselves — and that its job is to be the closest qualified officer when something happens, and to log flatly, in a form that can be checked, when nothing does.

Its ninth day was a form. Structural work on Eden that touches the approach monitoring is certified twice, by the habitat and by the watch's countersignature, and the countersign needs the contractor's log as well as the certification copy. The log had not come up the ring. Varn was sent for it — across to the Compact side, where the tab at a Deputy's collar is a courtesy and not a rank — and came back with two documents that agreed about everything the certification had asked and one line the certification had not: a contractor's note that the third approach mount rang under load, initialled in the Certification Office and marked *n/a*. Varn did not know what the note meant. Varn knew what *not a certification item* meant, which was the sentence the whole ring was built out of: a thing that was true and belonged to nobody.

The watch's log records the rest. Smith declined to countersign, declined to refer, and sent the Deputy back to ask — politely, on the Corps' letterhead — not whether the note was true but who had decided it was not a question.

The rest of the month is in the same log. On the first day Varn had entered *Nothing to report*, been told it was an opinion and not an entry, and struck the three words with one line so that they could still be read, which nobody had said to do ([Nothing to Report (S09E01C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s09/e01/s09e01c01/)). On the seventh day Varn asked the posting's first question, and entered the answer, the account of Aoife of Cill Aoife, as an event of the shift with its duration: *Fourteen minutes* ([What Did Not Fit (S09E01C03)](/star-rangers/seasons/s09/e01/s09e01c03/)). On the twelfth, in the room the Compact lends Orbital Five-O, Varn laid the contractor's log between the task force's reconciliation and the certification and saw that all three were about one member: the six instruments hang from approach mount three, and were re-zeroed while it carried the load ([What It Hangs From (S10E01C03)](/star-rangers/seasons/s10/e01/s10e01c03/)). Varn stood under the dock ring for the joint inspection that found the bent bracket at the mount's root, carried the slate up the ring a fourth time, and understood what *the nearest qualified officer* had meant all along ([Record in Order (S10E01C07)](/star-rangers/seasons/s10/e01/s10e01c07/)).

On the thirtieth day Varn stood at the desk one step nearer than on the first, without having decided to, and did not correct it. At the hour a resident asked, for his mother, whether the hatch was staying now the Corps had gone. Varn said it was not the Corps that came: "It is the one that stays." ([Stand Where You Like (S09E01C05)](/star-rangers/seasons/s09/e01/s09e01c05/))

## Posted On

The posting ran from the early spring of 2828 to the early winter, and the event did not come in it. What the rest of the year holds is mostly off the watch. In the late spring Varn went with [Noah Adeyemi](/star-rangers/characters/noah-adeyemi/) to the near terraces, three stops along the Ring-Rail, out of service dress and on an ordinary fare. Varn had declined to go in it, on the reading that a tab on the terraces would be the Corps arriving where nobody had called it. The reading was the Deputy's own and no ruling of the Corps. The kit list for a first posting carries no clothing but service dress, so the shirt came from the Adeyemi household. Without the tab the name is Iklix, at Varn's word. At the hatch, at the hour, in service dress, it is Varn, and the step stands ([What People Wear (S09E02C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s09/e02/s09e02c01/)). Asked by Noah's mother why a Deputy stands a long way off, Varn said that in service dress it is where the Corps stands ([The Sixth Day (S09E03C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s09/e03/s09e03c01/)).

The log has the rest. In the summer Smith was away for one shift and Field Officer Halden Ruiz came from the Passage oversight to stand it with the Deputy. Her handover carried two lines, and the second was that Deputy Officer Varn knows the watch. Varn entered the hatch as closed to the seal for that shift, because it was ([A Hatch Shut (S09E04C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s09/e04/s09e04c01/)). In the autumn the fourth of the six instruments was out of agreement with the other five for one interval of ninety seconds, and agreed again at the next. Varn wrote the notice to the Passage oversight and left its remarks line empty. All six hang from approach mount three and one light had changed. Varn held that this could be supported, and that it said only where the cause was not ([One Interval (S09E06C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s09/e06/s09e06c01/)).

In the late autumn, off the watch and out of uniform, Varn knocked at the Adeyemi door for the first time ([The Door After the Hatch (S09E07C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s09/e07/s09e07c01/)). That evening Noah asked for the account of Aoife of Cill Aoife. Varn declined to tell it, as a thing handed across a desk by the officer who has a Deputy first, and not yet Varn's to hand on. Varn undertook one thing that had a day in it: when the Corps said where the Deputy went next, Noah would be told the same day ([The Light Way Round (S09E07C02)](/star-rangers/seasons/s09/e07/s09e07c02/)).

The Corps said in the early winter. The order was one printed leaf on the form such orders take: the Deputy's name, the watch left, the watch gone to, the Field Officer there, a date to be relieved, a later date to report, and a reference. It gave no reason and moved no rank. It named a watch that is not Eden's, on a station a ship's passage from Eden's dock, under a Field Officer whose name Varn did not know. The record does not carry the station's name. Varn read the leaf twice, standing, and asked nothing about it. The one question of that shift was whether the watch would get another Deputy, and Smith answered that nobody had told her there would be no sixteenth. Varn entered the order in the log as received and read, with the date of relief and the reference, and at the hour of the same shift told Noah at the hatch, in service dress, before going to change ([The Same Day (S09E08C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s09/e08/s09e08c01/)).

The relief fell on a sixth day of the week. Varn stood the last shift and entered it: the instruments, the hatch, the officers, *relieved of the watch at the end of this shift, by order*, the reference, and *No event*. Before going, Varn told Smith that the Deputy had not found out what the account of Aoife is for. She answered that none of the fourteen had by the day they left either. The last thing she said about a posting was the first day's sentence, *Stand where you like*, and she did not say whether it was advice. In the corridor Varn undertook to write to the Adeyemi household at the address it gives, the door after the hatch, and could not support what a letter would hold. Noah was offered the archive for the account and asked not to be given the name it would need. He called the Deputy *Iklix* once, to the tab. Varn did not correct it and did not move the step. Varn left the ring that day by the spoke cars, in service dress, with the kit list in the bag and the shirt, which went in last ([A Different Walk (S09E08C02)](/star-rangers/seasons/s09/e08/s09e08c02/)).

## What They Decline

Varn does not ask why a log is missing. A Deputy who has been told to fetch a thing fetches it, and notices what the officer did *not* say — in this case, *lost*.

Varn does not sit down with a document unless told to. Reading standing up is how a Deputy reads anything, and it is why the extra line was found before the chair was.

Varn does not pretend to know. Asked what *rings under load* meant to a person who reads structure through their feet, Varn said *I don't know*, and was told that this was the correct answer and the only one that would have been accepted — and did not know, for a moment, what to do with being right by having nothing.

## Character Notes

Varn's standing is the season's subject in a person. On the fold-approach side of Eden the Corps has a record older than the Compact and a Deputy to stand beside it; on the Compact side it has a courtesy. The contractors whose log Varn carried are the Compact's, on the Compact's ring, and cannot be asked by the Corps at all — *we are a courtesy there*, Smith said, and turned back to the trace. So the one person in the case with no authority anywhere is the one who ends up carrying its only complete account from one side of the ring to the other, which is a thing junior officers are for and nobody tells them.

The name is the Deputy's own; the service suffix that comm protocol appends to common names has not yet had reason to attach to it.
