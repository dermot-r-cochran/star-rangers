Jorun Madaki kept a count for nineteen years on the reasoning that a count nobody keeps is a count somebody will later round. The station sent her to the one room where the new corps has to answer, and she went with a book ruled in two columns.

## Known History

- Kept Halyard Station's supply route book for nineteen years to 2723 UCSD and sat on the station's council of fourteen. She filed the supply request that the Directorate of Operations' tables recorded as one hundred and twenty-two days from request to authorised order, and counted the days as they went. When Delegate [Petra Solano](/star-rangers/characters/petra-solano/) read that row to the Consolidation Hearing in 2719 UCSD as the row had it, Madaki sent for the page of the transcript and did not mention it.
- Six weeks after the Charter's signing, 2723 UCSD, she initialled the council's sheet appointing Solano to the Oversight Council and wrote *term?* beside her initials. Solano declined the seat in the room. Asked what anyone should be held to in it, Madaki gave three conditions: three years, a written quarterly report in open session of what was asked and what was told, and recall by a counted vote. The convenor added a fourth. The council appointed her by eight in fourteen; her own hand went up for someone else. [Counted (S00E04C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s00/e04/s00e04c01/).
- That night she read the charter from the front and entered her first question for the Council, on the voluntary outer-station contributions and who keeps the row.

## Character Notes

She reads a document as she reads a manifest: for what is on it, and then for what is not. She was glad of the six votes she did not get.

The record does not hold what she was told. What the first session answered, how her three years went, whether the route book came back to her, and anything of her life outside the supply office and the council room are absent, and this page does not supply them. She is placed nowhere after the night of her appointment.
