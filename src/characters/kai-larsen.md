Kai Larsen resigned his Survey Corps commission the third time a case died at a self-governing habitat's border — not because the habitat's government refused to help, but because nobody serving under the Solar System Defence Command had the standing to ask. Rangers [have no authority inside self-governing orbital habitats](/star-rangers/lore/military-space-command/); neither, in practice, did anyone else with the training to actually run an investigation. The resignation had to come first: a serving Ranger cannot be seconded into a body with police authority inside a habitat. His Corps certifications lapsed with his commission, and whatever distinctions his guild had awarded him stay on its record ([Qualifications, Distinctions and Guild Awards](/star-rangers/lore/guild-distinctions/)).

Governor [Petra Voss](/star-rangers/characters/petra-voss/), presiding over the five habitats bound by the [Orbital Habitats Compact](/star-rangers/lore/orbital-habitats-compact/), had been arguing the same problem from the other direction for two years: her habitats had jurisdiction and no investigative capacity worth the name. She commissioned a standing task force to close the gap and gave Larsen the commission, on the theory that a former Ranger frustrated enough to resign over a jurisdictional wall was exactly the kind of person who would enjoy taking one down.

The unit answers to the Governor directly for operational tasking, bypasses the Compact's slower consultative councils entirely, and covers all five member habitats. Administratively, it also reports through Chief Commissioner [Dorian Calloway](/star-rangers/characters/dorian-calloway/)'s office — the coordination layer that keeps five habitats' Commissioners from being blindsided by a Five-O visit — though Calloway has no say in where Larsen actually goes. Everyone, including the Compact's own administration, calls it Orbital Five-O. Nobody has proposed a better name, and Larsen has stopped pretending he'd accept one.

## Character Notes

Larsen runs the unit the way he ran a Ranger boarding team: small, trusted, and answerable to him personally rather than to a chain of command that might, somewhere upstream, decide the case isn't worth the paperwork.

He does not raise his voice. He has never needed to. Officers who worked under him in the Survey Corps describe the same quality that now unsettles habitat administrators who assumed a Governor's task force commander would be easier to manage than an actual Ranger.

## Jurisdiction

Five-O's authority runs exactly as far as the Compact's five habitats and not one metre further — a limit Larsen enforces on himself as strictly as he'd have once enforced Ranger boundary protocol, on the theory that an investigator who ignores his own jurisdiction is not better than the system he left, only quieter about it. The same reasoning gave the unit its standing courtesy. Since the late autumn of 2826 UCSD, Five-O writes to a habitat's Commissioner before it comes onto that habitat's deck on its own account, saying what it will do, when, and on what reference. A visit at a bureau's own call is a different matter ([Nobody at the Desk (S04E00C02)](/star-rangers/seasons/s04/e00/s04e00c02/)).

## The Form in the Bottom Drawer

The oldest open file in the task force's cabinet was a referral from Eden's bureau. In the late autumn of 2826 UCSD Detective Inspector [Wendell Albercombe](/star-rangers/characters/wendell-albercombe/) handed Larsen a ration manifest for eleven crates signed out of Eden's stores, with four transfer stamps and no fifth ([The Desk That Called First (S02E01C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s02/e01/s02e01c01/)). Larsen had the Compact's manifest show the routing code that Eden's screens left out, and printed it ([The Office That Reads Everything (S02E01C02)](/star-rangers/seasons/s02/e01/s02e01c02/)). He then went to Halcyon without writing first to its Commissioner, and copied the Governor ([A Very Ordinary Call (S02E01C03)](/star-rangers/seasons/s02/e01/s02e01c03/)). On Halcyon he asked Commissioner Duarte about the carrier, Threnody Logistics, and read its contract, which was in order. He did not show the form to Halcyon's stores or to anyone else there. He brought it back, and it stayed in the cabinet while the inquiry stood on the quarter's sheet as *carried* at eight closes ([Not Shown (S04E07C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s04/e07/s04e07c01/)).

In the autumn of 2828 UCSD [Antariksha](/star-rangers/lore/antariksha/)'s bureau sent the task force its first referral: a transfer form for six cases of lamp tube, with three stamps and no fourth. Larsen wrote one letter for both forms, to all five Commissioners, copied to the Governor and the Chief Commissioner. It asked whichever stores held either receipt to strike the stamp on the form itself, against their own ledger. It asserted nothing about either consignment, said that the task force had held Eden's form since 2826 and had shown it to no stores, and carried no annex. Halcyon's Commissioner's office answered within a day that its stores ledger held the eleven crates, received in the autumn of 2826. Eden's answered the day after that its own stores held Antariksha's six cases. Larsen sent each original to the stores that held its receipt, and did not ask for either form to come back through his office ([Not Shown (S04E07C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s04/e07/s04e07c01/)).

His close of the 2826 inquiry found no offence, and nobody was charged. It states on the task force's own record that the task force had read a missing stamp as somebody's doing, had gone to Halcyon to find whose, and had not shown the form to Halcyon's stores. It does not say why the leg was reassigned, which is on neither paper and which the task force did not ask. It recommended nothing. To the Governor it referred a seam: a habitat is not shown a leg reassigned after its own dock, is not told that another habitat's form waits on its stamp, and has no office to ask. Antariksha's bureau asked whether forms of that kind should come up to the task force in future. Larsen answered that they should not, since a Commissioner's office can write to the other four through the liaison line the Chief Commissioner keeps, and only an answer that is something other than a stamp is the task force's ([Struck Late (S04E07C02)](/star-rangers/seasons/s04/e07/s04e07c02/)).
