Kirsi Lund heard the same sentence in two rooms that were each certain what it meant. The record has one page of hers, with a line at the foot she expected to lose a mark for.

## Known History

- Born on Mars; sixteen in 2719 UCSD, when the nine seconds of General [Maren Solveig Krast](/star-rangers/characters/maren-solveig-krast/)'s testimony ran for a week on a wall screen outside her district's school hall. Her mother switched it off at home on the last word.
- An officer cadet at Solar Command in 2723 UCSD, in an intake under the Solar System Defence Command's name. In its tenth week a captain of engineers gave the general's sentence as a toast, and she stood with the mess.
- The next afternoon, held at a pressure door on the car to her mother's district, she asked the civilian beside her whether the fourth section of *On the Cost of Speed* was ever examined, and then whether she should read it. She read it. She did not ask the woman's name, and took the paper's author for long dead. [Not Examined (S00E05C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s00/e05/s00e05c01/).
- Her one-page summary gave the conditions a paragraph and ended: *Query: by whom are the conditions tested?*

## Character Notes

She can tell, on a car, the people who work for the Command from the people who used to. She had ridden under hold boards all her life and had never read one to the end.

The woman on the car was [Sildron Drey](/star-rangers/characters/sildron-drey/). Lund did not know it, and nothing in the record says she learned it. What mark the page received, whether she was commissioned, and everything after that evening are absent. She is placed nowhere after 2723 UCSD.
