Lukas Akande is a [Principal](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-command-hierarchy/) on the general operations track. In the autumn of 2828 UCSD Chief Ranger [Tissadelle Shepherd](/star-rangers/characters/tissadelle-shepherd/) put his name on the roster of the general operations department she was forming for the Survey Corps' seventh revisit to [Counterpane](/star-rangers/lore/counterpane-archecluster/). He was one of three Principals among its fourteen names. By then he had been on the Corps' lists nineteen years and had stood custody on four benches, none of them through a fold.

## Known History

- **2829 UCSD, Late Winter:** on the ninth night of the department's ten-day rehearsal carry at the revisit's staging site, standing the middle custody watch alone as the bill then had it, he could not account for four minutes. The seals were intact and the count correct. He struck *attended throughout* from the custody line and entered the interval as not certified. The Chief Ranger let the entry stand, began the carry again, and amended her own bill so that no custody watch was stood alone. He kept the middle watch, with a Deputy he had been teaching in his own forenoons beside him ([The Carry (S03E04C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s03/e04/s03e04c01/)).

## Character Notes

Akande does the sum. He reads a seal number against the line with a finger under it when it matters, and reads an entry back for a word that claims more than he knows and for one that claims less.

Asked what a duty has cost him, he says that he is fine.

The record holds nothing of Akande before the roster beyond the nineteen years and the four benches: no birthplace, no age, and no earlier posting by name. It does not say where the Corps sent him when the department ended in the late spring of 2829.
