Meril Duskkin keeps a flood-gate. On [Verdance](/star-rangers/lore/planets/verdance/) the fenlands are a vast tidal marsh network fed by seasonal flood cycles, and the [Fenmarch Republic](/star-rangers/lore/planets/verdance/#four-countries) that governs them does so through a rotating engineering council, answerable to the **Tidewrights' Guild**, which maintains the flood-cycle infrastructure the Republic's stilted cities depend on and treats that maintenance as the Republic's core civic duty. A gate across a fen channel is a piece of that infrastructure, and keeping one is his trade, and so, without anyone in the Guild having to say so, is keeping a stilted city's water where it should be. He is the first person of the fenlands the record names, and the second [Verdani](/star-rangers/lore/verdani/) after [Isren Farrowkin](/star-rangers/characters/isren-farrowkin/), who is of the Canopy Concord and has never, in any account the record holds, stood in fen light. The Archive calls him the first of nothing else; a gate is somebody's work.

He was bonded into the **Duskkin** lineage in the fenlands, in a settlement the record does not name, in a year it does not hold. The people's page names Duskkin among the kinstrain names an outside observer notices first, beside Farrowkin and Rootkin, and says what such a name is: not a birthplace but the thallenwort lineage a child was bonded into through direct kinbed-to-kinbed contact in infancy, which Verdani culture treats as the foundational kinship act and Verdani law as kinship in the fullest sense it recognises. Every Duskkin he has never met is his kin. His kinbeds run the duller olive-brown of a fenland strain adapted to reduced light beneath marsh mist, which the page gives as a reasonably reliable indicator of kinstrain and home biome and of nothing about him beyond that, and the Verdani caution outsiders against reading more. The record reads no more.

The name is given as the Federation record gives it: a personal name and a kinstrain name, in the order the people's page describes. What *Meril* means, and whether the Verdani give a personal name at bonding or later, the record does not state. The record renders him as *he*, on the translation default; the people's page declares no pronoun system, and the record's other Verdani are rendered by the same default.

## Known History

- Bonded into the Duskkin lineage in the fenlands of Verdance, in the Calyx system, in a settlement and a year the record does not hold. No Verdani age is given anywhere in the record, which holds no span for a Verdani life and no conversion of Verdance's local years to UCSD.
- A tidewright of the Fenmarch Republic's Tidewrights' Guild in the record's present, 2826 UCSD; keeps one flood-gate across a fen channel. How long it has been his, and how a gate comes to a tidewright, the record does not say.
- Never off Verdance, and never out of the fenlands, in any account the record holds. The people's page gives the reason as a cost rather than a choice: a strain is adapted to one biome's light quality, humidity and canopy density, and a Verdani carrying a fenland lineage into any other country's light is carrying a partner that will do less well there, permanently.
- Not a member of the rotating engineering council, not a delegate of any kind, and not placed by the record at any session of the Fourfold Accord or of the [Federation assembly](/star-rangers/lore/federation-of-sentient-beings/).

## The Work

The planet's page gives the fenlands in one clause and the Republic in one more, and the trade sits where they meet: a marsh network fed by seasonal flood cycles, and cities built on stilts above it that depend on infrastructure to keep the cycle where the cities need it. The gate is the one piece of that infrastructure the record has pictured. On the planet's own page, across a broad fen channel, runs the long low pale line of a flood-gate, plain and seamless, the kind of works that keeps a stilted city's water where it should be. It is his, or one like it. What the gate is made of, the record does not say, and does not need to: the people's page says what Verdani industry is, cultivation rather than furnace, grown materials and bred organisms and processes measured in seasons and run at ambient temperature across very large areas, a civilisation that never looked like industry to anyone arriving expecting smoke. A tidewright's works are of that industry. The record holds no account of how a gate is grown, set, or mended, and this page does not supply one.

What it does hold is the pace. The Verdani are the record's clearest case of a technological people limited by energy per day: a symbiont-supplemented metabolism is a reliable one and not a large one, and it cannot be forced, so haste has a real price, paid in metabolism rather than money, and a people for whom this is literally true develops a politics in which patience is not a virtue but an accounting fact. In the fens the budget is thinner than anywhere else on Verdance, since the strain is bred for reduced light under mist, and the cycle the gate answers is a season's, not an afternoon's. So the trade is slow in the way the people's page says all Verdani work is slow, and exact in the way the Guild's word for it, *duty*, implies. A flood cycle that arrives is not an emergency. A flood cycle that arrives and finds a gate not ready is the failure the Guild exists to prevent, and it is prevented in the seasons before, at the pace the light allows.

Across the Republic's council sits the **Drylanders' Union**, a growing bloc that wants permanent drained land and views the Guild's flood-cycle orthodoxy as holding the Republic back. The planet's page states both positions and adjudicates neither, and this page follows it. The record does not hold Duskkin's view of the Union, has no reason to think a tidewright has been asked for one, and notes only that a drained fen is a fen whose light has changed, and that the people's page says what a changed light costs a lineage bred for the old one. Whether that is the Guild's argument or merely a fact the Guild lives beside, the record does not say.

## What He Cannot Do

Hurry, as the people's page says of every Verdani: he cannot decide to have a harder afternoon than the light allows. Leave the fens for the canopy, the terraces or the steppe without his partner doing worse for it, permanently. Hold still in good light without eating, since a Verdani's vanes opening is not display or mood but a meal, and under marsh mist there is less of it to take. Stand upright, present a face, or look at one thing: the people's page gives no single face, sensory structures distributed along the leading margins where the vanes meet, and a body that orients continuously toward the light in the way a person aboard a ship holds their balance. Speak for the Republic, which the engineering council does, or for Verdance, which the Fourfold Accord does.

## Character Notes

The record holds his manner from his trade and from the people's page, since no chapter has met him, and both are exact in a way an outsider mistakes for slowness. A tidewright's season is budgeted the way a delegate from Verdance budgets anything, by deciding in advance what it will not contain; the one Verdani the record has watched in a chapter did exactly that with two days in a slot's light, and nothing on the people's page suggests the habit is hers alone. He does not mend in haste what the cycle will test in its own time. He does not treat the Guild's orthodoxy as orthodoxy: on the planet's page it is maintenance, and the maintenance is the Republic's core civic duty, and the record has no account of his finding that a heavy word.

He lives under the dimmest sky his people keep. The people's page ranks the four strains by their light, from the deep green of the filtered canopy to the pale, high-reflectance strains of the unfiltered terraces, and the fenland strain's olive-brown is the answer to the least of it: reduced light beneath marsh mist, taken by vanes bred to take it. In the chapter the record holds, Isren Farrowkin's kinbeds, High-Reach green and raised in the Understory's shade, went out to a low sun after two folded days and began, at last, to eat. What a fenland Verdani's vanes do when the mist thins over a channel, the record has not watched, and this page does not perform it.

Above him, eight thousand kilometres up, [Greenward](/star-rangers/lore/greenward-habitat/) keeps a watch nobody wrote into its instrument: the only place from which certain planet-wide changes on Verdance are visible as they happen rather than as they are reported. A fen drained, or a gate failed, would be one such change. Greenward does not report them; it keeps the record, dates it, and holds it against the day somebody with standing asks. The record places no one from Greenward in the fens, since nobody from Greenward may go down, and places Duskkin at no window looking up. The two watches have never met, and the record does not say which of them would be surprised by the other.

The record gives him no kin by name beyond the lineage, no day, and no account of his own in his own words. It places him on the works of a flood-gate across a fen channel, in the fenlands of Verdance in 2826 UCSD, under marsh mist with a season's water on one side of the gate and a stilted city's on the other, and nowhere else.

## What the Record Does Not Carry

His age, and the span of a Verdani life, neither stated anywhere; the record holds no conversion of Verdance's years. The settlement he was bonded in and the city his gate serves, since the planet's page names no fenland place. What *Meril* means, when a personal name is given, and whether a tidewright is addressed by it or by the lineage. How a gate is grown, set or mended, what it is made of, and how one comes to a tidewright; whether a gate has one keeper or a watch of the Guild. What raises the tide the fenlands are fed by, which the record has not named. What lives in the fens, native or bred, since the record names no organism on Verdance beyond the thallenwort. Whether a fenland Verdani carries any partner but thallenwort, which the record's own file on heredity says is likely common on Verdance far beyond the Verdani and has not shown. Whether he has met a Ranger, a surveyor or a delegate, since the record brings none of them to the fens, and nobody from Greenward may come down at all. His view of the Drylanders' Union, and whether the Guild holds one view or several. How Verdani speak, since the record has described no channel of their language; what the Duskkin name says in it. Any chapter. The body, beyond what the people's page gives every Verdani and the fenland strain's colour: the portrait, when it is made, will be the picture's reading and not the page's.
