Merrin Aldwater wrote *The Round and Other Impossibilities*, printed by Second Watch Press in 2825 UCSD. The library on Eden Space Habitat shelves a copy as fiction, and the Codex carries its first story, *[The Hour Nobody Had](/star-rangers/codex/the-hour-nobody-had/)*, with the author's closing note.

## Known History

- Kept a closure ledger for nine years before writing stories, by the closing note's own account: the bookkeeping in which the offsets logged around a circuit of courier runs [must sum to zero](/star-rangers/lore/universal-cosmic-stardate/#reduction-and-closure).
- Has read about [Drithane](/star-rangers/lore/planets/drithane/) and its custom of putting the lights out for the sparks, and has not been there.
- Counts a timekeeper among their acquaintances, who read the story and remarked only on the length of the round.

## Character Notes

Aldwater's fantasy is built from the working furniture of the present: a courier's round, a ledger, a [plainmark](/star-rangers/glossary/plainmark/) on a hatch frame, a mind that declines. The stories make these do what they cannot. The closing note then says which three things were invented, and adds that the rest was not. It is a writer's courtesy to the trade the stories borrow from, and the reason the book is easy to file: nothing in it is offered as an account of anything that happened.

The record holds nothing of Aldwater beyond that story and its note. No species or age is stated. Nor is a home, or where the ledger was kept and for whom. The record does not say what the book's other stories are, or whether there is a second book.
