Mette Sarpong had never, in twenty-six years, been stopped at the first step of the method. At Eden she was, and the finding she wrote there is a leaf and a half.

## Known History

- An assessor of the Solar System Safety Standards Authority for twenty-six years to 2723 UCSD: pressure seals, life-support integration, and for her last nine years the instruments of boundary-proximate stations. She came into the [Safety Corps](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-safety-corps/) when the Authority was absorbed at the founding, and struck the old heading through on her certificate blanks by instruction.
- Three months after the Charter's signing she chaired the certification board at [Eden Space Habitat](/star-rangers/lore/eden-space-habitat/) that certified the first transit profile for the Eden corridor: to the confirmed transit point and return, with a turn-back criterion read before the threshold, and a requirement that every order under it state its limits and where they come from. She lodged it with the Defence Command's monitoring post at the ring, whose file of authorised envelopes had been kept in order since 2712 and held no entries.
- She declined to certify the *Constant Margin* transit of 2712 as flown, finding no fault in the flying and no written criterion it could have failed against, and certified [Beatrix Kilbride](/star-rangers/characters/beatrix-kilbride/) as instructor on the bounded profile. The Survey Corps' recruiting officer had asked for the other finding. He carried this one as written, and she entered in the board's minute that he had not asked again. [As Flown (S00E06C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s00/e06/s00e06c01/).

## Character Notes

She put the stamp down before telling a Survey Corps officer what he could and could not ask of her, so as not to be holding it. She did not enjoy setting a turn-back line from the log of one hull, and noted in the standard that she had.

Where she was born, what she assessed before Eden beyond the three kinds of work named, and where the Corps posted her afterward are absent from the record. She is placed nowhere after 2723 UCSD.
