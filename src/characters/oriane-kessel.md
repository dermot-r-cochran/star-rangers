Oriane Kessel keeps the shielding. On [Corryn Prime](/star-rangers/lore/planets/corryn/) every settlement is built for it, because the planet's natural fields interfere with human equipment, and a thing built for a purpose has to be kept to it: seals age, bonds loosen, a loop that was continuous when it was laid is found one morning to be two loops with a gap. Finding the gap is her trade. She is the first person of the colony the record names, and the Archive calls her the first of nothing else: a settlement's shielding is somebody's work, as its glass or its ledgers are elsewhere in the record.

She was born in the colony, in a settlement the record does not name, to a family that had been there for generations the record does not count. The colony itself is older than any family in it. It was chartered during the [Imperium](/star-rangers/lore/the-imperium/) period, by a prospecting venture backed by one of the industrial holdings behind the Imperium's fleet, and it stood for the better part of two centuries at a scale the planet absorbed. The Imperium fell in 2609 UCSD; the settlement did not notice in any way the record holds, and nothing arrived to replace what had chartered it. The concern that holds the charter now is the [Halvern Combine](/star-rangers/lore/halvern-combine/). The settlement is the Combine's, and so, in the only sense the charter provides, is the work she does in it.

The name is given as the record holds it, and nothing more is known of it. The record does not hold the colony's languages, its naming customs, or what two and a half centuries seven hundred light years from the Inner System have done to either, and does not say whether *Kessel* is a family's name or a settlement's. The record renders her as *she*.

## Known History

- Born in the colony on Corryn Prime, in a settlement the record does not name, in about 2788 UCSD. The year is the record's reading from her working age in 2826 and is not a date the colony has given.
- A child when the mining and agricultural expansion began, in about 2796 UCSD, that the planet does not absorb. She does not remember the settlement at its older scale in any account the record holds, and the record has not asked her for one.
- Keeper of the shielding at her settlement in the record's present, 2826 UCSD. How long she has kept it, and from whom she learned it, the record does not say.
- Never off Corryn Prime. The record places her at the settlement and nowhere else, and has no reason to place her anywhere else.

## The Work

Shielding. The colony's page gives the fact and the irony in one sentence: the settlements are built for electromagnetic shielding because the planet's fields interfere with human equipment, and *the colony shields itself carefully from the fields, and shields the Spiralites from nothing*. The second half of that sentence is a finding about the colony. The first half is Kessel's working day.

A shielded building is only as sound as its worst seam. Her work is the seams: the bonding where one panel meets the next, the seals at every opening, the loops laid in the ground under the building and bonded to its skin, which have to be continuous or they are nothing. She walks the line of a building with an instrument that reads what is getting in, and where the reading rises she has a seam to open, clean, and close again. On a bad day she has a loop to dig. The planet's fields are not weather; they do not pass. A seal that fails is a seal that has failed for good until she gets to it, and the equipment inside tells the building's people before any instrument does, by doing what shielded equipment is not supposed to do.

The work is quiet in the one sense the record can be exact about. Inside a sound building, the planet's fields are out. Whether the [Carillon's](/star-rangers/lore/planets/corryn/) hum, which ordinary equipment detects, is out with them, and where the system's own scientists do their listening and from what, the record does not say. What the colony's mining and agriculture put out across the bands the [Spiralites](/star-rangers/lore/planets/corryn/) use is on the page, measured and undisputed, and none of it is hers to keep in or out. Her shielding faces one way, and it is the way every shielding on the planet faces.

## Character Notes

The record holds her manner from her trade and from nothing else, since no chapter has met her, and the trade is exact. A gap in a loop is in one place and not another, and a keeper who guesses digs twice. She does not volunteer a reading she has not taken. When a building's people tell her their equipment has gone strange, she asks what it did and when, and goes to look, and the record has no account of her telling anyone what they ought to have noticed sooner.

She lives under a sky that has the Carillon in it. At certain phases its rings carry colour the eye can see, and the colony's page says the association between the Carillon and the dreams some people in the system report is old, widespread and entirely sincere. Whether she is one of those people, the record does not say, and the mechanism the record is firm about, that a mind near a thinning place registers [the membrane next door](/star-rangers/lore/dreams-across-the-interval/) and that nothing sends, would not be hers to know or to doubt. She keeps the fields out of buildings. What is in the sky is in the sky.

The word *remnant* is the record's. The colony's page calls the settlement a remnant of the Old Imperium, and so it is, by the charter: a permission issued by a body that regarded consent as a demonstrated failure, exercised two centuries after the body ended. Whether anyone in the colony calls it that, or calls it anything, the record does not hold. Kessel was born on a world her family had lived on for generations, in a settlement that has stood for longer than the [Star Rangers](/star-rangers/lore/formation-of-star-rangers/) have existed, and she has never been asked a question by anyone with standing to ask it, because nobody with standing has ever landed. The Combine's position, stated without heat, is that it holds a valid charter and has broken no term of it; every part of that is true, and none of it is a thing she was asked about either.

The record gives her no kin by name, no day, and no account of her own in her own words. It places her at a seam of a shielded building in a settlement on Corryn Prime in 2826 UCSD, with an instrument in one hand and the reading rising, and nowhere else.

## What the Record Does Not Carry

The settlement's name, where on Corryn Prime it stands, and what it mines or grows. Her kin. The colony's languages, its naming customs, and what either owes to the Inner System of the Imperium period. Whether the colony's people are the Combine's employees, its tenants, or something the charter has no word for, and who pays a keeper of shielding. How a settlement decides anything, and whether the colony has any body of its own beside the charter. Whether its machines are [Kernel-compliant](/star-rangers/lore/ai-safety-kernel/), which the record declines to settle because settling it would settle part of the standing case. Whether she has ever seen a Spiralite, or any person of the colony has, and how, since the record has not established how anyone meets a people who speak in a medium the colony is drowning out. Whether she knows the [Protocols'](/star-rangers/lore/frontier-transformation-protocols/) plain reading, or the Combine's filings, or anything of the case in which her settlement is the standing example. Whether the dreams are hers. Any chapter. The body, beyond what a working life out of doors under an orange star gives anyone: the record states nothing of it, and the portrait, when it is made, will be the picture's reading and not the page's.
