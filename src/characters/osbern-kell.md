Osbern Kell is a reciter: one of the people by whom the court of the [Kingdom of the Five Islands](/star-rangers/lore/planets/kingdom-of-the-five-islands/) keeps its chronicle of descent and deed, which is kept by being said. He is seventy-three, lives at Kalenjar on Ostrel, and has been saying the chronicle for fifty-one years.

## What the Record Holds

In the survey's third year on Vellamar he took a new line of the Castle's part of the chronicle from [Wren, of Knight Pellam](/star-rangers/characters/wren-pellam/), in her father's hall above Kalenjar: *a door was given, on Krilzat, in Pellam's time, on the right side past the ninth, and one stands in it*. A line is taken by being said and said back until the reciter can say it alone. This one has no name at the end of it, and he had to learn to stop where it stops.

At the next calm he carried the line up the steps of the Abbeylands on Aduine, as the court does when a line is new, and asked whether the Long Accounting has the room. He came back with the Abbess's answer: the Accounting has the room, it was always there, and it has a name for the one standing in it, which the Abbey does not offer. He did not ask for the name. He has the court's letters and cannot read the Abbey's hand. The record holds his errand from the Abbey's side in [*Always There* (S13E02C02)](/star-rangers/seasons/s13/e02/s13e02c02/) and from his own in [*Said Back* (S13E03C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s13/e03/s13e03c01/).

He had climbed those steps twice before, once for a crowning and once for a death.

In the survey's fourth year he climbed them again, ten days before a crowning, since a crowning's line is new and a new line goes up the steps before it goes under the Grove. He said it at the top in the reciting voice, asked what the court sent him to ask, and was answered. The old monarch's close had gone up at the season's end in another reciter's mouth.

At the crowning he said the chronicle under the Elder Grove with the court's reciters, his second crowning. The line given in Pellam's time was said there by Wren, alone, with no name. He stood in its stop with the rest, and none of the nine from the court's long room made a sound in it. The reciters came in together on *and the rooms are more than the names*, with nobody beating time, and he heard one voice at the rim say it at the pace of a line and finish after them. He did not go out to the rim afterwards. The record holds the morning in [*As Long as a Name* (S13E08C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s13/e08/s13e08c01/).

## What the Record Does Not Carry

Who taught him the chronicle, and whether he has seen the Castle. His house or rank, if he has either. Which crowning and which death he climbed for, and what the Accounting answered. What the court sent him to ask in the survey's fourth year, and what he was answered. Anything the delegation knows of him beyond a line said at the top of the steps, which its linguist wrote down with the pauses marked.

## Character Notes

He holds the chronicle the way reciters hold it, by the ends of its lines, and is exact about where a line stops. His reading of the Castle's people is the court's and never the record's.
