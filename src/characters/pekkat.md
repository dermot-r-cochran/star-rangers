Pekkat proves things in rock. A Chthonari burrow settlement is tuned as it is dug, so that the settlement itself rings, and in the part of the [Undersong Belt](/star-rangers/lore/undersong-belt/) where Pekkat works, some of that tuning is mathematics: a relationship between parts of the structure that holds, felt directly by anyone standing on it, or does not.

The name is a struck signature, as every [Chthonari](/star-rangers/lore/chthonari/) name is: a strike, the same strike held, and a scrape left open to ring, spelled the way a human ear renders it. The record renders Pekkat as *they*, as it renders every Chthonari person.

## Known History

- Born in the Undersong Belt, in a burrow settlement the record does not name. Which settlement, and what it is tuned to, is the Belt's own business.
- Tutors Federation mathematicians who come to the Belt to study under Chthonari teachers, the practice the species page records. A student who wants to learn from Pekkat has to stand on the rock. Nothing Pekkat teaches reaches a student through air, and nothing reaches a student who is not in contact with structure.
- Active in 2826 UCSD. The record does not fix how long Pekkat has taught, or how many students have come.

## Character Notes

Chthonari philosophy has argued for millennia whether the distinction between a numerical ratio and a harmonic one is worth maintaining at all, since to a people who perceive resonance directly the two are close to the same fact. Pekkat keeps the distinction, and the reason is one a human mathematician would recognise. A settlement can be retuned. A theorem cannot. If retuning the rock would change the result, what was carried there was a harmony. If the result survives every retuning, it was a theorem, and the rock was only the way it was written.

That is the same line the record draws in [Mathematics and Its Notations](/star-rangers/lore/mathematics-and-its-notations/): the theorem is discovered and holds across the whole regime, and the notation is a people's own. Pekkat's students report what Federation students of Chthonari tutors have always reported. They arrive at the same theorems by a different road, and what took them years of abstraction is, for a Chthonari student, nearer to hearing.

The record does not translate Pekkat's proofs into written mathematics, and it would be wrong to. A proof carried in resonance and its written counterpart prove the same theorem. They are not the same proof, and the record keeps them as two.

## What Pekkat Cannot Do

Hear a voice, or read a face. Teach anyone who will not stand on the rock. Carry a proof off the Belt except as a description of one, which, by Pekkat's own standard, is a different thing.
