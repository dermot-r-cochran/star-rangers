Sevrane is [Krenyi](/star-rangers/lore/krenyi/), one of the Quiet-Built, formerly of the [Star Rangers](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-command-hierarchy/), and the author of one document in the record, [What Still Does Work](/star-rangers/codex/what-still-does-work/): a history of her people set down in her own hand at the Survey Archive's receiving desk at Threshold Station, in the Archive's working language, at the Archive's asking through the Corps' rolls of former officers. The receiving note says what she was asked for and what she gave: the desk asked for the history in whatever form her people keep one in, and she gave it in their own count, which the reader does not have, since that was what the desk had asked for.

*Nai* before her name is the state-marker her people use, a courtesy meaning *still relevant here*, and not part of the name. She is *she* in the account and in the desk's note, as every Krenyi woman on the record is.

## Known History

- Came to the Corps as her people do, by asking counsel of those whose counsel she wanted, being given it, and going; the account gives no count for it, because the Corps holds the station's date for her going on its rolls, and a count of hers set beside a date of the reader's would be a conversion, which she declines to make ([What Still Does Work](/star-rangers/codex/what-still-does-work/)).
- Served, in a corps and at a rank the account does not give, long enough beside mortal-lifespan colleagues to see one of them out: a man who was good at the work and was asked about it. Her grief, she writes, was real and finished; what she keeps of him is a way of reading an instrument, which she has passed to two others who do not know where it came from.
- Left the Corps in a year the record does not hold. Her rank left her speech on the day the work stopped, as [a Krenyi rank does](/star-rangers/lore/the-ladder-in-other-tongues/#krenyi-what-the-officer-does-now); it is on the Corps' rolls, and the receiving desk agreed, at her asking, not to set it beside her name.
- Answered the Survey Archive's asking for a history of the Krenyi by one of them, came to the receiving desk at Threshold Station, wrote the account there, read it back once and passed it. The year of the asking is not given in the receiving note. Asked by the desk to set the Archive's date beside her own, she declined, with the reason the account gives.

## Character Notes

The account shows a person who gives a history the way her people keep one, as the things that still return something to the present, each at its place in a count, and who says at the start that the shortness is not a withholding and then marks exactly what is withheld: the count's beginning, which would be a placing; its unit, which set beside the station's would be a conversion; her own going, for the same reason; and her rank, which does no work now. She gives the oldest kept thing in her people's count as a gap, two figures kept together with nothing at either, and calls it the thing that does the most work of anything they keep. She says of her people, in her own voice, what their page says of them, that they are poor keepers of what has not proved its use, and does not soften it.

She writes at the reader's speed, in full sentences, and gives the three exchanges she quotes in her people's own register, short. The one dry line in the account, that the settlement's write-up of a Champion is a measurement of the settlement, is written in the register for things that are the case, and the desk did not mark it as anything else. Asked where her people are from she says *here*, says in words that this is a declining and not an evasion, and says that a secret hides that there is anything to tell and she is telling the reader that there is.

## What the Record Does Not Carry

Her age, in either reckoning. Her corps, her rank and her postings, which the Corps' rolls hold and the account does not. The community she came from, which the record neither names nor places, as it names and places no Krenyi's. What the count she gives runs from, and the length of its unit, which she withheld; whether every Krenyi keeps the one count, which the Archive did not ask. Where she lives now, and what she does. Whether she knew [Syra](/star-rangers/characters/syra/), [Toval](/star-rangers/characters/toval/), [Ilvane](/star-rangers/characters/ilvane/) or [Arashobal](/star-rangers/characters/arashobal/), the record does not say; the Corps is large and a Krenyi officer is a rarity on any roster. The colleague she saw out, whom the account does not name. Her body, which no page describes beyond her people's: built on the human frame, the only other people in the record who are, almost human at a glance and not at a second look. No portrait is on file.
