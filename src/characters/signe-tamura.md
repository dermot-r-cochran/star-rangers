Signe Tamura is a [Principal](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-command-hierarchy/) on the Survey track. She came out to Halyx Relay Station in the late spring of 2828 UCSD to take the backlog of archived Hegemony survey telemetry that the [Survey Corps](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-survey-corps/) clears under its standing data-exchange with the [Cerebraun Hegemony](/star-rangers/lore/cerebraun-hegemony/). The hand-over file she was given held a ratified reclassification, a closed consultation, and a method of running a raw stream as audio, four paragraphs long.

## Known History

- **2828 UCSD, Late Spring:** took the Halyx queue. In her first week she ran the [Sentinel](/star-rangers/lore/the-sentinel/)'s stream by the method and heard it as documented.
- **2828 to 2829 UCSD:** by a rule of her own, gave every stream whose classifier note read *no protocol match* one minute as sound before confirming the note. In fourteen months she heard nothing in 2,214 streams. One, in the spring of 2829, she heard once as a figure and not again on four replays, and counted as nothing.
- **2829 UCSD, Summer:** asked by the Survey library for a statement of use, she gave the count, the one stream, the control, and the sentence that she could not say whether there had been nothing to hear. The library filed the method as a technique sheet and recorded her streams as unverified ([Statement of Use (S03E05C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s03/e05/s03e05c01/)).

## Character Notes

Tamura keeps the count on a slate of her own, because the queue's form has no line for it. She writes for a reader who has never met her.

The record holds nothing of Tamura before Halyx: no birthplace, no age, and no earlier posting. It does not say how long her rotation there ran.
