Tavin Ledgekin holds a right. On [Verdance](/star-rangers/lore/planets/verdance/) the high terraces are a mineral-rich highland range of thin air and mineral-heavy soil, and the [Stonewake Dominion](/star-rangers/lore/planets/verdance/#four-countries) that governs them does so under an authority split in two: the **Deep Clans**, hereditary mining lineages who hold the mineral-extraction rights the Dominion's economy runs on, and the **Terrace Assemblies**, elected bodies of the alpine farming communities who supply the Dominion's food. The Ledgekin are one of the Deep Clans, and the right they hold is to take mineral from one stretch of the terraces. It is his because he is theirs. He is the first person of the Dominion the record names, and the fourth [Verdani](/star-rangers/lore/verdani/) after [Isren Farrowkin](/star-rangers/characters/isren-farrowkin/) of the Canopy Concord, [Meril Duskkin](/star-rangers/characters/meril-duskkin/) of the fenlands and [Elsha Rootkin](/star-rangers/characters/elsha-rootkin/) of the Survey Corps, none of whom has stood on the high terraces in any account the record holds. The Archive calls him the first of nothing else; a right is somebody's, and this one was somebody's before it was his.

He was bonded into the **Ledgekin** lineage on the high terraces, in a settlement the record does not name, in a year it does not hold. The people's page says what such a name is: not a birthplace but the thallenwort lineage a child was bonded into through direct kinbed-to-kinbed contact in infancy, which Verdani culture treats as the foundational kinship act and Verdani law as kinship in the fullest sense it recognises. The planet's page calls the Deep Clans *hereditary*, and on Verdance that word has two things it can mean, since the people's page holds parentage and bonding apart and calls the second the more binding. This page reads a Deep Clan as a kinstrain, so that its right passes as the strain passes, by bonding and not by birth, and a child bonded into the Ledgekin is Ledgekin whatever parentage brought the child to the bed. That is a reading of one word, and the record has shown no case either way. His kinbeds run the pale, high-reflectance colour of a high-terrace strain adapted to intense unfiltered highland sun, which the people's page gives as a reasonably reliable indicator of kinstrain and home biome and of nothing about him beyond that. The record reads no more.

The name is given as the Federation record gives it: a personal name and a kinstrain name, in the order the people's page describes. *Ledgekin* is not among the three kinstrain names the people's page lists, Farrowkin, Duskkin and Rootkin, which it gives as the ones an outside observer notices first, *and others*; what it says in any Verdani channel, and whether it names the clan or only the strain, the record does not state, and this page does not say the name means a ledge. What *Tavin* means, and when a personal name is given, the record does not state either. He is *he* in the record's translation register, on the same default that renders Meril Duskkin *he* and Isren Farrowkin and Elsha Rootkin *she*; the people's page declares no pronoun system, and this page asserts no sex.

## Known History

- Bonded into the Ledgekin lineage on the high terraces of Verdance, in the Calyx system, in a settlement and a year the record does not hold. The planet's own filed picture shows small settlements on the red upland where the four biomes meet, which is the terraces in that picture's reading of the four; it names none, and neither does this page. No Verdani age is given anywhere in the record, which holds no span for a Verdani life and no conversion of Verdance's local years to UCSD.
- Of the Ledgekin, one of the Stonewake Dominion's Deep Clans, in the record's present, 2826 UCSD; holds with his lineage the right to take mineral from one stretch of the high terraces. How a stretch comes to a clan, how long the Ledgekin have held theirs, and how many of them hold it with him, the record does not say.
- Never off Verdance, and never off the terraces, in any account the record holds. The people's page gives the reason as a cost rather than a choice: a strain is adapted to one biome's light quality, humidity and canopy density, and a high-terrace lineage carried down into the canopy's filtered light, the fens' mist or the steppe's seasons is carrying a partner that will do less well there, permanently.
- Not a member of any Terrace Assembly, which are the farming communities' bodies and not the clans'; not a delegate of any kind; not placed by the record at any session of the Fourfold Accord or of the [Federation assembly](/star-rangers/lore/federation-of-sentient-beings/).

## The Work

The planet's page gives the terraces in one clause and the Dominion in one more, and the right sits where they meet: a mineral-rich highland range of thin air and mineral-heavy soil, and an economy that runs on what is taken from it, under rights the Deep Clans hold. What is taken, and how, the record does not say, and the silence is wider than it looks. The people's page says what Verdani industry is: cultivation rather than furnace, grown materials and bred organisms and processes measured in seasons and run at ambient temperature across very large areas, a civilisation that never looked like industry to anyone arriving expecting smoke. Verdance never developed the furnace culture a fire-using people takes as the spine of its history. So whatever the Ledgekin take from their stretch of the terraces, the record gives them no fire to put it in, names nobody who takes it from them, and names no place it goes: the people's page gives the Verdani orbital trade platforms and a Federation seat, and the planet's page gives the Dominion's revenue only as a thing the Terrace Assemblies want a share of. A working of the Ledgekin is a working of the industry the people's page describes, and that is all the record holds of what one is. This page does not supply a method, a tool or a season.

What it does hold is the light. The people's page ranks the four strains by the sky each was bred for, and the high terraces are the top of the scale: intense unfiltered highland sun, met by a strain that is pale and high-reflectance, which is to say a partner bred to turn part of the day's light away rather than take all of it. What that comes to as a budget, against a canopy strain's filtered light or a fenland strain's mist, no page has measured, and this page does not guess; the people's page says only that no Verdani budget is a large one and none can be forced. Whether a working of a Deep Clan goes below the ground, the record does not say, and the clans' name is not glossed. It is noted here only that a body which eats by its surface eats nothing under a roof of rock, that the people's page would count that cost as it counts every hour in poor light, and that the record places Tavin Ledgekin on the terrace, in the sun, and nowhere under it.

Across the Dominion sit the Terrace Assemblies, the elected bodies of the alpine farming communities who supply the Dominion's food and increasingly demand a share of extraction revenue proportional to that dependency. The planet's page states both positions and adjudicates neither, and this page follows it. The record does not hold Ledgekin's view of the Assemblies' claim, and has no reason to think a holder of one right among a clan's has been asked for one. It notes only what the planet's page already implies: that a Deep Clan eats twice, once from the sun by its own kinbeds and once from the terraces by the Assemblies' work, and that the second meal is the dependency the Assemblies are counting. Whether the Assemblies argue it, or merely count it, is not on the record.

## What He Cannot Do

Hurry, as the people's page says of every Verdani: he cannot decide to have a harder afternoon than the light allows. Leave the terraces for the canopy, the fens or the steppe without his partner doing worse for it, permanently; a strain bred to turn away part of an unfiltered sun has nothing to turn away under a roof of leaves. Fold against the highland sun and still eat, since a Verdani's vanes opening is not display or mood but a meal. Stand upright, present a face, or look at one thing: the people's page gives no single face, sensory structures distributed along the leading margins where the vanes meet, and a body that orients continuously toward the light in the way a person aboard a ship holds their balance. Hold the right alone, since it is the lineage's. Speak for the Ledgekin, who are many and mostly unmet; for the Dominion, whose authority the planet's page splits between two bodies and gives whole to neither; or for Verdance, which the Fourfold Accord does.

## Character Notes

The record holds his manner from the people's page and from what his lineage holds, since no chapter has met him, and both are exact in a way an outsider mistakes for slowness. A right that was somebody's before it was his, and will be somebody's after, is held the way a Verdani holds anything, at the pace the light allows and with the season decided in advance; the one Verdani the record has watched in a chapter budgeted two days in a slot's light by deciding what the afternoon would not contain, and nothing on the people's page suggests the habit is hers alone. He does not take in a season what the terrace will still hold the next. He does not treat the right as a possession: on the planet's page it is held, and held by a lineage, and the record has no account of his finding *holds* a small word.

He lives under the hardest sky his people keep. The people's page runs the four strains by their light, from the deep green of the filtered canopy, through the olive-brown of the fens' mist, to the pale, high-reflectance strains of the unfiltered terraces, and his is the answer to the most of it: a partner that meets the sun by giving part of it back. In the chapter the record holds, Isren Farrowkin's kinbeds, High-Reach green and raised in the Understory's shade, went out to a low evening sun after two folded days and began, at last, to eat. What a terrace Verdani's vanes do at highland noon, with more light arriving than the strain was bred to keep, the record has not watched, and this page does not perform it.

The record's other Verdani look up. Isren Farrowkin's case to the Federation is that her people have an unusually direct stake in knowing exactly which light a given patch of ground receives; Elsha Rootkin asks of every shaded plot, first, how much light reaches the floor; Meril Duskkin keeps a gate in the fens, where the record notes that a drained fen is a fen whose light has changed. The Deep Clans are the one set of lineages on Verdance the planet's page gives a stake that runs the other way, into the ground, under the light and out of it. The record sets that beside the fact that Tavin Ledgekin's body orients to the sun as continuously as any Verdani's, and draws no line. He has not been asked which he would keep, and the record does not say that anyone on Verdance has had to choose.

The planet's page puts the Deep Clans and the Terrace Assemblies in one Dominion and in no room together, and the record has not brought Tavin Ledgekin into one with anyone: no farmer, no Assembly member, no clan-kin by name, no Ranger, no delegate. Above him, eight thousand kilometres up, [Greenward](/star-rangers/lore/greenward-habitat/) can see the terraces as it sees all of Verdance at once, and nobody from Greenward may come down; what a Deep Clan's working looks like from there, or whether it can be seen at all, is not on the record either.

The record gives him no kin by name beyond the lineage, no day, and no account of his own in his own words. It places him on the Ledgekin's stretch of the high terraces of Verdance in 2826 UCSD, in the open, in unfiltered highland sun with his vanes out to all of it and the thin air clear to the edge of the range, and nowhere else.

## What the Record Does Not Carry

His age, and the span of a Verdani life, neither stated anywhere; the record holds no conversion of Verdance's years. The settlement he was bonded in, since the planet's page names no place on the terraces. What *Tavin* means, when a personal name is given, and whether a holder of a clan's right is addressed by it or by the lineage. What *Ledgekin* says in any Verdani channel, whether it names the clan or only the strain, and how the Deep Clans are named at all. Whether the Deep Clans are kinstrains, which is this page's reading of the planet page's *hereditary* and not the planet page's own word. What a working of a Deep Clan takes from the terraces, how, with what, in which season, and whether it goes below the ground; what is done with what is taken, by whom, and where it goes; what the Dominion's revenue is paid in. Whether a Deep Clan's right can be divided, set down or given up, how one comes to a clan, and how many hold the Ledgekin's with him. What the high-terrace strain's budget comes to against the other three, and what it costs to live in thin air, which the planet's page gives the terraces and says nothing more about. What lives on the terraces, native or bred, since the record names no organism on Verdance beyond the thallenwort; what the Terrace Assemblies grow. Whether a terrace Verdani carries any partner but thallenwort, which the record's own file on heredity says is likely common on Verdance far beyond the Verdani and has not shown. Whether he has met a Ranger, a surveyor or a delegate, or anyone of a Terrace Assembly by name. His view of the Assemblies' claim, and whether the Deep Clans hold one view or several. How Verdani speak, since the record has described no channel of their language; what the Ledgekin name says in it. Any chapter. The body, beyond what the people's page gives every Verdani and the high-terrace strain's colour: the portrait, when it is made, will be the picture's reading and not the page's.
