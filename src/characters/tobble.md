Being taken for somebody's animal is tradecraft only when you chose it, and Tobble never did.

**Tobble** is a member of [Tissadelle Shepherd](/star-rangers/characters/tissadelle-shepherd/)'s [plural](/star-rangers/glossary/plural-minds/) system: a distinct person sharing the system's one body, on the terms Concord personhood standards have recognised for well over a century. He is a ginger cat and uses he/him. That is not a costume, a coping image, or a figure Shepherd invented to make something easier: he is a nonhuman member, a person whose own identity is simply not the species of the body he shares. Shepherd's page carries the system as a whole. This page carries what the record holds of him, all of it on the system's own account, which is the only authority there has ever been on the subject. The house on the kept hill passed to him on the morning the Dock Seven file closed on a founding ([None to Correct (S15E05C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s15/e05/s15e05c01/)).

## On the record

For most of Shepherd's service Tobble was in no file the Corps held. The plural registration she filed after the Starwarden posting of 2829 UCSD names him ([Not Asked (S05E00C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s05/e00/s05e00c01/)), and says that the system has more members than the form has boxes for, and nothing further. *Because that is what I am* is on the record as his answer to *why a cat*.

The mistake made about him is institutional. The [Smart Pet](/star-rangers/glossary/smart-pet/) programme has trained a whole service to hear *cat* and think *animal*, and the assumption holds until it is applied to a person. Shepherd corrects it once and does not raise it again, and she does not let it pass in front of him.

## At the front

He fronts when the work is with cats and comparable animals, because he is better at it, and the system uses whoever is better at the thing in front of it. At work he can be professionally quiet for a whole day and then say what he made of it. He has opinions, and usually shares them with her; about Karla Wender, at least once, he kept them to himself.

## The deeper layer

The inner world the system shares is structured like a high-fantasy role-playing game. Tobble's own part of it sits deeper than the questing layer and runs on a different discipline: kept records, checked procedure, arithmetic done twice because once is an opinion. Deeper describes structure, not standing. There is no hierarchy in it, and neither architecture is the truer one.

He and Shepherd speak inward, privately, and no one else in the room hears it. It was not a thing she did on duty. At Dock Seven she did.

## Dock Seven

At Dock Seven, in Mid-Autumn 2831 UCSD, Shepherd was fronting and carrying everything, and Tobble was carrying nothing. When the load came apart he was what was left with structure to spend, and he spent it, with the lost ship's own records for a scaffold ([What Came Off the Ship (S05E06C03)](/star-rangers/seasons/s05/e06/s05e06c03/)). What went in was some of her and not her, on his account, and he has never once called it a rescue. What followed is told from Season 6 on ([First Light, Off the Record (S06E01C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s06/e01/s06e01c01/)).

The Fellowship of Light's house on the Boyne reads it as a crew of two in one vessel ([Two in the One Currach](/star-rangers/codex/two-in-the-one-currach/)). That reading belongs to the house, and the record files it as theirs.

## The book

On the kept hill he kept a book, in his own hand and with no title. It is an account of the world the system kept before Dock Seven, which did not come across with the hill: its named places and their rules, its offices and who held them, and the work taken up, carried and finished. The head of its first leaf reads *What was there. Entered from what is held. Each figure got twice, or marked.* Where he did not hold the reason for a rule he entered that the reason was not held. Shepherd knew at least two of those reasons, and he never asked her for them ([None Asked (S15E03C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s15/e03/s15e03c01/)).

Some leaves he wrote and then smoothed flat himself, some over pages that were nobody's business and some over pages where, on review, he had figured too well. Nothing on them is struck through. He opened the book at the first leaf and squared it to her chair on the morning she first read it. He had an answer ready in case she asked what was under a smoothed leaf, which was that it is kept. She read the book twice through and never asked ([None Asked (S15E03C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s15/e03/s15e03c01/), [None to Correct (S15E05C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s15/e05/s15e05c01/)). What is under those leaves is not in this record, since it is not in his.

## The watch below the hill

Below the hill, in the records of the *Sufficient Cause* that he used at Dock Seven, he kept a night watch that nobody had given him. The last range of those records ends at 0551 in the middle of a line. Past the end of that line is what he calls the break, which has not closed. Things came to it one at a time and called. He logged each by what it did, a leaf to a night, in the form a desk uses for a call and under a blank heading, and he gave them nothing. He does not know what any of them is. None came further in than the end of the last range, and he cannot support that the one fact was the cause of the other. One of them greeted him in Shepherd's voice and manner, and then supplied his answers itself ([None Admitted (S15E04C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s15/e04/s15e04c01/)).

He sent nothing of the break to the floor above. It is the one line of the log that he decided and did not observe, and his grounds are stated: she could not check a report in his voice, could do nothing about what it reported, and would carry it. The log was to be hers on the day she came down for it. [Asteria](/star-rangers/characters/asteria-headmate/), who sat the end of some watches with him, held that Shepherd was owed the knowledge, and he entered that as well ([None Admitted (S15E04C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s15/e04/s15e04c01/)).

On the first night after the founding the greeting said, for the first time, something of itself: that it had not gone anywhere. He looked at it, which he had not done on any night of the log, and found no error in it. Then he answered it with an entry: that she had gone through the day before, at the gold; that he had her last words and they were not those; and that no place in the house is held for her. He entered the act under a word the log's form had not carried, *declined*. He could not support that speaking did anything, as he had never been able to support that silence had. The greeting came on two more nights, less of it each time, and on the fourth night nothing came to the break at all. He put no cause between any two of the four nights, and he does not hold that the thing has ceased. The log stays in the stores, in order. By his account it is still hers, and it goes with the house ([None Seated (S15E06C02)](/star-rangers/seasons/s15/e06/s15e06c02/)).

## The house, and the founding

Before the morning Shepherd went through, he was asked, and he considered declining, which was open to him. What decided him was neither duty nor Shepherd, who had refused to ask. It was the house. That morning she put her hand down and he pressed his head against it. He did not answer her last words in words. Afterward he said good morning in full, to somebody who could not hear it. Later he entered the day in the book in one line, and glossed it ([None to Correct (S15E05C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s15/e05/s15e05c01/)).

From the second morning he made the morning's entry himself, in the book, at the table, with the book still squared to her chair. He greeted each morning from the wall. On the fourth he entered that no council is seated ([None Seated (S15E06C02)](/star-rangers/seasons/s15/e06/s15e06c02/)).

In a later year, at an interval from the founding that the record does not number, the universe was still [proto](/star-rangers/glossary/noogenic-protouniverse/), and he was a founder without a Council. He had walked its edge. The young ground past the holding had ordered itself by roles before law. From the low wall it had the shape of the world they had lost, and none of its magic, and he filed the resemblance under structure ([You're Up Early (S15E07C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s15/e07/s15e07c01/)).

One morning someone stood at that edge with Shepherd's face. The record does not say what stood there, and this page does not either. It holds what he did. He filed first that he wanted it to be her. He put her mother's words to it, *You're up early*, and then told it to do the arithmetic, and he took his findings from what it did that she never had. When it claimed a title, the young ground leaned toward the word, by enough to measure. He answered in the grammar of the world she had kept, where that title was the first rung, and the ground stopped leaning. He named, inwardly and in full, that he had wanted her back on every morning since her last words; then what she had been, by what she declined to do; then that what stood at the edge was not her. He said no. He did not fight it, and it did not go. He still wanted her back. He entered the day in the book in full, both sides of it, and did not gloss it ([You're Up Early (S15E07C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s15/e07/s15e07c01/)).

## What the record does not hold

The record does not say what any of the things at the break is, or what stood at the edge, or whether it ever leaves.

The record holds no account of when he came, and has not been asked for one. It holds no photograph of him, and never will: a member of a system has no body of his own for a camera to take. An impression has been painted from the system's description, as one was for [Asteria](/star-rangers/codex/asteria-the-sage-impression/), and the record files it as an impression and not a likeness, which is the only kind of picture he can have.
