Winifred Adebayo keeps the correspondence. The [Commonwealth](/star-rangers/lore/the-commonwealth/) has no common legislature, no shared jurisdiction, no budget worth the name and no power to bind a member to anything, and what it has instead, a table and a protocol, has to be kept by somebody: the communities written to, the replies filed, the comparisons arranged, the invitation renewed. That is her trade. She is the first person of the Commonwealth the record names, and the Archive calls her the first of nothing else; an association's letters are somebody's work, as its minutes are elsewhere in the record.

She was born on [New London Space Habitat](/star-rangers/lore/new-london-space-habitat/), at Earth–Moon L5, in about 2781 UCSD, in one of the three rings, which the record does not name, to a family descended, as most of the habitat's are, from the British evacuation programmes. The habitat is older than any family in it. Construction began in the 2180s CE (2380s UCSD) as a hedge against Earth's failure; Earth recovered, and the habitat did not close. By her birth it had been at L5 for four centuries and had spent three of them doing something subtly different from what it was chartered for, maintaining a heritage at a remove from the place it came from. The Commonwealth is anchored there and, in practice, hosted there, and it is New London's foreign policy; the secretariat that keeps it is where she works.

The name is given as the record holds it. New London's archives keep whatever travelled up to L5 well enough to be archived and print the old era beside the new, and what the name owes to which part of the Isles, or to the generations since the lift, the record does not say and has not asked. The record renders her as *she*.

## Known History

- Born on New London in about 2781 UCSD. The year is the record's reading from her working age in 2826, and not a date the habitat's own registers have been asked for.
- Clerk of the correspondence at the Commonwealth secretariat in the record's present, 2826 UCSD. When she entered the secretariat, and in what capacity, the record does not say.
- Never off New London in any account the record holds. The Commonwealth's communities are written to, and the writing does not require the writer to go.
- Not a member of the [Council of State](/star-rangers/lore/new-london-space-habitat/), not a member of Parliament, and not a delegate of any community to the Commonwealth's table. She keeps the table. She does not sit at it.

## The Work

The Commonwealth's page gives the association in one sentence: it has a table, a protocol, and a continuous existence across the worst three centuries in the human record, which turns out to be harder to arrange than any of the others. The arranging is the secretariat's. Adebayo's share of it is the correspondence, and three things pass through her hands.

The first is the writing-to. Membership in the Commonwealth is held by communities rather than by territories, attendance is the only test anyone has ever applied, and the association's own secretariat cannot produce a roll. What it can do is write. A community that attends is written to about the next sitting, and a community that has stopped attending is written to about the next sitting, and the sitting after that, for as long as somebody keeps writing. The Commonwealth's page says that such a community is not expelled so much as gradually stopped being written to. The word *gradually* names her desk, and she is slow at it.

The second is the archive-sharing. Members hold fragments of the same wrecked record, and the Commonwealth is the mechanism by which a genetic archive on Earth, a parish register at L5 and a diaspora community's oral chronicle get compared against each other at all. She does not do the comparing. She arranges it: which fragment is held where, who has the standing to send it, which protocol it travels under, and the letter that asks.

The third is the invitation. The [Celtic Union of Planets](/star-rangers/lore/celtic-union-of-planets/) is, by any geographic reading, the archipelago reassembled in space; it has never been a member and has never applied, and the Commonwealth's standing invitation, renewed and unanswered for generations, has become a piece of protocol both sides find comfortable exactly as it is. It is renewed in her hand. How often, and in what words, the record does not say. That it is renewed exactly as it was last renewed is the protocol, and she keeps the protocol.

## Character Notes

The record holds her manner from her trade and from nothing else, since no chapter has met her, and the trade is exact in a way that is easy to mistake for a small one. A letter goes to the address the last reply came from, under the protocol the last reply was sent under, and says what the protocol says. She does not amend a form the table has not amended. She does not strike a community from the list of those written to, because there is no list and no striking; there is a letter, sent or not sent, and she sends it. When a reply comes back from a community nobody at the table remembers, she files it as a reply.

She holds no view on record of the Union's absence, and the record has not asked her for one. The Union's founders left because they judged that an institution which treated ambiguity as something to be re-categorised would eventually do the same to language communities that did not fit; the Commonwealth's own answer to ambiguity is to rule every attempt to settle it out of order; and the invitation between them is a document that says the same thing each time. She writes it.

She lives in the weather the founders left behind. Ring lighting holds a perpetual soft overcast, it rains on a schedule nobody publishes, the bells are recorded and the birdsong is from species the rings do not host, and the temperature is held between sixteen and eighteen degrees, which is why she dresses in layers, as the habitat does, by habit and not by costume. The Crown-in-Parliament under the recorded bells passes real legislation; the association she writes for has no legislature at all. The habitat's own answer, when pressed, is that a culture is entitled to decide which of its inheritances it keeps working and which it keeps merely present. The record does not hold which of the two she takes the Commonwealth to be, and has no reason to think she has decided.

The record gives her no kin by name, no day, and no account of her own in her own words. It places her at the end of a long plain table in a small room with a window onto the ring's interior, on New London in 2826 UCSD, with a letter going out, and nowhere else.

## What the Record Does Not Carry

Her kin. Which ring she was born in and which she lives in. What the name owes to which part of the Isles, or to the four centuries since the lift. The secretariat's size, who heads it, how a clerk is appointed, and from what a secretariat is paid under a budget not worth the name. The medium the correspondence travels in: the habitat keeps some old forms working and some merely present, and the record does not say which its letters are. How often the invitation to the Celtic Union is renewed, in what words, and whether she has ever read a reply, since none is on record. Whether she has been to Earth, to any member community, or up to the Spire. Whether she carries a title of the kind the constitution declines to notice, and whether anyone in her family does. Whether she has ever heard birdsong that was not a recording. Any view of the Union's absence, the vacant throne, or the regency proposals that surface once a generation and have never carried. Any chapter. The body, beyond what a working life indoors at sixteen degrees gives anyone: the record states nothing of it, and the portrait, when it is made, will be the picture's reading and not the page's.
