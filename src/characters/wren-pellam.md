Wren is the youngest of a Knight's house, the floor of the [Kingdom's](/star-rangers/lore/planets/kingdom-of-the-five-islands/) hereditary ladder, which presides and administers nothing and still sends its youngest to the Castle in every generation to learn the chronicle. That is the custom the record has her for: the court's record of descent and deed is kept by being recited, and it is learned in the Castle's rooms and about them, and a house that cannot put a reciter under the Elder Grove at the next coronation is a house that has let its part of the record lapse. Pellam has never let it lapse. Wren is fifteen and is the one sent.

## What the Record Holds

She crossed from Ostrel to Casimor at the start of a storm season and came up to the watch-post on Krilzat with the chronicle's first names already by heart, learned on Vellamar from a reciter who had never seen the Castle, which is the ordinary way. What she had not seen, and what the chronicle is for, is the Castle's people: the figures the court's record of descent begins with, seen as light, walking the long hall at their hours, never answering. She saw the first of them on her first evening and had its name, and the keeper's son, who had watched it all his life, had its way. The record holds the exchange in [*The Names Go By* (S13E01C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s13/e01/s13e01c01/).

At the storm season's first calm she and the keeper's son went down the right side of the long hall past the chronicle's last right-side name and past the furthest notch, and found a door standing open, with one of the Castle's people standing in the room beyond it, which the chronicle has no name for. She carried back the line the chronicle could make of it, *a door was given, on Krilzat, in Pellam's time*, for the reciters. The record holds it in [*A Door Was Given* (S13E01C02)](/star-rangers/seasons/s13/e01/s13e01c02/).

In the survey's fourth year on Vellamar the court's letter called her from Krilzat to say the chronicle for her house at a crowning, the old monarch having died at the storm season's end. By then she had the ways for rather more than half the names: the watch's account of which way each of the Castle's people goes in the long hall, and at what hour. She stood at the rim of the Elder Grove, where the Knights' houses stand, and said the chronicle through with the court's reciters and every house's. After the ninth door on the right every mouth stopped, since a line new since the last crowning is said first, alone, by the house in whose time it was given. She said *a door was given, on Krilzat, in Pellam's time, on the right side past the ninth, and one stands in it*, with no name, and stopped where a name would go, for as long as a name is. The reciters came in together on the line that follows. She said that line at the pace of a line, and finished after them. She did not have the Long Accounting's name for the one standing in the room, and the chronicle has none.

Under the Grove she saw that each house's reciter was once its youngest and was sent to the Castle, as she was. When the record leaves her she meant to ask whether the boat for Casimor had room for her, and nobody had sent her this time. The record holds the day in [*As Long as a Name* (S13E08C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s13/e08/s13e08c01/).

## What the Record Does Not Carry

What she makes of the Castle's people beyond the chronicle's own reading, which she holds as the court holds it. Anything the delegation knows of her, since the delegation has not been to Krilzat.

## Character Notes

She is the court's strand's young keeper and the reader's proxy on the young-adult rung for a record kept by speaking: a person learning to hold, by heart, a list that begins with names she can watch walk past. The chronicle's reading of what walks past is hers in every POV block and is never the record's. The pairing the strand runs on is hers and the keeper's son's: the court has the names and has never seen them; the watch has seen them every day and has no names.
