*Accurate, and who is it for?*

**Wyn** is one of the three people of the [plural](/star-rangers/glossary/plural-minds/) system that calls itself the Archivist and goes by [Sen Lovax](/star-rangers/characters/sen/) outside it, with Cael and Sen, sharing one body and one desk at the Survey Archive on Threshold Station. Wyn is a person on the Concord's ordinary terms, not the archivist's conscience given a name. Sen's page carries the system and the desk as a whole. This page carries what the record holds of Wyn.

## What the record leaves out

Wyn reads the same report Cael has just verified and asks a different question: who was protected by the closure, and who paid for it. The desk's unsigned notes name the gap between what a station filed and what its instruments logged, and the cost of the silence that let the gap stand. Naming the cost is Wyn's half, and it has been Wyn's for as long as there has been a desk ([Not Under a Name (S07E01C04)](/star-rangers/seasons/s07/e01/s07e01c04/)). Wyn names a cost only where the cost is on the file ([Sen Lovax](/star-rangers/characters/sen/#what-the-desk-does-not-do)).

On the Shepherd submission in Late Autumn 2831 UCSD, Wyn named who had paid: a family that kept a rule for six generations about not being looked at, now in an inquiry file under a heading, and the person whose name was on the heading not there to be asked whether she minded ([On the Record First (S06E02C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s06/e02/s06e02c01/)). In Deep Winter, of three lines the Archive had never held, Wyn said that somebody on that hillside had kept them, and that out of everything she had, those were what came back. To Sen's *We know what the signal carries*, Wyn answered: *I am saying what it cost to carry* ([Enough Is Enough (S07E01C03)](/star-rangers/seasons/s07/e01/s07e01c03/)).

## In Wyn's own words

Wyn does not hurry, and people who have only ever heard Cael tend to take Wyn's patience for Cael's. When the short sentences have done their work, Wyn builds a long one.

Wyn wants the desk's notes signed. *Everything this desk writes is true unsigned. That is why nobody has ever had to do anything about any of it.* A note with a name under it has to be answered, and Wyn wants the Council to have to write back. *I want the desk to be answerable for what it found.* Sen declines, Cael says Cael has no view and Wyn does not believe it, and Wyn has said it before and will say it tomorrow ([Not Under a Name (S07E01C04)](/star-rangers/seasons/s07/e01/s07e01c04/)). The difference is theirs, and none of the three expects to settle it.

Wyn's section of [Shepherd Among the Comparanda](/star-rangers/codex/shepherd-among-the-comparanda/), the comparative-archive submission the desk prepared and withdrew, opposes the filing for a reason of its own: it would console. It would give a lost officer a lineage and make the loss mean something, and a shape that fits perfectly, arrives as comfort and cannot be checked is the danger the station already has on file. *She was not an instance of anything.* Wyn says, there, that Wyn had wanted to file it from the first week and had known why.

## What the record does not hold

The record holds no account of when Wyn came, of how Wyn came to share Sen's desk, or of anything Wyn has not said at it. It has no picture of Wyn, and a member of a system has no body of their own for a camera to take.
