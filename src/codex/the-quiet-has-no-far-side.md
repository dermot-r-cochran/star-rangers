### Translation desk's note

*The Survey Archive asked, through the Federation record and then through Thavren's own meeting-chorus, for the history of the Ovruhn by one of them. The meeting-chorus answered that it keeps the loud people and not the Ovruhn, that a history is the thing the oldest chorus still carries, and that the asking should go there. The oldest chorus consented to one recital with a desk present. What follows is one keeper's line through it, flattened to a single voice, as the desk's earlier rendering from Thavren was: chorus-keeping is performance-with-checking, and the chorus's confirmations, corrections and silences are marked only where their content was unmistakable. Twice the under-song went into the register the Ovruhn keep for loss and for nothing else. The desk marks where, and renders neither. The keeper reviewed this rendering and is recorded as asking that the two marks be left where they are.*

---

This is the keeping of the Ovruhn, as the oldest chorus holds it. Check me.

You have asked for a history, and the desk has told me what your record means by the word: the whole of what happened, back to the first thing, held at one resolution, so that a reader a thousand years on may have it as the first keeper had it. We do not have one of those. Hear this first, and hold it under everything after: **a history, among us, is the thing the oldest chorus still carries, and it has a near edge.** It begins at the reach of the oldest voice in the room. Everything before that edge was kept once, by keepers who are gone, and was checked, and was true, and is not here. I am not telling you we lost it. I am telling you what a history is.

*(The chorus confirms. The desk is advised that the under-song here is level: this is not the register for loss. It is the register for stating a thing that is the case.)*

### The edge

I will give you the edge before I give you anything behind it, because the edge is the oldest thing we keep, and because your record keeps the other end first.

When a keeper dies, the chorus sings their part without them. Not to mourn; that comes after. To hear. A part held by many voices is not thinner by one. A part held by three is. A part held by one is gone the hour they stop, and the chorus hears the hole where it was, and sings around the hole, and what it sings around the hole is the register the desk has said it will not give you. Then the chorus decides nothing. There is nothing to decide. What was held by one is not held, and the chorus goes on carrying what it carries, and the edge has moved.

That is the whole of our loss, and I have given it plainly. The desk has read me what your record says of us, that loss is the central experience of our history, and that is correctly heard, and I would turn one word of it. Not a loss. The loss. We have one, and it is always the same one, and it has happened as many times as there have been keepers, and it will happen to the part I am singing now.

*(The under-song here went into the register for loss. The chorus held it and came back. The desk marks the place and renders nothing.)*

### The fire, heard

The oldest thing behind the edge is fire, and we heard it before we had it.

Thavren's air burns willingly; you have been told this, and your record says we had fire early and cheap, which is true. What your record does not say, because nobody asked it, is how we knew to want it. Fire is loud. A burning thing on Thavren is heard from a long way off, and before any living keeper's edge there were keepers who had heard one and said what it sounded like, and that is the oldest part we carry: not a fire, but a description of one, in the cadence of someone who had not yet stood near it. We carry it as the first thing we learned about the world by listening to something that was not alive.

Then we had it. Then we had what fire gives, which among us is not light. It is ringing. Hot metal rings, and cooling metal rings differently, and a flawed casting rings wrong, and a people who hear through solids do not have to guess. The chorus keeps the first wrong ring. It keeps the keeper who heard it and said *not that one*, and the keeper who asked how they knew, and the answer, which was the start of a craft: *Sound it again.* We have been sounding it again since. Every method we have is a thing demonstrated and demonstrated again until the chorus holds it, and your record calls that slow, and it is slow, and nothing we have made has failed that we did not hear failing first.

### The quiet above

Now the thing this keeping is named for. Check me closely here, because this is the part the young ask for and the part a keeper most wants to sing well, and wanting to sing it well is where a keeper drifts.

Our air is deep and it will carry a body up, and we went up early, in craft that cost almost nothing, into the haze that no season thins. The ones who went were not looking for anything. They were listening. The first went up until the sound of the ground went strange under them, and came down, and said so. The next went higher, until the ground was gone and only the air was left, and came down, and said that. The chorus keeps their parts in the order they went, and I give you the last in the order, who went up until there was no air to carry a sound at all, and came down, and said: *there is a quiet above us, and it has no far side.*

We checked them. We sent others. The others said the same. So we knew that the world was round, and that it was finite, and that it was wrapped in a silence that did not end, and we gave the silence the name we give to a room where no one is singing, with the word turned, so that anyone who heard it would know we meant the one that does not stop. That is still our word for where you come from.

We did not go into it. I want you to hear that correctly, because your record says it in one line, and the line is right and is not the whole part. Nothing stopped us. We had the craft; we could have built the rest. We did not go because there was nothing there to hear, and a people who go toward what they hear do not go toward nothing. We are not ashamed of it. We held the quiet in the keeping and left it where it was, above the roof, for as long as the chorus carries.

*(The chorus confirms. The desk notes that the under-song carried, under the confirmation, what it has rendered elsewhere as the signature of old amusement, and that the chorus did not withhold it.)*

### The three days

Then something came down out of the quiet, and we heard it for three days before it landed.

I give you that as the chorus holds it and not as your record holds it, since yours holds the landing and ours holds the hearing. For three days the whole of Thavren that was in reach had one part: *something is coming down.* Nobody sang anything else. It was heard thinning the air as it came, and then it was heard in the air, and then it was heard in the ground, and by the time it landed every keeper in reach knew its weight and its shape and that it was not falling. Nothing arrives on Thavren unannounced. Nothing had ever announced itself like that.

What came out of it was loud. That is another chorus's keeping and I will not sing it badly: the meeting-chorus holds the loud people, and the keeper of that chorus has already given you an account and found your rendering of it quiet but not wrong, and I defer to that keeper on everything that walks out of a landed thing. I give you only what the oldest chorus kept of the day, which is this: that they had come out of the quiet, and so the quiet had a far side after all, and we had been wrong, and the wrongness was the best thing that had happened to us in the reach of any living voice.

### The chorus's own time

They asked us to join something. You know the name of it; your record holds Thavren as a member world, and that is true, and I will tell you how it became true in our measure and not in yours.

The asking went to the chorus, and the chorus took it in the chorus's own time. Your record asked, once, how long that was, and was told *unhurried*, and I give you the same word, and I will tell you why it is the right one. A thing the chorus decides is a thing every keeper in reach holds and agrees, and the agreeing is heard — you have been told that nothing among us is private — so there is no vote, and there is no day on which it was decided, because there was no day on which it was not already audible what the chorus was coming to. It was coming to yes for a long time before it arrived there. The desk has asked me for the day. There is no day. There is a part, and the part changed, and the chorus heard it change.

What we gave them is what we were already for. Hearing the living through smoke and through structure, and settling a crowded place with a low song, is the same work there that it is here. The chorus keeps the first of ours who did it somewhere other than here, by the part they held, and I do not give you that part, since it has someone's name in it and the name is theirs.

### The ones in the quiet

The last thing behind the edge is not behind it. It is happening.

Some of ours go into the quiet now. Your Rangers have a word for themselves, the plain one, and ours for it is *one who goes into the quiet*, and the two are the same office and are not the same sentence. When one of ours goes, the chorus does not lose them. Hear that exactly. They are not dead and their part is not unheld. But they are out of reach of the under-song, which none of ours had been from the whole of their people, before the quiet opened, for longer than a climb, and the chorus has no register for that, because no keeper ever needed one, and so it uses the one it has.

*(The under-song here went into the register for loss a second time, and the chorus held it and came back. The desk marks the place and renders nothing. The keeper, reviewing, asked that this mark be kept beside the first, and said why: "so that your reader hears that it is the same song, and that they are not dead.")*

They come back, most of them, and they bring their parts, and the chorus takes them up: a hull that sang its stress, a shelter deck that settled, a quiet so deep they ran on instruments and small eyes and did the work anyway. None of it is in this keeping. It is not history. It is held by the ones who did it, and they are here, and you may ask them.

### What was said of us

One more thing, and then check me.

The first of yours who came and listened to us wrote three words for what we were, each the name of an animal of your world, in one breath, and your talkers made one word of the three, and it is still said. The meeting-chorus keeps that, and keeps it kindly. I raise it here because the oldest chorus keeps something under it that is ours and not yours: we keep the three words in the cadence of the one who said them, which was the cadence of someone describing a thing by what they had met before. We know that cadence. It is the cadence of the keeper who had not yet stood near the fire.

### Check me

That is the keeping of the Ovruhn as the oldest chorus holds it: a near edge, a fire heard before it was lit, a quiet with no far side, three days, a long yes, and the ones who are away. It is short because it is all that is held by the voices in this room, and because everything behind it was held once, and was true.

Your record will keep this rendering after every voice in this room has stopped. Hold it well. It is the first part of ours that will not move the edge when we are gone.

Check me.

### Translation desk's note, continued

*Filed Cultural Record, valid for its keeper, as the chorus's own account. The Archive has set no year to anything in it and has asked for none: the Ovruhn keep no reckoning a desk could convert, the contact between Thavren and the Federation is undated in the record by the record's own choice, and the chorus's measure for its membership deliberation is the one the Archive already holds, unhurried. Five things in the account the Archive holds in its own voice, on the [Ovruhn entry](/star-rangers/lore/ovruhn/): that Thavren's air burns willingly and the Ovruhn had fire early; that their foundry practice is a listening discipline; that their early ascents found quiet and not stars, that their word for space is a form of their word for quiet, and that they did not go into it; that the Federation's descent was heard for three days before it landed; and that membership was taken by the chorus's own deliberation, in the chorus's own time. Where the keeper says the Ovruhn were wrong about the quiet's far side, the desk notes that the physics the keeper describes, air thinning until no sound carries, is the one the record holds, and that the correction the keeper celebrates is the keeper's own and corrects nothing in the record. The two places the under-song went into the register for loss are marked and not rendered, at the keeper's asking as well as the desk's, under the Archive's own rule that [what it cannot carry is marked and not supplied](/star-rangers/codex/marked-absences/). Who first went from Thavren into Federation service, and who first went into the quiet with the Rangers, the chorus holds and the desk did not ask; neither is on file.*
