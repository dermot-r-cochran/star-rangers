### Translation desk's note

*The Survey Archive asked, through the Federation record, for the history of the Ollune by one of them. The asking went into Freyari the way anything goes into Freyari: carried by a Star Rangers courier, under the role the people's page gives the Rangers there, and given in the band. One shoal answered, through the courier that works its band, that the one who marks its passings would cry the history at the hull's flank at the shoal's next passing with the hull, and would mark afterwards that it was theirs. The hull held station through the passing. Its pressure instruments took what follows, and the desk rendered it, as the survey's instruments have rendered the far word since contact. The far word carries no speaker's identity. Everyone in the band heard it; several in the band cried over it, and the desk marks where, and renders none of them, since no body has marked those words as theirs. That the history was Vellire's the desk knows from one source only, which is the mark at the end.*

---

This is the history of the held, cried in the far word. The far word binds nothing. Hear everything that follows as that.

The desk has told me what your record means by a history, and asked me for ours in whatever form we keep one in. We keep one in two forms, as we keep everything, and I will give you both, and I will tell you which is which, because you are a people who have built institutions on not keeping them apart. What is cried is what everyone heard. What is marked is what a body answers for. A history, among us, is mostly the first kind, and I am about to spend a passing proving it.

### The oldest thing we say

The oldest thing we say is a bearing.

Before anything happened to us, we were somewhere, and somewhere is a depth, a bearing from the Eye, and a phase of the drift, and the three together are a time, because nothing in Freyari stays where it is except the Eye. The Eye stays. Everything we have ever reckoned, we have reckoned from the one thing in the air that does not move, and the far word has carried positions since before it carried anything you would call a thought. A position cried into the band is the oldest shape our speech has. It is also the only kind of far word that is nearly always true, since there is no profit in lying about where you are to people who are drifting past you.

So I will tell you where the oldest things happened, and the desk will tell you it cannot turn where into when. That is not a withholding. I have given the desk every position in this account in our notation, and the desk has it, and the desk cannot make a year of it, because your year has no bearing in it. Hold that beside everything you read here. We are not vague about time. We are exact about it in a way your record cannot hold.

### Holds

We were held before we were anything else.

Every one of us was carried before we could pay for ourselves, and every one of us will be carried tonight, and that is the first history and the whole of it: a body that stops paying goes under, and a hold does not let it. The desk read me what your record says about this, and most of it is correctly heard, and I will turn one thing. Your record says that trust among us is a load being borne by named individuals right now. Yes. But a hold is not a thing we are proud of. It is a thing we are. You do not thank the air for holding your lift, and we do not thank the hold for holding ours; we tether to it, and we pay our own share while we are awake, and that is the entire ceremony.

*(Here the band came in over the author for some time. The desk renders none of it.)*

The far word says a great deal about the first hold. It says it was eight, and it says it was thirty, and it says which band, and it gives three bearings that cannot all be the same place, and it says what the first one to go under said when they were caught. None of this is marked by anyone. It is the kind of thing we say, and I have said all of it myself, in my own band, on nights when the band wanted something to say.

### Passings

Here is what our history is made of. It is made of passings.

A shoal is in its band and goes at its band's speed, and the shoal beside it in the same band goes at the same speed, and so the two of them never meet, however near they look, and never will. The shoal above goes at another speed, and comes past on its cycle, and is in reach of the near word for a part of a day, and then is gone for longer than some of the young in my hold have been alive. That is a passing. Everything that must be settled between two shoals is settled in it or waits for the next one. Everything we have ever agreed, we agreed in a few hours, with people we would not see again for years, and then we did what we had marked, or did not, and found out at the next passing which.

That is where my own office comes from, and it is why the desk's asking came to me. I mark the passing for my shoal. What my shoal sends across the shear is my near word, composed at the flank of whoever has consented to hold station with us, slowly, since the near word is slow, in the hours we have. How my shoal comes to what I mark is not for this channel, and you have not been beside me. What I can tell you is what the marking is for, which is that the people I am marking to cannot ask me what I meant. They will not be here. So a mark for a passing says what is to be done, and by when in the drift, and what is to be done if it cannot be done, and what is to be done if nothing is heard, and the last of those is the part we are known for. A term for the silence. Every people drafts terms for what happens. We draft terms for what happens when nobody can tell you whether it happened.

The far word says we are good at this. The far word would. I have read marks older than anyone in my hold that held exactly as they were written, by bodies that never met again, and I have read marks that failed in the first phase after they were made, and I will tell you a thing about the two kinds that your record does not hold: they are the same length. A mark that holds is not longer or finer than one that does not. The difference is in whether it was true when it was made, and nothing in the mark can tell you that. Only the next passing can.

*(The band came in here, briefly. Not rendered.)*

### The thing that did not fall

Now what you came for. I will give it in the words we gave it in, which are alarm words, since those were the words we had.

Something was above us, at a bearing from the Eye that I have given the desk, and it was coming down. Coming down is the oldest alarm there is. We cried it as we cry it, which is: *falling, there, falling*, with the bearing, and the band took it up, because a falling thing is everyone's business and nobody's responsibility, and whoever cried it first had said nothing they could be held to. Hear that as it was, because your record holds this moment as a failure of your instruments, and it was not a failure of ours. We saw it. We said so. We said so at once, and freely, and for as long as it was true.

Then it stopped.

Nothing stops. A body that stops going down is being held, and we cried to ask who held it, and nobody answered, because nobody did. It hung there at its depth, paying nothing that we could hear, with no hold around it and no lift of its own that we understood, and it did not go under, and it did not come up, and the band had no word for it. We used the alarm words anyway, because they were the words there were, and the oldest shape in our speech, the position, went on being cried at it for a long while, as the band does when it does not know what else to say: *there. Still there. There.*

*(Here the band came in over the author, at length, and in more voices than the instruments could separate. The desk renders none of it, and notes that the author did not stop.)*

Your record says you were listening to all this and took it for weather. I believe that. We were doing the same to you. A thing that does not answer in the far word is weather, and we steered by it, as we steer by the Eye, and we said everything about it that a band can say about a thing that will not answer. We said it was a shoal of a people who paid nothing. We said it was a dead thing that had forgotten to sink. We said it had come up from below, where things stop, which is the one direction we are certain nothing comes from, and we said it had come from above, where the air ends, which is the other. Every one of those was far word. Not one of them was testimony. Nobody who said any of it has ever been asked to answer for it, and if you ask me now which one I said, I will tell you I said all of them, in order, with everyone else.

Then it answered. I will not cry you the first thing it said, because the far word has it a dozen ways and I was not there, and the desk would write down whichever I chose. I will tell you what it was like, which is a thing the far word is for. It was like a position call from a shoal in your own band. A thing that cannot be, from a place that cannot reach you, in your own grammar, badly.

### What went further than a passing

Here is the only part of this that changed us, and I will give it in one breath, because it is one thing.

Before the thing that did not fall, no mark had ever gone further than a passing. A near word is made at a flank and read at a flank, and it goes where the body that made it goes, which is along its band, forever, at its band's speed. Then a thing came that could cross the shear in an hour and did not pay for it, and it could carry a mark. It could carry a mark to a shoal I will never be in reach of, in a band I will never cross into, and bring one back, inside a phase, from parties who had not conferred and could not. You have a name for the ones who do this now, and so do we, and I am told your record keeps ours beside yours in a ladder: *one who goes where the air ends*. The far word says that four shoals made the first mark that was ever carried. That is the far word doing what it is for. One of them did. It is not marked which, and it is not marked what it said, and the mark is not lifted by anyone now. Everything I know about it, I know in the channel that binds nobody, and that is correct, and it is what a history is.

You asked us, in time, to be held with you. I am told your compact says that a people need not come to you to be counted, and that in our case the sentence is doing work it does not do elsewhere. We did not know we had been asked to prove anything. We could not have come, so it was a kindness to write down that we did not have to, and we have been told it was written down, and a carrier explained signing to us, which is a mark made by the hand and not the body, that any hand could make, that you say is yours. We hold your signing as near word, because you say so and because it can be read back later, and we have been told, more than once, that we hold it more exactly than you do. That it was marked on our side, I know. By whom, and which shoal first, I have the far word, and the far word has it several ways, and I give you none of them.

### The room we are not in

There is a room where the held of all your worlds sit and settle things, and we are not in it. A long-lived witness says so at the start of every sitting, I am told, and it is noted, and your record holds this as a thing to be sorry for.

Hear this as we hold it. We have never been in any room. The shoal along my own band from mine, near enough that its far word reaches me inside a breath, is not in my room either, and never will be, and we have settled things with it regardless, or we have not and have waited. Our mark goes to your room by courier, lagged, and someone who is not held reads it out in a voice that is not ours, and it ends, every time, with the clause every mark of ours ends with when it goes to a party that cannot answer inside the passing: *keep this until an answer can be carried back*. I am told the room finds this moving. It is the term for the silence. We put it on everything.

Whether an answer has ever been carried back to any of our marks in that room, I do not know in the near word, and I will not say in the far word, since I would be guessing, and guessing is what the far word is for and I would rather not spend it there. A question kept is not a question refused. That is the whole of what the clause means, and we did not write it for you.

### The ones who are set down

Some of ours go up now, where the air ends, in hulls you make for them that cost more than any other member's way of coming to you. In a hull under way, I am told, lift costs nothing. A body can let go of its own warmth and does not sink. For the first time in a life, nobody has to carry it, and some who feel this do not afterwards settle back into a hold, having learned that there is a way to rest that is not being held.

We have a word for the ones who come home from that. It is old, and it is gentle, and it is older than you by the whole of our history, because it was first said of a body the hold caught on its way down, and brought back up, and held until it could pay again. We did not make a new word for the ones who come back from your hulls. We found we had one.

### None of this binds

That is the history of the held, as I have cried it: a bearing, a hold, the passings, the thing that did not fall, the mark that went further, the room we are not in, and the ones who are set down. It is longer than it needs to be, because the far word is free, and shorter than you wanted, because the rest is marked, and the marks are lifted by the bodies that made them, along their own bands, and you have not been beside them.

Nothing in it binds anyone. I said that first, and I say it last, and then I will stop crying and start marking, because the marking is slow and the passing is not long. What follows is the only part of this a body answers for.

---

### The near word

*(The following was composed at the hull's flank through the latter part of the passing, with the hull holding station by consent, and carried out. It is the terms of a drafter who marks passings, written for a party who cannot be asked what was meant. The desk renders it as the Federation record's readers render a mark from Cirrane, and has set nothing beside it.)*

> The far word cried at this flank on the history of the held, through this passing, was this body's. The body answers for having cried it. It does not answer for any of it being so.
>
> This body consents to the carrying of what was cried, and to its being read by anyone who is close to it, and to its being kept where nothing lifts it.
>
> Read what was cried as cried. Where it gives a position, the position is this body's and is exact, and it is not set beside a reckoning of anyone else's, and none is to be.
>
> Where what was cried differs from what your record holds, your record is not corrected by it, and this body is not corrected by your record. Keep both.
>
> If this is not received, nothing is owed. If it is received and nothing is carried back, nothing is owed. Keep this until an answer can be carried back.

### Translation desk's note, continued

*Filed Cultural Record, valid for its author, as the author's own law requires and the Archive's agrees: the far word is read as the author's account and binding on nobody, the near word as five terms binding on the one body that made them. The Archive has set no year to anything in the account and the author set none in any reckoning the Archive can convert. The positions the author gave are in the people's notation of depth, bearing from the Long Eye and phase of the drift, and the Archive does not reproduce the notation, as it does not reproduce it for the author's shoal; the ordinary conversion of a passing's interval into the record's years was not applied, since the author's band and cycle are not on file and the author gave no interval the Archive could set beside anything it dates. Five things in the account the Archive holds in its own voice, on the [Ollune entry](/star-rangers/lore/ollune/) and the [Cirrane entry](/star-rangers/lore/planets/cirrane/): that the far word binds nothing and the near word everything; that the first Ollune record of contact is in their alarm vocabulary, since a descending vessel was an object falling and falling objects were the only category available; that the survey logged the far word for several weeks as an atmospheric resonance; that Ollune agreement-drafting practice entered Federation compact procedure in a generation the record does not fix; and that the word for those who come home from a hull changed is old and was first used of survivors recovered from a fall. Where the author says that four shoals made the first carried mark and gives none of them, and that membership was marked and does not say by whom, the desk notes that the record holds neither either, and that how a shoal arrives at the position it sends is not established. The passages the band cried over are marked and not rendered, under the Archive's own rule that [what it cannot carry is marked and not supplied](/star-rangers/codex/marked-absences/): the far word carries no speaker's identity, and no body has marked those words as theirs. The mark is held in the general collection as the object it is. The Archive notes, without setting it beside anything, that the mark's last term is the one the Federation record's readers know from Cirrane, and that the Archive has not been asked by the author to carry anything back.*
