*A note on what this document is. It is an Institute paper, and like every Institute paper it declines to use the [Cosmic Cascade](/star-rangers/lore/cosmic-cascade/). It tests one practice of the record, not the record's cosmology. Its finding is narrower than its title, and its null results are printed at the same length as the finding.*

---

## 1. The objection, at full strength

The record files every physical claim at a stated width: a force's realm, a zone, a membrane, a cluster, a mathematical regime. [The Scope of Physical Law](/star-rangers/lore/the-scope-of-physical-law/) calls a claim with no width "not yet a claim". This fellowship regards the practice as admirable, and that is exactly why it deserves the hardest test the Institute can give it.

The objection runs as follows. A theory that may narrow its claims after a contrary reading can never be refuted. Whatever is found, the claim is restated at a width that excludes it, and the theory survives with its record of success intact. Karl Popper called this a conventionalist stratagem in 1934 CE (2134 UCSD), and Imre Lakatos described the same move in 1970 CE (2170 UCSD) as a protective belt thrown round a theory's core so that the core is never exposed. Counterpane is the obvious case. When the three-source statistics there came back not ours, the record answered that *identical at base* had always been an archecluster-scoped identity. If that answer was made after the reading, the scope discipline is not a discipline. It is an escape hatch.

## 2. The test

The objection is a claim about sequence, so it can be tested on the record's own pages. This fellowship reads institutions' registers as the output of a slow instrument. The question for the instrument is plain: was the width written before the reading, or after it?

The Counterpane finding was accepted at home in 2815 UCSD, after the party's instruments had passed audit on a floor that had never left. [Principal Yusuf Adair](/star-rangers/characters/yusuf-adair/), who reviewed it, went to the record rather than to the instruments, and reported that the record had only ever claimed its arithmetic *within any given Concordant* and *throughout the local cluster*. That is one reviewer's reading. This paper repeats it independently, on the filings the scope page itself names as load-bearing: the calibrated second, the cavern constraint and the harmonic substrate.

The result, stated at its width: **in every filing examined, the wording that preceded 2815 UCSD was already cluster-wide in fact.** Where an older filing says *everywhere*, it says so of measurements every one of which was made inside the local archecluster. Before the Counterpane survey, every laboratory, every archive and every calibrated second in the record sat inside the cluster, so *everywhere* and *every measurement ever made* named exactly the same set. The later restatement at archecluster scope changed the words. It did not change what any filing had claimed, because no filing had ever had a reading from outside to claim anything about.

That is the difference between a clarification and a retreat, and it can be checked. A retreat narrows a claim to exclude a reading the claim had covered. A clarification states a width the claim had always had. On the evidence of the record's own text, the scope ladder made a clarification.

## 3. What the test cannot show

The Institute prints its null results at the length of its findings. These are this paper's.

**It cannot show intention.** The test establishes that the width was written before the reading. It cannot establish that anyone writing *everywhere* in the record's earlier centuries meant *within the cluster*. They may simply never have imagined anything else. A clarification that is true of the text is not thereby true of the author.

**It cannot show the future.** A practice that has never narrowed a claim after a contrary reading may do so after the next one. One case passed is one case passed.

**It is one case.** Counterpane is the only surveyed measurement made outside the local cluster. The ladder's lower rungs have been tested by it. Its upper rungs, the formcluster and the mathematical regime, have not, and on the record's own account a separation across formclusters cannot be measured at all. A rung no instrument can reach cannot be tested this way, by this fellowship or any other.

**It reads the record, not the world.** The test asks whether the record's words came before its readings. It says nothing about whether the readings are right, which is the Survey's work and the Science Corps' audit, not this fellowship's.

**It does not reach the cosmology.** The ladder hangs its rungs from the Cascade's tiers. The Institute declines to use those tiers, and nothing in this paper asks it to begin. What this paper finds is that the record's practice of filing claims at a width is kept honestly. Whether the widths correspond to anything the Cascade describes is a separate question, and this paper leaves it exactly where it found it.

## 4. A standing audit

This fellowship proposes that the test be repeated whenever the record restates a claim at a narrower width. The rule is simple. A restatement passes if the earlier text, read at its own date, already supports the narrower width. It fails if the narrowing excludes a reading the earlier text had covered. A failure would not refute the record. It would mark the restated claim as a retreat, filed as such beside the original, which is what an honest register does with its own revisions.

The proposal asks nothing the record does not already do. It asks only that the check be written down, so that the next reader does not have to take the record's word for its own consistency.
