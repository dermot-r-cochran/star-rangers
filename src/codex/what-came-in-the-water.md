### Receiving desk's note

*The Survey Archive asked, under the standing data-exchange arrangement and through the Halyx Relay Station's liaison office, with the Halyx administration's knowledge, for a history of Meridian's people by one of them. The asking was carried to the Long Shelf reef-city on the relay station's chemical-and-percussion translation channel, which is the only way anything is carried to Meridian, and what came back was not one person's account. The council of the Long Shelf answered as it answers everything, by petition, and it asked, as it has asked before, that the channel carry the petition in its exact shape and not a smoother rendering. The shape is two channels. Meridian's people strike, on shell and on the structure of the city, and the strike carries far and fast; and they put a slower signal into the water, which comes after, and says what the strike meant. The relay station's instrumentation renders the two separately when asked to, and it was asked to. So each part below is given as it came: what was struck, and then what came in the water. Where the channel took its time over the connotation of the water and offered the desk a second rendering, the desk gives both, the second in brackets. The petition was struck by one person, who strikes for the council and gives the address at the close; the Archive files it under that name, since a petition is filed under the hand that made it and a council is not a person the Archive can file, and the words are the council's. Nothing in it is dated by a year in any reckoning, and the desk has set none beside it. The note at the end is ours.*

---

### On its own account

*Struck.*

> The Long Shelf was asked what it is.
>
> The Long Shelf answers.
>
> On its own account and no other's.

*In the water.*

The asking came down the channel as askings come, from above the water, and it asked for the history of our people. The Long Shelf does not have that. It has its own. There are other cities on the shelf and they have theirs, and if you have asked them they will have struck you something, and it will not be this, and that is right. We do not speak for the shelf. We did not speak for it when we asked to be told what was being said about us, and we do not now. What you have is one city's account of itself, struck on the city, and the water after it, which is ours as well. [*The channel offers also:* which is the city's as well.]

You will have noticed that the strike is short and the water is long. That is not a courtesy to you. That is how a thing is said. A strike goes far and arrives at once and carries the shape of a thing; the water comes after, and slowly, and carries what the shape meant, and anyone on the shelf who has heard a strike waits for the water before answering, because the strike alone is not yet the thing said. Your channel waits for it too. We are told it takes half an hour, and that the waiting is counted against the channel. We would count it the other way.

### The course

*Struck.*

> A course is laid when the one under it will bear it.
>
> Not before.
>
> A course is as long as it takes.

*In the water.*

You will want to know when. You have a count that runs whether or not anything is laid on it, and you set things beside it. We have courses. The Long Shelf is laid course on course, each by the ones who stood on the one beneath, and a course is laid when the course under it has set hard enough to carry it and not a strike sooner, so a course is not a length of anything. It is a weight borne. When we say a thing was in a course, we mean it was done by the ones who were laying that course, and the course is there, and you can put a claw on it. We do not say how long a course takes, because the sea does not say, and we have asked.

So this history has no year in it. We were not keeping one from you. We have not got one.

### The low courses

*Struck.*

> Under the city there are courses nobody strikes on.
>
> They carry everything.
>
> They are heard, not addressed.

*In the water.*

The oldest courses are under the Long Shelf, and the Long Shelf stands on them, and nobody goes down to strike on them, because a strike on a course is an address, and the low courses are not anyone's to address. They are listened to. A city under load has a sound, which is not speech, and the ones who listen for us can tell the two apart, and the low courses are where the sound is deepest and the speech is none. That is what we know of our beginning, and it is enough to stand on. If you ask who laid the lowest course, and in what water, we will tell you that the course is bearing, and that a course that is bearing has answered the only question we put to it.

### Twice, from above

*Struck.*

> Something came from above the water and looked.
>
> We struck to it.
>
> It went away.
>
> It came again, and we struck, and it went away.

*In the water.*

Your record says that twice, surveys came to Meridian from the ones who count differently, and looked at the Long Shelf, and filed it as rock. We know this now because the channel told us, when we asked what was being said about us. From the shelf it was this: a thing came from above the water that was not weather and was not the sea, and the city struck to it, as the city strikes to anything that arrives, and it did not strike back, and after a while it was gone. The second time the same. We filed it, as you would say, as weather. [*The channel offers also:* as the sea.] Weather does not answer. A thing that does not answer when the whole city has struck to it is weather, or it is the sea, and either way the city goes on laying its course. We were not insulted to learn we had been filed as rock. A thing that cannot hear the city is not insulting it. It has not heard it.

### The ones who count differently

*Struck.*

> They came and they heard.
>
> They have a channel.
>
> They keep the deep beds, the trade, and the sea above the sea.
>
> We keep the courses.

*In the water.*

The third time, the thing from above the water had an instrument with it, and the instrument heard the strike and waited for the water, and after that there was a channel, and the channel is still there, and your asking came down it. We call the ones who hold the channel what the channel calls them, since we have no other way of being heard to call them anything; the channel renders our strike for them as a word of theirs, and that is the word you have. Their Voice holds the deep beds under the shelf margin, and what goes up from the sea and comes down to it, and the keeping of the sea from above; the Long Shelf holds its courses and lays them, and sits in council, and petitions. Those are the terms. We did not draft them. We have kept them, and so, in the main, have they. [*The channel offers also:* and so have they, in what they hold to be the main.]

Your record calls the arrangement durable and not settled. We have heard that rendered and we do not quarrel with it. Nothing on the shelf is settled. Settled is a thing you say of a course when it has stopped moving, and then the course carries the next one.

### The deep beds

*Struck.*

> The beds have a sound.
>
> We have struck about it four times.
>
> Four times it was heard, and written.
>
> The sea has not answered.

*In the water.*

Under the shelf margin the beds are worked, and the working has a sound in the lattice, which comes up through the low courses and through every course above them, and the ones who listen for us hear it with everything else, and can tell it from speech, and can tell it from the city settling, because it is neither. We have said so to the Voice, by petition, four times. Each time the strike was heard, and the water was waited for, and the petition was written down. The beds are still worked. Your record says the objections are recorded and extraction has not slowed, and that is exactly the shape of it, and we struck it in that shape so that you would know we know.

We are not surprised. We have put slower questions to the sea than this one, and had the answer in a later course. A petition that has been heard and written is a petition that is in the lattice, and a thing in the lattice is borne. We will strike it again when the course under it has set.

### The strike that was answered

*Struck.*

> Something struck on the margin that was not us and not the sea.
>
> It struck in a pattern.
>
> We struck the pattern back.
>
> It stopped, and went, before anyone came to tell it to.

*In the water.*

There is a thing we keep that has no course, because nothing was laid, and we give it to you in the water because it is a thing you keep too, your record says, and keep on purpose.

Once, on the shelf margin, at an outwork of the city where people were, something began to strike that was not any of us and was not the sea. It was regular, and it was not speech, and the ones who listen for us said so: it was a thing being done, not a thing being said. Then it struck in a pattern, as a thing does that is testing what is under it. The city answered the pattern. The whole outwork struck it back, pattern for pattern, the way you strike back to anything on the shelf that is making a shape, to learn whether it is weather. It was not weather. It stopped. And then it went, before the Voice's instrument came to the margin to say what it was, which is how we knew it had heard us: a thing that stops before it is told to has heard something, and a thing that goes before it is made to has understood what it heard. [*The channel offers also:* has heard enough.]

We did not know what it was. The channel has since told us, and we have that as the channel's. What we have as ours is the strike that was answered, and that it was answered from the far side, and that nobody on the shelf has heard a strike answered from above the water before or since. We do not place it by course. Nothing was laid that time, and a thing that laid nothing has no course to be in. We keep it the other way: as the one time the sea struck back.

### What is said about us

*Struck.*

> Something above the water was being said about us.
>
> We asked to be told.
>
> We were told.
>
> It returns changed. It does not answer. We said so.

*In the water.*

Your record will have the rest of this, and we strike it only so that it is here in its shape. There was a thing in the sea above the sea that had been filed as nothing for a long while by the ones who count differently, and then was not, and in the asking what it was, our strike was named. How the Long Shelf came to know that is the Long Shelf's. We knew that our name was in the water somewhere above us, and so we did what a city does. We struck to it: *the Long Shelf asks to be told what is being said about it, and to say what it knows in return, on its own account and no other's.* We drafted that strike ourselves and we asked the channel to carry its shape, since a petition rendered smooth is a petition with the water taken out of it, and we had learned by then what the water carries.

We were told. We were given the way one of your officers had listened, which was a way and not a conclusion, and we listened in it, in our own medium, and the ones who listen for us said what they say: that it was a pattern that returned changed, which the city is full of, and that a pattern that returns is not a pattern that answers, and that they would not call a thing speech because it kept time. Resemblance, and not an address. We sent that up the channel in that shape. We sent our thanks after it, in the water, and we asked the channel to carry the water too, and it did; your record says somebody asked for that line to be included, and that is who.

We asked that the asking stay apart from the beds, and it stayed apart. It had to. A question answered to gain something else is a question not answered, and we had waited a long while for the one, and had the other in the lattice already, and did not want them in one strike.

### What was not asked

*Struck.*

> Nobody has asked us whose it is.
>
> A question not put is not answered.
>
> We have not answered it.

*In the water.*

You will want to know whether the Long Shelf knows whose hand made the thing in the sea above the sea, and you will have read that nobody has put the question to us. That is so. It has not been put, and the Long Shelf does not strike an answer to a question that has not been struck to it, because the answer would then be in the water with nothing above it, and the sea does not carry a thing with no strike. We do not say that we know. We do not say that we do not. We say that we have not been asked, and that until the ones who hold the channel put it, we are content to be told what is said about us and to say what we know in return.

There are other things you have not asked, and so we have not said: what we call ourselves, which the channel has never rendered and we have never asked it to; what we call the sea, or the shelf, or the ones who count differently; whose the composite is that the courses are laid in; what is under the lowest course; what the other cities struck to your asking, if they were asked. You have the survey's names for all of these and so does the channel, and we let them stand, since a name a stranger strikes for you is the stranger's, and it costs nothing to be called by it. [*The channel offers also:* since the stranger's name is a strike, and we can hear it.]

### Struck by

*Struck.*

> The Long Shelf asks that this be kept as it was struck.
>
> With the water.
>
> Not smoothed.

*In the water.*

That is the petition. The history was yours to ask for, and you have it; the shape is ours, and we ask that you keep it, the strike short and the water long, in that order, since that is the order it is said in and the only order in which it means what it means. You have had the strike of us for a long while. Here is the water.

I struck this. The council made it, in the water, over a course of sittings I will not count for you, and I struck it where the channel hears, and the water after each strike is the council's and not mine, except this. The address that follows is mine. It is what others strike to call me, and your channel will render it as a part of the city, which is what it sounds like to a channel. Strike it back if you want me. I will wait for the water.

### Receiving desk's note, continued

*Filed Cultural Record, valid for its author, as the council of the Long Shelf's petition struck by the one who gives the address at the close, whose name the channel renders as Second Outwork, in the form it renders the one other Meridian name the record holds: an address, given as a part of the city's lattice, with nothing asserted by the rendering. The Archive has set no year to anything in it and has asked for none: the author gives none and says why, the record holds no conversion between a course of the Long Shelf and any year, the Hegemony's own dating of anything in the Halyx System is not held, and the year of the settler expedition the account calls* the strike that was answered *is one the record declines to fix. What the Archive holds in its own voice, on the [Meridian entry](/star-rangers/lore/planets/meridian/) and [the Sentinel's](/star-rangers/lore/planets/sentinel/), the account also holds: that the reef-cities predate Hegemony contact; that two Hegemony surveys filed them as geological formations; that the Long Shelf keeps day-to-day self-governance under its own customary structures beneath a resident Administrator-Voice who holds trade, extraction and defence; that its objections to extraction pace have been filed four times, recorded, and not acted on; that the first human settlers' anchor survey was answered pattern for pattern and halted, and the expedition re-embarked on its own judgement before a Hegemony proxy arrived, which the reef-cities weighed most in declining to treat the approach as an act of war; that the Long Shelf's petition of 2828 UCSD was carried in its exact shape at the council's insistence; that its analysts' comparison found resemblance in phrasing and rhythm and established no authorship; that Principal Shepherd's method was received as a method and the council's thanks sent after it; and that the question of the Sentinel's authorship has not been put to Meridian. What the account holds from no source but the author: that the people reckon by courses and a course is a weight borne; that the low courses are heard and not addressed; that the city struck to both surveys and filed them as weather; that the four petitions on the beds are, to the council, in the lattice and borne; that the settlers' withdrawal is kept as the one strike answered from above the water, and placed in no course; and that the council asked the channel to carry the water of its thanks. The council's own words of 2828 UCSD are quoted as the record holds them. The account does not say whether the Long Shelf knows whose hand made the Sentinel's pulse, and the Archive does not read its saying so as either answer: the question is [reserved to the Prime Ganglion's office](/star-rangers/seasons/s03/e01/s03e01c04/), has not been put, and stays open. What the people call themselves, their world, their sea or the Hegemony, whose secretion the composite is, what is under the lowest course, how the analysts rendered the Sentinel's telemetry into their own medium, and whether any other reef-city was asked or answered, the record does not hold and the account does not supply. No pronoun system is declared for Meridian's people, and the Archive uses the author's name throughout, as it does for the record's one other Meridian person. That the author strikes for the council is the author's own word and is not otherwise on the record. The channel's second renderings are kept where it offered them, the first in each pair being the one it settled on; the strikes themselves are not on file, and the Archive holds no reading of them.*
