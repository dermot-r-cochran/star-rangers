**Arilonian** names the family of devotional traditions descended from the [Arilon](/star-rangers/lore/arilon/) accounts. It is named for the figure, as such traditions usually are, and is deliberately silent on what the figure was. [Champion](/star-rangers/glossary/champions-heroes/), [avatar](/star-rangers/glossary/avatar/), or the incarnation of [Kieme](/star-rangers/glossary/kieme/) itself: every reading is an Arilonian reading, and the word endorses none of them.

The term scales, and the scaling is the point. *Arilonian traditions* is the broadest stratum: wider than any organisation, and narrower than *devotional tradition*, which covers every faith the Archive files. Beneath it sit particular bodies (the [Fellowship of Light](/star-rangers/lore/fellowship-of-light/) is one), and beneath those, single chapters and congregations. A statement true of the Fellowship is not thereby true of Arilonians, and the record is careful about the direction of that inference.

The Archive uses the word where a devotional position needs attributing to the stream rather than to a body, as in *several Arilonian traditions hold the adversary of the codices to be real and personal*. In the usual manner, it registers such positions without ruling on them.
