In Survey Corps field language, a **coherence leak** is a working model for measurable disagreement between local conditions at a boundary zone. It describes how adjacent regions with locally valid physical and Etheric rules can produce effects that do not agree across the interface.

The phrase is not a claim that coherence is a substance escaping through an opening. By itself, a coherence leak does not establish that matter or information crossed the boundary, or that an agent caused the effect. It is an interpretation of observations, not a separate boundary class or a confirmed mechanism.

At Threshold Station, Syra uses the term for the chronometer discrepancy and related readings. Those observations are consistent with boundary interference and instrument drift; they do not, on their own, identify the source or intent of the oscillation.
