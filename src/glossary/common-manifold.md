The **Common Manifold** is the higher-order spacelike and timelike structure lying beyond any single universe's dimensional floor. It governs cross-[membrane](/star-rangers/lore/concordant-membranes/) coherence, causality buffering, and fold survivability, and every universe the record has ever reached in the [Grand Ensemble](/star-rangers/lore/ensemble-multiverse/) is seated in it. That is what the name records, and it is a statement about the record's reach rather than about the Ensemble. The manifold is a [Formwright's formtype](/star-rangers/glossary/formcluster/), common to its formcluster and not beyond it, and a differently-woven formcluster is seated otherwise; [Wholecloth](/star-rangers/lore/wholecloth-formcluster/) is the reported case. Universes that agree on nothing about their own dimensional structure still meet here.

Each **coherent universe**, whatever its origin, occupies its own bounded [cosmic membrane](/star-rangers/lore/concordant-membranes/) within the manifold, separated from its neighbours by [the Interval](/star-rangers/glossary/interval/). A [Concordant](/star-rangers/glossary/concordant/) Zone is a region *inside* a membrane, never a membrane in its own right, and one [Worldwright](/star-rangers/glossary/worldwright/) authors many primary membranes.

**It lies above the bulk.** Membranes sit in a [local bulk](/star-rangers/glossary/local-bulk/), and one bulk can hold a very large number of them; the manifold contains the bulks and seats each one. The bulk is M-theory's word, and nothing in M-theory lies above its eleven dimensions, so the manifold is the record's own layer: see *For a Reader Who Knows the Physics* on the [lore entry](/star-rangers/lore/post-eleven-dimensional-manifold/#for-a-reader-who-knows-the-physics).

**Why Survey Corps says "post-11."** Our home universe's floor is eleven: three extended dimensions, seven [compact](/star-rangers/glossary/compact-dimensions/), one of time. The manifold begins where that floor ends, so the Corps has always written it as the **post-11 manifold**. Both terms are canonical. "Post-11" is the working shorthand and remains correct in Survey Corps usage; "Common Manifold" is the formal name.

The shorthand is parochial by construction, and knowingly so. Eleven is a local fact rather than a cosmic constant; analysts seated in a universe with a different floor would count from their own number and mean this same structure. The Corps names it from where it happens to be standing, the same habit that gave the setting three names for one boundary phenomenon (see [Overfold](/star-rangers/glossary/overfold/)).

That parochialism has one operational edge, and it is not academic. Instruments calibrated to eleven dimensions cannot resolve a boundary event whose far side has a different floor. Survey Corps grades those as a distinct hazard class from ordinary membrane proximity.
