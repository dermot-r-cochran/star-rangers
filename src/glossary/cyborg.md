**Cyborg** designates a living being, human or animal, carrying physical cybernetic modification to the body itself: replaced or augmented limbs, sensory hardware, or neural interfacing wired directly into biological tissue. It is a distinct category from [Smart Pet](/star-rangers/glossary/smart-pet/), which covers an AI cognitive subsystem running *alongside* an unmodified biological body. An individual can hold either status, both, or neither, and the two are certified and regulated separately.

## History

Cyborg technology saw its widest and least restrained deployment under [the Imperium](/star-rangers/lore/the-imperium/) (2543–2609 [UCSD](/star-rangers/glossary/ucsd/)), whose military augmentation programs treated a soldier's body as just another piece of equipment to be issued, upgraded, and reclaimed. Imperium cyborg soldiers were built for the front line, not for civilian life after it. That design priority shaped decades of postwar medical and welfare literature on what an augmentation program owes the person wearing it once the war it was built for is over.

## Regulation

Cyborg augmentation has been heavily regulated ever since. The Military Space Command came first: it wound down the Imperium's military programs and replaced them with a certification regime. Every government since has tightened the standard rather than loosened it. Cyborg modification now sits among the most tightly controlled categories of technology in the Concord. It is certified independently of, and in addition to, any AI cognitive subsystem the same individual might also carry under [the AI Safety Kernel](/star-rangers/lore/ai-safety-kernel/)'s Kernel Accords. The regime is restrictive, not merely supervisory: the default answer to a proposed modification is no, and the certification process exists to define the exceptions.

At the far end of even this regime sits preservation at [cyber-revenant](/star-rangers/lore/the-unbroken-command/) depth: indefinite, open-ended reconstruction undertaken to continue a duty rather than to treat an injury. The Military Space Command's standard forecloses it outright for any new case. The confirmed revenants persist only because nothing in the standard reaches backward to unmake a person already living under it.

The tightening has more than one cause, and the record does not rank them. One is safety. A made part inside a living body cannot be stood apart from the person it serves: if it fails or misjudges, it does so inside them, and it cannot be switched off or withdrawn without harming them. And the [AI Safety Kernel](/star-rangers/lore/ai-safety-kernel/)'s constraints apply to the made part as they apply to any made system, so, by extension, they reach the whole person who carries it. Another is ownership and accountability. A made part fitted to a body is at once part of a person and somebody's built thing, made by one party, certified by another and maintained by a third, and when it acts, the line between what the person did and what the part did is hard to draw. The Imperium settled the question by owning the soldier with the hardware, issuing and reclaiming both; no government since will accept that answer, and none has found one clean enough to make the category ordinary. A third is the welfare literature above: what the Imperium's programs never accounted for in the people who wore them. The last is political. Each confirmed cyber-revenant is an Imperium officer still at a post under an order no living authority gave, sustained by reconstruction that only the Imperium's permissiveness allowed, and each discovery has put the Imperium's augmentation programs back in public view as something still running rather than something ended. The restriction on cyborg modification, and on research toward it, is in part a reaction to them: a refusal, renewed by government after government, to leave open the road that made them. Cyborgs are not outlawed. They are rare, and what any one of them is certified to carry is limited in scope. The restriction falls on modification of the body, not on artificial intelligence in general: a made mind under the Kernel Accords, or a Smart Pet's subsystem in its collar, stays outside the body it works beside, so whose it is and who answers for it stay clear. That is not a lighter rule. Any tool that carries a made mind, a pair of spectacles included, is bound as tightly by the Kernel as the part in a cyborg's body, which is why the commissioning gate has never passed a [subsystem worn on the face or in the ear](/star-rangers/glossary/smart-pet/#why-an-animal-and-not-a-person).

Where a cyber-enhanced animal is posted to an [Orbital Habitats Compact](/star-rangers/lore/orbital-habitats-compact/) member habitat, a standing local welfare authority holds jurisdiction over it; at Eden, that is [the Eden Warden](/star-rangers/characters/eden-warden/)'s Custos persona. The jurisdiction follows specifically from the animal being cyber-enhanced. It does not automatically extend to a Smart Pet who carries no physical modification at all.
