The **local bulk** is the higher-dimensional space a set of [cosmic membranes](/star-rangers/lore/concordant-membranes/) shares. Each membrane is one universe. The bulk is the space they sit in, and none of them owns it. The word is the physicists' own: in brane-world cosmology the *bulk* is the full space and a *brane* is a surface within it.

**One bulk, many membranes.** A bulk may hold a very large number of membranes, spaced side by side along a direction that is the bulk's own. The separation measured along that direction, between neighbours, is [the Interval](/star-rangers/glossary/interval/) as Survey Corps has always measured it. Membranes in one bulk are separate universes, each authored or derived on its own account, and none is a branch of another. That is why a [Membrane Shadow](/star-rangers/glossary/membrane-shadow/) can resemble someone but is never their double.

**One floor for the whole bulk.** Every membrane in a bulk has the same dimensional floor, because the floor is the bulk's count. The home bulk's is eleven: three extended dimensions, seven [compact](/star-rangers/glossary/compact-dimensions/) and one of time. One of the seven compact dimensions is the bulk's own direction, the one the membranes are spaced along; a membrane has extent in the other ten. A universe with a different floor is in a different bulk. A [Worldwright](/star-rangers/glossary/worldwright/) sets a bulk's floor and authors its membranes into it, which is how one Worldwright comes to author many primary membranes.

**What reaches through it.** Matter, ships, beings, electromagnetics and Nature stay on their own membrane. Gravity does not stay: it is the geometry of the whole bulk, and it reaches through. That is the physics behind the rule that the far side of a narrowed Interval can be weighed but never touched. Timing and Etheric templates cross by the bands of the [Common Manifold](/star-rangers/glossary/common-manifold/), which run through every bulk it seats. See [What Crosses the Interval, and Why](/star-rangers/lore/concordant-membranes/#what-crosses-the-interval-and-why).

**Above the bulk.** The Common Manifold is a higher layer that contains the bulks, and it seats each bulk as each bulk holds its membranes. Universes with different floors meet only there, and no crossing between bulks is confirmed.

**Where the physics stops.** An eleven-dimensional spacetime is M-theory's, and so is the picture of matter on the branes with gravity alone in the bulk (Petr Hořava and Edward Witten, 1996). Neither is confirmed by experiment. Many branes in one bulk is allowed by brane-world models and predicted by none. Anything above the bulk is the record's own, since M-theory has nothing above its eleven dimensions. The full account is *For a Reader Who Knows the Physics* on [the Common Manifold](/star-rangers/lore/post-eleven-dimensional-manifold/#for-a-reader-who-knows-the-physics).
