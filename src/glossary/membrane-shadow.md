A **Membrane Shadow** is the Physical-layer counterpart to [Intermembrane Bleed](/star-rangers/glossary/intermembrane-bleed/), which is the [Etheric](/star-rangers/glossary/etheric/) one. It occurs at Type III inter-membrane proximity. A coherent mass in the neighbouring *universe*, usually a living being, sits on the far side of a narrowed [Interval](/star-rangers/glossary/interval/) and patterns the geometric strain between the two membranes with its own outline. That strain takes its shape from the neighbour and its strength from the boundary. It bends and dims ambient light, producing a full silhouette with no physical object on this side to cast it.

Nothing crosses the gap. The mass responsible stays entirely within its own [Concordant](/star-rangers/glossary/concordant/). What is seen is the shadow of something real, cast from one universe into another it never touches. If it happens to resemble someone the observer knows, that is coincidence or convergent design. It is never an alternate-timeline double, since neighbouring membranes share no common history to have diverged from.

Not to be confused with the Class III non-sapient incursives described in [Meta-Dimensional Beings: Classification](/star-rangers/lore/meta-dimensional-beings/), which have physically entered material space. A Membrane Shadow shows no thermal or mass signature on standard instruments, and only a cross-membrane gravimeter registers it directly. That absence is itself how the two are told apart.
