**Sel** (pronounced: **SEL**) is the record's pronoun for a **working** of [the Convened](/star-rangers/lore/the-convened/): the person that exists among them only while many bodies act as one on a task the season holds. The paradigm is registered here once, as every coined pronoun is, and applied wherever the record speaks of a working.

| | |
|---|---|
| Subject | **sel** — *sel changed the plan when the bank turned to rock* |
| Object | **selm** — *nobody could ask selm afterwards* |
| Possessive | **sels** — *the den was sels* |

**Why the record coined one.** The translation register would give a working *they*, and *they* is already the word for the bodies, so every sentence about a working would read as a sentence about many animals. *It* is the word for one body, the den and the instrument. Neither says the thing the record needs to say, that one someone is there for as long as the work lasts. The Survey's coinage, from *self* and *season*, says it. A single body stays *it*, and the people as a whole stay *they*.

**What the word does not claim.** It is the Survey's word, not the Convened's: they have given the record no pronoun, as they have given it no name. *Sel* says that a person is there, and nothing about how the Convened mark one, if they do. When the work ends the word has nobody left to refer to, and the record does not use it of the bodies asleep in the den.
