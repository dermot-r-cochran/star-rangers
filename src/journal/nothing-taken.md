This is a rough cut of one chapter, [Nothing Taken](/star-rangers/seasons/s01/e00/s01e00c11/), and it is filed as an impression, not as the record.

On a still afternoon three goats move together in a field for no dog. Straw gets up off the ground by the gable of a house, goes along the yard wall and lies down. A man stands on his back threshold with his head down, beside a door that is open outward and lying flat against the wall, on hinges hung to open inward. A Ranger walks down the lane with a case and puts her hand to the same door. In the morning the door opens inward again, and he walks out through it.

*This film was withdrawn on 20 November 2026 and is no longer shown on the site. At Dermot's word, the rough cut of D2 is kept in the story bible as a negative example, with its frames and its faults, and this page is unlisted.*

## Where it departs

- **The door.** A door swinging outward to lie flat failed twice as a generated motion, so those shots start from a still of the door already open. The film never shows it opening the wrong way.
- **The house changes.** It has a rough stone gable and a slate roof in the first two clips, and pale panels under a dark roof after that.
- **The goats and the straw.** The goats come to the near yard wall, not the far wall of the field. The straw is a low wisp, where the chapter gives a skirt of straw the height of a man.
- **The dog.** It turns its head to the straw. The brief asked that nothing else in frame should move.
- **The writing.** In the chapter the Ranger tries the door and writes. No writing is seen: she stands holding the sheet.
- **The bowl.** It moves from the left of the step to the middle between clips, and she walks out past it. In the chapter she steps over it.
- **The night and the morning.** The film has no night, no dog across the front step, and no sweeping. It cuts from dusk to the man walking out.
- **Faces.** The brief asked for none. The edge of his cheek shows for a few seconds on the threshold.

## Where it stands

It is fine as a Journal impression. It would need more polish before it could be attached to a chapter or an episode, and until then it is not linked from the chapter it comes from.
