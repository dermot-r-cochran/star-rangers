This is a rough cut of one chapter, [The Character of the Light](/star-rangers/seasons/s01/e01/s01e01c03/), and it is filed as an impression, not as the record.

Three figures walk out along a stone spit toward a white lighthouse, seen from behind, and a woman stands in its open door. Inside, in a whitewashed room, a man asks her about the clock that times the light, and she answers. A finger runs down one column of an old register, and no entry can be read. On the gallery the lantern shows its character, two flashes and then dark, again and again. She goes down the stair with her back to the camera and says why the light is kept. The film ends on the sea, with a low dark form and three small lights far off on the water.

*The Character of the Light. 1 minute 58 seconds, the first rough cut. It was made as a chain of 10-second clips in Grok Imagine, each new shot starting from a still made first on Gemini, and cut together locally. The sound is Grok's own. The six subtitled lines are the chapter's. An impression, not the record.*

## Where it departs

- **The rhythm.** The chapter gives fifteen seconds from one group of flashes to the next. In the film the groups come about five seconds apart.
- **The window.** The window of the room shows an island. The brief asked for the far low form on the water, and the record puts no island there.
- **The robot.** The third walker on the spit is slim and white. The brief, and the chapter's own picture, give a boxy grey-khaki figure. Rook-7's own page has since moved to a third body, broad and pale grey, and the chapter's picture has yet to follow.
- **The clock.** The chapter has a clock that times the light, and the brief put a plain wall clock in the room. What that clock looks like in 2826 is still an open question. In the stills it looked old-fashioned for 2826, and I had it taken out. So the man asks about a clock the film never shows.
- **The stair in the room.** In the stills it appeared to turn back on itself, and I had that taken out too. The later shot on the stair is kept, because its steps run one way.
- **A cheek.** The brief asked for no faces. The edge of her cheek shows twice in the room.

## Where it stands

It is fine as a Journal impression. It would need more polish before it could be attached to a chapter or an episode, and until then it is not linked from the chapter it comes from.
