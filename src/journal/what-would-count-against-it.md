*The real-world notes behind a question a hard science fiction setting should be able to answer about itself: if the cosmology were real, what would show it, and what would count against it? The names here are from our century, and the Archive's own pages name none of them.*

The question deserves a precise answer, because the setting is built in a way that changes what the answer can be. [Physics Comparison](/star-rangers/lore/physics-comparison/) states the architecture in its first paragraph: the cosmos "mimics real-world physics at the Material and Physical layers within any given Concordant", and "the differences emerge from the Etheric layer upward". One consequence follows directly, and most fiction lacks it. **Whatever real physics discovers next becomes this setting's physics as well**, unless it collides with one of the deviations the record has specified. So the useful question is not whether science could overturn the cosmology as a whole. It is which specified deviations are exposed, and how far.

Karl Popper's test, from *Logik der Forschung* (1934), is the right instrument: a claim is empirical if some possible observation would count against it. Applied layer by layer to the [Five Layers](/star-rangers/lore/five-layers/) and the [Cosmic Cascade](/star-rangers/lore/cosmic-cascade/), the claims fall into three groups.

**Claims real experiments already test.** The most exposed claim in the record is a modest one. Probability in this cosmos is "identical at base" to real quantum mechanics but carries "a faint Etheric texture that can be sculpted within strict bounds", the kind of correction a Levril makes when a sensor sweep is nudged by a fraction of a degree. Our experiments bear on that directly. Three-slit interference experiments look for any term beyond what quantum mechanics predicts, and Urbasi Sinha and colleagues bounded one at about a part in a hundred in 2010; later work has tightened it. The loophole-free Bell tests of 2015 matched quantum predictions exactly. Long efforts to show that intention can bias a random-number generator have not survived replication.

A null result at ever greater precision would count against the texture, if it held everywhere. It does not need to, and the record already says why. On [The Scope of Physical Law](/star-rangers/lore/the-scope-of-physical-law/), the Etheric terms take local values: "quiet here, loud near a boundary". A laboratory on Earth sits in a quiet zone, so the canon predicts exactly the null results our laboratories return. The claim is exposed only where a story puts an Etheric effect inside an ordinary quiet zone, in front of instruments that ought to see it.

Two other claims sit close to real data. The record's seven compact dimensions are consistent with every short-range test of gravity so far: Eric Adelberger's group at the University of Washington has confirmed the inverse-square law down to about fifty micrometres. Nothing can practically falsify them, because compact dimensions can always be smaller than the next experiment reaches. And Concordants, as zone-scoped physics, would find their nearest real echo in constants that vary across the sky. Claims of a gradient in the fine-structure constant from quasar spectra exist, and remain contested.

**Claims real science could reach only in principle.** If our universe once collided with another bubble universe, the collision could leave a disc-shaped imprint on the cosmic microwave background. Stephen Feeney and colleagues searched the WMAP data for one in 2011 and found none. A confirmed imprint would be the most striking support the record's membranes could ever receive: evidence that universes border one another. Its absence says only that no collision happened within our horizon.

Fold transit is the claim most likely to lose its real-world footing. A traversable shortcut through spacetime needs negative energy, and the quantum energy inequalities derived by Lawrence Ford and Thomas Roman in the 1990s limit negative energy to small amounts for short times. A future theory of quantum gravity might go further and forbid changes in spacetime topology altogether. If it did, the setting would bend exactly where it already says it departs from physics. Fold transit belongs to the one licensed deviation, and *What the Record Refuses* files it as kept but priced, not as physics our century can build.

The claim that minds couple lawfully to the Etheric layer is the hardest of this group. Neuroscience can narrow the room for any extra coupling, but it cannot prove the coupling is zero. No experiment can establish a null for an effect whose size has not been specified, and the question of why physical processes are experienced at all is not one data settles.

**Claims the record files as beyond any instrument.** Above the Etheric, the record is explicit, and explicit in the right direction. Of the general resurrection it says that nothing about it registers, "and nothing about its absence would register either", so the claim is "neither testable nor falsifiable in the sense the Survey Corps means by those words — not unproven pending better instruments, but outside what any instrument is shaped to reach." The traditions that hold the subtle influence say themselves that it is invisible to instruments. The Cascade's invariants are stated as what the Survey Archive "can establish by invariant-only inference", not by measurement.

Popper would call these claims non-empirical, and the record agrees with him. That is why they live as structure, as devotion and as Codex accounts, and never as instrument findings. It is also why **no discovery could confirm a Worldwright**. Every measurement is equally consistent with a Concordant that merely happens to have the shape it has. A designer is not a quantity an instrument returns. The record says as much whenever it files a question of intent as *insufficient basis for any finding*.

**What follows for the writing.** Ranked by exposure, the claims run from the Etheric texture on probability, through the fusion ceiling and the bans on faster-than-light signalling and retrocausation, where real physics already agrees with the record, to fold transit, then the compact dimensions, membranes and Concordants, and last the upper Cascade, which no experiment can touch by design. The weak point is not the cosmology. It is the scene. As long as Etheric effects appear near boundaries, or in constraint-literate perception rather than in an ordinary laboratory's readings, every experiment our century has run, and every one it is likely to run, stays on the record's side.

That is the same discipline the setting asks of its own Archive: state each claim at its width, say which ones an instrument could reach, and do not let a reading carry an inference the data has not earned.
