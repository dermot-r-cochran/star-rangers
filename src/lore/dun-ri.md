The record knows Dún Rí from the inside of its walls, and almost nothing of it from outside.

## The House

**Dún Rí** is a fortified household on high ground above a tidal river, the Boyne. Its centre, as far as the record shows it, is a walled terrace garden: a gravel path, a balustrade, a sundial, an east wall, a well, roses late into the autumn, and a gate at the bottom of the garden. The household keeps a kitchen with its own folk words for the places where the light is wrong, a footman, and a household sage.

[Elvira](/star-rangers/characters/elvira/) grew up there as its warden-heir. The sage taught her a method and asked what she had found, never what she was. *The layer underneath*, the phrase she later used at the Marsh Causeway, was his before it was hers. Her last months there, in the autumn of 2822 UCSD, are the prequel chapters of Season 1, from [Below the Bells](/star-rangers/seasons/s01/e00/s01e00c00/) to [What the Stones Confirmed](/star-rangers/seasons/s01/e00/s01e00c02/). She reached the outpost at the [Marsh Causeway](/star-rangers/lore/boundary-zones/) in the early spring of 2823.

[Aldera](/star-rangers/characters/aldera/), the household's cat, left first. In the late autumn of 2822 she went out by the garden gate rather than the terrace, of her own choosing, and took ship. On the tidal rock of [Carraig Bán](/star-rangers/seasons/s01/e00/s01e00c03/) she met [Nessa](/star-rangers/characters/nessa/), who pointed her south and west, to the Causeway.

## The Abbey Below

Down from the house, on a tidal stretch of the Boyne, stands an old river abbey, a contemplative chapter of the [Fellowship of Light](/star-rangers/lore/fellowship-of-light/) kept by [Brother Daire](/star-rangers/characters/brother-daire/). Elvira walked down to it twice in 2822 and told nobody in the household the real reason for either visit. The abbey has its own telling of [Saint Aoife](/star-rangers/lore/saint-aoife/), [The Champion at the Thorn Well](/star-rangers/codex/the-champion-at-the-thorn-well/), and its keeper's reading of a crew of two in one vessel, [Two in the One Currach](/star-rangers/codex/two-in-the-one-currach/).

## The Meeting of 2806

In 2806 UCSD [Galahad Thorne](/star-rangers/characters/galahad-thorne/), then seventeen, came to Dún Rí with his father on an errand about a boundary of the ordinary sort: a field line that two families had been arguing over politely for a century. He met Elvira on the terrace step, and he used the kitchen's word for the wrong light by the garden gate. The record has only his memory of it, and her recognition of the word twenty years later in [The Word She Did Not Like](/star-rangers/seasons/s01/e03/s01e03c05/). The Archive files that meeting as one man's recollection and confirms nothing by it.

## The Chronicle That Shares the Name

A recited chronicle of the Fellowship's chapters, [The Flight of Aldera](/star-rangers/codex/flight-of-aldera-chronicle-of-dun-ri/), tells of a Princess Elvira of Dún Rí and her cat. The Codex files it in its own voice. The Archive's habit holds: a shared name is not evidence of a shared identity, and the chronicle's figures are not confirmed to be the warden-heir and the cat of Season 1.

## What Is Not Established

The record names the river and no world outright, but the Boyne and the kings the abbey has outlasted place the house on Earth. It does not say who holds the house now, how Elvira came to be its warden-heir, or what the household knew about her. Whether the river abbey's Brother Daire is the scribe of the same name at Cill Aoife fourteen centuries earlier is left open on [his page](/star-rangers/characters/brother-daire/). All of these stay marked absences until a chapter needs them.
