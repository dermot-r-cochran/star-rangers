Two institutions work aboard the station, and neither one is its guest in quite the same way.

## What It Is

**Halyx Relay Station** is a station in the Halyx System, with Cerebraun and human staff aboard, the system of [Meridian](/star-rangers/lore/planets/meridian/) and [the Sentinel](/star-rangers/lore/planets/sentinel/). The system is the [Cerebraun Hegemony's](/star-rangers/lore/cerebraun-hegemony/), and so is the station's chief office: the Halyx System liaison office, on the staff of the Administrator-Voice, [Sohrel](/star-rangers/characters/sohrel/), who has held the Halyx portfolio for about three centuries.

The [Survey Corps](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-survey-corps/) keeps a liaison archive aboard, under an exchange agreement that releases Hegemony survey telemetry to the Corps a decade or two after the fact. The Rangers work there as guests: in Shepherd's words, a relay station at the edge of someone else's jurisdiction. The station also has a review chamber where the Corps hears its own officers' findings, and an outer ring of transit corridors.

## The Translation Channel

The station keeps the channel between the Hegemony and Meridian's reef-cities. Its instrumentation renders the reef-cities' claw-percussion and slow chemical signalling into language a Cerebraun can read, and sends Hegemony language back the other way. [Tavesh](/star-rangers/characters/tavesh/) keeps it. The Administrator-Voice's office maintains it mostly out of obligation and rarely trusts it past a paragraph. The Survey Archive's request for a history of Meridian's people by one of them went out on it, and [Second Outwork's](/star-rangers/characters/second-outwork/) answer, [What Came in the Water](/star-rangers/codex/what-came-in-the-water/), came back on it in its exact shape.

## 2828: The Sentinel Reheard

In the late winter of 2828 UCSD, Principal [Tissadelle Shepherd](/star-rangers/characters/tissadelle-shepherd/), eleven days in rank and posted as the Survey Corps liaison officer, was clearing the archive's backlog queue. File four hundred and twelve was the Sentinel's, catalogued for three centuries as an automated hazard beacon. She routed its waveform to audio and heard [structure in it](/star-rangers/seasons/s03/e01/s03e01c01/). The finding was reproduced without her, by [independent verification](/star-rangers/seasons/s03/e01/s03e01c02/) in the station's review chamber. In the early spring the Long Shelf reef-city's council petitioned, through the translation channel, to be consulted about the Sentinel ([What Meridian Asked](/star-rangers/seasons/s03/e01/s03e01c03/)). Before her rotation ended that spring, the Prime Ganglion's office asked Shepherd for an assessment under her own name ([For the Record](/star-rangers/seasons/s03/e01/s03e01c04/)).

## 2831: The Refusal to Certify

In the late summer of 2831, the recertification of the outer-ring transit corridor stalled for six weeks on Hull Systems Specialist [Qiren Tal's](/star-rangers/characters/qiren-tal/) refusal to certify. Sixteen of the seventeen strike points on the junction answered as they always had. The fault was a certified-equivalent seal substitution, introduced by a maintenance consolidation, that loaded the corridor frame asymmetrically only under transit strain, below the sampling window of the certification monitors ([Refusal to Certify](/star-rangers/seasons/s05/e01/s05e01c01/)). Line Captain Shepherd's report credited the refusal as the detecting instrument. Since then the station files a specialist's refusal to certify as a reading that needs a disposition, not as a performance matter.

## What Is Not Established

The record does not say where in the Halyx System the station keeps its position, who built it or when, or how many people it holds.
