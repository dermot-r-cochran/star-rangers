A force tells a thing where to go. A rule tells it whether it may stay. The Etheric layer is written in the second kind of mathematics, and most of the confusion about it comes from reaching for the first.

## Constraint, Not Force

The [Five Layers](/star-rangers/lore/five-layers/) file Layer 3 as the rule-template layer: archetypal templates constrain what physical and material expression can remain coherent. Every word of that is a word from the mathematics of constraint. The Physical layer supplies the dynamics, the account of how a configuration changes from one moment to the next. The Etheric layer supplies none. It supplies admissibility: of all the configurations the dynamics could pass through, which ones are the kind that hold.

The difference matters because the two kinds of mathematics behave differently. A force added to a system gives it somewhere new to go. A constraint added to a system can only take places away. Lay a constraint over any set of possibilities and the set gets smaller or stays the same; it never grows. That single property is what [the generalisation of quantum mechanics](/star-rangers/lore/generalised-quantum-mechanics/) means when it says the extension adds couplings and never permissions, and the whole of this page is that property stated at length.

## A Template Is a Test, Not a List

The Five Layers put it in one line: a template says what may cohere, not which things did, the way a grammar holds no list of the sentences said in it. In that mathematics, a template is a **test**. Present it with a configuration and it answers one of two ways, admissible or not. It does not enumerate the admissible configurations, and it could not: the set it describes is open-ended, like the set of grammatical sentences, and a test is the only finite way to describe an open-ended set.

So a template holds no record, and the Archive's finding that [only the Conceptual layer keeps a register](/star-rangers/lore/five-layers/) is not a limitation of Layer 3 but its shape. Asking a template which instances there have been is asking a test what it has been applied to, and a test does not remember.

The record uses the word *admissibility window* for the same thing seen from the Cascade's side: an [Archewright's](/star-rangers/lore/archewright/) archetypes enter the Etheric as rule templates and become physical admissibility windows. A window is a region of configurations the test admits. Nothing inside the window is compelled to occur; nothing outside it can last.

## What Persistence Means

The one observable the Five Layers give Layer 3 is **persistence after the local trigger is removed**, and the mathematics of that is older than any physics. Take a pattern a system is showing. Remove whatever was driving it. Under the kernel's own dynamics the pattern should then decay at a rate the kernel predicts, and in a quiet region it does. Where a template admits the pattern, it does not decay on that schedule. The configuration is one the dynamics do not carry away from itself, or carry away far more slowly than they should.

That is a statement about time, and it is measured as one: how long a pattern outlasts the removal of its cause, set against how long the kernel alone would give it. The [dryadic trees](/star-rangers/lore/dryadic-trees/) give the record its plainest case. Their leaves answer the wind a fraction of a second late, between a tenth and four tenths of a second, consistently enough to serve as a field indicator, and the lag is read as the foliage answering a coupled constraint after it has answered the air. That number is a response of one organism in one grove. It is not a coefficient of the theory, and the record is careful not to promote it to one.

The boundary case the Five Layers already state follows from the same arithmetic. A physical expression under no template is not running loose. It occurs, and under the kernel's dynamics it goes, on the kernel's schedule. Transient by construction, not by suppression: nothing has to police it, because decay is what the kernel does to an unsupported pattern anyway.

## Where the Terms Enter

A test is not a term in an equation, and the Physical layer is written in equations. The two meet in the extension. The generalisation of quantum mechanics describes the full theory as the kernel plus lawful coupling terms, each weighted by how strongly it applies at a given place and in given company. Written as plainly as the record allows:

> what happens next = what the kernel says + ε₁ · (first coupling) + ε₂ · (second coupling) + …

Each ε is a number that depends on where the system is and what is near it. In deep quiet rock, in boundary-remote space, in unminded apparatus, every ε is zero, and a term multiplied by zero is not a small correction but no term at all. That is why the generalisation calls the quiet case an exact restriction rather than an approximation holding well, and why a sealed [cavern](/star-rangers/lore/quantum-computing-and-the-cavern-constraint/) is a laboratory old Earth would recognise. Near a boundary, or wherever a mind is present, some ε is not zero, and the kernel needs its larger frame.

The couplings the record has named, filed page by page, are the known terms: the faint texture on quantum randomness, the meaning-field overlays, the second decoherence channel, and the harmonic admissibility structure that corridor transit runs on. Whether that list is complete, and whether some of its entries are one term seen from two sides, the record does not know.

## Why the Terms Add Nothing

The part of this that is mathematics rather than filing is the claim that the extension cannot grant a permission the kernel withholds, and the record can say why without a single constant.

The kernel's prohibitions — no signal faster than transit, no influence running backward, no reading of an outcome before it is drawn — are not facts about particular numbers in the theory. They hold for every lawful evolution the kernel admits, whatever its terms, so long as the terms are of the kernel's own kind: a further weak coupling, a further way for a system to lose coherence. Each term in the extension is of that kind. Adding one gives the system another thing it may couple to and another way it may decohere. It leaves the total probability of all outcomes at one, it changes nothing about which outcomes an instrument can show, since a coupling alters how a state evolves and not what can be measured of it, and it leaves every prohibition standing, because the prohibitions were never about the terms.

What a coupling can do is shift the weights. It can make one outcome somewhat likelier and another somewhat less, as every ordinary interaction already does. That is the whole of what [a Levril's](/star-rangers/lore/levrils/) probability work amounts to in this frame: a sculpted gradient, never an altered outcome, and the generalisation's worked example of a lawful operation whose restriction to the kernel is the identity. Every claim on record that an Etheric effect did more has died in review the same way: on inspection it needed a term that was not of the kernel's kind, and no such term has been found.

## Which Scope Each Statement Carries

Nothing on this page is said wider than its ground, and [the scope ladder](/star-rangers/lore/the-scope-of-physical-law/) places each statement.

The mathematics of constraint itself, what a test is, what it means for a set to shrink and never grow, what persistence under a dynamics is, belongs to the **mathematical regime** the Metawright settles. It is the same mathematics on every page of the record and would be the same in [Counterpane](/star-rangers/lore/counterpane-archecluster/), whose instruments brought back a different physics and no different arithmetic.

Which templates exist, the vocabulary of tests a universe can carry, is the Archewright's and so is **archecluster**-scoped. Whether Counterpane's extension differs with its kernel is a question the one surveyed membrane cannot answer, and its file says so.

Which of those templates a given universe carries, and through which compact dimensions they have extent, is **membrane**-scoped: the physics-plus-Etheric envelope a Worldwright selects.

And the value of each ε, loud here, zero there, is **Concordant**-scoped, since a Concordant is by definition a region where one physical and Etheric rule-set holds. A template's local values are the most local thing about it, and the only part of it an instrument has ever read.

One consequence is worth drawing out, because it is where the spatial grammar misleads. A template has geometric extent through a Concordant's [compact dimensions](/star-rangers/glossary/compact-dimensions/), which is where a Layer 3 effect becomes measurable. The mathematics does not make the Etheric a place. A test is defined over configurations, and a configuration has a position; the test does not. What is spread through the compact dimensions is where the test is applied with non-zero weight, not the test. The Etheric remains a causal mode, and [its glossary entry](/star-rangers/glossary/etheric/) holds the line this page's mathematics only confirms.

## The Hand Without the Theorem

The record files mathematics as [the theorem and the hand](/star-rangers/lore/mathematics-and-its-notations/), and the Etheric is the place where the two are furthest apart. Every working practitioner of [constraint literacy](/star-rangers/glossary/constraint-literacy/) is applying the test. None of them holds the test's statement.

Elvira's chalk and thread are a procedure for finding, by trial, where the window's edge runs: lay a line, see whether what it marks holds, move it. That is a hand. It is a good hand, and it has held at the Marsh Causeway. A Levril-trained practitioner can tell before the attempt which actions the local structure will reject, which is a better hand. Neither is a theorem. Nobody in the record can write down a template and prove from the writing what it admits, and nobody has shown that such a writing exists in any notation the Material layer can hold.

This is not the usual gap between discovery and proof, which time closes. [The Levrils](/star-rangers/lore/levrils/) perceive Etheric structures directly, because those structures are expressed in bands they inhabit; a Material-layer instrument reads the extension only through its correlates, because an instrument is a kernel device. The practitioners who have the best hand are the ones with the least reason to want a theorem, and the archivists who want the theorem have no hand at all.

## What the Record Does Not Hold

The record states the form of the Etheric mathematics with some confidence and almost nothing else, and it keeps the two apart.

**No constant has been measured.** Not one ε has a value in units. The leaf lag, the second decoherence rate in a given cavern before sealing, the persistence drift of a fold, are each a number on its own page, each the behaviour of one system, and none has been reduced to a coefficient that would predict another system's response. The Engineering Corps working paper [*Coherence Debt as Quantum Interest*](/star-rangers/codex/coherence-debt-as-quantum-interest/) is the record's own model of how such a bench model is honestly written: it has no constants, and it excludes every Etheric term and says so.

**How a template is represented is not known.** The record knows what a template does, admits or refuses, and where it acts. It does not know what mathematical object a template is: whether a region, a function, a structure through the compact dimensions, or something a Material-layer notation cannot write. Every candidate the record has tried is a description of the test's effects, not of the test.

**Whether the list of couplings is complete is not known**, nor whether its entries are independent. The four named terms may be four, or two seen from different stations.

**Whether the extension could be derived from the kernel is answered only in the negative, by construction.** If it could, it would be the kernel. The record holds no proof that it cannot, and files the question as the generalisation files its own: closed by structure rather than by answer.

**Whether the vocabulary is one throughout the cluster** is unmeasured. Which templates a membrane carries differs from membrane to membrane, and the record has read a neighbour's templates twice, as [bleed](/star-rangers/lore/concordant-membranes/) across a narrowed Interval and as a body built to them. Whether every membrane in the cluster draws its templates from one vocabulary is what *archecluster-scoped* asserts, and no survey has been made that could test it.

The last thing the page can say is the one the Five Layers said first. The Etheric does not keep a register, so the mathematics of the Etheric is a mathematics without a history: it can say what may last, and it holds nothing about what did. The Archive, which is a register, has to read the layer the only way a register can read a rule, by watching what stays.
