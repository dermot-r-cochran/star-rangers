When the local dimensions explain the injury but not why the clocks keep disagreeing, Survey Corps analysts look higher. That suspicion points into the Common Manifold.

The **Common Manifold** is the higher-order spacelike and timelike structure that lies beyond any single universe's own dimensional floor, governing cross-[membrane](/star-rangers/lore/concordant-membranes/) coherence, causality buffering, and fold survivability. Every universe in the [Grand Ensemble](/star-rangers/lore/ensemble-multiverse/) is seated in it. It is the one piece of architecture they demonstrably share.

Within it, each **coherent universe** occupies its own bounded [cosmic membrane](/star-rangers/lore/concordant-membranes/), whatever its origin, since coherence rather than authorship is the criterion. Each is separated from its neighbours by the [Interval](/star-rangers/glossary/interval/): manifold separation, measured across the Common Manifold rather than through either universe's own space. Concordant Zones are regions *inside* a membrane, not membranes themselves.

**The bulk lies between the membrane and the manifold.** Membranes are not seated in the manifold one by one. They sit in a [local bulk](/star-rangers/glossary/local-bulk/), the space a set of membranes shares, and one bulk may hold a very large number of them, side by side and with no common history. The manifold is the layer above: it contains the bulks and seats each of them as each bulk holds its membranes. So the Interval has two scales. Between neighbours in one bulk it runs along the bulk's own direction, which lies inside the manifold and inside neither universe. Between bulks it runs through the manifold's higher dimensions, and no crossing at that scale is confirmed.

## Why Survey Corps Says "Post-11"

For our home universe, the local dimensional floor is eleven: three visible physical dimensions, seven [compact dimensions](/star-rangers/glossary/compact-dimensions/), and one dimension of time. The manifold begins where that floor ends, so Survey Corps has always written it as the **post-11 manifold**, and still does.

The name is accurate and parochial at the same time. Eleven is a local fact, not a cosmic constant. A Worldwright authoring elsewhere in the Grand Ensemble can select a different split entirely, and analysts seated in a universe with a different floor would count from their own number and mean this same structure. "Post-11" records where the Corps was standing when it started counting. "Common Manifold" records what it was counting into. Both terms are canonical; the first is the working shorthand, the second is what it is.

This matters operationally at exactly one point, and it is not a nicety: a boundary event whose far side has a different dimensional floor cannot be read off instruments calibrated to eleven. See [The Grand Ensemble Multiverse](/star-rangers/lore/ensemble-multiverse/).

## Beyond the Local Floor

Three bands are distinguished, numbered here from the home universe's floor of eleven. The numbering is local bookkeeping; the bands themselves are not.

- **Dimensions 12-15 (Extended spacelike lattice):** distribute geometric strain when universes overlap and prevent local metric tearing.
- **Dimensions 16-19 (Extended timelike channels):** absorb timing disagreements between overlapping causal frames and prevent timeline shearing.
- **Dimensions 20+ (Trans-concordant constraint field):** encode nonlocal boundary conditions that Worldwright and Telearch operations must satisfy, and carry archetypal templates *between* membranes across the Interval.

These dimensions are not directly navigable by Material beings. They are inferred through lawful observables: persistent instrument drift, boundary resonance asymmetry, and stable anomaly recurrence windows.

**The bands and the bulk are together the whole answer to what can cross the Interval.** **Gravity** reaches a neighbour in the same bulk through the [bulk](/star-rangers/glossary/local-bulk/) itself: gravity is geometry, the geometry of the whole space, and it is not confined to a membrane. The 12-15 band carries the same geometric strain one level up and spreads it, so a narrowing stays local instead of tearing. The 16-19 and 20+ bands carry causal timing and archetypal templates. That is why gravity is measurable across a narrowed Interval while electromagnetics and Nature are not: they are confined to their own membrane and have no band. Matter and beings have neither, which is the physical content of the rule that nothing crosses. See [What Crosses the Interval, and Why](/star-rangers/lore/concordant-membranes/).

Note the division of labour with the local floor, since the two are easily confused: a universe's own [compact dimensions](/star-rangers/glossary/compact-dimensions/) are where its [Etheric](/star-rangers/glossary/etheric/) templates have geometric extent *at home*, inside one membrane. The trans-concordant constraint field is what lets one membrane's templates become readable *in another* when the Interval narrows. Same layer, two scopes — local extent and cross-membrane reach — and they are not the same dimensions.

## Nobody Made It

The question comes up as soon as anyone learns the Cascade has authoring tiers in it: who built the manifold? The answer is that the question has the wrong shape, and the entry above already contains the reason.

**No [Worldwright](/star-rangers/glossary/worldwright/) makes it.** A Worldwright creates a primary universe — the whole vessel at once, physics envelope, Etheric coupling, interaction regime and internal zone structure — which is one vessel. The manifold is what every vessel of every [archecluster](/star-rangers/glossary/archecluster/) and every [formcluster](/star-rangers/glossary/formcluster/) is seated in, shared regardless of dimensional floor and regardless of origin. It is also, in its 20+ band, what encodes the nonlocal boundary conditions **Worldwright and Telearch operations must satisfy** — and a condition something must satisfy is not a thing it built.

**So which tier does make it?** Three are involved, and separating them is the whole of the answer.

[**Conseleme**](/star-rangers/glossary/conseleme/) fixes that **seating obtains** — that coherent universes are distinct, that they are separated, and that the separation can carry what the Cascade requires it to carry: geometric strain, causal timing disagreement, archetypal template propagation. That is a condition, not an object. It settles that there must be a manifold, and nothing at all about which.

A [**Metawright**](/star-rangers/lore/cosmic-cascade/) settles the mathematics a manifold could be expressed in. A manifold is a mathematical object, and mathematics is a conceptual realm's business — so no manifold is describable, let alone instantiable, until some realm has supplied the terms.

A [**Formwright**](/star-rangers/glossary/formcluster/) instantiates the manifold itself, as a **formtype**: a lawful template built from that realm's mathematics, satisfying Conseleme's condition, and standing as the arena everything downstream is seated in. This is the tier the object belongs to. [Archewrights](/star-rangers/lore/archewright/) draw archetypes and possibility space *within* it; Worldwrights seat universes *in* it.

The ladder is the same one the Conceptual layer already follows, extended by one rung: Conseleme fixes what any mathematics must satisfy and a Metawright settles which there is; Conseleme fixes that seating must obtain and a **Formwright settles which manifold there is.**

**So a different manifold is not merely conceivable — it is what other formclusters have.** Each is a different formtype, possibly in different mathematics, satisfying the same Conseleme condition and answering to nothing else. Which means a [formcluster](/star-rangers/glossary/formcluster/) is not only a common origin but a **common seating**: the universes that share templates share the arena those templates are laid out in. That is the deeper reason a shared formcluster explains why two universes are commensurable at all, and it is why [archecluster](/star-rangers/glossary/archecluster/) membership remains orthogonal to adjacency — descent is an Archewright fact, position is a Formwright one.

**Which makes the entry's own title parochial twice over.** *Post-11* records where the Corps was standing when it started counting. ***Common*** records how far the Corps can see — every universe it has ever reached is seated in this manifold, and that is a statement about the Corps' reach rather than about the Grand Ensemble. The Archive has kept the word and stopped defending it.

This sharpens the operational warning accordingly. The three bands above are one formtype's structure, not a census. A boundary event whose far side belongs to a different formcluster is not merely calibrated to a different dimensional floor; it may not be describable in bands at all, a band being an artefact of the template doing the describing. The record holds no confirmed case of anything crossing between manifolds — all documented Interval traffic is within one. Whether that is a limit of observation or a limit of the thing, the Archive declines to say, and notes it would expect the record to look identical either way.

**What a Worldwright does make, and what comes with it.** Creating the universe creates the membrane, because a membrane is what a universe occupies by virtue of being one. There is no second act in which a skin is fitted to a finished vessel. Note the consequence that follows: membrane-hood is **not** a Worldwright privilege. Derived universes receive no Worldwright and occupy membranes regardless, exactly as coherence-not-authorship predicts.

**And nothing can be moved.** If seating is an Upper Structure condition, then no Worldwright, Telearch or Universal Authority can shift its universe relative to another, closer or further, ever. Membrane proximity is something that **happens to** universes rather than something anyone does to them. That is the structural reason a boundary event is discovered rather than caused, and why the record has never once produced a party to hold responsible for one — not because the investigation failed, but because the category of responsible party does not apply at this tier.

## For a Reader Who Knows the Physics

The manifold borrows the geometry of M-theory as shape, never as claim ([The Third Sum Rule](/star-rangers/journal/the-third-sum-rule/)). A reader who knows that geometry will have questions, and the answers below keep three things apart: what physics has established, what it has proposed and not tested, and what is the record's own.

**Is there anything above the bulk in M-theory?** No. In M-theory spacetime has eleven dimensions, ten of space and one of time, and that is the whole of it; the *bulk* is that eleven-dimensional space. A layer above the bulk that contains it and seats other bulks is the record's addition. F-theory is sometimes called twelve-dimensional, but the two directions it adds to string theory's ten are a mathematical device for tracking how the strength of the string coupling varies from place to place, not a twelfth direction anything could sit in.

**Is our universe a brane?** Nobody knows. Brane-world cosmology, in which the visible universe is a membrane in a larger bulk, is a family of hypotheses. No experiment has found an extra dimension: collider searches have come up empty, and gravity has been measured keeping its ordinary law down to separations of a few tens of micrometres.

**Can one bulk hold many branes?** The models allow it, and none predicts it. Where there are several, matter and the forces of ordinary physics stay on their own brane, and gravity alone travels through the bulk. Petr Hořava and Edward Witten's picture of 1996 has exactly that division, with a brane at each end of the eleventh dimension, and the [Interval](/star-rangers/glossary/interval/), with its rule that gravity is the only force to register across a narrowed one, is borrowed from it. That the [home bulk](/star-rangers/glossary/local-bulk/) holds a very large number of membranes is the record's, not a result.

**Is that not a multiverse?** It is, in the ordinary sense of the word, and so is the [Grand Ensemble](/star-rangers/lore/ensemble-multiverse/). What the record refuses is narrower: Hugh Everett's many worlds, in which every quantum outcome happens, and branching or alternate timelines of any kind ([The History That Happens](/star-rangers/journal/the-history-that-happens/)). Membranes in one bulk are separate universes side by side, with no common history to have branched from. In Max Tegmark's scheme the Ensemble is a Level II multiverse, never Level III.

**Do universes really differ in their number of dimensions?** Partly. In M-theory the total is always eleven. How those eleven divide between extended and compact can differ from one solution to the next, so a universe with a split other than three, seven and one is a different but standard solution. A bulk whose total is not eleven, or one with more than one dimension of time, is beyond M-theory. The record allows a [Worldwright](/star-rangers/glossary/worldwright/) to set one, and that allowance is the record's own. It is also why such a bulk is seated in the Common Manifold beside ours and never inside it.

## Operational Consequences

- Fold operations that ignore the manifold's timelike channels produce unstable re-entry clocks.
- Boundary-zone incidents intensify when manifold spacelike strain cannot dissipate.
- [Levril](/star-rangers/lore/levrils/) interventions often target manifold constraint gradients rather than local physical geometry.

The practical rule for Survey Corps and boundary practitioners is simple: if a universe's own dimensions explain the symptom but not the persistence, the cause is likely anchored in the Common Manifold.
