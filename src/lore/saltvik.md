Ask a Knarrheim delegate which Line settlement actually keeps the Solar System's shipyards supplied, and the answer is never Knarrheim itself.

**Saltvik** is a working cluster of six major ore-refinery platforms and dozens of smaller claim-tenders, threaded through a metal-rich rubble field the Line has worked continuously since the cooperative's earliest Belt surveys. The name means "salt bay." Its founders kept it from a home-village fishing cove none of them ever expected to see again. Where [Knarrheim](/star-rangers/lore/knarrheim/) grew into the Line's political and cultural centre almost by accident, Saltvik has never been anything but what it was built for. It is the Line's largest single source of refined ore, and the settlement every other Line habitat depends on more than it likes to admit in public.

## The Rubble Field

Saltvik's claim sits inside an unusually dense rubble cluster rather than a single large body. That geological accident makes the field both rich and dangerous to work, since no two claim-tenders' charts of it fully agree from one survey season to the next. Line pilots train on Saltvik's approach lanes because they are considered the hardest routine navigation task in Belt-registered space. By unspoken but universal Line custom, a tender crew that has worked a full season at Saltvik is never questioned on its piloting again anywhere else in the fleet.

## Six Platforms, One Bell

Saltvik's six refinery platforms operate under separate local foremen but share a single, deliberately old-fashioned safety custom. A physical bell hangs at the cluster's central relay platform and is rung by hand. Its ring signals an all-stop across every platform in the field at once, the instant any one crew calls a hazard. Automated hazard broadcasts exist and are used for routine coordination. The bell is original to the settlement's earliest years, has been replaced twice and has never been retired. It is kept because every platform's crew has been trained since childhood to stop first and check the automated feed second. Line safety records credit the custom with more prevented incidents than any single piece of Saltvik's considerably more modern hazard-detection equipment.

## People and Custom

Saltvik's population skews younger and more transient than Knarrheim's settled residential rings. Claim-tender and refinery work draws Line crews for a working generation rather than a lifetime. A family with deep Knarrheim roots commonly sends its newest crew generation to Saltvik first, on the understanding that a season worked there is closer to a rite of passage than an ordinary posting. Swedish predominates as the working language on the refinery floor, a demographic accident of which founding cooperatives first staffed the cluster. Saltvik's common-room signage stays deliberately multilingual out of long habit. The settlement sends its full delegate allotment to [the Thing](/star-rangers/lore/knarr-line/) every session it convenes. Across the rest of the Line, Saltvik delegates have the reputation of being the least inclined to leave a disputed allocation formula unchallenged.
