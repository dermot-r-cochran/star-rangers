canon_facts:
  - "The line's notice of Dock Seven reached the fold-approach post that had been Shepherd's first command with the forenoon summary on the second day after. It gave the Sufficient Cause not recovered, eleven taken off in forty-one minutes and all eleven fit, and Line Captain Shepherd's position logged at the failure point at closure, and it made no further entry about her. The post then answered to another Starwarden, whom the record does not name, and Emeka Lindahl was still its Chief Ranger."
  - "The post's watch had called Shepherd the dead Starwarden since the morning her hull cleared, as it calls every Starwarden who goes up, meaning nothing by it. After the notice the watch stopped using the word, without being told to, and by the sixth day it used her name with no rank in front of it, which it had not done before for an officer who had gone up."
  - "Lindahl sent the notice on, in clear and in his own name and rank, to Chief Ranger Galen Kane of the Frontier Corps, whose office answered to nobody on that line, as it had reached the post and with nothing changed. He added two things the post's log held from her time: one pouch under a Frontier Corps frank with the first stores hull of spring 2830, and her open request the same week for a file of Kane's with its dissents. He wrote nothing to her, and the post has not written after her."
  - "Kane learned of Dock Seven from Lindahl's message on the third day, at his region's roster office, and learned from it that she had sent for the file of the four-month passage. He sent an open request for the Field Triumvirate log with dissents, read it on the fifth day from the dissent entry, which records that there was none, and judged the record of the decision complete. He entered the minute of closure in his own book and gave it no number among his closures. He began a sheet with her name and wrote nothing after it, and acknowledged Lindahl in three words: received as sent. The record does not carry whether she read the file."
