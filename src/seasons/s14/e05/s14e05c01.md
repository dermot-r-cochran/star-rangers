::::: scene 1

::: pov teddy

There were more pieces in the warm than there should have been.

This is told the way things are told below the roof, by someone who was there, and the someone was lying in the middle counting, which is where the someone usually is. Carried-It-Sleeping counts the small ones from habit, the way you breathe. The count had been the same for so many tellings that it had stopped being a number and become a shape. And now the shape was one over.

Carried-It-Sleeping counted again, the slow way, piece by piece.

One over.

It was not a mistake. It was in the middle of the middle, where the warm is deepest, with the small ones lying round it in a ring. It was very small. It did nothing at all. It was the newest one, and it had not been there the last time Carried-It-Sleeping slept.

The warm had known for a while that it was coming. Now it was here, and everyone was being quiet about it in the way people are quiet when they are extremely pleased.

The small ones were not quiet for long. They looked at the newest one until they had seen everything it did, which did not take long, and then they went back to the game.

A line of stones across the floor. One on it, very straight. The rest bunched below, all one warm piece. The game had a new part that the small ones had been trying to put into it for some tellings, and it was not going well, and that is another telling. This was the old way. A hand to a stone.

The hand stayed there.

The warm went still for it, the way it does, old ones and all. Carried-It-Sleeping went still with the rest, and did not mind, and knew whose hand it was without looking, as everyone did. For as long as it takes to say a name. Longer.

The stone moved. Once, well.

"The smallest!" said the one on the line, before the sound had finished, the way the one on the line always did.

And then nobody said anything, because every small one in the ring had turned to look at the newest one, in the middle of the middle, which could not have lifted a hand to a stone if you had put the stone in it.

"Wrong," said several of them at once, delighted. "That was not the smallest. The smallest is asleep."

The one on the line came off it, arguing.

Carried-It-Sleeping looked across at Keeps-A-Hand-On-It, who still had a hand on the stone, and who had the look of somebody who has put a foot down where a step has always been.

::::: scene 2

::: pov keeps-a-hand-on-it

It was a strange thing to lose.

Keeps-A-Hand-On-It had a name. A real one, said by the old ones, gone all the way round. Nobody had needed to say *the smallest* for a long time. They said it anyway, in the game, because they always had, and Keeps-A-Hand-On-It had never thought about it. It was like the wall you lie against. You do not think about a wall.

And now it was somebody else's wall, and nobody had even moved a stone first.

Keeps-A-Hand-On-It went and looked at the newest one properly.

It was not much to look at. It was warm. It was all one colour. It breathed. Now and then one of its hands opened and shut on nothing, as if it were practising.

An old one was lying beside it, one of the ones from the edge of the amber, and while Keeps-A-Hand-On-It watched, the old one bent close and said the first thing. The thing every small one is told before the first warm seep, before anything.

*The people are called the Told.*

Then the rest of it. Why. What a name is. All of it, at the pace it goes.

The newest one did not know a word of it. You could see it not knowing. It looked at the amber on the wall the whole time.

The old one did not seem to mind. The old one finished, and made the small sound, and lay back down.

Keeps-A-Hand-On-It stayed there, thinking.

That had been done to Keeps-A-Hand-On-It once. It must have been. And after it the seep, and the long dark, and all of them, one at a time, until they came to the up-people. The humming stone. The two stones. Stone-First on the line. Keeps-A-Hand-On-It had been born after all of that. Every part of it had come as a telling, from somebody who was there, to somebody who was not. Everyone older could remember when the up-people were new. Keeps-A-Hand-On-It could only remember being told.

The newest one would be told all of that as well.

And then more. The game. The name. The telling that went up the shelving in Stone-First's mouth. *They stayed.*

For the newest one, every bit of that would be a told thing. As old as the seep. Somebody else's.

Keeps-A-Hand-On-It sat down, quite suddenly, on the floor of the warm.

*I was there for that.*

::::: scene 3

::: pov teddy

Keeps-A-Hand-On-It came across the warm with a stone moved first, which was not needed between two people lying a few places apart, and Carried-It-Sleeping understood from that it was going to be a proper asking.

"The time the second sound did not come," said Keeps-A-Hand-On-It. "When we all waited. Who tells that?"

"Nobody," said Carried-It-Sleeping. "Everyone was there. A telling is from one who was there to one who was not. Stone-First told us the line, because we were not on the line. The waiting, we were all in. There was nobody to tell it to."

"There is now."

Carried-It-Sleeping looked at the middle of the middle.

Went-Round had said it, a season ago, about another thing that everyone was there for. *You tell it to the ones not born yet.* The warm had taken that, and kept the telling, and waited. It had not occurred to Carried-It-Sleeping that one of them would go and get born.

"There is now," said Carried-It-Sleeping.

"Who tells it? If everyone was there?"

That was a real question. There is a way to settle who tells a thing, and it is who was there, and for once that settled nothing at all. Carried-It-Sleeping thought of going to ask Went-Round. Then thought of how long that would take, and of who was standing here having asked.

"You had a hand on a stone when it began," said Carried-It-Sleeping. "The game stopped under it. You kept it there the whole time, so as not to make noise over the line. Is that right?"

"Yes."

"Then it is as much yours as anyone's. And nobody else has asked."

Keeps-A-Hand-On-It stood very straight, the way the one on the line stands. Then went, at once, to the middle of the middle.

And came back.

"It is asleep."

"Then you wait."

"Can I not tell it asleep? It does not know a word either way."

Carried-It-Sleeping had carried a sleeping thing a long way once, and had thought about sleeping things more than most people.

"The humming stone was there for the first stone," said Carried-It-Sleeping. "On the line. Right at the front. It was asleep. Does anybody say the humming stone was told?"

Keeps-A-Hand-On-It thought about it.

"No."

"No. You wait."

::::: scene 4

::: pov keeps-a-hand-on-it

Keeps-A-Hand-On-It knew about waiting. It was the one thing Keeps-A-Hand-On-It knew more about than anyone in the warm.

This was a different kind.

With a hand on a stone, the waiting is yours. You are the one holding it. Everyone else is still because of you, and you can make it a little longer, and then a little longer than that, and when you have had enough you move the stone and let them all go.

There was no stone here. Keeps-A-Hand-On-It lay down next to the newest one, close, because the people do not leave a sleeping one alone, and there was nothing to put a hand on and nothing to move. The newest one had the whole of the waiting, and did not know it had it, and breathed.

It went on for a long time.

It could not be made longer. It could not be made shorter, either. Keeps-A-Hand-On-It tried wanting it shorter, and that did nothing.

So this was what it was like for everybody else.

Then one of the small hands opened, and did not shut. The newest one was looking at the amber.

Keeps-A-Hand-On-It stood up.

A telling is told standing up. Everybody knows that, and Keeps-A-Hand-On-It had seen it done more times than there are stones in the warm, and had never once done it. It is higher than you would think. The small ones in the ring looked up. Then the old ones nearest. Then it went out to the edge of the amber, the stillness, faster than any telling goes: a small one was standing up in the middle.

Keeps-A-Hand-On-It bent toward the newest one, and began.

"There was a stone. And then. There was. Not a stone."

"Is that how it was?" said Carried-It-Sleeping, from just behind.

"It is the newest. It does not know anything. I am telling it small."

"Were you told the seep small?"

"I do not remember."

"I do. I was lying next to you. It was told whole. All of it, at the pace it goes. You looked at the wall."

Keeps-A-Hand-On-It breathed in, and started again, and told it whole.

The game, going in the middle of the warm. The hand on the stone, and the warm gone still for it. Far up, from the line, the ground saying *someone*, small and late: the up-people's stone. The other sound that always comes on top of it, and how it did not come. The stillness changing what it was for. The hand kept where it was, so as not to make two stones at once. The first warm seep, told through in the head to measure it, to the end. The long dark, most of the way, to the part where the warm is found again. Then the ground, from the line, in Stone-First's way. And what the whole warm said afterwards, all at once.

Somewhere in the middle of the long dark, Keeps-A-Hand-On-It looked to see.

There was nothing to see. The newest one was looking at Keeps-A-Hand-On-It's foot.

It was hard to go on, after that. The words were all still there and they had nowhere to land.

"Stone-First did not look to see," said Carried-It-Sleeping, quietly, behind. "Up on the line. You do not, with a small one. You tell it."

Keeps-A-Hand-On-It looked at the amber wall instead, where the newest one had been looking before, and went on from the place where the warm is found again, and did not look down. It was easier. It was like keeping a hand on a stone, only the stone was the telling, and you did not let go of it until it was done.

"*They stayed*," said Keeps-A-Hand-On-It, at the end, and stopped, because that was the end.

The warm made the small sound. All of it. Old ones and all.

::::: scene 5

::: pov teddy

Keeps-A-Hand-On-It sat down harder than was meant.

"It did not know a word of it."

"No," said Carried-It-Sleeping.

"Shall I tell it again?"

"Not to the same one. You have told it. Now it lies in the warm and hears it go round, the same as you heard everything. One day it will tell it to somebody and get the long dark in the wrong place, and you will have to lie still and not say so."

Keeps-A-Hand-On-It thought that was a long way off, and said so. Carried-It-Sleeping did not answer. It is not as far off as you would think. Carried-It-Sleeping had found that out lying in this exact place.

The telling was already going round. Not the words. The warm knew the words; everyone had been there. What went round was that it had been told. A thing nobody could tell, for want of anybody to tell it to, had somebody now, and somebody had heard it, and so it was the people's, and a small one had told it first.

Carried-It-Sleeping listened, from habit, for a thing said twice and three times. There was not one. Nothing grew. Keeps-A-Hand-On-It had a name already, and had not done anything this time but tell what happened, and that is not the kind of thing a name is made of. It is only the kind of thing a person is made of.

Here is the other thing, and it is kept as Carried-It-Sleeping's.

Keeps-A-Hand-On-It was born after the two stones. For that one, the up-people were always a told thing, true and old and somebody else's. The warm had said so often. It had been the whole of what was different about the smallest.

Now there was one in the middle of the middle for whom the up-people *staying* was a told thing. And Keeps-A-Hand-On-It was on the other side of it. One of the ones who was there.

Nobody had said *the smallest* to Keeps-A-Hand-On-It since the game. Carried-It-Sleeping noticed that Keeps-A-Hand-On-It had stopped listening for it.

The count was still one over. Carried-It-Sleeping counted it once more, slowly, and let it become the shape.

Then the working time came. Far up, from the line, the ground said *someone*, small and late. And on top of it, before it had finished, the other. A good one. At once.

The newest one's hand opened and shut.

It had heard one sound, with two parts, the way all the small ones do. Next to it, Keeps-A-Hand-On-It had heard two. Keeps-A-Hand-On-It had been there on the time there was only one of them, and had told it, and it was a told thing now.
