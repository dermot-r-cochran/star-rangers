  <h1 class="lore-entry__title">Boundary Readings Classified as Etheric Interference</h1>
  <p><span class="character-badge">~2780 UCSD</span></p>
</header>
<div class="lore-entry__body">

About 2780 UCSD, [Survey Corps](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-survey-corps/) analysts begin filing a class of reading under its own heading: **[Etheric](/star-rangers/glossary/etheric/) interference**. The readings are the ones a [boundary zone](/star-rangers/glossary/boundary-zone/) produces and recalibration does not remove, and they are filed beside instrument drift because both show up at the same posts for the same reason, and because nobody has a tighter word for either. The heading names a symptom, in the vocabulary of the Five Layers, and names no mechanism.

The classification is an instrument's finding, and it is late. The [Fellowship of Light](/star-rangers/lore/fellowship-of-light/) had been calling the same lawful thing the [Overfold](/star-rangers/glossary/overfold/) for centuries, by long attention and no chronometer; the record keeps the three names in the order they were coined and ranks none of them. What the Survey Corps adds is the filing: a reading that survives correction is written down as a reading, and from here the boundary posts carry a second column beside the first.

The record dates the practice by its age rather than by a day. It was decades old when the [Boundary Monitoring Protocol](/star-rangers/codex/survey-corps-protocol/) of 2811 UCSD forbade Etheric-layer assessment without a certified analyst on the team, and it took longer still for the Etheric column to be read against the same geometry as the physical one.

**Canon facts:**
- *Etheric interference* is a Survey Corps classification of a symptom at boundary zones: Etheric-layer readings that persist after recalibration, filed beside instrument drift. It names no coupling and no mechanism.
- The classification was in use for decades before the Boundary Monitoring Protocol of 2811 UCSD, which presupposes certified Etheric-layer assessment; the record holds no first analyst, no first post and no first filing for it.
- The Fellowship's *Overfold* is older than the classification, names the same lawful phenomenon, and is not a lesser name for it.
- The year is approximate. The record fixes the order of the three names and the age of the practice, and nothing closer.
