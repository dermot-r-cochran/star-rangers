  <h1 class="lore-entry__title">The First Party on Counterpane's Membrane</h1>
  <p><span class="character-badge">2814 UCSD, Late Autumn</span> <span class="character-badge">The first party's shelter, on the membrane later registered as Counterpane</span></p>
</header>
<div class="lore-entry__body">

In 2814 UCSD the [Survey Corps](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-survey-corps/) puts a first party on one membrane of a neighbouring [archecluster](/star-rangers/glossary/archecluster/), reached by [fold](/star-rangers/lore/ftl-mechanics/) because the manifold is common and a fold does not care whose vocabulary the far end uses. No harmonic corridor opens to the party's drive lattice; its transits run fold-in and fold-out with no harmonic leg. The shelter is placed at the quietest gradient the party can locate, which is not a Lagrange point of anything.

For ninety days the party logs nothing anomalous, because nothing single-system is anomalous there. An atom is an atom; the spectrometer finds the handbook's lines; the calibrated second realises as it does at home. In the late autumn a routine correlation calibration run by Section Lead [Syra](/star-rangers/characters/syra/), a [Krenyi](/star-rangers/lore/krenyi/) boundary analyst, returns a residual in the joint statistics of three independent sources, outside its budget. She applies every correction anyone can predict. The residual survives them. She files it as *survives correction* with the raw three-source statistics beneath it, and declines to countersign anything softer ([Survives Correction (S01E00C05)](/star-rangers/seasons/s01/e00/s01e00c05/)).

Nothing is a finding yet. The value is carried home, as every value read on that bench has been carried since.

**Canon facts:**
- The first party reached the membrane in 2814 UCSD and logged nothing anomalous for its first ninety days; the residual surfaced in a routine correlation calibration in the late autumn.
- The residual is in the composition rule, the joint distribution of three or more independent quantum sources, and in nothing an instrument asked one question at a time can reach.
- Syra filed *survives correction* and the raw statistics, and no softer word; the party logged a discrepancy for a week and then stopped.
- The membrane was not yet registered as Counterpane. The first party reached it, read it and carried the reading home; acceptance, the registry name and the revisits are later entries.
