  <h1 class="lore-entry__title">The Counterpane Reading Accepted at Home</h1>
  <p><span class="character-badge">2815 UCSD, Summer</span> <span class="character-badge">Survey Corps Standards Office, calibration floor, Earth</span></p>
</header>
<div class="lore-entry__body">

The first party's filing crosses to the Science Corps in the spring after the 2814 field season, and Principal [Yusuf Adair](/star-rangers/characters/yusuf-adair/) takes four months to be sure of it. He wants it to be the instruments. The instruments pass their audit on a floor that has never left, and the same three sources, braided on the same bench at home, return the local statistics to budget. So he goes to the record instead, and reports that the record had only ever claimed its arithmetic of combination *within any given Concordant* and *throughout the local cluster*. The residual contradicts nothing the record holds. It is accepted in the summer of 2815 UCSD, and filed as what it is: a **reading at the width of one membrane**.

The Survey had already retired the word *anomaly* for the membrane within a week of the first calibration, when the party stopped logging a discrepancy; the acceptance makes it the record's rule that a discrepancy which survives correction is a reading. The rule that governs every revisit since is written from this floor in the same year: nothing read on another membrane is a finding until it has come home and passed audit, because the one thing a party cannot calibrate against there is the place itself. Adair's own working paper of the same year, [*What the Law Does Not Decide*](/star-rangers/codex/what-the-law-does-not-decide/), is written after the review closed, when he went looking for what else the record had claimed only for here.

The registry names the membrane's archecluster **[Counterpane](/star-rangers/lore/counterpane-archecluster/)**, a quilter's word, a cover stitched to a different pattern on the same bed. The record holds the name as following the acceptance and gives the registration no day of its own.

**Canon facts:**
- The Counterpane finding was accepted at home in 2815 UCSD, after the party's instruments had passed audit and the bench, braided at home, had returned to budget.
- The Science Corps review found that *identical at base* had only ever been stated within the local archecluster; the finding was filed as a reading at the width of one membrane and promoted no further.
- The revisit discipline dates from this acceptance: a value read on the membrane is carried as cargo and becomes a reading only after audit at home.
- The membrane was registered under the archecluster name Counterpane after the acceptance; the record does not date the registration separately.
