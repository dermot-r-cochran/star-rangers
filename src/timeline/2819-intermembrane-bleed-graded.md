  <h1 class="lore-entry__title">Intermembrane Bleed Graded Against the Boundary Types</h1>
  <p><span class="character-badge">~2819 UCSD</span></p>
</header>
<div class="lore-entry__body">

About 2819 UCSD the [Survey Corps](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-survey-corps/) gets the Etheric half of the boundary picture into the same diagram as the physical half. The [Common Manifold](/star-rangers/lore/post-eleven-dimensional-manifold/) had given the analysts the geometry of drift: strain distributed in one band, timing disagreement absorbed in another, which is why two chronometers at a boundary post can each be right and still disagree. The Corps now extends the same structure one band higher and names the coupling it had spent decades filing as [Etheric interference](/star-rangers/glossary/etheric/): **[Intermembrane Bleed](/star-rangers/glossary/intermembrane-bleed/)**, one universe's archetypal templates reaching a neighbour's across a narrowed [Interval](/star-rangers/glossary/interval/), graded against the Boundary Types already in use. At Type II the signature is Membrane Resonance, detectable and non-displacing. At Type III it is Intermembrane Bleed, neighbouring templates competing for local admissibility.

The renaming is not cosmetic. *Etheric interference* described a symptom; the new term names what produces it, and gives an analyst something a chronometer discrepancy can be diagnosed against. It also fixes what kind of boundary a boundary post sits on. A bleed signature is available only between membranes, and there is no Interval inside a universe, so every boundary zone on the Survey Corps' books is the inter-membrane kind: what registers across one is a neighbouring universe, read through a gap nothing crosses. Older filings that speak of *the edge of two Concordants* are read from here as the loose usage of their period.

The grading came too late for one post to use it at once. [Threshold Station](/star-rangers/lore/threshold-station/) had carried its forty seconds as a hardware fault since 2815 UCSD and held no certified analyst to read them otherwise; they were read as a Type II boundary reading in 2826 UCSD.

**Canon facts:**
- Intermembrane Bleed and Membrane Resonance are Survey Corps terms graded against the Boundary Types (Type I undetectable, Type II Membrane Resonance, Type III Intermembrane Bleed); they name an Etheric-channel coupling, where *Etheric interference* named a symptom.
- The grading postdates the Boundary Monitoring Protocol of 2811 UCSD, which speaks only of Etheric-layer assessment, and precedes the Gravimetry Desk's working paper of 2825 UCSD, which already treats the Interval and the far side's mass as settled vocabulary; the year between is the record's, and approximate.
- Under the grading, every boundary zone on the Survey Corps' books is an inter-membrane boundary: a narrowing of the Interval between two universes, never an edge between two Concordant Zones of one.
- The grading conferred no remedy. A membrane-proximity event does not resolve by recalibrating instruments on one side of it, and Etheric-layer certification exists to read Membrane Resonance before it develops into Bleed, not to prevent either.
