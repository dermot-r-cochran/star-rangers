## The season in one line

The up-people do the last of the Told's manners without knowing it is manners, and leave the place; the Told keep the line on the times when nothing happens, which is what keeping it always was.

**A proposed focus line for the season**, marked *proposed* until he approves it, and not in `focus.md`: *one side stops coming to the line and neither says so: above it is an entry with no because in it, below a telling with its edges and no middle, and the line is kept.*

Proposed strand lines, on the same footing. **A, below:** *every time since, and the first working time that was not.* **B, above:** *a presence that cannot state its need at grade, lifted entire, and the one thing it cannot lift.*

## The premise, and why

Through three seasons the survey has learned one of the Told's two manners and has been answered for it on every shift. The second has never been done from above, because nobody on the roof knows it is manners: *leave a place to a stranger until they ask*. The Told withdrew from the upper levels on that rule when the survey came. They have never asked the up-people for anything, to come or to stay.

The survey leaves in its fifth year. It leaves for its own reasons, in its own grammar, on a page that says what is so and not why it matters. From below it cannot be told as a going, because nobody below was there for one: no stone said *someone is leaving*. The loud goes up the long passage at the end of a working time, as it has on every working time, and does not come down again.

So the season is the meeting of those two facts. Above, a party that keeps only what it sees and what it is given finds it cannot write, at a grade it can stand behind, a need to remain. Below, a people who can only tell what somebody was there for find that the up-people have become a telling with a beginning, a long row of stones, and no middle that says what they were.

**Why the survey leaves (proposed; choice 1).** The run of the fifth year brings the boundary side's answer to Ferrant. It takes her third line at its word: the countersign is *"instrument discipline"*, *"not theirs to staff"*, and it is passed to the Survey Corps' own side with Nakagawa's request beside it. The Corps then asks the station what its boundary side asked one officer, in the same words: state continuing need at grade, or return with the run. Nakagawa cannot state one. The conduct has not changed in three years. Everything that has happened since the first stone was either seen, and is filed, or was given to one person at a line, and is not the survey's. And *"a reading its reader wants is entered last or not at all"* (S14E04C01): the survey lead wants to stay. The statement is three lines, as Ferrant's was, and its third is the one that sends everybody home.

The Protocols' window does not close. What ends is the presence. *Lightest presence* was always a scale with nothing at its end.

## What the season must not do

- **Cross the line**, in either direction, for any reason, including to say goodbye.
- **Change the knock.** The last one is a stone, a step back, the thing said, the interval, the crack.
- **Tell the Told.** Nothing is left, marked, said or arranged so that the people below will know. The party has no means, and the season does not find one.
- **Carry a word said at the line.** The junction's channel is off for every knock to the last, and the handheld's sound channel is off.
- **Let the survey know who stood on the line.** The log has *Individual Four* against one profile in the second year and no method for ageing a Pandoid. It still has neither at the end.
- **Give the Told a reason.** No piece of the telling says why the loud stopped, or that the up-people went kindly, or unkindly, or went at all. *"The people know what the up-people did, and do not know what they did not do"* (S11E07C01).
- **Make a rule or a word below the roof.** The thread has made none in three seasons.
- **Say what the shapes were, what the boundary feature is, or what Ferrant's page is called.**
- **End anybody.** Nobody dies, on either side, and nobody's office is handed on. Whether keeping the margin is an office is still open (`mind-design.md`), and stays so.
- **State how fast a small one grows.** The newest is looked at, lain beside, told to, and asleep. It is given no act that needs a body the record has not described.

**Why this order holds.**

- **The bright one comes first** because its form is the finale's form. A reader who has watched a telling with edges and no middle told down to somebody in E01 knows, in E07, what becomes of the one the warm puts together about the up-people, without being shown it.
- **The cause sits early and small** (E02), so that the rest of the season is a year of ordinary knocks under a date. The children's reader is given a leaving that takes a long time and is mostly work.
- **E03 and E04 are one act and two records**, the thread's proven move, placed mid-season so that the finale does not have to carry it.
- **E06 holds the last knock without marking it.** The reader learns in E07C01 that it has already been read. A last time is known only afterwards, on both sides of the line and on the page.

## The finale

**E07C01, above: the entry.** The last shift, told after the reader has heard its knock from below. From the roof it is the same knock: a stone of the upper levels, the handheld pointed low with its sound off, the stone set a hand from the feet of the one who answers, the step back, the thing said, the rock at once, the interval waited to its end on purpose. At the junction the channel comes on and Aravena kneels to the unit: knock answered. The unit hums. Then the unit goes up on Aravena's back, as it has at the end of every shift since it was lifted from the line, and this time the imager and its stand go up after it.

Aravena is last up the passage, as on every shift. At the bottom of the long passage, where the question was asked of the dark for sixteen shifts in the second year, Aravena stops, with the light held low, and says nothing, and waits the interval, and goes. It is not ordered and it is not filed. *(A reading, his: choice 8.)*

At the station Nakagawa writes the entry in the form the log has had since the first stone: the party announced itself at the line; the one who answers was present and answered; nothing said is recorded; the line was not crossed. Under it, the counts that close: the knocks, and the answers, which are the same number; the darks owned and countersigned; stones set, and stones found displaced, none; stones left as set. Ferrant countersigns the last interval, *nothing to grade*, and asks, as she did once before, for her name on the line that says who was there. **The last beat above** is that line: the four names, and where each stood.

There is no line after it that says what any of it was.

**E07C02, below: the line kept.** Four scenes.

1. *The working time.* Stone-First goes up the shelving, moves a stone at the top, and stands at the row's end. The loud does not come down. Below, the warm stops for it piece by piece, old ones and all, as it stopped for the first three stones and for the hand that stayed. It is quiet for longer than the first warm seep told through. This is **the silent hour**, met on the side nobody wrote a failure mode for. The one of the Told who is not listening is the newest, asleep in the middle of the middle, and what the small ones say, and then the old ones, because it is the one true thing anybody below the roof can say that working time, is that the smallest is asleep.
2. *The working times after.* The warm argues it, in the grammar it has had since the second year. Is the up-people's stone late? Went-Round: a stranger who leaves moves a stone, and the ground says so, and nothing has said so. *Every time since* has no word in it for stopping. Stone-First: the people know what the up-people did. Nothing is settled, because nobody was there for a going, and nobody can be.
3. *The telling.* It is put together the way the light out of the wall was, a piece at a time, from the ones who were there for each: the loud that came on instead of settling; the humming stone carried up asleep; the first stone, late, and theirs; the row; the hand kept on the rock and the up-person who stayed; the working time two of the people stood on the line; the stone at the row's end; the loud going up. It has its edges. It has no middle: nobody below the roof has a piece that says what the up-people were. A small one asks for a piece that says they will come back, and is asked whose piece that is. It is kept, with nothing taken out and nothing put in, for the ones not born yet. Keeps-A-Hand-On-It, who once claimed that work on the ground of being *the one who will be here longest*, looks at the newest and says who will be. By then what the warm says of the newest has gone round, in old ones' voices. **A name has grown** *(the name itself is his: choice 4)*.
4. *The line.* **The last beat below.** At the next working time Stone-First-Who-Waited goes up the shelving and moves a stone at the top, and stands at the end of the row with the whole body still, on a time when nothing happens.

**Why it is a recognition and not a stop.** The ending test asks that the end change how the beginning reads. It does, twice.

- Season 11's third chapter defined the work: Stone-First keeps the margin *"on all the times when nothing happens, so as to be there on the time when something does."* For four years that sentence has read as a description of waiting for the up-people. After the finale it reads as what it says. The keeping was never for them.
- Season 11's first page gave the manners for a stranger in your place: *leave it to them until they ask*. The first treatment said the story would be over *"the moment one of them does the other's manners"*. The up-people do it, late and without knowing, and theirs, as they did the first. No page says so. Aravena's unfiled interval at the bottom of the passage is the only place the two touch.

## Choices for him

Each has a recommendation and its basis. None is settled here.

1. **Why the survey leaves.**
   - (a) **Ferrant's answer from grade, turned on the station.** The boundary side takes her third line at its word and passes the matter to the Survey Corps' own side, which asks the station the same question; Nakagawa cannot state a continuing need at a grade the survey lead can stand behind, and says so in three lines. **Recommended.**
   - (b) Ferrant alone is recalled. With no certified officer on the planet the ordered dark is unowned, Nakagawa will not hear the line and will not knock unowned, the party works only after a knock, and so the station closes.
   - (c) The run's end, with no cause on the page: a date on an order, described and never explained, on either side.
   - (d) The stones. Egede's third number comes due and the junction has no more fit to carry. Not recommended: the second passage has stones, and it would make the leaving an accident.
   - *Basis:* S14E06C01 put the words *"state continuing need at grade or return with the run"* on the page and had Ferrant write the line *"that could send her home"*. Nakagawa's rule that the survey keeps *"what it sees and what it is given"* leaves a survey lead nothing to state, and *"a reading its reader wants is entered last or not at all"* is Nakagawa's own. Under (a) the leaving is the party's own honesty and nobody's verdict on it. Under (b) it is a consequence of staffing, and reads as one. Option (c) is the cleanest for the children's rung and gives the survey side less to do.
   - *One fact (a) proposes about the Corps:* that it reviews a station's attendance as its boundary side reviews an officer's. He may prefer the question to be Nakagawa's own, asked of the party with no page from outside.

2. **What ends.**
   - (a) The presence ends and the window stands. The record does not say whether a party ever returns. **Recommended.**
   - (b) The withdrawal is said to be final.
   - (c) A later party is named or dated.
   - *Basis:* the Protocols' window is *"multi-generational"*, so a window that closed in five years would contradict the page. The Fliade entry has the party able to *"leave whenever it chose and return with whatever it liked"*. Under the 20 October ruling the story has no last page, and (b) would write one.

3. **The last knock.**
   - (a) Unchanged in every part. The Told are not told. **Recommended.**
   - (b) Aravena keeps a hand on the stone, in the Told's own new grammar, and says something for as long as it takes, with every channel off; nothing of it is in any record.
   - (c) The last stone is Egede's, from the plain.
   - *Basis:* *"a knock is not changed to find out"* (S11E04C01), and the one piece's *"explains nothing"*. Option (b) is the strongest scene on offer and the one I would most like him to weigh: it would be the up-people doing the Told's third-season grammar back to them. It would also make the going a thing somebody below was there for, and so a telling with a middle, and the finale is built on its having none. Option (c) was refused twice on the page.

4. **A name for the newest.**
   - (a) A name grows on the page, in the finale's lower chapter, out of what the small ones have already called in the game: *"The smallest is asleep."* The name is put as choices in the chapter that does it, as Keeps-A-Hand-On-It's was. **Recommended.**
   - (b) No name grows. The season ends with *the smallest* and *"the record does not say that none will"*.
   - (c) A name grows and the record does not hold it, as it does not hold the old one's.
   - *Candidates under (a), for the chapter:* **Slept-Through-It** (recommended); *Was-Asleep-For-It*; *Will-Be-Here-Longest*, from Keeps-A-Hand-On-It's saying.
