# Focus lines

Unpublished authorial note. One line for each edition, thread, season and
strand: what that unit is about, stated so that a new chapter, page or choice
can be judged against it. Dermot asked for it on 9 November 2026, verbatim:
*"A focus or core theme for each edition, thread, strand or season"*. He approved
the lines below the same day (*"Approve and build"*), after a Fable read of the
first draft against the chapters (`intake-2026-11-09.md`).

**This file is the single place these lines live.** Other notes point here and do
not restate them: the edition profiles (`edition-reader-profiles.md`) and strand
rule 3 in `story-bible-summary.md`, whose shared throughline is the season's line
below. The treatments keep their own *in one line* sections, written before this
file and used as sources by the Fable read; where a treatment's line and the line
here differ, the approved line is the one here.
The `showrunner` skill reads this file for the unit it is asked about.

**Why "focus line" and not "theme".** In this repository *theme* already names the
colour palette (`THEME`, `src/css/theme-*.css`), and *core theme* names the music
(`music-prompts.md`).

**What a line is, and is not.**
- A line is a theme to judge by, not a summary of the plot. It asserts no fact
  about the world. The chapters and the lore do that.
- An edition's line is framing only: who reads, and what the door is for. It
  follows the edition's reader profile and never states a world fact.
- A season's line sits inside its thread's line. A strand's line is that strand's
  angle on its season's line.
- The file stays unpublished. A line may lean on canon that a page should not
  pre-tell. No line quotes *the shape of the saga*, whose last four beats wait
  for the terminus to be published.
- **Lines change only at Dermot's word.** A proposed line is marked *proposed*
  until he approves it. A new unit gets its line proposed when it is registered,
  or when its first chapter is drafted.

Status for every line below: **approved 9 November 2026**.

**Notes on the seasons.**
- **s02.** The line covers the season's Undercover Pets chapters. Episodes 1 and 2
  (Larsen and Calloway; the Safety Corps officer) are Orbital Five-O and Safety
  Corps chapters that sit inside the season the registry gives to
  `undercover-pets`. They answer to the `orbital-five-o` thread line.
- **s06 and s07** have separate season lines and share one pair of strands, below.
  A new chapter in either season is judged against its season's line and its
  strand's line together.
- **s14.** The line opens with the Told's third rule, word for word (S11E01C01's
  prose, and S14E01C01's `canon_facts`; S11E01C01's `canon_facts` paraphrase it).
  That is allowed here, because this file is unpublished. If a
  season line ever fronts a page, it is rewritten so the record does not quote
  itself as a theme.
- **Seasons without strands.** s00, s02, s03, s04, s05, s08 and s09 have no
  recorded strands. Seasons 3 and 5 pair one dominant point of view with one
  contrasting local one, which is the bible's recorded exception and not a
  strand pair.
