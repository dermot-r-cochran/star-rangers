## The direction — a character called Bumble

No attachment, no species, no slot, no thread named.

### What it says, in one line

One character, whose name is Bumble. Everything else is a reading.

### Readings taken, flagged for confirmation

- **Bumble is a bumblebee.** The name is the plain-meaning reading in an
  English children's register, and the corpus's two other readings were
  weighed and set aside below (a dog; a small robot). It composes with three
  things already on the record: Drithane's *agriculture runs under glass*,
  which is where bumblebee colonies work in the real world; the planet's
  *cold, dry, and high* air, which is where bumblebees out-fly every other
  bee; and the portfolio's own two bumblebee frames, which under the
  3 September ruling (*anything a camera can photograph comes from Dermot's
  own frames*) make her the first character in the record whose portrait is
  a photograph of his rather than a generated image or a card. **Confirm the
  species.**
- **She lives in Gleann na gCaorach, on Drithane, in the wool-shed.** The
  valley is the pets thread's second home (S02E03–E04), Sorcha Nic Dhiarmada
  reads animals for a living and the chapters already give her a wool basket
  by the stove; a shed for the wool is the smallest possible addition. Eden
  was considered and set aside: nothing in canon has Eden growing anything,
  so a bee there would first need a grow deck, a larger assertion than the
  bee. **Confirm the placement.**
- **The name is the valley's, rendered.** *Bumbóg* is the Irish for a
  bumblebee; the page says the record renders it *Bumble* the way it renders
  everything else said in an Irish-speaking valley — the translation default
  the 7 August pronoun rule names, applied to a name. The Zhulik and Teddy
  precedent: a name is a fact about the namers, and the page says so on its
  first line. **Confirm that Bumble is the rendering and Bumbóg the valley's
  word, or rule that the valley says *Bumble* itself.**
- **"A character" read as one name for one nest's queen, whichever queen it
  is.** A bumblebee queen founds one nest and lives one season; the page
  makes that its subject rather than hiding it — *there has been one Bumble
  for as long as there has been a wool-shed, and there have been a great
  many* — on the Zhulik pattern (*on a habitat with one cat, every cat is
  that cat*). This is the reading the draft turns on and the one most likely
  to be wrong about what was meant. **Confirm, or choose one queen; see the
  second question below.**
- **Status *Active*.** Under the 11 September vocabulary, *alive and present
  in the story's present*. True of the line at every moment and of no
  individual queen for long; the page's reading makes it honest.
- **The tone rule.** A queen's season ending is on the page in one clause,
  said plainly and gently, in the register the pets thread's chapters use for
  a young reader. Nothing dies on the page. **Confirm that the lifecycle may
  be stated.**

### Canon check

Nothing in the corpus is contradicted; the page extends four settled things.

- **Drithane** (`src/lore/planets/drithane.md`): *cold, dry, and high — a
  thin but breathable-with-acclimatisation atmosphere*; *agriculture runs
  under glass*; exports *wool, preserved goods, precision cold-climate
  botanicals*; *Irish remains the world's working language*. The image alt
  already shows glasshouses. A bumblebee is the one bee that pollinates
  under those conditions, and the page's physiology is the real thing:
  bumblebees warm the thorax by working the flight muscles before flight and
  forage near freezing; bumblebees have flown in a chamber at air pressures
  equivalent to above the summit of Everest; honeybee colonies cluster below
  about ten degrees; red clover needs a long-tongued bee, which is why
  bumblebees were shipped from England to New Zealand in 1885 CE
  (2085 UCSD, on the *dates are absolute* rule); glasshouse bumblebee
  colonies are boxed commercial stock replaced on a schedule; bumblebees
  leave scent footprints on visited flowers that later bees read and avoid,
  fading at roughly the flower's refill rate; bumblebees sense floral
  electric fields through body hairs; carder-group bumblebees comb fibre
  over a surface nest. No new physics; nothing above the Material layer.
- **The valley** (S02E03C01–C03, S02E04C01): Sorcha's holding, her
  collies, the wool basket by the stove, the Survey delegation's crossing
  visit, the instrumented spring-2827 crossing with Thrum, and the survey
  record's open question whether the valley's animals quiet on crossing
  nights because they feel the drithle. The page adds a wool-shed, a nest,
  and one ground sensor on the shed floor during the instrumented crossing —
  a trace kept and marked as saying nothing, which leaves the open question
  exactly as open as S02E04C01 left it.
- **The crossing-interval trap** (`open-questions.md`, Drithane's orbit):
  the page commits to no interval, counts no crossings, and speaks of
  *seasons* only in the sense a nest has one. Bumblebee lifecycle is stated
  as *one nest in her life*, never as a length of time.
- **Bees and dogs already in canon.** Brother Fintan's hives and the bee POV
  block of S08E01C01 are the church-space overlay, tier-gated; the page does
  not link to them, so the children's edition sees no placeholder. Dogs
  already did the Muffin reading (S02E03C02's canon fact on the collies), one
  reason the dog shape below is not recommended: a dog on Eden would be
  measured against Muffin at once, and the result is already filed.
- **Smart Pet** (`src/glossary/smart-pet.md`): a certified harness is a
  collar; assessment measures what a candidate's own cognition does with an
  asymmetry. The page says a bee could never be a candidate, which is a
  consequence, not a new rule.
- **Muffin.** One sentence, marked as an absence: the valley's file does not
  say whether the visitor came near the shed. Nothing on the page advances
  or closes the Muffin question.

### What stays open — three questions, put as choices

**1. What Bumble is.**

- **(a) A bumblebee in the Drithane wool-shed, as drafted** — *recommended*:
  composes with the planet's stated conditions and economy, takes the first
  portrait from Dermot's own frames, keeps clear of the Muffin engine, and
  gives the children's thread a small creature that is neither cat nor
  rabbit. Costs nothing settled.
- **(b) A dog on Eden.** The obvious gap in a children's pets cast, and the
  name fits a clumsy friendly dog as well as a bee. Two sub-shapes: a Smart
  Pet dog at the agency, which inverts the agency's cover (a dog is the one
  animal nobody can avoid talking to); or a resident's working dog on the
  small-livestock register, exact paperwork like Knopka's. Either would be
  read against Muffin at once, and the collies' reading is already canon.
- **(c) The bureau floor's name for the housekeeping unit.** The night-pass
  robot Barsik rode in S02E09C01, named by the floor the way Eden's staff
  named Muffin. Not a pet, not an animal, and a machine the record has so far
  kept nameless on purpose; a good joke and a poor character page.

**2. One queen, or the line.**

- **(a) The line, as drafted** — one name, one corner, many queens, the page's
  own subject. *Recommended*, because it is the only reading under which
  *Active* stays true and the page needs no date.
- **(b) One queen** — the spring-2827 foundress, met by the Survey. A cleaner
  individual and a page that goes stale in a season; would need the
  lifecycle either hidden or made the page's ending.

**3. The portrait.**

- **(a) Dermot's own frame, as filed** — *Bumblebee on Giant Scabious*
  (Farmleigh, Dublin, 2024) cropped from 3:2 to the 16:9 slot at 1200×675,
  nothing else done to it. *Recommended*, and the first character portrait
  on the site that is a photograph of his. The alt text describes the frame
  (a scabious against an overcast sky), not the shed; the page does not claim
  the picture was taken on Drithane.
- **(b) The other frame** — *Bumblebee on Golden Rose* (St Anne's Park,
  July 2026): warmer, the bee smaller and softer, the flower a garden rose.
- **(c) A generated frame under glass**, with his bumblebee as reference. Not
  permitted by the 3 September ruling as it stands, since a bee is a thing a
  camera can photograph; listed so the refusal is visible.
