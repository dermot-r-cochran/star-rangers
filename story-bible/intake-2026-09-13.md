## The five decisions

then, on the shapes:

The five, each with the shape taken:

1. **The timeline entry titled *Timeline Event*** — (a) retitle it from its
   own summary. Taken: *Causeway Convergence: Elvira, Galahad, and Aldera*,
   the title the page's own body already carried. Mechanical.
2. **Ten Season 1 chapters on the children's site** — (c) take the pets tag
   off the ten chapters, so the domain is Season 2 alone, on the tier being
   about register: those chapters were written at the general register with
   Court-Fae and a failed ritual, while Season 2 was written with the
   children's shortest blocks. Cost named and accepted: the domain goes from
   23 chapters to 13. Aldera's and Nessa's pages stay by their own tags.
   **One consequence realized with it:** a "View from" page on the tag route
   now also requires its chapter to be on the build (`isCharacterPovIncluded`
   in `lib/content-filter.js`, with a unit test), because Aldera's page
   stays on the domain by tag and her Season 1 viewpoints would otherwise
   have rendered there under chapters that had become placeholders.
   `CHARACTERS` stays an outright grant. `CLAUDE.md`'s scene-POV bullet
   carries the amendment.
3. **The Seasons index in the general register** — (a) an edition may carry
   its own plain-register intro. Realized as `seasonsIntro` in
   `lib/editions.js` (validated like the Reading Plan), rendered by
   `src/seasons/index.md`; the pets edition carries two plain paragraphs.
4. **The Threads page and the *More stories* line** — (a) reword the pets
   Reading Plan's *More stories* to say the other stories are on the
   grown-ups' site. Realized without a hyperlink, on the 2 September ruling
   that the children's placeholder carries no link to the adult site because
   a child cannot judge it; the site is named in words. With Season 1 gone
   the section no longer renders on the domain at all, there being no other
   chapters to list, so the wording waits for a day it does. (b), hiding the
   Threads item, was not taken.
5. **Lore, Atlas and Story Engine in the header while empty** — (a) hide a
   header item when its section is empty on this edition, the homepage
   cards' rule. Realized in `src/_includes/base.njk` for Timeline, Lore,
   Atlas, Glossary and Codex; Story Engine stays, since its page carries its
   own prose and is not empty. The children's header is nine items.
