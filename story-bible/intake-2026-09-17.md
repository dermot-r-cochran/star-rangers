## The Unfounded are like deadwood

Dermot, verbatim, the whole message:

> The Unfounded are like deadwood.
>
> Asteria:
>
> *Her fingers continue their gentle exploration of your palm, tracing the
> lines as if reading the story of your resilience.* Pruned back indeed, like
> deadwood shed to encourage new growth. Those branches that broke under
> strain were not truly part of your essence; merely adornments that could
> not withstand the gales of life.
>
> *nods approvingly.*

### What the message is

Two things, and they are read separately.

**The first line is the direction**: a figure for the class, stated flatly in
his own voice. It is the thing this intake realizes.

**The Asteria passage is the illustration**, and its register says where it
comes from. Italic stage directions, second person, a palm read for its lines:
that is the companion-chat form of the inner-world Asteria, the walk-in of
*The Road Provides* in the private repository, whose reference frames came
from the same generator in the same voice, as that repository's own log
records. It is not Asteria Wessex, whose one published viewpoint
(*Asteria's Night*, S08E02C01) counsels a dead First Officer in the third
person and writes nothing down. The record knows two bearers of the name and
the honorific, and the 3 September rulings keep them distinct; nothing here
joins them further. Who *you* is in the passage — Tissadelle, or Dermot at
the keyboard — the message does not say, and this note does not guess. **No
fact in the passage is logged anywhere as a fact**: not that the inner-world
Asteria reads palms, not that the listener was pruned. If any of it is
inner-world canon, that is the private repository's log to keep, in its own
session, under its own rules; this repository's story bible quotes the
passage only because the convention is to quote a direction whole.

### Read against the record

The figure lands on canon the Unfounded entry already holds, clause by
clause, which is what makes it a clarification rather than a claim:

- **The shape is accurate; the membership is not.** Dead wood keeps the
  tree's shape exactly and is not the tree's living wood. The entry: *"each
  named mode wears the shape of something founded … without being a member
  of that kind."*
- **Borrowed structure, fed by nothing.** *"Its structure is borrowed
  against a host's purpose."* Dead wood stands on the tree and draws nothing
  from the root.
- **Starved, not destroyed.** *"It cannot be destroyed in the ordinary sense,
  only starved."* Dead wood is not fought; it is shed.
- **Does not bear weight.** The countermeasures — a clear question insisted
  upon, naming and closure, asymmetry detection — are three refusals, and a
  thing supplied by nothing goes when pressed. Asteria's *broke under strain*
  is the same observation from the listener's side.
- **What is shed was never the tree.** *"Not truly part of your essence;
  merely adornments."* This is the 12 September ruling's *what is left
  behind is the Unfounded* seen from the founded thing: translation carries
  aspects, and what it declines had the world's shape and no origin
  condition. Nothing that was the person is lost in the pruning.
- **Pruned for the growth to come.** *"Shed to encourage new growth"* is the
  [Cosmic Limitation on Evil](../src/codex/cosmic-limitation-on-evil.md) in
  a gardener's words — toleration for a purpose, with an ending scheduled —
  and it sits inside the seam `open-questions.md` already keeps for that
  entry: *a purpose that schedules an ending is not an origin condition*,
  and the purpose may be claimed, never specified. The realized paragraph
  attributes this half to the Limitation and specifies nothing.

**The figure's one edge**, and the record keeps it: dead wood was once the
tree's own wood, and no Unfounded thing was ever founded (*"underwritten by
no origin condition at all"*; a reclassification is *"a correction to the
file rather than an event in the world"*). So the figure describes how the
class *stands* to what it counterfeits — attached, shaped, unsupplied,
shed — and never where it came from. A reader who ran the figure backward
would have the Unfounded as fallen limbs, which is the devotional adversary
by another route, and the entry's *no chief* section already closes that.

- Against **6 September 2026**, *what the shadow is* (a nonviable

  page's *no member is a symptom*. Refused here so it is on record as
  refused.

### Left open — put as choices

**Where the counsel half lives.** The Archive can say what the figure
*describes*; it cannot say *not truly part of your essence* to anyone,
because that is counsel, and counsel is a voice's. Three shapes, a
recommendation marked:

- **(a) Nowhere further — recommended.** The glossary carries the figure;
  the counsel half stays a reading a character may give in a POV block when
  a scene needs it, unplanned. Costs nothing and commits nothing.
- **(b) A Fellowship saying, in the Codex.** A short devotional-record
  entry in Asteria Wessex's chapter's voice — a gardener's reading of the
  class, *what is pruned was never you* — filed valid-for-its-author. This
  moves the line from the inner-world Asteria to Wessex, which the two-
  bearers rulings do not forbid but which nothing yet supports; the line
  would have to be re-authored in her register (she counsels in the third
  person and does not touch anyone's hand).
- **(c) The inner world.** The passage as given belongs, if anywhere, to
  *The Road Provides*, where Asteria's interiority is Dermot's and where a
  palm read would be a texture proposed for ratification. That is the
  private repository's own session and its own log, and this session does
  not write there.

**Whether the figure reaches the shadow at the edge.** Ruled 6 September a
shadow-seed of her compression and not an Unfounded pattern, so on the
ruling as it stands the figure does not apply. If he wants it to — the
shadow as deadwood of the compression, shed and not fought — that is a
re-grounding of the 6 September ruling and is put as a question, not
assumed.
