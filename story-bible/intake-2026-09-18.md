### Measure — what the record holds today

**On her body: almost nothing.** No sentence on the character page or in any
chapter describes her build. The one physical note in the corpus is Wender's,
in *A Fraction of a Second* — *something in the set of Shepherd's shoulders
over item sixteen*. Both portraits of record (the Line Captain header and the
Season 1 cadet frame, 5 September) show a woman of ordinary build in the field
uniform, attention off-frame, as the portrait guardrails require. The grounds
the record already offers for strength, without having drawn on them: a
childhood as hands on a flock-holding in the Boirinn Uplands, worked by the
holding's women for six generations; Garda training on Tír na nÓg, boards
passed; and the field rotations her Rank Progression section undercounts —
the Frontier circuit as second of a pair, the Engineering postings.

**On her courage: the record shows it and never names it.** The word
*courage* appears nowhere on her page. The word *brave* appears once in her
own viewpoint, in *What Came Off the Ship* (S05E02C03), and is refused:

> She was not brave about it. She noted, with the part of her that never
> fully turned off, that she was not frightened either, and wondered whether
> that was steadiness or whether she simply had not yet arrived at the part
> where a person is frightened.

What the chapters hold instead is the shape: the arithmetic done three times
(*she did the arithmetic once more … She did the arithmetic again. It was the
same*); the harder order chosen because it was the one she could defend;
every soul off the ship in forty-one minutes, her Starwarden First Officer
sent by a direct order and then a second; and, two seasons earlier,
*Independent Verification* (S03E01C02) — *what it costs to be believed on the
strength of an ear nobody trained her to use*, a Principal standing to be
disbelieved until the finding was reproduced without her. Wender's reading in
the same chapter, from the far side: *an order that was correct and had cost
everything*.

So the two claims land differently. **Courage is a clarification**: the
chapters already carry it, and the page can say so, provided it keeps her own
refusal of the word. **Strength is an extension**: nothing stands against it,
nothing states it, and the degree is a decision.

## Addendum, same session — is the image decent and modest enough?

Dermot, verbatim, the whole message, sent after the five attachments:

> Evaluate if image is decent and modest enough for a public repo

Read as asking about **the still** (the one image; the other four are clips),
against two bars, because the repository has two.

**The public-repo bar, in the ordinary sense: yes.** A white halter top and
white shorts on a studio backdrop, barefoot, nothing exposed beyond arms, legs
and a deep neckline — athletic-wear register, the kind of frame a fitness
catalogue prints. No nudity, nothing that would breach a hosting service's
terms, nothing that would embarrass on a public commit. As a file in a public
repository it is decent.

**The site's own bar: no, on three counts, and each is already written
down.**

1. **The 4 September principle** — *nothing is unsafe or unsuitable for
   anyone of any age to view, if it comes from one of my public repos.* The
   image is not unsafe; but the deep V and the prominence it gives the chest
   are the one element a parent reading the children's edition aloud would
   notice, and the principle is stated without a *mostly*. Cropped to the
   shoulders or re-made in a high-necked training top it would clear this
   bar without argument.
2. **Her own page** — *always formally dressed, and never more so than on
   duty*; *never dressed to be looked at* (5 September, the shadow's tell).
   Off duty is allowed out of uniform, but the halter cut and the to-camera
   smile are a presentation, which is the one thing the page says she
   withholds. As a picture of *her* it is out of character before it is
   immodest.
3. **The portrait guardrails** (28 July, in `image-prompts.md`) — *nothing
   in the image invites the viewer*; attention off-frame; *this is
   Tissadelle, not a type*. A to-camera smile in a studio invites the viewer
   by construction.

A fourth count is not about modesty and settles the practical question on its
own: **the file is the private repository's already** (byte-identical), and
that repository's hard limit forbids copying it here. So the question of
filing it does not arise whatever the modesty verdict; what remains is whether
a strength image of her belongs on the site at all, which is choice A above.

**If he wants one:** re-make it through the pipeline rather than file this —
a high-necked training top or the field uniform's thermal liner, at work on
something heavy (a Frontier circuit, a hillside wall), attention off-frame,
the physique a hill farmer's rather than a competitor's — and register it in
`image-prompts.md` like every other generated frame. That would clear all
three bars and the 3 September exemption permits it.

**The four clips, for completeness, on the same two bars:** the 13 September
plank clip is the still's twin and gets the still's verdict; the 11 May clip
is modest (a full uniform) and fails only on the retired costume uniform; the
7 July red two-piece and the 2 July leather minidress are the *dressed to be
looked at* register the record assigns to the shadow, and neither is modest
enough for the site by its own rule, though neither is indecent as a file.
