## Irish titles for the ranks, and alien equivalents for each — a direction, given as a session's task

Verbatim:

> gaelic titles for star ranger ranks and alien language title equivalents for each rank

### Canon checked before drafting

- **Irish is the Celtic Union's charter language** (`celtic-union-of-planets.md`)
  and the working language of Tír na nÓg's administration (`planets/tir-na-nog.md`).
  The Union keeps six languages. *Gaelic* is read as Irish, and the other five
  are marked as unfiled rather than invented.
- **The rendering is made the Union's and not the Corps'.** No page names a
  Corps working language, and `language-design.md` recommends keeping it that
  way. The draft does not make Irish, or English, an official Corps language.
  The filed styles on `star-rangers-command-hierarchy.md` govern, and the Irish
  is the host's courtesy.
- **No conlang** (`language-design.md`, *Traps*). Every alien equivalent is
  rendered in translation and none in a people's own sounds. Only peoples whose
  channel or grammar the record already describes get a rendering: Krenyi,
  Ilveth, Ovruhn, Chthonari, Cerebraun and Ollune. Each rendering follows from
  what that people's page already establishes. The Veyr, Serephine, Prismeri,
  Mnemari and the Told are marked as absences.
- **No one language per people.** Each rendering is described as one language's
  reading, taken from that people's Rangers or translators.
- **Fian-ilchruinne stays out of the story** (`the-title-and-its-risk.md`).
  The Irish renderings avoid *fian* and *fianóglach* for that reason, even
  though *fian* is the obvious word for a sworn band.
- **No branch is military** (2026-09-10). The Provost duty title is *Ardphropast*.
  *Propast* is the academic head's word, and none of the ceremonial military's
  words are used.

### What was drafted

`src/lore/the-ladder-in-other-tongues.md`: the Irish table (all seventeen rungs
and bodies, plus the Chief duty titles), six tables in translation, the
absences, and a closing section, *Why the Tab Does Not Translate*. That section
extends `rank-insignia-and-uniform.md`'s existing line that a rank is read the
same way on every body. It is registered in the canonical guide as *Maor Réalta
and the Irish rank styles (proposed)*, with See Also links from both rank pages.
All of it is **extension**: no published line changes.

What it commits the record to, beyond the words themselves:

- Irish *maor* covers ranger and warden alike, so the Irish carries the founding
  entry's *a ranger is a warden* argument on every tab.
- The Irish never had the pre-2833 *Starwarden First Officer* confusion, because
  it names the two warden ranks for station and ship.
- The Krenyi have no form for a former rank.
- The Ovruhn rendering changes verb at Chief: *checked against* below it,
  *answered through* above it. This hears the guild boundary without knowing it.
- The Cerebraun had to coin a *voice for what* against a *voice for how* to
  render the ladder, and whether any Cerebraun has served is left unsaid.
- The Ollune measure seniority by what an officer holds up, never by weight, and
  render a fixed post as *a standing appointment*.

### Choices put (open — see `open-questions.md`)

1. **The captaincies in Irish.** (a) *Captaen Líne / Captaen Réalta /
   Ardchaptaen*, keeping the naval inheritance the English keeps —
   **recommended, and drafted**. (b) *Ceannaire* (leader), steering clear of
   any word the Union's ceremonial military might use.
2. **Chief in Irish.** (a) *Ardoifigeach*, high officer — **recommended, and
   drafted**. (b) *Saoi*, a master of a craft, the ceiling of the technical
   track. (c) Render the whole certification half after the old grades of the
   learned classes, with *ollamh* at the top. This one is the richest, and it
   also asserts the most.
3. **The other Union languages.** (a) Leave the Scottish Gaelic, Welsh, Breton,
   Cornish and Manx renderings unfiled — **recommended, and drafted**. (b) Add a
   Scottish Gaelic column.
4. **The two species whose Rangers serve without a rendering (Veyr and
   Serephine).** (a) Leave them marked as absences — **recommended, and
   drafted**. (b) Give each one a channel first, which is a canon decision in
   `species-design.md` / `language-design.md`, and then a rendering.
