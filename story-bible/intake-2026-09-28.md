## Tissadelle's intake through the Music Corps, and Slipwave as part of the role — a direction

Verbatim:

> Tissadelle joined the Star Rangers music corps as a novice and cadet,
> specializing a study of how alien cultures and physiology understand music
> or its equivalent. As part of her role she was in the Slipwave band.

Earlier the same day, in the private repo's context: *"Tissadelle's real life
band was called Slipwave."*

### One line

Her way into the Corps was a corps the record had not yet named, with a study
the record had already been circling, and the band the record already held
becomes part of that intake rather than something before it.

### Measured: what the record holds

- **Slipwave is already Archive canon** (`src/glossary/slipwave.md`,
  `src/characters/tissadelle-shepherd.md` under *Before the Charter*): a
  three-person spacer-folk band on Eden Space Habitat's civilian arts circuit,
  "a little under two years", Shepherd on vocals, Ashgrove on percussion,
  Calloway on strings; the anthem written and sung "roughly a year before her
  cadet intake"; the band "quietly stopped playing together" before she "left
  for cadet training"; the other two enlisted "not long after" and carry the
  Corps' **musician specialization** as Officers.
- **The musician specialization exists** (`star-rangers-command-hierarchy.md`,
  the sideways-not-up passage) but belongs to no named corps.
- **No Music Corps exists**, and the record leaves room for one: the Academy is
  "a college for each corps, and a school for each branch", its list runs
  "Survey, Science, Safety, Provost, Frontier, Liaison, Medical, Engineering,
  and the rest", and "the list of branches is open."
- **Novice is a rank**: "provisional intake, under evaluation before formal
  Cadet standing is confirmed" (the ladder). The Academy says "the college
  confirms Cadet standing, not the command chain." Her Season 1 opens at Cadet
  on the general operations track; tracks are keyed to service, never to corps.
- **The study has instances in the record already**: the Chthonari, for whom
  "music and mathematics are nearly the same thing"; the Ovruhn Under-Song and
  law "chanted"; the Krenyi Quiet-Built who "take little interest in … music";
  the Undersong Belt worked from "something closer to a musical score"; and the
  Sentinel signal, which "sounded, unmistakably, like music", read by an
  officer whom "Slipwave taught … to read the space between the notes."
- **The motif**: the one who is called in "arrives with an instrument, a page
  and a question, not a weapon" (intake 2026-09-08; CLAUDE.md).
- **No branch is military** (Dermot, 2026-09-10); the trope default is reject.

### Readings, flagged for his eye

1. **What Slipwave was, under the new intake.** Two readings satisfy his
   words. **(a)** Slipwave stays what the glossary says, a civilian dock-circuit
   band, and was her *placement*: the Music Corps takes its novices on
   provisionally and puts them with working ensembles, civilian ones included,
   for the year the study needs, so "as part of her role she was in the
   Slipwave band" is true and every existing sentence about the band stays
   true. **(b)** Slipwave was a Corps ensemble, which would make the glossary's
   "civilian arts circuit", "never played anywhere Shepherd would later call a
   stage" and "the Corps still commissions nothing" need rewording.
   **Drafted under (a), recommended**: additive, and it explains a thing the
   record left unexplained, why two members of a dock band both carry the
   musician specialization.
2. **A corps, or a branch of one.** His words say *music corps*. Drafted as a
   corps of its own, with a college at the Academy, on the record's own open
   list. The alternative, a school inside Survey beside the Xenolinguists, is
   smaller but would make "joined the music corps" wrong.
3. **The study's name.** Drafted without coining one: "how the peoples the
   Rangers meet understand music, or whatever stands in its place, in their
   cultures and in their bodies", in his order (cultures, physiology). A
   one-word name is his to give if he wants one; nothing in the record needs it.

- `src/glossary/slipwave.md`: the band's standing under the intake, one

- `src/lore/star-rangers-command-hierarchy.md` and `star-rangers-academy.md`:

### What it commits the story to

- A Music Corps exists, a college among the colleges, no more military than the
  others, whose Rangers arrive with an instrument. The anthem's status is
  unchanged: the Corps commissions nothing.
- Shepherd entered as a Novice through that college, carried its study, and
  had her Cadet standing confirmed by it; her Season 1 general operations track
  is unaffected, since tracks are not corps.
- Her study gives the Sentinel section its continuity: the officer who heard
  music in the telemetry had been trained to ask what music is to someone
  else. Nothing about the Sentinel changes.
- Slipwave's chronology is unchanged: the band, the anthem a year before Cadet
  standing, the end of the band before she left for cadet training.

### An inconsistency noticed, not changed

The character page's *Rank Progression* puts the Sentinel telemetry duty in
Season 2 (the Survey rotation); its *Sentinel Signal* section says Season 3,
"newly made Principal". Pre-existing, outside this direction; raised as an
issue rather than settled here.

### Direction check

No branch military: the corps trains musicians and researchers. Trope default:
no bard, no battle-song, no music-as-magic; the study is comparative and the
instances are the record's own. The Archive rule: a corps, an intake and a
specialism are what the record's institutions know about their own; nothing
here reveals a chapter fact early. The private sibling's Lane Log 049 and Road
Log 133 hold the private side of her music (fronting by context, the band
named) and stay private.
