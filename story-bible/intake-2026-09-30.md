## The Krenyi glossary entry and the word enslavement

His correction, verbatim: *"Krenyi glossary entry wrongly names enslavement
when it meant the opposite"*.

### Canon check

`src/glossary/krenyi.md` stated the trait twice as a resistance: *structural
resistance to fear, fury, enslavement, mythification, and grief-predation* in
the summary, and *resistant to glamour, enslavement, and mythification* in the
body. The lore page's bullet said *Unenslavable: no physical, social, or
metaphysical mechanism for enslaving them exists within the story's
cosmology*, and its Levril section says *they are unenslavable in the strict
sense the cosmology gives that word*. S01E01C01's canon fact says *cannot be
enslaved or mythified*. The 27 September intake noted that *slavery* appears
in canon only of the Krenyi. So the record's meaning was never in doubt; the
glossary's phrasing named the thing by its absence and, in a list, invited
the opposite reading.

### Realized, as a clarification

The glossary's summary and body now state the freedom itself: structural
rather than won, no mechanism in the cosmology able to own or compel one,
cooperation only ever extended (the lore page's own floor). The lore bullet
says the same in place of *Unenslavable* and names the older filing; the
Levril section's sentence stands, since it defines the word as it uses it.
The chapter's canon fact is untouched.

### Put as choices — his

The wording, since the correction names the fault and not the replacement:

1. **Freedom stated as structure** (realized, recommended): *a freedom that
   is structural rather than won — no mechanism in the cosmology can own or
   compel one*.
2. **Autonomy as the defining word**: *structural autonomy* in the summary's
   list, with the same explanatory sentence in the body.
3. **Drop the item from the summary altogether** and leave the body's
   sentence to carry it, on the ground that a glossary summary should not
   list what a people cannot be subjected to at all.

### Ruled, the same day

Verbatim: *"Option 1"*. The wording stands as realized: freedom stated as
structure. Nothing open; PR #898 merged on green.

His note after ruling, verbatim: *"I simply misread it, but the
clarification is even better."* So the fault was a misreading the phrasing
invited rather than an error in the record's meaning, and the clarification
stands on its own merit.

## The Krenyi are the strangest people because they are the nearest to human

His direction, verbatim: *"Krenyi are the strangest non-human people because
they look superficially humanoid and could pass as almost human."*

### Canon check

The record already ran on this without saying it. The 13 August ruling made
the Krenyi the only other people on the human frame; the lore page opens on
*structurally distinct from humans in several important ways* and lists what
differs (no deep fear or fury, no organ to point at, an age that will not
resolve, blood hazardous to a human, no interbreeding); the Syra portrait
brief in `images.md` says *the Quiet-Built look almost human and decline to
say what they are* and *the stillness is the alien*; the 18 August Arilon
work said they *pass as a person to a low-context observer*. The 22 September
rule refuses the too-humanoid alien *unless that is the point of the story*,
and for the Krenyi it is. So this is a **clarification**: the page said the
differences and never said why the frame makes them land harder.

### Realized

A section on `src/lore/krenyi.md`, *The Nearest Frame Is the Strangest*,
placed after the characteristics list: the frame is what makes them strange,
because a people far from it is met with nothing assumed and a Krenyi is met
as a familiar kind, so every difference arrives as a correction; they could
pass as almost human and do not try to, the passing failing on time rather
than inspection and never resting on a claim, since they use no social
deception; and survey teaching trains cadets on the nearest frame. One
sentence on the glossary entry. A dated paragraph in `species-design.md`.

### Readings, flagged

1. **Strangest is the observer's experience, not a rank of the peoples.**
   Read so, and written so: the Archive ranks no minds. Confirm.
2. **Passing is possible and never attempted.** The record says the Krenyi use
   no social deception and decline to say what they are; the draft holds both,
   so a Krenyi is never written as passing on purpose. Confirm.
3. **Survey teaching trains cadets on the nearest frame.** New in-world
   practice, stated in one sentence and asserted of no page. Confirm or cut.
4. **The direction is not a licence for a second near-human people.** The
   species-design note says so. Confirm.

### Direction check

A clarification of the 22 September exception and the 13 August ruling; the
18 August correction that the Arilon pattern is species-neutral is untouched,
since the passing here is a fact about the Krenyi and not about the pattern.
The converse of the silhouette instrument is worth naming here and not on the
page: a human isolate's silhouette fires and is never confirmed; the Krenyi
silhouette fires and is refuted, by descent, by blood and by the calendar.
Tone: strange, never uncanny-as-horror; the section says nothing a parent
reading aloud would pass over.
