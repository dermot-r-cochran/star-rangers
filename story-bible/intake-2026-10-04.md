## The Smart Pet entry's related terms on undercover-pets.com

Verbatim: *"Smart Pets glossary has links to Cyborg that cannot be resolved
from Undercover Pets"*.

**What was wrong.** The glossary layout resolved each `related:` term
through `glossaryUrl`, which matches titles against `collections.glossary`
and `collections.lore` — the build's own collections, already narrowed by
the content filter — and on a miss fell back to `/glossary/`. On
undercover-pets.com the only glossary entry carried is *Smart Pet*
(tagged `undercover-pets.com`); none of its five related terms (*The AI
Safety Kernel*, *Kernel-Compliant*, *Cyborg*, *Krenyi*, *Heritable
Modification Protocols*) is on that build, so all five linked to the
glossary index, which lists one entry and none of them. *Cannot be
resolved* is exactly right.

**Fixed, engine, first tier**, merged when green: the resolver is its own
module (`lib/related-terms.js`, null on a miss, never the index), the
layout links only the terms that resolve on the build in hand and omits
the Related Terms aside when none do, `glossaryUrl` is kept for any
other caller with its old fallback, and `test/related-terms.test.js`
pins the contract including the narrowed-build case. Verified with a
narrowed build on the children's edition's filter: the Smart Pet page
renders no Related Terms aside. On the full site nothing changes, since
every term resolves there; a retitled page's stale term now vanishes
from lists instead of linking to the index, which is what
`check-related-terms.js` already catches.

**What the fix does not touch, and one choice.** Inline links in the
entry's body — *cyborg* twice, the AI Safety Kernel, Rook-7, the Orbital
Habitats Compact — still go to those pages' placeholders, which on the
children's edition is the plain *Not on this site* notice with a way
back and no cross-domain link, as the 1 September design has it. That is
the *no link ever 404s* promise working, and a child meets a closed door
rather than a wrong room. If he would rather the Cyborg entry itself were
on the children's edition — it is about Aldera as much as anything, and
the Smart Pet entry's own plain line already says a Smart Pet is not a
cyborg — the change is one tag, `undercover-pets.com`, on
`src/glossary/cyborg.md`, and it is a content decision put as a choice:
(a) leave Cyborg off the children's edition, the door closed (as now);
(b) tag it on, so the link and the related term both resolve there. The
tiers are not content ratings, so (b) is a question of what the children's
floor is for, not of suitability.

**RULED the same day, verbatim *(b) or create an alternative simplified
cyborg glossary entry if needed*.** Realized as (b) with no alternative
entry, because none is needed and one would be wrong: every glossary
entry renders an *In short* plain-register line on every edition
(`plain:`, the Smart Pet entry's being the model), and the Cyborg entry
simply lacked one; and the children's About page promises that *nothing
is rewritten for them*, which a second, simplified Cyborg page would
break while putting two entries under one title. So `src/glossary/
cyborg.md` gains the `undercover-pets.com` tag and a `plain:` line in the
children's register — a cyborg's body is changed, a Smart Pet's is not,
Aldera is both, an empire once built cyborg soldiers and treated their
bodies like equipment, the answer now is usually no. The line is the
draft's wording and his to change. Verified on a children's-filter
build: the Cyborg page is real content there, the Smart Pet page's
Related Terms aside is back with Cyborg as its one term, and the
glossary index lists two entries. The Cyborg entry's own inline links to
the Imperium, the Military Space Command, the AI Safety Kernel, the
