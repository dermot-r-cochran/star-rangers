## The children's glossary — RULED, REALIZED

Verbatim, as a question: *"Should I make almost all glossary entries
available to the Undercover Pets edition?"*

**What was found before answering.** The pets edition carried two
glossary entries, *Smart Pet* and *Cyborg*, both by the
`undercover-pets.com` tag; the other 81 rendered as the plain-register
*Not on this site* placeholder. The Season 2 prose links exactly one
entry, *Smart Pet*, and names no other glossary term in its bodies apart
from UCSD in timestamps; the pets cast pages add *Plural Minds* and
*Cyborg*. The glossary index shows each entry's `short`, which is adult
register (*Cyborg*'s reads around grade 15), and the `plain:` line,
written for this reader, renders only on the entry page; 13 of 83
entries carried one. The ladder permits widening — tiers nest, filtering
only subtracts, and the tiers are not content ratings — so the question
was completability, not safety.

**Why not almost all.** The reader profile's named failure is a wall of
text, and 37 of the 83 entries are Cosmology or Cosmic Cascade pages at
the general tier's register; a child opening the Glossary shelf expecting
*what a word means, if you meet one you don't know* would meet eighty
words they never met. The direction of travel since 13 September has been
narrowing (Season 1 off the domain, the Five-O cast kept out, empty header
items hidden). And the tag is documented as a page opting into the deploy,
which tagging eighty pages would empty of meaning.

**Three shapes put, with a recommendation:**

1. **Reachability rule (recommended).** An entry joins the pets glossary
   when a pets chapter or pets-cast page links it, and it carries a
   `plain:` line; the index shows `plain:` under the primer posture.
2. **The thirteen with a `plain:` line.** Mechanical, but Plainmark,
   Difference Form and Enrolled Text have no road into the pets story.
3. **All 83, done properly.** About seventy `plain:` lines to write and the
   index changed to show them — an authoring project, not a filter flip.

Whichever, on `CHILDREN_TIER` rather than the pets entry alone, since
young.fianilchruinne.com had the same one-entry glossary and inherits the
floor.

**Ruled**, verbatim: *"Option 1, on CHILDREN_TIER"*.

**Direction check** (`decision-making.md`): an extension. It adds a rule
where none was written — which glossary entries a children's edition
carries had been whatever happened to be tagged — and reverses nothing:
the 13 September narrowing stands, the tag keeps its meaning, canon stays
centralised and filtering still only subtracts. The `plain:` lines it
requires are clarifications of entries already published, never second
accounts.

**Realized the same day**, PR on `ccr-e32ad86c-urk9v8`:

- The rule, as recorded in `CLAUDE.md`: a glossary entry joins the
  children's tier when a page the tier carries links it and it carries a
  `plain:` line, by taking the tier's topic tag `undercover-pets.com`,
  which `CHILDREN_TIER` names in `lib/editions.js` and every children's
  edition inherits. No filter change was needed.
- The gate, `scripts/check-children-glossary.js`, in `npm test` after
  `check-related-terms.js`. It runs once per edition at the children's
  tier with that edition's own filter (the Told edition extends the floor's
  threads and carries pages the pets edition does not) and scans every
  page the edition carries plus the edition's own copy in `lib/editions.js`
  for glossary links. A link to an entry the edition renders as a
  placeholder fails; an entry the tier carries without a `plain:` line
  fails; an entry nothing on the tier links warns. The link checker cannot
  see the first case because a placeholder is a real page.
- **One hop, not a closure.** The first cut of the gate enforced a carried
  glossary entry's own onward links too, and that reproduced the question:
  *Boundary Zone* links *Etheric*, *Interval*, *Intermembrane Bleed*,
  *Membrane Shadow*, *Concordant* and *Instrument Drift*, each of which
  links more, so the closure is the cosmology's whole web — the almost-all
  shape he declined, by the back door. The shape he chose was one hop from
  the story (*a pets chapter or pets-cast page links it*), so the gate
  enforces links from every carried page except the glossary's own entries;
  an entry's onward links stop at the tier's edge, where the child meets the
  plain-register placeholder, which is the edition's designed behaviour for
  the edge of its set. An entry one onward link reaches may still join on it
  — *UCSD* did, from *Cyborg* — but need not. The gate prints the count of
  edge links per edition (six on both children's editions today) rather than
  warning on each, so the number stays visible without becoming a warning
  anyone learns to skip.
- Its first run found one reach the hand survey had missed: Aldera's
  timeline entry, on the pets edition by tag, links *Boundary Zone*. So the
  rule reaches five entries, not four: *Plural Minds* (from the Eden
  Warden's page), *UCSD* (from *Cyborg*) and *Boundary Zone* join *Smart
  Pet* and *Cyborg*. *Plural Minds* and *Boundary Zone* gained the `plain:`
  lines they lacked, each written to the entry as published and naming the
  case the pets edition reaches it through (the Eden Warden; Aldera at the
  Marsh Causeway); *UCSD* already had one.
- `src/glossary/index.md` on a children's-tier build shows a plain intro
  and each entry's `plain:` line in place of its `short`, keyed off the
  edition's tier, not the presentation mode, since the register is the
  tier's and a reader can switch the posture.
- `CHILDREN_TIER`'s comment, the pets reader profile, `TestingStrategy.md`
  and the changelog record it.

**Nothing open.** The two `plain:` lines are proposals like any draft;
