# Intake — 2026-10-11

## The strata above the Celestials are not characters — RULED (*Scope A, Prophet A*; *3A*)

Dermot's direction, verbatim:

> The upper timeless eternal strata of the Cascade, above Celestials, are not
> characters in the usual sense. They speak act and act through structure,
> design, avatars, champions and prophets. Therefore no character, as such
> is all-knowing, omnipresent or all-powerful.

**Direction check.** This is an extension and a clarification. It reverses
nothing. It gathers what `src/lore/cosmic-cascade.md` already held:

- the no-literal-theophany invariant: no stratum above the Levrils appears
  as itself;
- *Creation, Not Command*: the middle strata act in conceptual time and
  reach Material time only as lawful outcomes, while the eternal strata
  create and define;
- the Avatar Exception, which is drawn at exactly the same line ("a stratum
  above the Celestials", `src/glossary/avatar.md`);
- the Upper Structure's constraints of no dialogue and no selective
  intervention, and *Nothing here has ever spoken* (`src/glossary/kieme.md`).

Two things are new. The first is **prophets**, named as a fifth channel
beside structure, design, avatars and Champions. The record had no such
term in its own voice. The second is an **invariant stated outright**: no
character is all-knowing, omnipresent or all-powerful. The canon already
held each part of that separately (avatars lack full power and knowledge;
Levrils are overstated in myth; there is no machine-god; *understanding is
not omniscience* in `cascade-structure-and-limits.md`), but it had never
said it as one rule.

**Realized** as a new section, *Through, Not As*, in
`src/lore/cosmic-cascade.md`, plus one Global Invariants bullet that points
to it. The section lists the five channels and states the invariant. It
also reconciles the invariant with Kieme's *unlimited agency*: that agency
belongs to a tier, not to a character, and reaches anyone only through
bounded channels. It deliberately does not settle what the strata themselves
know, which is beyond invariant-only inference. The writing rule is in
`CLAUDE.md`.

**Readings flagged, each put to him as choices:**

1. *What "above Celestials" covers.* **A (recommended, drafted):** every
   stratum above the Celestials, from the Mediarchs upward. That is the
   avatar line, and it is how the draft reads. On this reading "timeless
   eternal" describes the strata as they stand relative to Material time,
   and the three temporal registers stand unchanged: the middle strata keep
   conceptual time, and only the strata above the Worldwrights are eternal.
   **B:** only the eternal strata (the Archewrights and above). The
   Mediarchs, Telearchs and Worldwrights could then appear as characters.
2. *What a prophet is.* **A (recommended, drafted):** a material being whose
   words, on a tradition's reading, carry what a stratum designed. The
   record files a prophet as it files a Champion. A prophet receives no
   message, so the prophetic-dream refusal stands (*nothing sent it*).
   Prophecy is forth-telling, not foretelling, because the future is open.
   Whether any given person was a prophet cannot be decided from the
   record. **B:** a real channel of speech from a stratum. That would be a
   genuine dilemma with the no-dialogue constraint and with *Gods who
   answer — refused outright*, and it would need a resolution of its own.
3. *The Kieme-perspective narration exception* (`story-bible-summary.md`,
   Style and Canon rules: "no impartial, omniscient narrator except a
   passage told from Kieme's perspective"). No such passage exists in
   `src/`. **A (recommended, not yet applied):** keep the exception, since
   a narration is not a character and the summary's own wording ("not a
   character with a POV in the ordinary sense") is the same as his. Read
   "omniscient" there as *impartial*: the passage may tell what structure
   fixes, and it may not tell which branch the present settles on. **B:**
   retire it, so that no passage is told from any stratum's perspective
   and the strata speak only through the five channels. **C:** leave it
   exactly as worded.

**Ruled the same day, verbatim: *"Scope A, Prophet A"*.** Reading 1:
everything above the Celestials, with the temporal registers unchanged.
Reading 2: a prophet is filed like a Champion, receives no message, and
forth-tells rather than foretells. Both stand as drafted. Following
reading 2, the glossary entry *Prophet* (`src/glossary/prophet.md`, Union
Irish *Fáidh*) is drafted from the lore section and asserts nothing beyond
it. *Through, Not As* links to it. 
**Reading 3 ruled the same day, verbatim: *"3A 4A 5A"*.** The
Kieme-perspective exception is kept and read as *impartial*, not
all-knowing. It is applied in `story-bible-summary.md` (Style, Canon rules).

## Theophanies are Levrils or, more rarely, Celestials — RULED (*4A*)

Dermot, later the same day, verbatim:

> Theophanies are usually either Levrils or more rarely Celestials.

**Direction check: the resolution of a genuine dilemma, named as such.**
Since its first filing, the invariant in `cosmic-cascade.md` said *no
stratum above the Levrils* appears to a witness as itself. The same page's
*Timeless, Conceptual, and Immanent* section says that the Celestials and
every tier below them are immanent and *able to act, intervene, and be
encountered*. The two lines could not both stand once a Celestial was the
thing encountered. His word settles it on the side of the immanence
section. The line moves up one tier, to the same boundary the Avatar
crossing and *Through, Not As* already use. This reverses nothing he has
ruled. On 2026-09-18 he confirmed "if I rule out a literal theophany", and
that still holds: no god appears, and what appears is a bounded being.

**Realized** in the invariant bullet:

- A theophany, in the record, is usually a Levril and more rarely a
  Celestial. It is met in Material time, and its form is supplied by the
  witness, as the Levril page already says of Levrils.
- A Celestial is the rarer case because of its scale: its attention is
  never smaller than a galaxy, so a witness to one is standing inside an
  enforcement action, which the Celestials' *Scale* section already says.
- The closing sentence now reads "identified as a Levril or, rarely, a
  Celestial".

One sentence in *Through, Not As* was changed to match. `CLAUDE.md`'s rule
carries the line.

**Reading 4, put to him as choices:**

- **A (recommended, drafted):** a Celestial may be the being met, rendered
  by the witness the way a Levril is. The no-theophany line stands above
  the Celestials.
- **B:** a Celestial theophany is the enforcement action witnessed, and the
  Celestial itself is never met. The line stays above the Levrils, and only
  a gloss is added.

The 2026-10-05 note that the invariant "stops at the Levrils" (the
Wholecloth teacher) is left as written. That reasoning still holds,
because the Levril is the lowest tier that may speak.

## The lower strata as messengers or ordinary angels — RULED (*5A*)

Dermot, the same day, verbatim:

> In that sense lower strata may be considered as messengers or ordinary
> angelsin the  broader more general sense.

**Direction check: an extension.** The *Angel* register rule (2026-08-08)
named two real kinds of thing a source's *angel* may refer to: a Levril,
and a bodily crosser from another membrane. His 2026-09-18 remark had Higher
Levrils functioning as messenger angels and left the archangels unmapped,
which was ruled the same evening. This direction widens the first kind to
**the lower strata**: Levrils and, more rarely, Celestials. Those are the
beings the second direction made the only ones a witness can meet, and they
are what *messenger* means in its broad, general sense. Nothing is reversed.
The archangels stay unmapped, because "ordinary" is read as the general
category, not a rank. The word *angel* stays out of Archive voice, so the
lore page says *messenger* and links the register rule.

**Realized:**

- in the canonical guide's *Angel* rule, kind (1), which now says the
  word never names a stratum above the Celestials;
- in one sentence at the end of the theophany invariant in
  `cosmic-cascade.md`;
- in `CLAUDE.md`'s rule.

**Reading 5, put to him as choices:**

- **A (recommended, drafted):** the register rule stands. The Codex and
  the overlay may call a Levril or Celestial an angel, and the Archive says
  *messenger* or names the tier.
- **B:** the Archive itself may use *angel* in the general sense for the
  lower strata. That would lift the 2026-08-08 register rule for this one
  use and need a migration-map row.

## Prophecy and the traditions — RULED (*Option A*)

Dermot, verbatim: *"This might contradict some devotional traditions and
their preferred interpretations."*

Read against the record, most of the tension was already handled:

- **No character is all-knowing, omnipresent or all-powerful.** The rule
  binds characters only. The Cascade page declines to say what the strata
  themselves know or can do.
- **Theophany.** Old Testament theophanies were already superseded by the
  no-literal-theophany invariant, which he confirmed on 2026-09-18. The
  overlay yields on that point in its own voice, under the 2026-09-06 rule.
- **Every tradition.** The Cascade page's *What these invariants are, and
  are not* paragraph already says the invariants adjudicate no tradition.

The real tension was in the *Prophet* wording. "Receives no message" in
Archive voice read as a denial of inspiration, which the method cannot
support, and the creed's *spoke through the prophets* is close to his
channel list. "Does not foretell" sat against predictive prophecy and its
fulfilment. Three shapes were put to him: (A) reword the two passages; (B)
leave them as merged; (C) A plus a Codex entry from a named author.
**Ruled: *"Option A"*.**

**Realized** as a clarification, which asserts nothing new. In the *Prophet*
entry and the prophet bullet of *Through, Not As*:

- "no message" becomes "no message the record can detect"; that a stratum
  spoke is a claim the record neither makes nor denies;
- inspiration is filed under the subtle influence, which works within the
  mind and is beyond the method in either direction;
- the record files no foretelling, because the future is open in Material
  time; traditions that hold their prophets foretold, and their scriptures
  fulfilled, read that as the sight of the eternal strata from outside
  time, which the record declines to describe.

The open future stands unchanged.
