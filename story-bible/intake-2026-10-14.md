# Intake — 2026-10-14

## Inward conversations in Tissadelle's POV — RECORDED; nothing open

Dermot's direction, verbatim:

> Tissadelle's POV can include internal private unseen conversations with the
> more public facing members of her internal world such as Tobble or Asteria
> the Sage

**Direction check.** A clarification and a small extension. The form is
already in the corpus: in *What Came Off the Ship* (S05E02C03) she speaks to
Tobble *inward*, and he answers, in italics with an ordinary attribution. The
direction makes the practice general to her POV and names Asteria, who has not
yet spoken in any published chapter, as the second member it may include.
Nothing published says otherwise.

**Canon checks.**

- *Very close third, one head at a time* (`prose-style.md` §5) is not broken.
  The block stays in one head; the head holds more than one person. The
  conversation is recorded there as a paragraph under §5.
- *Every member is real* and *a speech difference is never a tell for the
  dangerous one* (the dialect device, `intake-2026-10-10.md`) bind every inward
  line. Tobble and Asteria each speak in their own idiom.
- *Answered prayer and spiritual voices are never the record's own claim*
  (2026-08-26) is not touched: a headmate is a person, not a voice from
  elsewhere. A `canon_facts` line may record that a conversation took place
  and what was said, as it would for any two people.
- *Unseen* holds in two directions. No other character hears it, and the
  narration of another character's block never reports it. Only Tissadelle's
  block, and Tobble's or Asteria's where they hold a POV, can.
- The existing line in S05E02C03, *which was not a thing she did on duty*,
  stands. The direction says what her POV *can* include, not how often.

**Extended the same session**, verbatim: *"Likewise for Sen's headmates and
non-human plural systems"*. So the same holds inside a POV block of
[Sen](../src/characters/sen.md): Cael, Wyn and Sen may speak to one another
inward. All three are named on the record, so all three count as public-facing.
It also holds for any non-human plural system. Two notes on the second half:

- The record holds no non-human plural system yet, apart from Tobble, who is a
  non-human member of a human system. The rule waits for the first one. That
  system's people decide what *inward* means for a body that does not speak
  aloud the way a human does, and its species page decides it when the
  system is written (`language-design.md` asks the channel question first).
- [The Eden Warden](../src/characters/eden-warden.md) is the case the record
  declines to file as plural or as two specifications sharing one system
  (`src/lore/what-a-mind-is-carried-by.md`). An inward exchange between Ward and
  Custos is consistent with both readings, so writing one does not settle the
  question, and a draft that has one must not let the narration settle it either.

**Settled by precedent, so not put to Dermot:** the typography (italics,
*she said, inward*, as published); that the first inward line Asteria speaks
on the page is drafting work, Fable's, when a chapter calls for it.

Nothing is drafted from the direction itself.
