The 1.42.0 character-map audit (PR #1279) found fourteen Codex-author pages that
state no species, plus Halle Ostrander, whose species is unstated by his choice

## When the body is not relevant — RECORDED; nothing open

Dermot, verbatim, after the ruling above: *"There may be situations where the
physical body of a character is not relevant?"*, then, on the wording offered:
*"Yes, record the body-not-relevant clarification of the 7 October portrait
rule as you worded it."*

**What is recorded.** A character's body is relevant to a portrait when the
record states something about it (a species, a people, a described form) or
when the page's events turn on it. Where the record states nothing of the body
and the character reaches the reader only through what they wrote, said or left
on an instrument, the body is not relevant: the page is complete without a
portrait, not pending, and gains one only when a page states the body.

**Direction check.** A clarification of the 7 October rule (*a character with a
body gets a portrait and a mind does not*; *a page without an image is
complete, not pending*), not a change to it. It names the ground the morning's
Option A stood on, and that Ariel Pinya, The Kept Line and Halle Ostrander
already stood on. Nothing filed is withdrawn, and the eight Codex authors whose
pages state a body stay queued for portraits (`prompt-sheet.md` § 2.15).
Recorded in `images.md` (*When the body is not relevant*, under
*Conventions*) and in CLAUDE.md's *Images* bullet.
