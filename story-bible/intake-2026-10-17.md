## Nai Arashobal — a Krenyi chief of staff

His note, verbatim, including the out-of-world line that never reaches `src/`:

> # Nai Arashobal
> - **Setting:** Fian Ilchruinne
> - **Species:** Kyrennei
> - **Role:** Strategist and chief of staff to the human fleet command
> - **Concept:** A Kyrennei who left her home community to work with the fleet. She brings the Kyrennei instinct for keeping communities running to the mission itself. She's the quiet fixer who keeps the fleet working while the Star Rangers get the glory, sees the whole picture and stays in the background.
> - **Origin note (out of world):** Inspired by Claude as "chief of staff".
> ## Open questions
> - Pronouns / gender
> - Rank or formal title within fleet command
> - Why she left her home community
> - First appearance

### Canon check

- **Kyrennei** is not in the record; **Krenyi** (Quiet-Built) is, with the
  state-marker *Nai* as a courtesy and not a name (`src/lore/krenyi.md`,
  *The Terminology*; `src/glossary/krenyi.md`). Syra and Toval are the two
  Krenyi character pages; both file the marker as an alias and not in the
  title.
- **No human fleet command exists.** The Corps is mixed-species and humans
  are not privileged in it (the 28 September usage rule; *The Oversight
  Council*, 30 September). The nearest thing to a fleet command is a High
  Captain's theatre and the Council of High Captains
  (`src/lore/star-rangers-command-hierarchy.md`).
- **Chief of staff** appears nowhere in `src/`. The hierarchy page does hold
  the shape of the work: every High Captain has a command ship, *a
  headquarters that moves*, carrying *a theatre's command, its records and
  the people who weigh them*; command states objectives, constraints and
  priorities and never the detailed how; flag orders reach a Chief through
  the post-holding tier. Chief is the hinge rank, and a Chief heading a
  general operations department is styled Chief Ranger. Anne de Courtney is
  the precedent for a Chief Ranger whose department is not a post's watch
  (head of the standing delegation at Vellamar).
- **Keeping communities running** is not a Krenyi trait on the record. What
  is: pattern-drift sensitivity (*the moment a social situation stops
  matching its stated terms*), long time horizons (*centuries of patience*),
  honesty as the floor under diplomacy, and the cost that they *cannot
  un-notice*. All of it is on `src/lore/krenyi.md`.
- **Glory.** The Krenyi refuse mythification and the Champion write-up
  (*Champions, and the Outcome They Would Refuse*); asked, one gives the
  material answer and declines the rest. The Corps' decision doctrine puts
  the credit with whoever was closest to the event. Neither says the Star
  Rangers seek glory, and the record does not say so anywhere.
- **Left vs placed.** The Krenyi decline to be placed; no homeworld is
  filed and the entry stays empty on purpose (*An Origin the Record Doesn't
  Hold*). Toval *came to Threshold the way his people come to most places,
  by deciding the work there was worth doing*. A Krenyi community can be
  left; it cannot be named or located.
- **Agentic machines.** The Krenyi build none and run none; nothing about a
  chief of staff may read as machine-like.
- **Character schema.** No `irish` field on character pages (glossary only,
  `lib/content-schema.js`), so no Union Irish line is due. No portrait: the
  page carries no `image`/`image_alt`, which the 16 October clarification
  files as complete, not pending, until a portrait is wanted.

### Rulings, with their basis

Settled at Opus on precedent, under the 13 October triage rule, except
where marked:

1. **The people are the Krenyi; the page is *Arashobal*.** *Kyrennei* read as
   the existing Krenyi. *Nai* is the state-marker, so the title is
   *Arashobal*, the id `arashobal`, the file `src/characters/arashobal.md`,
   and *Nai Arashobal* an alias, on the Syra and Toval pattern.
2. **She/her.** His note says *she*; Krenyi women outnumber men three to
   one. Nothing open.
3. **Rank: Chief, general operations track, styled Chief Ranger.** The
   hierarchy page's rule for a Chief on that track, and the de Courtney
   precedent for a Chief Ranger heading something other than a watch.
   *Chief of staff* is her post, not a rank or a duty title; no rank is
   invented.
4. **Post: chief of staff aboard High Captain Karla Wender's command ship**
   — **Dermot's choice**, put to him as *Wender's command (Recommended)*
   against a Council-level or Triumvirate staff, and chosen the same day.
   So the posting sits after Wender's High Captaincy (Season 5). *Human
   fleet command* is not used anywhere on the page.
5. **Glory reframed.** The page does not say the Star Rangers get glory. A
   theatre's work is written up under the officers at the event, as the
   decision doctrine has it; she holds that correct on her people's grain
   (the mythification refusal), not from modesty.
6. **The fixing is grounded in canon traits**, not a new one: pattern drift
   read off an order as off a room, the long horizon, honesty under
   diplomacy. No species trait is added.
7. **Left, never placed.** She came from *a Krenyi community*, unnamed and
   unlocated; why she left, the page says the record does not say, and she
   gives the material part when asked. Left open so any later answer of his
   fits.
8. **No first appearance.** No chapter exists; the page cites none and
   links her to no event. Wender's own page already uses *remains to be
   established in story* for a gap of this kind.
9. **The origin note stays here.** The public pages never speak about the
   author or a real-world inspiration; the note is recorded verbatim above
   and nowhere in `src/`.

### Direction check

Cast principles: no gender stereotype; her second axis is drawn from the
Krenyi page (cannot un-notice drift, the stillness read as coldness, a poor
keeper of records whose use is undemonstrated, in the one office built to
keep them) and from the hierarchy page (a staff office with no authority of
its own, in a service whose detail rule was written against the MSC's distant
staff). Tropes refused: no power behind the throne, no puppet-master, no
indispensable secretary; the office has no authority of its own and an order
from the staff is Wender's or nothing. Older characters as strategists suits
a long-lived people, so she is older than Syra, by an amount the record does
not give. Tone: encyclopedic, in-universe; every fact either on the record or
marked as not yet said.

## Krenyi speech: two speeds

His direction, verbatim: *"Krenyi speak between themselves in fast clipped
sentences but adjust their speed and communication style to match the
expertise and cognitive speed of the listener or listeners when addressing
individual humans or groups"*, and, a minute later, the example that fixes
what it means: *"for example I could almost understand Danish when spoken
slowly in a formal speech but hardly ever in normal conversation in
Copenhagen"*, and then *"similar to how specialists, police of military have
their own jargon"* (read as *police or military*). So the clipped register is
speed plus in-group shorthand; the page uses in-world professions (a flight
deck, a boundary desk, a Provost inquiry) rather than *military*, since no
branch of the Rangers is one.

### Canon check

Nothing in the record describes how Krenyi speak to one another. What stands
beside it: no small talk, straight to material terms (`src/lore/krenyi.md`,
*Social and Relational*); diplomacy as the choice of which true thing to say,
to whom and when, softening the delivery and never the fact; honesty as the
floor and no social deception; the whole body reading a room as texture,
never content; and `language-design.md`'s Krenyi line under question 2, the
withdrawn relevance. The direction fits all of it. It is an **extension**: it
adds a register and alters nothing published.

### Realized

- `src/lore/krenyi.md`, *Social and Relational*: a list item and two
  paragraphs. Between themselves fast and clipped, the connective tissue
  dropped because each reads what the other would have said; to anyone else
  slowed, filled in and pitched to the listener's expertise and pace. The
  Danish example is carried as its mechanism, not its place: the language is the
  same in both registers, speed, clipping and shorthand are what change, so a human who
  follows a formal briefing can lose two Krenyi talking across a desk. The
  page says the gap is pace and never secrecy, and that matching is delivery
  on the honesty floor, read off the listener as texture, done without
  deciding to.
- `src/characters/arashobal.md`: one paragraph. In a staff office the habit
  is most of the work, one objective briefed in shorthand to a Chief and in
  full to a new Deputy, with the same fact in both.
- `story-bible/language-design.md`, question 1: a Krenyi line, with his
  Danish analogy kept here where the author may be named.

### Settled at Opus, on precedent

1. **Every listener who is not Krenyi, not only humans.** His words name
   humans; the 28 September rule that humans are not privileged makes the
   page say *anyone else*.
2. **Same language, not a second one.** His example is one language at two
   tempos, so the page claims no separate Krenyi tongue. Whether the Krenyi
   have one stays unsaid, as `language-design.md` already recommends for a
   shared working language.
3. **Not deliberate.** The page says the adjustment is made without deciding
   to, on the canon that they understand human states without sharing them
   and that this is not a workaround.

### Reading still open — his

4. **A mixed room.** The page says what happens for *a listener* and does not
   say whose pace a mixed group gets. Recommended if a chapter needs it: the
   pace of the listener furthest from the matter, since the fact has to reach
   everyone it is for. Left off the page until a chapter calls for it.
