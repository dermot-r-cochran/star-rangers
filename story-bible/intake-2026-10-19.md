## Distinctions outside the Corps, on leaving, and on secondment — RULED

Dermot asked, verbatim: *"Are the three certificate grades sufficient for the Stat Rangers are are they valid for people with are on secondment from the Stat Rangers or join another organisation like Five-O"*. Three shapes were put; he chose verbatim: *"A"*.

**Ruled:**
- Three grades suffice for Rangers. Added: **Honorary Fellow** for non-Rangers whose work advanced a branch. It is awarded, never applied for, kept on the guild's record and never worn.
- **A distinction follows the person; a certification lapses on leaving the Corps.** The record keeps that a certification was held. A new body decides for itself what either is worth.
- **No secondment into any body with police or military authority.** This follows from three standing rules: no authority inside a self-governing habitat, no branch police except the Frontier Corps, and Provost jurisdiction under immunity. Kai Larsen is the worked example: he resigned before taking Five-O.
- Secondment within the Corps keeps rank, post and plates.

**Left open:** secondment to an outside body with no police or military authority, such as an academic or Concord body. Shape C was not taken, so nothing permits it and nothing forbids it. It is filed in `open-questions.md`.

**Direction check.** An extension of the guild-distinctions page. It follows from the charter's limits and *Distinctions are not rank*, and the Larsen addition is a clarification of his published resignation.

**Realized** on `src/lore/guild-distinctions.md` (Honorary Fellow; *Leaving the Corps, and Secondment*) and `src/characters/kai-larsen.md`.
