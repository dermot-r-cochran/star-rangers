## Distinctions outside the Corps, on leaving, and on secondment — RULED

Dermot asked, verbatim: *"Are the three certificate grades sufficient for the Stat Rangers are are they valid for people with are on secondment from the Stat Rangers or join another organisation like Five-O"*. Three shapes were put; he chose verbatim: *"A"*.

**Ruled:**
- Three grades suffice for Rangers. Added: **Honorary Fellow** for non-Rangers whose work advanced a branch. It is awarded, never applied for, kept on the guild's record and never worn.
- **A distinction follows the person; a certification lapses on leaving the Corps.** The record keeps that a certification was held. A new body decides for itself what either is worth.
- **No secondment into any body with police or military authority.** This follows from three standing rules: no authority inside a self-governing habitat, no branch police except the Frontier Corps, and Provost jurisdiction under immunity. Kai Larsen is the worked example: he resigned before taking Five-O.
- Secondment within the Corps keeps rank, post and plates.

**Left open:** secondment to an outside body with no police or military authority, such as an academic or Concord body. Shape C was not taken, so nothing permits it and nothing forbids it. It is filed in `open-questions.md`.

**Direction check.** An extension of the guild-distinctions page. It follows from the charter's limits and *Distinctions are not rank*, and the Larsen addition is a clarification of his published resignation.

**Realized** on `src/lore/guild-distinctions.md` (Honorary Fellow; *Leaving the Corps, and Secondment*) and `src/characters/kai-larsen.md`.

Dermot, verbatim:

> Syra holds a Fellowship of the Survey Corps

**Read as.** Syra is a Fellow, the highest of the three grades, of the Survey Corps' guild. Her plate's Survey figure is raised.

**Checked against the record; no conflict.**
- She is Survey Corps. She was a Section Lead on the first Counterpane party in 2814, and she holds Threshold's boundary desk by 2831.
- Her record is the kind of body of work a Fellowship recognises: the 2814 calibration filed as *survives correction*, the 2826 discrepancy, and the Dock Seven boundary account.
- *Distinctions are not rank*, so the Fellowship gives her no rank. Her rank is not stated beyond the Section Lead of 2814 and the desk she holds.

**Settled at Opus, named so he can overrule.** The branch and the year of award are left unrecorded. Distinctions are awarded per branch, and no published branch of the Survey Corps (Archive, Planetology, Meteorology, Ecology, Xenolinguistics) plainly holds boundary analysis. Naming one, or registering a boundary branch, would be a larger step. Filed in `open-questions.md`.

## A treatment for Below the Roof's third season — DRAFTED; choices his

Dermot, verbatim:

> Draft a treatment for Below the Roof season three

Written as `below-the-roof-third-season-treatment.md`. The proposed premise is *a telling heard*. The Told spend their third rule, *a telling is not yours until somebody has heard it*, on the up-people. The up-people decide that the way to keep a telling from a people whose record cannot be seized is to hear it and not record it.

This answers the first treatment's deferred question, whether a telling ever reaches the Archive, in the record's own terms. The Archive gains the fact of the hearing and no words, so the glossary's *not recorded in any medium* stays true. The register is lighter, after the shadow season: a game the small ones make of the stones, and the smallest one's name settled.
