## Enough, and nothing wasted — RULED

Dermot, verbatim: *"29th century is post-scarcity in terms of basic survival needs but resources are allocated carefully and very little is wasted - there is no intentional wasted and larger projects still need careful budgeting and planning"*.

**Readings, settled at Opus by precedent:**
1. *Post-scarcity* is bounded to survival needs (air, water, warmth, food, shelter and care) in settled space. It does not mean unlimited goods.
2. *No intentional waste* is the norm. It is not a claim that nothing is ever lost. The Reclaimers and repair-in-kind (Knarrheim, *civilian-dress.md*) are already the record's mechanisms for it.
3. Larger projects are costed and planned. This matches the Corps' self-funding by revenue and the Safety Corps' budget.
4. No real-world economic system is named, under the 9 October ideology rule.

**Direction check.** An extension of the civilisation comparison's table and its *Waste handling* row. It does not conflict with the Tally's counting culture, the Chthonari's lack of scarcity, or *Krenyi*'s "a scarce competence is not wasted". Each is a local case of the same discipline.

**Realized** on `src/lore/civilisation-comparison.md`, as a table row and the section *Enough, and Nothing Wasted*.

**Follow-on, the same evening**, verbatim: *"so careers are chosen for the opportunities and learning experience as well as core values rather than survival; routine or hazardous work can be automated if no one wants to do it but robots will never disempower or exclude someone who chooses manual work as a craft or hobby"*. Realized as the section's last paragraph. *Robots* is read as any machine or automated system. The rule is stated as the age's practice, and no fifth invariant is added to the AI Safety Kernel. It is consistent with the stonemason in *Labour*, whose tools assist and do not replace. The machine characters (Jeeves, the Wardens) are unaffected, because nobody in the record has been displaced by them.

## A fast track for experienced entrants — RULED

Dermot, verbatim: *"there is a fast track progression for old Star Rangers entrants with previous experience; they might only spend a few days or weeks as a Novice and a few months as Cadet, rising to at least Field Officer within a year or two depending on their skills and experience as assessed by their corps, branch or guild within the Star Rangers"*.

**Readings, settled at Opus by precedent:**
1. *Old* means older entrants who join with prior experience, not long-serving Rangers. This rests on the sentence's own *entrants with previous experience*.
2. The track shortens time in rank and skips no rank. The no-skip rule of 18 October stands, and this direction fits inside it.
3. The track ends at Field Officer, where the ruling's *at least* sets the floor of what the assessment can reach. Above Field Officer, promotion is by the ordinary review.
4. The assessing body is the entrant's corps, branch or guild, as the ruling says. That is consistent with *Distinctions are not rank*, because the assessment sets pace, not rank.

**Direction check.** An extension of the rank page's *No rank is skipped*. Nothing published fixes a minimum time at Novice or Cadet, so nothing stands against it. It was passed the same day to the Fable agent drafting Shepherd's Novice chapter, with the instruction not to put her on the fast track unless the record supports it.

**Realized** on `src/lore/star-rangers-command-hierarchy.md`, after *No rank is skipped*.

## Distinctions outside the Corps, on leaving, and on secondment — RULED

Dermot asked, verbatim: *"Are the three certificate grades sufficient for the Stat Rangers are are they valid for people with are on secondment from the Stat Rangers or join another organisation like Five-O"*. Three shapes were put; he chose verbatim: *"A"*.

**Ruled:**
- Three grades suffice for Rangers. Added: **Honorary Fellow** for non-Rangers whose work advanced a branch. It is awarded, never applied for, kept on the guild's record and never worn.
- **A distinction follows the person; a certification lapses on leaving the Corps.** The record keeps that a certification was held. A new body decides for itself what either is worth.
- **No secondment into any body with police or military authority.** This follows from three standing rules: no authority inside a self-governing habitat, no branch police except the Frontier Corps, and Provost jurisdiction under immunity. Kai Larsen is the worked example: he resigned before taking Five-O.
- Secondment within the Corps keeps rank, post and plates.

**Left open:** secondment to an outside body with no police or military authority, such as an academic or Concord body. Shape C was not taken, so nothing permits it and nothing forbids it. It is filed in `open-questions.md`.

**Direction check.** An extension of the guild-distinctions page. It follows from the charter's limits and *Distinctions are not rank*, and the Larsen addition is a clarification of his published resignation.

**Realized** on `src/lore/guild-distinctions.md` (Honorary Fellow; *Leaving the Corps, and Secondment*) and `src/characters/kai-larsen.md`.

## The young adult and general registers re-grounded; courage without violence — RULED

Dermot, verbatim:

> The young adult reading tier should be as sensual and romantic as decency allows. The general adult reading tier should be mysterious and strategic. Courage and heroism, with patience and discretion rather than violence.

1. **Young adult: added, not replaced.** The rung reads *sensual and romantic as decency allows, with exploration and adventure*. *Mysterious* moves up to the general rung.
2. **"As decency allows."** *Sensual* in its root sense, of the senses: the felt texture of places and of attraction, attention and nearness, rendered and never displayed. *Romantic* means longing, devotion, courtship and choice. Nothing physical goes past what a parent reading aloud would pass. Romance between young characters stays with characters of an age for it. The modesty bar governs every image as before.
3. **General: added.** The rung reads *deeper, mysterious and strategic*: the institutions, the long game, the reasons under the text.
4. **Courage and heroism with patience and discretion rather than violence** is a standing rule at every rung.

**Direction check.** A **re-grounding** of the 22 September registers. Young adult's *mysterious* moves to the general rung, and young adult gains the sensual and romantic register; neither rung loses anything else. The any-age principle (4 September) and the modesty bar (18 September) are unchanged, and the decency bound is read through them. The ladder's invariant holds: a higher rung adds and never contradicts. The courage rule is an extension, the positive face of *no guns-and-spaceships*, *the case ends marked, not shot*, and the Rangers being neither military nor police.

Alternative: she sang in uniform and the room knew; rejected because it contradicts the Slipwave entry.
3. **Ordinary road, not fast track.** As above. Alternative: the Garda boards count as prior experience and her Novice time was weeks; rejected because the Slipwave entry fixes her Novice year at the band's last year.
