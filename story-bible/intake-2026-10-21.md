## The value of new Codex entries — RULED

Asked how much the Codex influences the narrative prose, then how much value there is in adding new entries, Dermot was given a measurement and a recommendation, and answered, verbatim:

> Also Yes to the codex decision.

**Measured on origin/main the same day.** 114 chapters, 81 Codex entries. No chapter's `related:` named a Codex entry (lore 94 times, glossary 53), no Codex entry's `revealed_by` named a chapter (lore 22, glossary 16), and no Codex title appeared in chapter prose. 72 of the 81 entries are linked from elsewhere in the record (median three inbound links: 59 from character pages, 41 from other entries, 40 from lore), so the Codex sits in the reference layer, not the story layer. A survey of who knows which entry found 62 of 81 known by nobody but their author; the one document chapters show passing from person to person is *The Life of Saint Aoife* (Daire to Smith to Varn; Sen's desk to Wender; Shepherd).

**The ruling, as recommended.** Keep the weekly *Histories in their own voices* series (20 October). Add any other Codex entry only when it does one of three things: it relieves lore, taking a contested or self-contradicting reading out of `src/lore/` under the lore/codex boundary; it gives a people its own voice; or a chapter meets it, so that it can be named in that chapter's `related:` and shown in *From the Archive*. Free-standing songs, ballads and hymns that no chapter, contest or people stands behind are low value and are not added for their own sake.

**First application, in the same PR.** The chapters that already meet a Codex entry now name it in `related:`, by the existing habit (*a chapter's `related:` names the entries its prose meets*):

- *The Life of Saint Aoife*: S06E01C03, S07E01C01, S07E01C03, S09E01C03, S09E01C05.
- *The Warm-Edge Correlation*: S07E01C01, where Sen's desk lays the two records adjacent.
- *A Proposal to Collar the Anstolik, Refused at Countersign*: S14E02C01, where Nakagawa and Egede recall the note on the page.

Borderline meetings (a passing "incident reports" in S01E03C01, Sen's desk in S06E01C02) were left out: the prose does not meet the entry itself.

**Direction check.** Additive. No prose changed; the Codex stays canon-for-its-author, and naming an entry in `related:` asserts nothing about its truth, only that the chapter meets it.

## A plural system whose members want different things — AGREED; options to come

A survey of the 148 character pages found Sohrel the only plural mind with an inner pull; Shepherd, Tobble and Asteria, Sen, and the Eden Warden are recorded as harmonious. Offered as an opening, Dermot answered, verbatim:

> Yes, agreed.

Then, verbatim:

> However I do not want plural systems to ever be seen as a mental disorder of any kind if is there are conflicting views within the system.

**Reading.** The opening is agreed, and which system, which member and what they want are canon and his: two or three options will be drafted (Fable) and put to him, grounded only in the record, and never against a page's *harmonious* line without saying so. The condition binds every draft and is now a rule in `CLAUDE.md`: disagreement between members is ordinary difference between real people sharing a life, never a symptom, breakdown, instability or case for treatment, and no clinical or Medical Corps reading of it stands in the record's voice. It extends `story-bible/ideas.md`'s standing line that plurality is never written as pathology.
