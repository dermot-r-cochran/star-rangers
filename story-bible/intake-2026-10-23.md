### Measure — what the record held this morning

- **No mind on a person**: *no human in the Concord carries an AI subsystem on or in the body* (`environmental-technology-design.md`, *AI: Present, Never Intrusive*). This settles the first and largest limit of a smart uniform before it is asked.
