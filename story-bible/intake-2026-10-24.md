## Citations of scientific and academic papers in the Codex

Dermot, verbatim, after the session's answer on wormholes and chronology protection:

> Allow citations of scientific or academic papers in codex

**The reading.** An extension of the 23 September direction (`intake-2026-09-23.md`), which let an in-world Codex source name real physicists and cite the historical literature. That direction was read narrowly, as physics. This one widens it to **scientific and academic papers in any field**: physics, mathematics, biology, the philosophy of science and the rest, by title, author, venue and year. The same day's Codex papers by Yusuf Adair, Ilsabet Corren, Oren Halsey and Ansel Thale already cite Popper, Lakatos, Dicke, Carter, Gödel, Turing and the physics literature in that way.

**What does not change.** The 23 September condition stands: nothing cited may raise a copyright or privacy issue, so a citation names a published work and quotes it briefly, if at all. Lore and the glossary still name nobody from our century, and the Journal still carries the real-world notes. Fiction keeps its own stricter rule (17 October): it may be cited only when it is in the public domain on both sides of the Atlantic, and only as fiction. A pre-adoption year is written CE first with UCSD beside it, under the dates rule. And a citation is a Codex author's account like everything else in the Codex: valid for its author, never a claim by the record.
