# Intake — 31 October 2026

## When Fable may extend canon lore

Dermot asked, verbatim: *"When should Fable be allowed or encouraged to extend
canon lore in a natural way?"*

The recommendation put to him (Opus, the same evening) was grounded in the
existing rules: the 25 August extension-or-clarification rule, the 20 September
revelation-as-revision rule, the shadow rule and its corollary of no repeatable
answer, and the daily Fable fill's practice of drafting from the worklists in
`open-questions.md`. It proposed:

- **Encourage it when all four hold:**
  1. *Derived, not invented:* it follows from what the record already states.
  2. *It adds without moving anything:* an extension or clarification only.
  3. *It closes no shadow and gives no remedy:* no new mechanism, no
     explained mystery, no repeatable answer.
  4. *A reader would recognise it as already true.*
- **The most natural moment** is just after a chapter lands, when the Archive
  records the revision.
- **Allowed as a draft with the choices named:** a new person, place or
  institution from a worklist line.
- **Never without him:**
  - a second instance of a singular exception;
  - an exception to the peaceable present;
  - anything ruled unestablished;
  - a people's designed body;
  - a tier gate;
  - the shut mysteries.

His answer, verbatim: *"Yes, record it"*. It is recorded as a bullet in
`CLAUDE.md`, *Content authoring conventions*, beside the 25 August rule, and in
the working folder's `fable-delegation` skill.
