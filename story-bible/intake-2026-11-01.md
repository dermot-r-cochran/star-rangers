# Intake — 1 November 2026

## Nothing preys on or infects across biospheres

Dermot, verbatim: *"No alien predators or viruses are biologically possible"*.

**Reading taken** (Opus, the same day, settled by the canon it has to sit
with, named here so he can overrule it): the sentence is about **crossing
biospheres**. Within one world the record already has predators and
disease: the grianmhór of Trígrian, Prismere's umbral ray, which hunts the
Prismeri, and the fever years of the Codex. A reading that removed predators
from every world but Earth would contradict published canon, so it is not
taken. *Alien* is his word; under the usage rule of 28 September 2026 the
record's own voice says *across biospheres*.

**The grounds, already in the record.** `src/lore/what-a-life-is-built-from.md`
and `src/lore/cross-biosphere-taxonomy.md` hold that no two biospheres share
a molecule of heredity, and Trígrian's page closes its seeding inquiry on
protein handedness. A virus multiplies only by having its host's machinery
read its record, and a predator hunts for a metabolism another world's
tissue cannot feed. The ruling is therefore a **clarification** under the
25 August rule: it says what the record's biology already entailed.

**Where it lands.**

- `src/lore/what-a-life-is-built-from.md` gains *Nothing Hunts or Infects
  Across Biospheres*, and its `plain:` line a sentence.
- `CLAUDE.md` carries the standing rule.
- `story-bible/ecology-design.md` no longer derives that Tír na nÓg's web is
  open *in principle to disease*.

**Tír na nÓg** (Opus's recommendation, applied): the rule holds there too.
The singular exception is compatibility of chemistry, food and soil; it was
never filed as a shared record, and `what-a-life-is-built-from.md` already
files its likeness as convergence, not a crossing. Nothing here adds or
removes an instance of the exception.

**What stays possible**, so the rule is not read wider than he gave it:
chemical injury between biospheres (a toxin, an irritant, an allergen;
Krenyi blood burns human skin), a large animal's defence, and predation and
disease inside one world. The meta-dimensional predators and the Unfounded
are not biological and are untouched.

**Checked against the corpus the same day:** no published chapter, lore,
glossary or codex page has a being of one biosphere prey on or infect one of
another. The Codex's *Krenyi Longevity Repair Agent* is consistent with it:
its agent is inert in human tissue.
