6. Body plan: **"Plan A, the keepers"**, then three study rounds, then

7. Names: the people **"The Convened"** (the Survey's English name, on the

**The world, proposed and accepted with the plan.** Aphel is on a strongly
eccentric orbit round a dim star: most of its year far out, dark and cold,
and a brief, fast close pass when the light is many times stronger (Kepler's
second law). What kills a large animal there is being caught awake and in
the open when the season closes. A person is convened because the season
holds work no single body can finish in time (deep shared dens, meltwater
channels, stores). Coordination is through the work itself (stigmergy), with
no telepathy and no new physics, so the person lives in the thing being
made; it is kept distinct from the Ovruhn chorus and the Told's tellings.

### 5. Rank up to Chief

Dermot, verbatim: *"In Star Rangers a made mind can hold rank up to Chief
level but not above."*

**Where it lands:** a new section, *A Made Mind's Ceiling*, in
`src/lore/star-rangers-command-hierarchy.md`, between *The Technical Track:
Chief as an Alternative Ceiling* and *Above Chief: Formal Mentoring*. An
**extension**. It names itself the one exception to the technical track's
*nothing prevents a decorated technical Chief from later crossing into the
command track*, and says plainly it is not a cap, freeze or quota of the kind
the ladder refuses, since the bound is on the command half, not on numbers.
It cross-links the citizenship line as the same shape.

**Checked against canon:** every character whose `species` is an AI, robot or
made mind (the Eden Warden, Jeeves, Lucene-9000, Lumbarian, both Reeves,
Rook-7, Xenon) holds a civilian post: a habitat collective, a household, a
bureau, a constabulary, a ship's complement. None holds a Corps rank, so none
is above Chief, and the section says the record holds no made mind at any
Corps rank. No conflict.

### 6. His example: a Chief Digital Officer
