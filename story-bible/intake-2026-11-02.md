## Tír na nÓg's fields and table are mixed

Dermot, verbatim: *"Tir na nOg mixed agriculture and ecosystem with some
planets and animals brought as seeds and frozen embryos from Earth, although
also mixed cuisine including some carefully cooked and prepared food from the
local planet's ecosystem."* (*planets* read as *plants*.)

**Where it lands:** a new *Fields and Table* section in
`src/lore/planets/tir-na-nog.md`, filed as an **extension**. It adds to the
page without changing anything already there.

**Checked against canon:**

- Seed and frozen embryos are permitted carriage: `what-the-record-refuses.md`
  counts frozen gametes and embryos as storage, not sleep.
- The Boirinn Uplands' *livestock descended from Currach Fleet stock*, and the
  page's *unmodified human agriculture*, already said Earth life is raised in
  the open there.
- The ruling of 1 November (`intake-2026-11-01.md`) holds: the two biospheres
  share food and soil and nothing infects across them. *Carefully cooked and
  prepared* sits alongside the page's *non-toxic biochemistry* in the way
  Earth's own foods that need preparation do.
- One clarification elsewhere: the line in `cross-biosphere-taxonomy.md`
  *nothing on Tír na nÓg was carried from Earth* now reads *none of its native
  life*, because the sentence was about native life and Earth stock is now
  stated as present.

**Left unstated, by choice:**

- which native foods need which preparation, and why;
- which Earth species were carried.

Both are marked absent on the page rather than invented. A chapter that needs
either supplies it.
