## Other ways on: reading options at the end of an episode

Dermot asked whether reading is linear or has several paths, and whether a reader could have *"two or three options for the next episode or lore entry to read"*. Told that the story cannot branch but the choice could be navigation, he answered, verbatim: *"As reading navigation options"*, then *"Linear within each episode is fine"*, then *"Each episode should also read as a self-contained short story or set of chapters"*.

So an episode's last chapter carries an *Other ways on* block beside the Next link, and no other chapter does. It offers an episode of another thread that opens at the same moment, and one Archive entry. Both are derived (`lib/reading-options.js`). This is not interactive fiction, and it does not touch *one canonical history, many witnesses*: the options choose what to read next, never what happens.

Against the *add no ordering metadata* consequence in `story-bible-summary.md`: none is added. The moment is read at build time from the existing free-text `timestamp`. A timestamp that names no UCSD year and season offers nothing, on purpose.

The third sentence is wider than the block. **An episode should read as a self-contained short story or set of chapters**, which is why the block always lands on an episode's opening and never its middle. Asked how far that reaches, he chose, verbatim: *"New episodes, reviews"*. It binds new episodes, and the weekly unit review reads each published episode against it and reports. Nothing published is rewritten to meet it, and no survey is run now.
