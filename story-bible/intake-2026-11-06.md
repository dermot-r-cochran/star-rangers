# Intake — 6 November 2026

## How far Tissadelle Shepherd's name travels

Dermot asked, verbatim: *"Tissadelle Shepherd as a famous Star Ranger or just a typical one that the story follows as an example of a Star Ranger career, or perhaps a bit of each?"*

The record already answered half of it. On 10 October he ruled that the story follows her as *"an exemplary Star Ranger career"* without making her the most important person (`intake-2026-10-10.md`; the Journal entry *No One Is Minor*). Her character page makes the career atypical in pace and breadth: several promotions a season, *"a pace well above the ordinary intake's"*, and rotations through almost every branch of every specialist corps. Nothing stated how famous she is in-world. Her temperament points against fame: she withholds the performance of confidence, and in public every member of her system answers to *Tissadelle* and a change of front is not announced. What travels is the *Star Rangers Anthem*, sung by her pre-enlistment band, Slipwave, and carried by cadets under the band's name.

Three options were put: (1) known inside the Corps, not famous outside it (recommended); (2) typical in reputation, her career extraordinary on paper and unremarked; (3) publicly famous. Dermot, verbatim: *"Option 1, record it"*.

**What this commits.** Inside the Corps her name is known: officers who read posting lists recognise it, and her crews' regard runs ahead of her reputation (the page's existing line). Outside the Corps she is not a public figure. The song she once sang is better known than she is. Recorded on `src/characters/tissadelle-shepherd.md` as a **clarification** under the canon rule: it states what the page and the record already implied and moves nothing. It is not a ranking of characters; the 10 October ruling stands.
