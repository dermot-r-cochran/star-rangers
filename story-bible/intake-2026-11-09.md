# Intake — 9 November 2026

## Focus lines and the showrunner skill

Dermot, verbatim, two asks: *"A show runner skill for each edition, thread or
season"* and *"A focus or core theme for each edition, thread, strand or
season"*.

**What was put to him.** One `showrunner` skill that takes the unit as its
argument, rather than one skill per unit. With nine editions, eight threads and
fifteen seasons, one per unit would have meant thirty-two copies of one method,
each holding facts that drift from the record. The unit's facts stay here, and
the skill reads them. Each unit's line was to be kept in one file,
`story-bible/focus.md`, and called a *focus line* because *theme* already names
the colour palette and the music. A draft line was offered for every unit.

**The Fable read.** Opus drafted the first lines, mostly from chapter titles and
the openings of the treatments. At Dermot's *"Fable read first"*, Fable read
each line against the chapters' front matter and `canon_facts`, the treatments
and the edition profiles. Of 47 lines it kept 8, revised 38 and struck 1. The
corrections that changed what a line says:

- **s09 and the `young-star-rangers` thread.** The draft said the job is what goes
  in the log when nothing happened. S09E01C01's `canon_facts` say the opposite:
  *Nothing to report* is not an accepted entry, because it states the officer's
  assessment and not what was observed.
- **s10.** *Certification as a form of trust* reversed the season. The countersign
  attests the record, not the ring, and is refused until the record says what
  the structure carries (S10E01C01, C04, C07).
- **s11.** *First contact* is a genre term the trope default refuses. It is also
  wrong on the facts, since the Told had been in the survey's log for two years
  (S11E02C02). The line now uses the treatment's own words: the up-people learn
  the manners by watching, and nobody crosses to teach them.
- **`founding-era`.** *Answerable afterwards* reversed S00. The Military Space
  Command refused liability (S00E02C01), and the Charter argument is about what
  the corps must swear never to become (S00E03C01).
- **The s06–07 interior strand.** *Memory out of order that is still true* was
  wrong. The interior fails the way a real memory never does (S06E01C03).
- **No basis in the record.** These phrases had nothing behind them: the
  fellowship's *conscience*, the pets edition's *kind about it* and Five Islands'
  *what each costs its keeper*. Each was replaced with framing from the edition
  profile or from the treatment.
- **Struck.** The Castle's third record, the Photographic Beings, was listed as a
  strand. It has no point-of-view cast and no chapters, and whether a third
  strand is added is still open in the treatment.

**Seven decisions, each put with a recommendation; the recommendation landed:**
1. s14's line may open with the Told's third rule verbatim, because the file is
   unpublished.
2. s06 and s07 keep separate season lines and share one strand pair.
3. The third record gets a note under the strands, not a strand line.
4. Editions with one thread of their own get reader-side lines from their
   profiles, not a pointer to the thread's line.
5. Church Space has one line, on the thread, and the edition points to it.
6. The s02 line covers the season's Undercover Pets chapters, and Episodes 1 and
   2 answer to the Orbital Five-O line. This is noted in the file.
7. The `tissadelle-arc` line points to *the shape of the saga* and never quotes
   it, because its last four beats pre-tell the terminus.

**Confirmed, in his words:** *"Approve and build"*.

**What landed.**
- `story-bible/focus.md`.
- A pointer in strand rule 3 of `story-bible-summary.md`.
- A pointer in `edition-reader-profiles.md`.
- The `showrunner` skill, which lives outside this repository with his other
  skills.

Lines change only at his word. A new unit gets its line proposed when it is
registered or when its first chapter is drafted.

## Young Star Rangers: a month, not a year

This came from the first `showrunner` run, on the `young-star-rangers` thread. The
thread is complete under the 18 October ruling. Season 9 is complete in itself,
ruled 3 October. Both hold the thread's focus line. The one gap was copy that
promised more than the page carries. Three places said *the year*: the thread's
registry description, the edition's hero line and the README row. Season 9 covers
the first thirty days of Varn's posting. Two ways were put to him: fix the copy
(recommended), or reopen the thread for the rest of the year, which would have
needed a treatment and would have undone 18 October.

Dermot, verbatim: *"Fix the copy"*.

The three places now say *the first month* (*the month* in the hero line). This
is framing only, and no canon moves. The reader profile's **Wants** keeps *a
year*, because it describes what the reader comes for, not what the page
promises.

## Young Star Rangers: a palette of its own, and no alias yet

Dermot asked for both, verbatim: *"Colour palette and alias domain or subdomain
for Young Star Rangers"*. Both had been open since September.

**The palette.** Until now the edition borrowed starquest's neon on near-black.
That palette is the procedural's, and the reader here is inside the Corps, whose
marks are muted, matte and industrial. A palette called `watch` was put to him
beside the borrowed one:
- a graphite page;
- links in slate blue, the canon general-operations tab colour, which is Zoe
  Smith's track;
- canon facts in the boundary-safety amber, for the fold-approach watch;
- the five point-of-view colours toned down to match.

Every pair clears WCAG AA. The lowest is 5.8:1, on `--color-surface-2`.

**The alias.** The 7 September ruling held an alias host until the working name
settles. An alias would only redirect to `young.fianilchruinne.com`. The
recommendation was none for now. If he wants one later, the cheapest is
`young.star-rangers.space` as a redirect.

Dermot, verbatim: *"Palette, no alias"*.

`watch` is a new theme in `scripts/generate-themes.js`, generated to
`src/css/theme-watch.css`. The `young-star-rangers` edition now uses it. The
edition profile, the open question and `sample-deploy.conf` are updated. The
alias stays held under the 7 September ruling.
