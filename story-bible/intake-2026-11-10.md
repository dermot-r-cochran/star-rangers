## Pages lock once readers arrive

Dermot, verbatim: *"Once I get readers I will not have that freedom, the current
work is development, but when readers appear then those pages get locked down.
Either the page analytics or comments should indicate which pages get locked."*

**What was found before the choices were put.** Neither comment board has a
reader comment. The only discussions are his two welcome posts, each with no
comments, so no page is locked. The site records no page analytics of any kind,
and the only per-page reader data is Google Search Console, which only he can
read, from his desktop.

**His picks, all as recommended:**

1. **The signal: comments automatically, views by hand.** A page locks when
   someone other than him comments, replies or reacts on its thread. He can
   also lock a page from Search Console clicks, as a desktop step. Nothing new
   is added to the site. The other choices were comments only, and adding a
   cookieless page counter.
2. **What locked means: assertions frozen.** Allowed edits are a typo, a broken
   link, image bookkeeping, or a plain error of arithmetic, chronology or
   terminology, each named and stated in the changelog. A clash with a locked
   page is settled on the unlocked side or explained inside the story. The
   other choices were frozen entirely, and frozen with extension still allowed.
3. **Tracking: a registry plus a CI check.** `lib/locked-pages.json` is filled by
   `scripts/lock-pages.js` from the comment boards, and by hand for views. CI
   fails a pull request that changes a locked page without a `Locked-Page:`
   trailer naming the page and an allowed reason. It was built the same day,
   while the list is empty. The other choices were a registry only, and the
   rule only until the first reader.

**How it sits with the earlier-approval rule.** That rule, from earlier the same
day, reaches only unlocked pages. Until a page locks, a clash is a dilemma he
may settle either way. Once it locks, the locked side holds.
