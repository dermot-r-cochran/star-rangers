# Intake — 14 November 2026

## The Common Manifold lies above the bulk

Dermot, verbatim: *"My definition of the Common Manifold implies a higher layer
above the Cosmic Bulk of M-Theory Cosmology and is a kind of superset. There
could still be a very large number of membrane-universes inside the same local
bulk, if that makes sense."* Then: *"Does the no multiverse rule refer
specifically to the Quantum Multiverse idea?"*, and later, *"A reader familiar
with physics might ask about this."*

**The reading put to him (Opus), and his answer: *"Yes, both"*** (the two-scale
reading, and a section for the physics-literate reader).

1. **"No multiverse" is narrower than its two words.** What the record refuses
   is Everett's many worlds and branching or alternate timelines
   (`the-history-that-happens`, `what-the-record-refuses`, the July rejection of
   standing alternate-timeline overlays). The Grand Ensemble of independently
   authored universes is canon, Tegmark's Level II and never his Level III
   (`the-third-sum-rule`). Many membranes in one bulk is a multiverse in the
   ordinary sense and a page may say so. It refines the rule and breaks
   nothing.
2. **Two scales of separation.** Between neighbours in one local bulk, the
   Interval runs along the bulk's own direction, which the manifold contains
   and neither universe does. Gravity crosses it through the bulk, as in
   Hořava and Witten's picture (matter on the branes, gravity in the bulk).
   Between bulks, the separation runs through the manifold's higher
   dimensions, and no crossing at that scale is confirmed.
3. **The floor is the bulk's.** The home floor of eleven is the home bulk's
   count, shared by every membrane in it. One of the seven compact dimensions
   is the bulk's own direction; a membrane has extent in the other ten (in
   Hořava–Witten each boundary brane is ten-dimensional and the eleventh is the
   interval between them). A different floor means a different bulk. A
   Worldwright sets a bulk's floor and authors its membranes into it.
4. **What crosses.** The bulk carries gravity between neighbours in it; the
   12–15 band spreads the same strain beyond the bulk and keeps a narrowing
   local; the 16–19 and 20+ bands keep timing and templates. Electromagnetics
   and Nature are confined to their membrane. The three bands are unchanged.

**Answers kept for the physics-literate reader** (on the lore page, *For a
Reader Who Knows the Physics*): nothing lies above the eleven dimensions in
M-theory, and F-theory's "twelve" is a bookkeeping device for the varying
string coupling; brane-worlds are untested and no extra dimension has been
found (colliders; gravity's ordinary law holds down to a few tens of
micrometres); many branes in one bulk is allowed, not predicted; a split of
eleven other than 3+7+1 is a standard M-theory solution, while a total other
than eleven, or more than one time dimension, is the record's own.
