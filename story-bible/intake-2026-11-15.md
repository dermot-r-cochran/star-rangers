# Intake — 15 November 2026

## Generated children on the site are shown grown

Dermot, verbatim, after looking at his Gallery: *"Looking at my gallery gave me
a question about AI generated images of children on the star rangers site and
if they should either removed or made child reader friendly in some way."*
Then, while the audit ran: *"All characters were children or young once so an
adult portrait is fine even if the character appeared in a chapter as a
child."*

**The audit (Opus, read from `main` at 5075458e).** Four filed portraits showed
a child, all text-to-image from Gemini 3.1 Flash Image, finished at strength 1:
`lev.jpg` (about eight, Undercover Pets, on the pets Plates page),
`bram-tollick.jpg` (fourteen), `wren-pellam.jpg` (fifteen) and
`oriel-fenwick.jpg` (sixteen, on the fellowship and starquest Plates pages),
the last three tagged for the young-adult rung. A fifth, `imogen-petrakis.jpg`,
is an adult portrait with a small child walking past in the background. All
five cleared the site's modesty bar and the 28 July guardrails: dressed to the
neck and wrist, at work, attention off the camera, nothing that invites the
viewer, nobody in danger. Not on the site but in the public repository: two
negative-example thumbnails (`lev-2026-11-12.jpg`, `una-ni-dhalaigh-2026-11-10.jpg`),
the face crop `reference-art/lev-face-2026-10-13.jpg`, and two unmade film
prompts with a child in them (*Four Seconds*, *Before the Close*).
tissadelle-inner-world publishes nothing.

**The options put to him:** A, keep the four and write a rule for how children
are shown; B, remake the four as adult portraits of the same characters; C,
withdraw the four and leave the pages bare. Recommended B, on his own message,
because every page keeps its picture and the site stops carrying photographic
faces of children who do not exist. **His answer: *"B: adult portraits"*.**

**What follows.**

1. The four portraits are remade as the character grown, at thirty to
   thirty-five, each `image_alt` saying *grown*. The pages keep the ages the
   record gives; a portrait is illustrative and not canon (13 October 2026), and
   an adult portrait asserts only that the character grew up. The child frames
   leave `src/` and stay in git history; they are withdrawn, not failed, so they
   are not negative examples.
2. The rule for every later image: **no generated or composited picture on the
   site shows a child as its subject.** A character the record holds as a child
   is pictured grown, or the page stands without a portrait. A child who is
   incidental to a frame (a small figure in a crowd, at a distance, not the
   subject) is allowed under the modesty bar, so `imogen-petrakis.jpg` stays.
   An unmade film prompt with a child as its subject is revised to the rule
   before it is generated.
3. Not taken: changing the children's tier, or any reasoning that the rule
   protects a reader. The tiers are still reading level and depth, not content
   ratings; this is a choice about what the site depicts, the same kind of
   choice as the modesty bar.
