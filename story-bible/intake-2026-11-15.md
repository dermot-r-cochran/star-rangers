a question about AI generated images of children on the star rangers site and

a child, all text-to-image from Gemini 3.1 Flash Image, finished at strength 1:
`lev.jpg` (about eight, Undercover Pets, on the pets Plates page),
`bram-tollick.jpg` (fourteen), `wren-pellam.jpg` (fifteen) and
`oriel-fenwick.jpg` (sixteen, on the fellowship and starquest Plates pages),
the last three tagged for the young-adult rung. A fifth, `imogen-petrakis.jpg`,
is an adult portrait with a small child walking past in the background. All
five cleared the site's modesty bar and the 28 July guardrails: dressed to the

the face crop `reference-art/lev-face-2026-10-13.jpg`, and two unmade film
prompts with a child in them (*Four Seconds*, *Before the Close*).
tissadelle-inner-world publishes nothing.

**The options put to him:** A, keep the four and write a rule for how children
are shown; B, remake the four as adult portraits of the same characters; C,
withdraw the four and leave the pages bare. Recommended B, on his own message,
because every page keeps its picture and the site stops carrying photographic
faces of children who do not exist. **His answer: *"B: adult portraits"*.**

**What follows.**

1. The four portraits are remade as the character grown, at thirty to
   thirty-five, each `image_alt` saying *grown*. The pages keep the ages the
   record gives; a portrait is illustrative and not canon (13 October 2026), and
   an adult portrait asserts only that the character grew up. The child frames
   leave `src/` and stay in git history; they are withdrawn, not failed, so they
   are not negative examples.
2. The rule for every later image: **no generated or composited picture on the
   site shows a child as its subject.** A character the record holds as a child
   is pictured grown, or the page stands without a portrait. A child who is
   incidental to a frame (a small figure in a crowd, at a distance, not the
   subject) is allowed under the modesty bar, so `imogen-petrakis.jpg` stays.
   An unmade film prompt with a child as its subject is revised to the rule
   before it is generated.
3. Not taken: changing the children's tier, or any reasoning that the rule
   protects a reader. The tiers are still reading level and depth, not content
   ratings; this is a choice about what the site depicts, the same kind of
   choice as the modesty bar.

   Concordant law, and their limits are of permission, not power

3. **The limit of faster-than-light communication.** There is no FTL

   `civilisation-comparison.md`, *Natural Limits of Reach*). A mind spread over

**How "prevented, by design" is read against the page that already exists.**

`cosmic-cascade.md` already says *"No made mind is a machine-god"*, which is

## What Muffin feeds on: energy, not food

Dermot, verbatim, after the four rulings of the afternoon (Muffin's body left
unrecorded, **A**): *"You are right. Muffin feeds more on electromagnetic
energy and other forces, including brain energy."*

**Recorded, not yet applied (Opus).** This opens the question the 5 August
2026 ruling kept shut, and it is his to open. It moves past **A** without
landing on **B** or **C**: what sustains Muffin is not food at all, so the
saucer question stops mattering. No page changes in this record. Three seams
are left for a Fable read, each with the line that meets it:

1. *"Nothing here eats, and nothing here reads a mind"*
   (`src/lore/predatory-entities.md`). Feeding on energy is not eating, but
   *brain energy* comes close to reading a mind. Whether the two are the same
   act is the first thing to settle.
2. The small yes. The record says the pattern *"exploits the disturbance
   produced by"* a micro-consent collapse. One reading is that the brain energy
   is that disturbance: the small yes gives off something Muffin draws on.
   Thrum and the collies give nothing, which fits that reading.
3. Plain physics. Under *maths valid and plain*, *brain energy* is real
   electrical and chemical activity in a nervous system, and *other forces*
   can only be forces the record already allows. No new force is coined, and
   no telepathy follows from it.

**His answer, the same afternoon:** *"Muffin does not read or even perceive
minds."* That settles seam 1. *"Nothing here reads a mind"* stands, and it now
goes further: Muffin does not perceive a mind either. Whatever Muffin draws from
a nervous system, it draws without knowing there is a mind there. Seam 2 has to be read the same way:
if the small yes is the source, Muffin takes up its by-product and never takes
in the yes as a yes.

The hub-fly settlement stands: a fly still gives Muffin nothing. The same
read should say whether the settlement's point 3 (no substitute, no bait, no
remedy) also covers a battery or a field, which a feeder on energy would
otherwise invite.

**The Fable read (15 November 2026).** Done from the record:
`src/characters/muffin.md`, `src/lore/predatory-entities.md`,
`src/lore/meta-dimensional-beings.md`,
`src/lore/mathematics-of-the-etheric-layer.md`,
`src/lore/what-a-mind-is-carried-by.md`, the hub-fly settlement in
`open-questions.md`, and the corollary of 30 October (*nothing acquires a
repeatable answer*). Nothing in `src/` changes. Each point is a reading,
settled by Fable and his to overrule. The figures are ordinary physiology and
physics, rounded to one significant figure.

1. **Energy, not matter, and not information.** His two sentences together say
   what is taken up and what is not. Muffin takes no substance from anyone, so
   the lore page's *"Nothing here eats"* stands. It takes no information about
   a mind either: *"nothing here reads a mind"* stands, and his answer goes
   further, since it does not perceive one. Physics has no difficulty with a
   thing that absorbs energy from a source and learns nothing about the source.
   A stone on a windowsill is warmed by a lamp and holds no picture of the
   lamp. So *"draws from a nervous system without knowing there is a mind
   there"* is an ordinary statement and not a paradox.
2. **No new force.** *Electromagnetic energy*, in the textbook sense, already
   covers nearly everything a habitat deck offers a small body: the heat that
   warm things radiate (infrared is light of a longer wavelength), visible
   light, and the electric and magnetic fields of powered equipment and of
   living bodies. Heat passed by touch or by moving air is the same
   interaction, between molecules. *Other forces* is read as the other forms
   of energy physics already has, and as nothing else: gravity and the two
   nuclear forces give a small body on a deck nothing it could draw on. The
   one thing beyond these that the record licenses is the Etheric coupling,
   which is a constraint and not a force, and which the lore page already
   names (point 4). No psychic energy, no life force and no quantum effect is
   implied: `what-a-mind-is-carried-by.md` says *"Nothing in the record has
   ever required a quantum coherence to carry a mind."*
3. ***Brain energy*, in numbers.** A resting adult gives off about 100 watts,
   nearly all of it as heat, and the brain accounts for about 20 of them. The
   brain's electrical activity is real and is very weak outside the head:
   tens of millionths of a volt at the scalp, and a magnetic field of about a
   million-millionth of a tesla just outside the skull. The energy held in
   that field, in a litre of space beside the head, is about 4 × 10⁻²² joule,
   which is less than the heat energy of one molecule of the air in that
   litre. So no field of the brain carries usable power to anything outside
   the body. A single decision changes the brain's power draw by less than
   one part in a hundred. One small yes, lasting about a second, therefore
   involves at most about 0.2 joule beyond what the brain was spending anyway:
   the work of lifting 20 grams one metre.
4. **How the small yes is the source.** The lore page is unchanged: a
   micro-consent collapse produces a disturbance in the Conceptual↔Etheric
   band, and *feeds on* reads *exploits the disturbance produced by*. His
   statement adds what is gained when the pattern does so: energy. The two fit
   as cause and yield. The disturbance needs a mind to make it: away from a
   boundary, the coupling terms are zero in *"unminded apparatus"*
   (`mathematics-of-the-etheric-layer.md`), so a vent or a power cell makes
   none. And this disturbance is made only by a small yes, so Thrum and a
   working collie give the pattern nothing. The energy is the ordinary
   electrical and chemical activity of the nervous system in which the refusal
   was given up.
   The channel between the two is a term of the kind that page allows:
   *"a further weak coupling acting where the system is, between things that
   are together and never a link between things apart."* Such a term can pass
   a little energy, as any weak coupling can, and cannot make any (*Why the
   Terms Add Nothing*). So the books balance. Whatever Muffin gains from one
   small yes, that person's nervous system has spent: at most the fraction of
   a joule in point 3, which would otherwise have left the person as heat.
   That is the size of *"Nobody on Deck 7 was harmed."* The reading states the
   form and no value. The record has measured no coefficient of any such
   term, and no page says any of this.
5. **Without perceiving a mind.** The lore page says a cute predator works by
   *"instinctive pattern-matching against vulnerability signals"*. A signal is
   something a body does: a slowed step, a turned head, a hand already half
   out. Muffin's page describes what it learns in those terms and no others.
   Barsik is *"a door that has never once opened"*, and the rerouted vent is
   an outlet it found on the fourth try. To Muffin a person is a door that
   opens or does not. It takes up the by-product of the yes and never takes in
   the yes as a yes. This holds on both readings of what it is. On the filing,
   an Unfounded pattern does not *"register anyone"*
   (`what-a-mind-is-carried-by.md`). On Barsik's reading, an animal of a cat's
   order follows warmth and habit with no idea of a mind. So his answer moves
   neither reading, and the ground the 5 August ruling reserves stays shut.
6. **What the arithmetic bounds, and what stays unrecorded.** A deck that gave
   a thousand small yeses a day, each at the upper figure, would supply about
   200 joules a day, an average of about two thousandths of a watt. A cat of
   four kilograms runs on about 10 watts. So *brain energy* could not keep a
   warm animal's body going: it falls short by a factor of several thousand.
   His sentence does not ask it to. It names electromagnetic energy and other
   forces first, and brain energy as included among them, and the page already
   shows the specimen keeping to the galley's warm-air outlet, following the
   outlet when it was rerouted, and spending eleven days in the laundry. What
   its presence costs to keep up, and how much of that any source supplies, is
   a fact about its body, which he left unrecorded the same afternoon (**A**).
   So the read fixes no figure for Muffin, and no chapter, page or instrument
   gives one. One further limit is physics and not a ruling. Warmth alone
   feeds nothing; only a difference of temperature can be drawn on, and
   between skin at 34 °C and air at 21 °C that is at most about four parts in
   a hundred of the heat that crosses.
7. **The settlement's point 3 covers a battery and a field.** Settled from
   the record, with nothing left to put to him. The corollary of 30 October is
   general (`CLAUDE.md`): nothing may *"acquire a counter-move a competent
   officer can run twice and expect the same result"*, *"instruments register
   without conferring leverage"*, and *"The entry it must never gain is a
   remedy."* Point 3 applied that to a diet. A power cell, a heater, a lamp, a
   field coil or a screened room is the same move with a different supply. So
   none of them ever becomes a way to feed the specimen in place of the deck,
   to sate it, lure it, hold it, starve it or drive it off, and no meter that
   reads what it takes becomes a way to manage it. The physics agrees, on
   three counts already on a page. The specimen spent nine months at a
   warm-air outlet on a powered deck, in more ambient energy than any cell
   would add, and went on working the deck, so ambient energy has never stood
   in for the small yes. A cell and a coil make no small yes, so by point 4
   they make nothing the pattern exploits. And a conducting screen stops
   electric fields and radio waves, while the coupling in point 4 is not an
   electromagnetic field, so a screen is no wall to it. The one counter the
   record holds is unchanged: the refusal, which *"works person by person, as
   it always has"*. *Starved, not destroyed* names what a deck's refusals do
   over time, never a device. **The drafting rule:** the specimen may be found
   in warm or powered places, as a cat is, and the behaviour is logged both
   ways, as Barsik logs the rest. No scene shows a source of energy working on
   it as bait, substitute, barrier or cure, and none shows one failing in a
   way that teaches an officer what would work.
8. **Read narrowly, and kept off the pages.** His answer names Muffin. It is
   not extended here to every cute predator, or to the Obligers and Gilded
   Saints, whose page says only that none reads a mind. No page changes:
   nothing published contradicts either statement, and nobody in the record
   knows either one, so under the Archive rule of 20 September both stay here
   until a chapter makes them known. The first chapter likely to need them is
   one set in the family's quarters, where food is put down for a kept animal.

**What is real.** Nothing in this list is invented.

- *Rings and charge.* A giant planet's rings are ice and rock in pieces from
  dust to boulders, in a sheet about ten metres thick and, at Saturn, about
  sixty thousand kilometres from inner edge to outer. Sunlight knocks
  electrons off the grains, and the thin gas of charged particles around the
  planet puts electrons on them, so ring dust carries electric charge. A grain
  a thousandth of a millimetre across, charged to a few volts, holds about two
  thousand electron charges.
- *A field that sweeps the ring.* The planet's magnetic field turns with the
  planet, about once in ten and a half hours at Saturn. Ring particles orbit
  in about six hours at the inner edge and fourteen at the outer. So at every
  distance but one, the field sweeps through the ring or the ring through the
  field, and a charge moving through a magnetic field is pushed sideways. That
  relative motion is a generator. It is how Jupiter drives a current of
  millions of amperes through its moon Io and back along the field to its own
  upper atmosphere.
- *Charged dust organises itself.* A gas of charged particles with charged
  dust in it is called a dusty plasma. In laboratories, since 1994, the dust
  has been seen to settle into regular lattices, to carry slow waves of its
  own, and to form clumps, voids and vortices, with nobody arranging it.
- *Spokes.* Since 1980, spacecraft have photographed radial streaks across
  Saturn's rings, thousands of kilometres long, which appear within minutes
  and fade over hours. They are fine dust lifted off the ring by electric
  forces. They gather where the ring's orbit keeps pace with the planet's
  field, and they come and go with the planet's seasons.
- *Natural radio that sounds like music.* Giant planets are strong natural
  sources of radio. Some natural radio waves, fed to a loudspeaker, are
  whistles falling in pitch, and others a chorus like birdsong. Nobody is
  sending them.

**What *interact like neurons* asks of that physics.** *Electromagnetic
particles* is read as electrically charged particles, the ring's dust and the
plasma around it, since physics has no other kind that could make a ring.
Stripped of biology, a neuron does five things. A patch of charged ring can do
the first four by the physics above.

1. *It gathers input until a threshold is reached.* A patch charges slowly,
   from sunlight and from the plasma.
2. *It then fires all at once.* When the electric force on the surface dust
   exceeds the forces holding the dust down, the dust lifts together. This is
   the event that makes a spoke, and it has a threshold.
3. *The pulse travels to other units.* Lifted charge in motion is a current.
   The disturbance runs along the planet's field lines to the upper atmosphere
   at both ends, where the gas conducts, and comes back along other field lines
   to other parts of the ring. The field lines and the conducting layer are
   the wiring.
4. *It must recover before it can fire again.* The patch has to charge again,
   which takes minutes to hours.
5. *Its links to other units strengthen or weaken with use.* Each firing moves
   dust, and where the dust lies decides where charge gathers next and how
   easily a patch fires. A ring therefore keeps a trace of its own activity in
   its arrangement, as a river keeps its floods in its bed.

**The one step past what has been observed** is the fifth, taken far enough:
that the traces add up to pathways which are specific, which last, and which
change in a way that lets the whole learn. No ring known to astronomy does
this. No law of physics forbids it, and it needs no new force, no quantum
effect and nothing faster than light. That is the standing life itself had
before any was found: permitted, and not predicted. The record's rule covers
it (`what-a-mind-is-carried-by.md`): *"No template at any tier specifies a
shape for the thing carrying a mind"*, and what carries one is *"local
contingency, found in whatever a world had to hand"*.

**Evolved, not engineered.** His word is taken in the record's sense: *"An
evolved system has no author and no point of design"* (the Made Minds page).
Nobody built the ring and nobody wired it. What plain physics offers in place
of a builder is persistence. A pattern of charging and firing that keeps its
dust in the ring lasts, and one that throws its dust away is gone. Over a
ring's age of a hundred million years or more, with a patch cycling about
once an hour, each patch has been through about 10¹² cycles. Whether
persistence of that kind is enough to make somebody is the question the record
keeps open for every carrier (*"What the record does not say is why
organisation of that kind is also somebody"*). This note does not answer it.
His ruling is that here it did.

**Its size and its pace, by arithmetic.**

- *Size.* Saturn's main rings cover about 4 × 10¹⁰ square kilometres. Divided
  into patches one kilometre across, that is about forty thousand million
  patches. A human brain has about eighty-six thousand million neurons. So a
  ring of that size is neither small nor large for the work.
- *Pace.* A neuron fires and recovers in about a thousandth of a second. A
  patch of ring recovers in minutes to hours, which is sixty thousand to four
  million times slower. If the comparison holds at all, what a brain does in
  one second takes such a ring between most of a day and six weeks. The
  signals are not the limit: a disturbance carried by the field crosses the
  ring in seconds to a few minutes, and radio crosses it in about a second.
- *So it is vast, slow and patient, and it is no superintelligence.* This is
  the 29 October point (`intake-2026-10-29.md`): *"Its medium of thought
  shapes it, not raw capacity."*
- *Power.* It runs on the planet's spin, through the field, and on sunlight.
  Neither is scarce.
- *Lifetime.* Rings are not permanent. Saturn's are losing material into the
  planet along the field lines and may be gone in a few hundred million years.
  A ring mind is mortal, on a clock that can be read.
