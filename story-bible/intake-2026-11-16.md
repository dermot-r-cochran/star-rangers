**Drafted** as S03E03C02 *On Assumption*, late autumn 2829 UCSD, two scenes, two heads. Scene 1, the watch desk: Emeka Lindahl, the post's Chief Ranger, who has held it thirty-three days; she reads the whole log standing and writes *Post assumed*; she does not sign *received as stated* under his initial on the state sheet; he gives her the line office's side once; she asks which line of his bill is the Starwarden's and takes it. Then her block: why the line in the log is unconditional and the sheet is not hers to sign, the sixty-six initials in one hand, and Tobble's count of the ring and of the nothing under her name. Scene 2: her first middle watch, a hundred and sixty agreements, her initial under four hours; then Lindahl's morning, the acknowledgement back in eighteen hours with the Line Captain's initial and no query.

**Arithmetic checked.** Ninety seconds into four hours is 160. Two watches a day for thirty-three days is 66. Nine hours up the relay and nine back is eighteen; a sheet sent at half past two returns at half past eight, in the first hour of a forenoon watch that opens at eight. Thirty-three middle watches in the Chief's hand is the one before hers and thirty-two before that.

**What it commits the story to.** Three canon facts: the assumption by one log entry after thirty-three days held by Chief Ranger Emeka Lindahl, nine years the post's Chief; the state sheet unsigned by her, sent with his initial alone and acknowledged without query; the six-watch bill, the middle watch the Starwarden's, stood by Lindahl through the gap and taken by her from the first night, with the bill and the office left as they were. The post stays unnamed. Nothing is said of where she came from. The pairing order and Wender's name do not appear.
