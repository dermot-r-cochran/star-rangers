## Boundary effects since the 21st century, and whether flexures change — RULED by Dermot the same day

**His words, verbatim.** *"Either boundary effects have increased since the 21st century or the rate of detection and understanding has improved"*, then, asked to choose, the recommended reading (*A+B, C open*). Then *"Do membrane flexures change slightly over time?"*, and, asked to choose, *"Address fixed, depth varies"*.

**The reading ruled (A and B, C open).** The record already made boundary zones standing creases set when a membrane formed, never made since and never moved (`src/lore/concordant-membranes.md`, *Where a flexure comes from*), and ruled out fold transit as a cause. So:
- **A, detection and understanding.** Earlier centuries had no category for a boundary effect and filed what they met as fault, failed calibration or an uneasy place's reputation. The record's instruments, monitoring posts, sensor robots and Smart Pets notice what was always there.
- **B, exposure.** Far more people now live and work within reach of a zone.
- **C, a real change in the rate,** is neither asserted nor denied: no earlier century measured, so the Archive has no baseline. Kept open on the shadow-shaped rule; it must never acquire an answer by measurement in the record's present.

**Flexures (address fixed, depth varies).** A flexure's address never changes (as published). How far it narrows the Interval may vary a little and slowly; the record's monitoring is too short to say how much or whether the variation is real. This is an extension, not a mechanism: it names no cause beyond the sheet settling under the strain the extended lattice carries (already published), gives no period, and offers no prediction. It is the only physical room C has, and it is enough to keep C honestly open.

**Applied.** `src/glossary/boundary-zone.md`: a closing paragraph in the Archive's voice. `src/lore/concordant-membranes.md`: a short paragraph after *Where a flexure comes from*. Both are extensions under the canon rule (they add without altering what stood). Neither page is locked.

**Never without him.** Any measurement in the record that settles C, any period or cycle in a flexure's depth, any new flexure or a moved one.
