## Disavowers — a new mode of the Unfounded, a direction given in chat — DRAFTED, name and feeding condition settled

**His words, verbatim.** *"A type of unfounded; metaphorically like a polite charming scorpion that says don't be at me and don't be shy; I won't bite or sting you - yet it does and acts surprised at how people react."*

**Reading, agreed in chat.** The pattern is specious reassurance followed by disingenuous surprise: a plausible, charming reassurance that the thing is harmless; the hurt; then surprise at the reaction, which makes the reaction the problem, so that the one who was hurt doubts the hurt, apologises and comes back.

**Read against what stands.** The Unfounded page holds that the list of modes is open, that a name records how a counterfeit runs, and that *the Unfounded do not feign, because feigning implies an alternative*. So the surprise is written as the shape of innocent surprise with nobody in it, never as a lie, a choice or a motive, and the scorpion stays his figure of speech: the mode is given no animal shape, since animal shapes are the Cute Predators'. It is told apart from their harmlessness framing by sapience and by its second half, the disavowal after the hurt.

**What the draft commits the story to.** One bullet in the Class II list of `src/lore/meta-dimensional-beings.md` and the name in that page's note on names; a section in the canonical guide after Ghost-Witches; one worn shape on the Unfounded page (*an innocent taken aback at the hurt*). **Name settled, 19 November 2026.** His answer: *"Yes, Disavowers"*, over the runner-up *Assurers*.
