## The Long White Trail joins the record — RULED the same day

**His words, verbatim.** *"If the trailer stands on its own merits, then consider if the existing canon or prose can be easily extended or expanded to match."* Put two choices, he chose **"Take the ring"** and, for the narrator, **"A future thread"**. **What was already true.** The name Lúnasa (13 November); the level rail along a causeway (`src/lore/ascent-javelins.md`, *The Rail*); the rank ladder, a Deputy rising to Chief and a Starwarden commanding a station; recalibration that holds about two days; and *ten years after the Last Stand*, which is 2841.

**What the record now holds (extensions, applied in this change).**
- **The ring.** Earth's ring of stations at geostationary height, the settled look of 30 October: thousands of stations in one circle above the equator, seen from Ireland as a beaded arc low in the southern sky that does not move. Lúnasa is one station in it. Its stations are installations below the habitat threshold, each run by its operator; a shared slot-and-traffic office allots positions and settles nothing else. The ring is not a polity and not the Compact's: Orbital Five-O's authority runs to the Compact's five member habitats and no further, so if a member habitat sits in the ring, that habitat alone is Five-O's. The record does not say whether any does. His remark the same day: *"It kind of explains why Rangers are stationed and work in The Ring"*: below the threshold a station's space is the Rangers' proper domain, so the ring is the largest stretch of near-Earth orbit theirs to work in without invitation; the Lúnasa page says so. The ring touches no ground, so the ruling against orbital towers is not engaged.
- **Lúnasa's wall.** A repeater of Threshold's station time hangs beside Lúnasa's own standard, so the same forty seconds can be read at the far end. It is the same comparison, not a second one. Lúnasa has a docking spine.
- **Carrigbawn and *Brightwater*.** Carrigbawn is a regional rail site on an Irish bay; *Brightwater* is one of the javelins thrown from its rail.
- **The violet light.** Lúnasa's standard is an optical clock of ytterbium atoms. The light that cools them is violet (about 399 nm), so the atoms glow faintly violet behind the chamber's port while it loads. That is the one light in the room, and it is lawful physics, not a departure.

**Maeve: a future thread, reserved.** The film's narrator joins as the new Deputy in 2841 and remembers from her terrace in 2894, with Starwarden Ciarán Doyle commanding. She is reserved for a later thread set after the record's present. Nothing about her is written into `src/` until a chapter is, and she has no character page; the film's other particulars (the wristband's ring time, her fifty years) wait with her. Her *layers* and *seam* are her own figures of speech; the record's term stays *membranes*.

**Still a departure.** The film's own dates are after the record's present; the clocks drawn as glowing columns in the room's open air (the record's light is behind the port).
