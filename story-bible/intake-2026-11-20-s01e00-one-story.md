## The three stories

A reader starting at C00 meets these three:

1. **The cat who left Dún Rí** (C00–C03, 2822). Four chapters, one cast, one quest. It ends on Nessa's *go* and Aldera's first field log. The road picks up in Episode 2, four years and nine chapters later.
2. **The children from the wreck** (C04, 2788). One chapter, and a complete found-family story: *"At the start, a family is a small word said on purpose. Everything after it is the keeping."*
3. **Readings at the edge** (C05–C13, 2809 to 2826). Nine chapters, nine casts and eight places: a membrane, a dock band, a post with two clocks, an assembly, Deadwater twice, a lighthouse, a goat holding, a hill gate. From inside, this part reads as an anthology in its own right.

Across the fourteen chapters there are 24 POVs. Only two characters are POVs in more than one part: Aldera and Elvira stay inside part 1, and Karla Wender is a child in C04 and a Senior in C09.

## What joins them in the record

- **Time: weak.** Everything happens before Episode 1 or beside it. This is the only join the index names (*"at different distances"*). The last five chapters do close in on autumn 2826 (C08 early summer; C12 spring to summer; C10 late summer; C11 and C13 *"the same autumn as Episode 1"*), but the numbered order hides that.
- **Place: none.** Threshold is a reference point, not a setting: C05's description, the Sceir Light's shore in C10 and the Survey Archive in C12.
- **Boundary events: ten of fourteen.** Most chapters meet the boundary under a local name: the Overfold (C00, C01), the Counterpane kernel's scope (C05), the Interval and the *Turquoise Dove* (C07), Deadwater (C09, C12), a clock that always corrects one way (C10), the sí gaoithe (C11), the Conjunction (C13). C04, C06 and C08 do not.
- **Theme: all fourteen.** This is the real join. Every chapter turns on what a record keeps and what it leaves out, and nearly every one ends on that line:
  - C02: *"It was one line. She dated it."*
  - C03: Aldera's *FIELD LOG*, *"No agency of record."*
  - C04: Anne *"gave the two numbers to nobody"*, and *together* goes into the intake record.
  - C05: *survives correction*, and nothing written over it.
  - C06: *"noted as answered"*.
  - C07: *"He did not write down which he had read first."*
  - C08: *"The chair of that day said: Noted."*
  - C09: *"She wrote the figure. She did not write anything else."*
  - C10: *"Rate as previously noted"*, while the margin keeps *the tower has its own habit*.
  - C11: *Open.* And *"The instrument had a tolerance. The door did not."*
  - C12: *Deadwater's*, and *"Nobody had been asked to fill that one."*
  - C13: *"the margin on the autumn page had nothing written in it"*.
- **The forward join: Season 1's people and readings.** Elvira and Aldera return in Episodes 2 and 3; Syra opens Episode 1; Tissadelle is the cadet of S01E01C04; the Sceir Light is in S01E01C03; C07 is told at *"another post with two clocks"*, a post like Threshold with its forty seconds.

## Why a reader cannot see the join

1. **The index names the weakest join.** It names time, lists the chapters *"not the order they happened"*, and says they *"read best after the station"*. In other words, it tells the reader to expect a miscellany.
2. **The first part promises a story the episode does not continue.** Four chapters build Aldera's journey toward the causeway, and C04 leaves it for 2788. Aldera does not come back in the episode. Nothing tells the reader that her road resumes in Episode 2. So the first part reads as a story broken off.
3. **The joins point out of the episode.** They lead to Episodes 1 to 3, which a reader who starts at C00 has not read. The index's own advice to read it after the station admits this.
4. **The one join between parts is hidden by name.** C09's prose calls her *Wender* and *the Senior*. Nothing in C09 says she was the five-year-old of C04.
5. **The theme lives in the endings, and the house voice withholds.** Every chapter closes quietly on a line that goes unwritten. Repeated fourteen times, that reads as a house manner, not as a thread, unless something names it once.
6. **The episode grew by accretion.** C05, C07, C09, C10, C11, C12 and C13 were each placed here because *"a new season needs a thread, which is his"*. Each choice was right on its own terms. Nobody was asked what the fourteen add up to.

## The read, in the next-improvement shape

**Good and must not move.**
- Every chapter as written, and especially the endings quoted above.
- The dated index line on every chapter, and the honesty about order.
- C04's *together*.
- C12's *Deadwater's*.
- The varied openings: no two chapters come in the same way.
- Chapter numbers, URLs and `comment_id`s.
- The `related:` lines of C04 and C05, which a fix in flight is moving (the Krenyi link).

**What it lacks.**
- **(a) The episode's principle is not stated anywhere a reader meets it.** UNSUPPORTED, literacy, P2.
- **(b) No last chapter gathers the episode.** C13 closes its own night and nothing more. UNSUPPORTED, creative entropy, P3.
- **(c) The Karla link between C04 and C09 cannot be found from the prose.** UNSUPPORTED, literacy, P3.
- **(d) Aldera's quest is left at *go*.** FEATURE, because her road continues in Episode 2. The only action is to make that findable, which (a) covers.
- **(e) Episode 0 is the default home for every Season 1 side-light.** So it will keep growing in the same way. Creative entropy, P3; put as Decision 2.

The CLAUDE.md rule allows *"a self-contained short story or set of chapters"*. Episode 0 is a set. What it lacks is the sentence that says what the set is.

## Decision 1 — the repair (his)

1. **Name the join on the episode index (recommended).** Opus does it. It is one added paragraph in `src/seasons/s01/e00/index.md`, and the present intro and dated list stay as they are. The paragraph says three things:
   - Every chapter turns on what a record keeps and what it leaves out.
   - Most meet the boundary before it had the name Season 1 gives it.
   - Each is the before of a person or a reading Season 1 meets. In particular, Aldera's road resumes in Episode 2, and the child Karla of C04 is the Senior of C09.

   It names the shape and explains no shadow. *Vary the way in* is untouched, since the index is not a chapter opening. Optionally, a small template change can add a heading over the C00–C03 run (*Dún Rí, 2822*) without reordering anything. **Cost:** about a hundred words and one PR, no chapter touched. **Cost of leaving it:** the first episode a careful reader is offered reads as three unrelated stories. **Proof it changes nothing good:** no chapter, number, URL or ending moves.
2. **A closing chapter, S01E00C14, in an existing POV, appended so nothing is renumbered.** Fable writes the prose; Opus plans it; the draft is a proposal. The suggested POV is Benedek Rónai at the scale desk of the 2826 archive pass on Threshold (C12), the summer before Episode 1. His pass meets the Survey filings of C05, C07, C09, C11, C12 and C13, and the reader alone knows what each filing left out. It would close the episode and become the last chapter that offers ways on, toward S01E01C01. A variant is Syra on the morning before Galahad arrives, with her own 2814 three words in the station's file. **Limit:** the Archive cannot reach the private records of C00–C04, C06, C08 and C10, so the chapter gathers part 3, not all three parts. **Cost:** one chapter of Fable quota, plus review.
3. **An Archive companion page.** Opus writes one codex working paper, for example the 2826 pass's schedule of the filings it touched, and adds it to every Episode 0 chapter's `related:` list. C04 and C05 wait until the Krenyi fix has landed. **Cost:** one page and front matter in twelve, later fourteen, chapters. Readers rarely follow `related:` links, so it does little for the reading itself.

**Recommended: 1 now. Keep 2 as the runner-up, for if the episode still reads as three on your next read after 1 lands.**

**Considered and set aside:**
- **Reordering the chapters by date.** This would show the 2826 convergence, but it renumbers fourteen published chapters and their URLs. Under the 2026-11-05 rule, nothing published is rewritten to meet it.
- **A bridging scene between C03 and C04, or an opening frame before C00.** Neither has a free slot without renumbering or opening a published chapter. That leaves the index as the episode's only free way in.
