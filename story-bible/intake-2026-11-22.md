## The Hyperfold Yield Combine's origins: the ownership stays unclaimed — RULED

**His words, verbatim.** Asked whether an origin for the Combine should name who stood behind it, he chose: *"Keep it unclaimed"*.

**What it settles.** No origin written for the Combine, in lore, a chapter, the Codex or an image prompt, names its owners, its founders' principals or the party behind the opaque instruments its ownership chain ends in. The two open points on `src/lore/hyperfold-yield-combine.md`, *What is still open* (nobody has claimed the loss; where the product went), are shadows under the shadow-shaped rule. They stay shut, and nothing gives them an answer.

**What an origin may still tell**, since none of it is established and none of it names an owner:
- the founding years (the page's *somewhat under a decade* before 2831 puts them in or near 2821–2824 UCSD);
- which line of the Imperium-era fold-drive patents its licence descends from, told as paper and not as people;
- how it found the narrowed Interval off Threshold;
- whose advice chose the name, kept at the level of *counsel* or *advisers* without naming who paid them;
- what it said it was for.

**Limits that still hold.**
- **No connection to the Halvern Combine** (`intake-2026-08-18.md`, § 16, *Live after this*): *Combine* is a corporate form, not a family. An origin that makes Hyperfold a Halvern offshoot would reverse a recorded line and is a canon question for him.
- **The Archive knows only what the record knows.** An origin enters the lore page only as a revision after a chapter, or stays in the Codex as an author's account.

**The natural vehicle.** The investor prospectus, *deferred, not rejected* (`tissadelle-arc-s6-7.md`, *Open for Dermot*). It is a Codex entry valid for its author: the Combine's founding as the Combine wished it seen, which can describe its supply chain at length without saying what the product is made from, and its ownership without saying who owns it. A prospectus is a document, not a narrative mode, so drafting it is Opus's work by default (`CLAUDE.md`, *Which model does which work*). Drafted the same day (next section).

**Kind of edit.** A new Codex entry, `src/codex/hyperfold-offering-memorandum.md`, valid for its author: the Combine as issuer, an institution, so no character page. One See also link is added to the unlocked Combine lore page, an extension. No `canon_facts`, and no chapter touched.

- **Dated Late Summer 2831**, inside the four months the aperture was held. Its *first output* and *all of it has been sold* then agree with the lore page's four months of rendering and its audited product, and no earlier aperture is implied.
- **Registered 2822 UCSD**, inside *somewhat under a decade* before 2831. Its trade before Threshold is transit services and aperture equipment, which the glossary's *proprietary aperture equipment* already implies.
- **It found the site in the public record**: Threshold's open drift reports, which the station carried as a hardware fault, read by the Combine as a boundary. Derived from the Archive being open; it explains no shadow.
- **Its stated purpose is cheaper, safer folding for small operators**, so the Combine is misguided and *for* something, under the cast principles, and never a villain.
- **The forty-year table** is a constant ten per cent a year fall in cost, with reach taken as its inverse. Checked: 0.9 to the powers 5, 10, 20 and 40 gives 59, 35, 12 and 1.5 (per 100), and the inverses give 169, 287, 823 and 6,765. The page makes no comment on the loop, as the lore page says the filings did not.
- **Ownership**: registered holding instruments, with nothing below the registered tier disclosed. The archivist's note says the subscriber list is not held. Unclaimed, as ruled.
- **The annexes were not found with the copy**, so the patent chain, the counsel's opinion and the feedstock's status are named and never shown.
