# Intake, 24 November 2026 — working ranges for the story's units

## The question

Dermot, verbatim:

> Ideal number of scenes per chapter, chapters per episode, episodes per
> season and seasons per thread or arc?

## The answer he took

Opus answered with ranges rather than fixed numbers, because three standing
rules already bear on the question: an episode reads as a self-contained
short story and needs no fixed shape (5 November); no structure becomes a
repeatable answer the reader learns to expect (*Vary the way in*, 30
October); and the POV block is the only unit with set lengths (4 August).

- POV blocks per chapter: 2–4 (about 1,500–3,500 words at the general tier).
- Chapters per episode: 3–6.
- Episodes per season: 3–6.
- Seasons per thread: open, since a thread has no planned end.
- Seasons per arc: not a measure, since an arc is a character's trajectory.

Asked whether to record them, he answered *"Yes, as guidance"*.

## Where it is recorded

`story-bible-summary.md`, *Craft: working ranges for the units above the POV
block*, just before the POV block length targets. The section carries the
counts of 24 November (Season 1 long, most later episodes short) and the
rule that nothing published is rewritten to meet the ranges. `CLAUDE.md` is
not changed: the ranges are guidance under its 5 November rule, not a new
rule beside it.
