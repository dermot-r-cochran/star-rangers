## The four tests

Three sentences go a step past restating a chapter's fact. Each was held to the four tests: derived, adds without moving, closes no shadow and gives no remedy, recognisable as already true.

1. Calloway's page joins the *standing liaison contact* it already describes to *the liaison line the Chief Commissioner keeps* of S04E07C02. Derived from both; it moves nothing; it is the Commander's answer and no remedy of the page's own.
2. The Compact page calls what S04E07 shows *a second gap beside the first*. The first is that no bureau can follow a case across habitat lines. The second is the seam in the close's own words. The page states it as the close does and proposes nothing.
3. Albercombe's page says *the task force's oldest open file began as a referral of his*. S02E01C01 shows the handing over, and S04E07C01 calls the file the oldest open one.
