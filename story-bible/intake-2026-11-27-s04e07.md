**The reading drafted.** Nothing was taken. The authorisation that Calloway signed is what told the manifest to stop showing the fifth code: it reassigned that leg to Halcyon's stores. Halcyon's stores signed the eleven crates in, on the day, in their own ledger. Eden was not shown the reassigned leg, so Eden's form kept a gap. Larsen and Calloway each read the gap as somebody's doing. Each kept back the one paper that would have read as an accusation: Larsen the form, Calloway the authorisation. So the two papers were never side by side, and the form sat in the task force's drawer for nearly two years. In autumn 2828 the question is put plainly, to all five Commissioners, in the open, and Halcyon's stores strike the stamp within the week.

The *gap between two authorities* is therefore double. One gap lies between Eden's form and Halcyon's ledger. The other lies between the task force and the Chief Commissioner's office, which between them held a Commissioner under an unwritten suspicion for two years. That second gap is the nearest the thread comes to the unaccountable authority its line warns of.

**The ending test.** From the end, S02E01 reads differently. Jeeves gave the answer in the first chapter. Duarte asked whether there was anything he should know, and was told no. Barsik was the only reader who left the line empty: *"He noted that he did not know."* Albercombe, Larsen, Calloway and Voss each put a cause on it, as the two floors do to Larsen's blank in S04E03.
