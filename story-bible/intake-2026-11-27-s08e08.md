**The reading drafted.** What comes back is something, and it is not an answer. It is the post's own return slip: four lines on grey card, giving the direction, a date in the deep winter, the far post's mark, and a signature in the addressee's own hand. The letter arrived and was taken into a hand. Whether it was opened or read is not known, and the right-hand column says so.

The slip is the middle one of the three states E06C01 names: called, acknowledged, corrected. It is also the season's own word turned round. For seven episodes the Keeper declines to sign for anything. What comes up the hill is somebody else's signature.

The watch ends on the Keeper's own trade. A watch is handed over by signature, and the incoming officer signs before reading the log. He reads the season's first verse again and finds the watchman in it: the morning ends a watch by bringing a relief, and explains nothing. He is careful not to make the slip the morning. He keeps the forty seconds for the one who signed, and sleeps *"like a man who has come off watch"*. A fortnight on, the carrier asks whether there is anything to go, and *"Not this time"* is true.

The second hive is alive on the first warm day: a cluster the size of his two hands, a laying queen and a palm of sealed brood. It came through on sugar, and he says so in those words. It is not signed for.

**The ending test.** From the end, S08E01C01 reads differently. The Keeper there prays for the watchers and reads *"more than watchmen long for the morning"* as a measure of longing. The coda has him find that a watch ends by relief and not by explanation, which is the one thing a retired Chief should have known about watches. The first chapter's *"like a man expected at a post in the morning"* is answered by *"like a man who has come off watch"*.

## Every assertion beyond Season 8

1. *The second hive is alive in early spring 2829, small, with a laying queen and sealed brood. It is not signed for before young bees fly.* Derived: E07C01 leaves it fed and *"a little heavier"*. It gives no remedy: sugar kept it, and the prose says so.
2. *The first and third hives are in order; the third has brood on three combs.* An extension of E06C01's *"small but in order"*.
3. *Through the winter he lifted the back edge of the second hive once a fortnight and entered the weight.* Derived from E07C01's hefting.
4. *The carrier came up through the winter with sugar and lamp oil. He never asked whether anything had come.* New, and ordinary.
5. *The rota's post returns a slip for anything it carries past the last landing and puts into a hand. It is grey card with four lines: direction, date, the far post's mark, signature.* **New, and the largest assertion here.** It rests on E07C01's *"the rota's post"* and on a record that is kept by filings and signatures throughout. It names no place and no distance. Choice 1.
6. *The signature is the addressee's own name in the addressee's own hand, and the Keeper knows the hand.* New. It says the addressee was alive and at that direction in the deep winter of 2828 to 2829, and that the Keeper has seen the hand before. It gives no name, sex, relation or place. Choice 2.
7. *The carrier asked at the landing whether there was more, and there was not. She goes down that Thursday without asking whether there is anything to go.* New. It is her tact, in his reading of it.
8. *A Corps watch is handed over by signing for the board before the log is read.* New as a stated practice. It is in keeping with the Corps' filings in Seasons 1, 9 and 10, and it is said by a retired Chief of his own decks. Flagged in case a published handover reads otherwise; none was found in Season 8, 9 or 10.
9. *The rota has sent nobody yet in 2829, and the circuit writes about the summer's candles.* Ordinary.
10. *The bee's first flight of the year, and a tree in yellow on the low ground past the wall.* The tree is not named. E04C01 puts a dead ash on the far side of the low ground; this is another tree.

**What no assertion says.** What the letter says, what it is about, who it is to, or where it went. Whether it was opened or read. Whether an answer comes. What the Keeper believes about the slip: the third line of the entry is left empty. Why the third hive went wrong in 2827: the coda refers to that hive only as the one whose remnant he carried across the stand. That any prayer was answered: he asks again, believes he is heard as on every night, and *"did not join the two"*. Nothing is said of the chapter house, the drowned ground or the children. The chaplain and the cadet do not appear, and neither is accounted for.

## What it pays, and what it leaves open

**Pays.** The letter (*"I don't know what comes back, or whether anything does"*, E07C01). The carrier's *"If there's anything"*. The second hive's winter. The Keeper's watch over *"the one in him"*, which ends as a watch.

**Leaves open, as the brief asks.** The overlay itself. The night office goes on, and so do the forty seconds, now kept for somebody. Whether the letter is read and whether anything is written back. Whether he writes again: *"Not this time."* The chaplain's *"The rota comes round"* and the cadet's posting. Both blank lines in the chapter-house strand, which this episode does not visit.

3. *The Episode 7 intro and the Season 8 index* are left as published. Episode 8 is marked as a coda, and the index has a new paragraph for it.

## Style-critic read

Scanner against `src/seasons`: every house device at or under the corpus rate (*does not / did not* at 0.8, *which is* at 0.7, no dash, no denial-then-assertion pair, one sentence opening *Not*). ## Choices for him

1. **What the spring brings back.**
   - (a) *The post's return slip, signed for, and no letter.* **Recommended, and drafted.** Basis: it is the least the spring can bring that still pays the letter. It is derived from E06C01's own three states, of which this is the second. It turns the season's *"I'd not sign for it"* into somebody else's signature. It says nothing about what the letter held, and nothing about whether it was read. It gives the watch an end without giving the matter one.
   - (b) *Nothing.* The carrier comes up in the spring with sugar and no more, and the Keeper finds that the spring was a term and has passed. It asserts least. It makes the end of the watch his decision alone, and it leaves the episode with only the hive to stand on.
   - (c) *A letter, unopened on the page.* It pays most. It would oblige the prose to withhold a thing the Keeper has read, and it comes close to answering the one prayer he made about the letter.
   - Changing to (b) is the second scene and about ten lines of the third. Changing to (c) is a new second and third scene.
2. **Whose signature.**
   - (a) *The addressee's own, in a hand he knows.* **Recommended, and drafted.** Basis: a clerk's or a stranger's signature would leave him not knowing whether it reached the person, which is option 1(b) with a card. This tells him one thing: it is in that hand now.
   - (b) *Signed by another at that direction.* It asserts less about the addressee and adds an unknown person to the record.
   - (c) *The far post's mark only, and no signature.* Delivered to a door.
3. **The second hive.**
   - (a) *Alive, small, queen laying, not signed for.* **Recommended, and drafted.** Basis: E07C01 fed it and found it a little heavier; hope is load-bearing in this voice, and the prose puts the survival on the sugar and not on the standing.
   - (b) *Dead on the first warm day.* It would echo E03C01 exactly. It would also make the fed hive and the watched hive come to the same end, which reads as a verdict on the feeding.
4. **The two lines flagged in the style read.**
   - (a) Keep both. **Recommended.**
   - (b) Cut *"A man who does not ask is not thereby a man who is not listening for the cart."* and keep the watch passage.
   - (c) Cut *"A watch has an end. The end is not the watchman's to bring, and the matter need not be settled first."* and let the ladder at six carry it.
5. **The title.**
   - (a) *If There's Anything.* **Recommended, and placed.** Basis: it is the carrier's phrase from the last line she spoke about the letter, it follows *Anything to Go*, and it does not print on every index what was in her coat. A change of title after merge keeps the `comment_id`.
   - (b) *Signed For.* The season's word. It tells the reader what comes back.
   - (c) *Not This Time.* The closing answer. The plan's choice 6 advises against a title that is the closing line.
