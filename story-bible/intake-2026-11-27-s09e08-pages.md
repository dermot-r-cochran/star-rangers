## The four tests

Four sentences go a step past restating a chapter's fact. Each was held to the four tests: derived, adds without moving, closes no shadow and gives no remedy, recognisable as already true.

1. Varn's opening and section say the watch was stood *from the early spring of 2828 to the early winter*. Derived from the timestamps of S09E01C01 and S09E08C01. It gives no count of days.
2. Varn's section says *the event did not come in it*. S09E08C02 has *"It had not come"* in Varn's own block, and S09E06C01 keeps the one interval as no event.
3. Varn's section opens *What the rest of the year holds is mostly off the watch*, and Noah's opens *The corridor was most of it … nearly all of what follows is in no log*. These are framing, and each is the chapters' own repeated statement (*It is in no log*, S09E03C01, S09E07C01).
4. Noah's *Character Notes* names the two things left open: a letter, and the waiting. Both are in the S09E08 intake's *What no fact asserts*. Naming them closes neither.
