**The reading drafted.** The order is ordinary. It is one printed leaf on the form every such order takes, it gives a watch, an officer and two dates, and it gives no reason. Varn reads it twice and finds that the question of why has not come: that is the whole of *a raw Deputy stops being one*. No rank moves. Varn is still a Deputy Officer, with three bars, going to a second posting under a second Field Officer. Field Officer Ruiz's *"Four bars inside two years, you"* (E04C01) is not borne out and is not mentioned.

Varn keeps the promise to the hour: at the hatch, in service dress, before going to change, because *"I did not want to find out how much of a day I had meant"*. The mother's *nobody tells us* is answered once, by one Deputy, and not as a change in what the Corps does.

The story is not handed on. Varn offers the archive, and Noah points out that he was never given her name and asks not to be. He says again that he can wait. Neither says that it will come to that.

The sixteenth arrives some days later and is seen first from the corridor, by the resident who now knows what three bars are and what is behind the hatch. The sixteenth knocks, as Varn did, and stands one step too far from the desk, as they all do. Smith says the first day's sentences in the same voice.

**The ending test.** From the end, S09E01C01 reads differently. A Deputy arrived with a kit bag, saw two residents walk past the hatch, and *"understood that they had never looked at it"*. The coda shows that arrival from the door beside the hatch. The residents know exactly where the hatch is, they know a Deputy when one stands reading the doors, and they know about how long it will be before the walk changes again. The first chapter's Deputy was being read by the corridor the whole time, and was not told.

## Every new fact, and what it rests on

**C01, *The Same Day*.**

1. *The order: one printed leaf, on the form every such order takes; name, watch left, watch gone to, the officer there, a date to be relieved, a later date to report, a reference; no reason. Varn remains a Deputy Officer.* Derived from E01C01's posting order and E04C01's relief order. The rank is unchanged by E07C02's *"Field Officer first. Years."*
2. *Where: a watch that is not Eden's, on a station a ship's passage from Eden's dock, under a Field Officer whose name Varn did not know. The station is not named.* Choice 1.
3. *A Field Officer reads a Deputy's order first. Smith has read fifteen. With it comes the form the Corps sends when a Deputy is moved on, asking what the Deputy did on the watch.* Derived from E01C05 (the first month's form) and E06C01 (*"The next form the Corps sent would ask what the Deputy had done on the watch"*). What she writes is not carried.
4. *Varn enters the order in the log as received and read.* New, on the season's own test: it happened on the desk, and somebody reading the column later could not otherwise tell when the watch knew.
5. *The order says nothing of the watch or of Smith; nobody has told her there will be no sixteenth.* Derived from E06C01.
6. *Varn tells Noah at the hour of the same shift, in service dress; the relief falls on a sixth day, the next but one; Noah's mother is told that night.* Pays E07C02.
7. *Smith closes the hatch when the Deputy goes out with the order, as she did once in the late autumn.* An echo of E07C01. Her reason for the latch is still given to nobody.

**C02, *A Different Walk*.**

1. *The last shift, the entry of relief by order, and no event. Varn leaves by the spoke cars in service dress with the kit list and the shirt.*
2. *Varn tells Smith that Varn has not found out what the account is for. She answers that none of the fourteen had by the day they left, and that it was handed over without a date.* It pays nothing and closes nothing: her waiting stands.
3. *Smith's correction, that one Field Officer without the account is one entry.* It keeps the question of who has the account open, and says nothing of the officer at the new posting.
4. *Her last words to the Deputy about a posting are "Stand where you like", and she does not say whether it is advice.* It leaves E07C01's seam where it was.
5. *Varn will write, to "Adeyemi, the door after the hatch, fold-approach side". The evenings between the order and the leaving were spent off the watch, once the whole light way round again.*
6. *Noah declines to have the account from the archive, and says again that he can wait. Varn: "That may not be a thing I can make happen."*
7. *Noah calls the Deputy Iklix to the tab, once. Varn does not correct it and does not move the step.* Choice 5.
8. *The sixteenth Deputy arrives some days later, unnamed, knocks, and stands a step too far. Smith says the first day's sentences.* Choice 2.
9. *Noah says "Evening" to the sixteenth's tab. In her tenure on Eden, Smith had not heard a resident of that corridor do so.* E02C01 has Varn notice that nobody had said it to the tab in two months.
10. *The sixteenth's order had been folded once and smoothed flat again: this one sat down with it.* A small difference from Varn (E01C01), so that the sixteenth is another person and not a repeat.

**What no fact asserts.** What the new watch is, what it stands over, or who its Field Officer is. Whether Varn and Noah meet again, and whether any letter is sent or answered. What the account of Aoife is for. Smith's reason for the latch. Anything about Smith's first station or her citation, which are kept by the ruling of 27 November and are not touched. The sixteenth's name, age or intake file. No rank, commendation or skill is given to Varn. The nasturtium does not flower.

## What it pays, and what it leaves open

**Pays.** Varn's reposting (*"the Corps has not said"*, E07C02). The undertaking to tell Noah the same day (E07C02). The mother's *nobody tells us* (E03C01), once. Smith's *"She would say it to the sixteenth"* (E06C01).

**Leaves open, as the brief asks.** The story Varn cannot hand on yet: it is paid only as far as E07C02 sets it up. Varn has no Deputy, so it is not told, and the one shortcut is declined by the person it was offered to. What the account is for. Whether *Stand where you like* is advice. Where the watch is, in the prose. Whether Noah's waiting comes to anything. What mount three might do, the Starwarden's *noted* and Smith's first station are not mentioned.

**Seams.**

1. *"The season's last evening."* The Episode 7 intro and the Season 9 index call Episode 7's evening the season's last. Both are left as published. Episode 8 is marked as a coda, set after it. Choice 6.
2. *"At the hour."* Season 9 uses the phrase both for the Deputy coming to the shift and for the Deputy leaving it. The coda uses it only for the leaving, and for Noah's coming home.
3. *Fifteen orders.* Smith's page says that Shepherd, one of the fourteen, was *"made Officer within the season"*. The coda says only that the fifteen leaves came on one form, and nothing of what any other leaf said.

## Style-critic read

Register: Star Rangers chapter prose, in Season 9's later manner (E02 to E07: no dashes in the narration, the season's refrains used as refrains). Scanner against `src/seasons`: every house device at or under the corpus rate, and no machine-prose shape present. *Does not / did not* runs at 1.2 and 1.4 times baseline, which is this thread's own rate. *Seemed / as if* scanned at about three times baseline in both chapters, at three uses each; one in each was cut. The stock phrases are the season's refrains (*on the first day*, *end of the shift*, *waited for the rest*), which are deliberate echoes.

*What lands:* *"It had also been a sentence about a Deputy, with the Deputy left out."* · *"I said the same day. I did not want to find out how much of a day I had meant."* · *"I didn't say I'd be let. I said I can."*

*Settled and applied:* two *as if* similes cut to plain statements. An explanatory sentence after Noah's *"It was not his to say"* cut to the fact. A cut-off line of speech given the season's dash in place of an ellipsis. *"it was like the other fourteen"* made *"on the form the others had come on"*, for seam 3.

*Flagged and kept, by the drafter's judgement under the 28 September ruling:* *"For the length of a name there were two people in the corridor and one step, and the step was only where one of them was standing."* It is close to an explanation of the line before it. It is kept because it is Noah's own reading, in his block, of a thing he has been working out since E03C01. Choice 5.

**Verdict:** in voice, with five line repairs applied and one line left for him.

## Choices for him

1. **Where Varn is sent.** The candidates are the places Season 9, Season 10 and the two pages already make plausible.
   - (a) *A watch that is not Eden's, on a station a ship away, not named by the prose.* The order names it, Varn says the name to Noah, and Noah has never heard it and will look it up. **Recommended, and drafted.** Basis: it is the posting Smith's page describes (*"watch rotations at monitored fold approaches … the kind that never make the record twice"*), and the record already speaks this way of the ninth Deputy (*"on a watch that was not Eden's"*). It is the most ordinary order, so it keeps *never the chosen one*. It asserts least, and a later season is free to name the place.
   - (b) *Threshold Station, at the other end of the Passage.* It is derived: the route the watch stands on ends there. It puts Varn on the main line's station nearly three years before Dock Seven, which every later Season 6 and 7 chapter would then have to allow for, and it reads as the Deputy being sent to where the story is.
   - (c) *The Passage oversight, a spoke car up from the ring.* It is the only other office of the Corps that Season 9 names: Ruiz came from it and the watch's notices go to it. It would leave Varn within a fare of the Adeyemi door. That softens the parting nearly to a remedy, and *moved on* would barely move.
