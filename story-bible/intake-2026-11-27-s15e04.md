**What was drafted.** One chapter, three scenes, general tier, no image.

- *Scene 1, Tobble.* He keeps a night watch in the stores below the hill: the records of the *Sufficient Cause*, standing in their ranges, the last of which stops at 0551 in the middle of a line. Past it is what he calls the break. Things come to it one at a time and call. He logs each in the form a desk uses for a call and gives nothing. Tonight a hail in a shuttle's form asks for a name, and something cries as a young animal cries. They overlap and neither alters. He does not know what they are, or what keeps them at the break. At the foot of each leaf are two lines of his own, *Admitted* and *Sent up*.
- *Scene 2, Asteria.* She comes down, as on some nights, unasked. A third arrival says *Good morning* in the voice and manner the woman at the wall keeps for Tobble, and then supplies his answers. It shows; Tobble does not look, and she does. What she sees is not set down. It speaks to her as it spoke to him. She holds that the woman upstairs ought to be told; he gives his grounds for sending nothing; neither moves the other. She finds a ground of her own for not carrying it up herself, says she cannot support it, and still thinks it is owed. He enters all of it, which is how she learns she is on the watch.
- *Scene 3, Tobble.* The grey comes. Her good morning comes down from the floor above and waits; the other one goes on answering itself. He answers the one that waits. Her entry for the morning comes down and he files it. He fills in the foot of the leaf, rules the next, and means to go up and sit somewhere warm.
