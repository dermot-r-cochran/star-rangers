## What was written

A premise, a season line, a proposed focus line and strand lines, an outline of seven episodes and ten chapters, a finale with a last beat on each side, a list of what stays open, and thirteen choices, each with a recommendation and its basis.

**The premise in two sentences.** The up-people have learned one of the Told's two manners, *move a stone first*, and in their fifth year they do the other, *leave a place to a stranger until they ask*, without knowing it is manners. Above, the leaving is an entry with no *because* in it; below, nobody was there for a going, so it becomes a telling with its edges and no middle, and the line is kept.

## The four tests, run on the season as a whole

- **Derived from what is on the page.** Every row above cites its chapter. The cause is built from words S14E06C01 already printed, and the name from a phrase S14E05C01 already printed.
- **Adds without moving anything.** No published `canon_facts` line is changed. Two lore entries would gain a sentence.
- **Closes no shadow and gives no remedy.** The shapes, the boundary feature and Ferrant's page are untouched. Nothing is done for the Told and nothing is asked of them.
- **A reader would recognise it as already true.** The season's two recognitions are both sentences from Season 11, read again: what keeping the margin is for, and what the second of the manners is.

## Contradiction search

- **The survey window.** `src/lore/frontier-transformation-protocols.md` has the window *"extended, multi-generational"*, and `src/lore/planets/fliade.md` has it *"written to run longer than the geology alone would justify"*. A window that closed in the fifth year would contradict both. The treatment therefore ends the presence and keeps the window, and says so (choice 2).
- **The unit at the junction.** A first draft of the finale had the unit lifted *"for the second time"* and *"not left asleep"*. S14E02C01 and S14E07C01 have it on Aravena's back up the long passage at the end of every shift, so the unit has not slept alone at the junction since the relay's move. The beat was rewritten to agree.
- **Later years.** `src/` was searched for Fliade, the Pandoids, the Told and the five named people of the upper strand against every year from 2835 on. Nothing places the party on Fliade after 2834. S02E13 (2837) and Season 15 do not mention the world.
- **Present-tense pages.** The Fliade entry, the Pandoids entry, *The Convened* and the cast pages speak of the survey as working. None gives it an end, and none says it has none. Two would need a sentence at the finale (choice 11); *The Convened* cites the doctrine and stands.
- **The second passage.** S11E06C01 has the junction's stones run to a number that is *"not as large as a survey lead would have liked"*, and the 27 November repair has the second passage *"not yet started late in year three"*. The treatment does not use the stones as the cause (choice 1, option d), and states no count.
- **Who stands on the line.** S11E03C01 has *"nobody was on the line at a stone but Stone-First"* for the first three stones. That is a fact about those working times and makes no rule. A small one (S11E01C04) and Went-Round (S12E01C03) have stood there since.
- **The row's length.** By the finale the row is something over three hundred stones at *"about a hand off"* each. No page says how long the line is. The treatment prints no count and no length, and leaves the number to the draft or to the entry's own figure.
