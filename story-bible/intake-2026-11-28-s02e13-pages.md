## The four tests

Seven sentences go a step past restating a chapter's fact. Each was held to the four tests: derived, adds without moving, closes no shadow and gives no remedy, recognisable as already true.

1. Lev's `role` says *on Eden Space Habitat from the spring of 2827*. Derived: his page's second paragraph, and the chapter's *"He had lived on the dock's side of that line since he was eight."* It does not say he lives there after the day of the loading.
2. Lev's page says *by his own reckoning*. The chapter's sentence is his thought at the ramp. The clause keeps it his, where the S02E13 intake's fact 9 calls it entailed.
3. Lev's page says *as it had gone towards his pocket once on a walkway*. Derived: scene 2 (*"It had done that once before, on a walkway, when he was eight"*) and the page's own *What He Declines*.
4. Barsik's page says *Barsik is on Eden*. The chapter puts him on the rail. That he is alive and there in 2837 is the new fact he ruled in choice 6 of the S02E13 intake. The page does not say he is posted there still, or to what.
5. Muffin's `status` says *in 2837 two decks down*. The chapter's words are *"two decks down, in the warm under a window"*. The qualification drops *a family's quarters* for 2837, since the chapter does not say whose window.
6. Bubochka's page carries a line that the file gives to *the rabbit*. The page says so in the next sentence. Choice 5.
7. Zhulik's page says *The record says no more of the cat at that date.* True of the chapter and of every page: Okoro's memory of *"a grey-and-white cat over his shoulder"* is of 2827.
