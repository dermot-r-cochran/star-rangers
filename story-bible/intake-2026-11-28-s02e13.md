**The reading drafted.** One loading, late summer 2837, ten years to the season after the walkway. Lev is eighteen, a Cadet with a new second bar, reading the manifest at the ramp of a Rangers' supply shuttle at Eden's transfer dock. The chain below him is Eden's dock hands, and it does what the chain has always done. A cup comes up it from a child, for her sister on a station: a rooted strawberry runner. Every hand is kind. It is not on the list. His hand goes out, and he stops it, and says *no* out loud in front of everybody, and it takes about four seconds. This time it costs him.

Then the two forms. The manifest has a narrow column headed *Not carried*, which nobody has written in. And there is a form of four boxes for asking to be put on a later list, which the quartermaster says has always existed. He reads the boxes and finds them *"in the right order"*, which is his own order: say it once, in sequence, and wait. The cup goes back down the chain with the form, as carefully as it came up.

One dock hand, Maud Okoro, at the fourth place from the ramp, hands back a tin of biscuits later in the same loading. Nobody else on the chain does. Barsik watches from the rail and enters her on his list. At the foot of the chain the child has filled in the form. Lev tells her he does not know whether it will go, and that it is more likely than not. She tells him to change the water every morning.

**How it answers the focus line.** *The one nobody is watching*: nobody watches a cadet read a list. *The plain thing the bureau has no form for*: here it has two, and neither does the work. The column was empty until a person wrote in it. *It works one person at a time*: the chain does not stop and is not mended. One cup stops, at the one place where a person stands with a list, and one more hand learns to ask.

**The ending test.** S02E11C01 ends with a boy telling a grown man to change the water, *"because nobody might have told him"*. The coda ends with a child telling Lev the same thing for the same reason, and him agreeing. S02E10C01's four seconds cost him nothing, and Barsik's doctrine line said why. Here they cost what they cost anybody, and the line is corrected in the file. E07C01 left open whether a chain would stop for a boy. The coda's answer is that it does not, and that a cup can.
