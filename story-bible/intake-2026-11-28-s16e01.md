**The reading drafted.** One episode, two chapters, nothing in either but the warm and one knock.

- *Someone to Tell* (C01, Carried-It-Sleeping and Keeps-A-Hand-On-It). Nobody has told the newest one the bright one, and there is no reason; nobody got up. Keeps-A-Hand-On-It asks, and has one worry, that a long telling may put its hearer to sleep, which nobody below the roof can settle. It is told whole, in the time after the working, to the newest one, just woken. Nobody who was there puts anything in, and each of them hears their own piece for the first time. Keeps-A-Hand-On-It leaves out their own reading of what it was. A small one asks again for the piece that says it was kind. Afterwards nothing is done with it.
- *Every Time* (C02, Went-Round and Stone-First-Who-Waited). The small ones try to make a name for the newest one by saying a thing three times, and nothing goes round. One of them says that everything it has, it has always had. Went-Round goes round *always*, asks the people one at a time, and finds the far side of *every time since* lying in the middle of the middle. It is kept as Went-Round's. Stone-First hears it, goes up, and answers the stone at once.

**The ending test.** The episode is a beginning and not an end, so the test is run on what it changes behind it. S12E03C01 ends *"After that it would have to stand up by itself. That was what the pieces were for."* Here it stands, and the people who made it find out what it sounds like. S11E03C01's *every time since* was found from the side of the ones who remember. Here it is seen from the side of one who cannot.

**Self-containment.** C01 says what the bright one was inside the telling itself, and who had which piece in the answer to *who was there*. C02 restates Went-Round's old reading in four sentences before it turns. A reader who starts at C01 and stops at C02 has two whole stories and one knock.

## Every assertion beyond the record

Each is marked against the four tests where it is more than ordinary.

1. *In the survey's fifth year the newest one is awake for longer at a time. It has no name.* The treatment's own, ruled. No length of waking is given and nothing is said of how fast a small one grows.
2. *The bright one had not been told to the newest one, and nobody had decided that.* **New.** S14E05C01 has the newest told the first thing and the waiting, and nothing else. The absence of a reason is the chapter's first joke, and it asserts nothing about the people beyond their lying where they lay.
3. *Keeps-A-Hand-On-It told it, on the ground of having every piece from the one who was there for it, and because nobody else had asked.* Derived from S12E03C01's `canon_facts` and from Carried-It-Sleeping's answer in S14E05C01. **The other ground, "the one who will be here longest", is not on the page.** The treatment keeps it for the finale, where Keeps-A-Hand-On-It looks at the newest and says who will be.
4. *Whether a telling is told if its hearer sleeps in the middle is not settled, because nobody has a telling of it happening.* **New, and left open on purpose.** It rests on two published rules that pull against each other (a stopped telling has a hole; a telling is not told twice to the same one). It closes nothing. Choice 4.
5. *It was told in the time after the working, with the whole of the people in the warm and Stone-First come down.* Ordinary. S12E01C04 and S12E03C01 have that time and that coming down.
6. *The telling as told.* Every piece is S12E03C01's, in that chapter's order, with *"about half of the two stones"* from S12E01C04. Nothing is added to the telling and nothing is taken out. It is given as a run of pieces, as S14E05C01 gives the waiting, and not as a speech.
7. *Nobody who was there put anything in, and each heard their own piece told by another to one who was not there, for the first time.* **New, and the chapter's centre.** The treatment's sentence, built from S12E03C01's two rules about checking and holding still. Kept as Carried-It-Sleeping's.
8. *"Not one of us turning", where Stone-First told it as "not one of you".* S12E03C01 already has the smallest say *"Not one of us turned"*. The chapter only notices it.
9. *Keeps-A-Hand-On-It left their own reading out of the telling, on the ground that a piece is what you were there for.* **New.** It applies Stone-First's *"do not give it a thing it did not do"* and Went-Round's *"nor has anybody"* to the teller. The reading stays kept as theirs. Choice 2.
10. *Carried-It-Sleeping asked "who was there" for the newest one, and the answer ended "nobody for all of it, and nobody for what it was".* The question and the longer answer are promised in S12E03C01. The last five words are Went-Round's finding there, put in the answer. Choice 5.
11. *The warm did not make the small sound.* Follows S12E03C01 and S12E07C01. Choice 6.
12. *The same small one asked again for the piece that says it was kind. Keeps-A-Hand-On-It asked whose piece it was. Stone-First looked and did not say it.* The treatment's beat. Who asks is the draft's. Choice 3.
13. *Nothing was done with the telling: no rule, no word, no second telling to the newest, no name.* The ruling of choice 5 of the treatment, shown.
14. *The small ones tried to grow a name for the newest one by saying things three times, and nothing went round.* **New.** The treatment's sentence. The three things tried are true of every newest one or are somebody else's doing. *"The smallest is asleep"* is not among them, so that the finale's name is not tried early and found wanting.
15. *Went-Round asked the people one at a time whether they remembered a time when the up-people's stone did not come.* **New.** The old one by the seep, Carried-It-Sleeping and the larger of the two who tried the squeeze are S11E03C01's and S11E07C01's.
16. *Keeps-A-Hand-On-It has the time before the stones as a told thing, says that having it is not the same as its having happened, and heard the stone from the line before being told there was a time before it.* **New, and it qualifies the treatment's wording.** Keeps-A-Hand-On-It was born after the two stones, so the treatment's *"there is now one below the roof who does not remember a time the up-people's stone did not come"* was already true of somebody. The draft raises that on the page and answers it. Choice 1.
17. *Went-Round's reading, kept as Went-Round's: "nobody remembers" was never the people all at once, it is one person at a time; the newest one does not remember such a time and has not been told one; for the newest one it is every time, a thing that is done.* **New.** The treatment's fact 9, with the step of item 16 in it.
18. *Went-Round would not say "every time" of the up-people's stone, having been there before, and holds that it is the newest one's to say. Went-Round said nothing of what the up-people do or why, and did not say the word for a thing that is done.* **New.** The first is the people's oldest rule, that a thing is said by who was there, turned on the reading itself. The second keeps the treatment's *"make a rule or a word below the roof"* refusal. Choice 8.
19. *The warm let it be kept and settled nothing. The small ones tried "Every-Time" as a name and it did not go round.* As S11E03C01 ended, and as item 14.
20. *Stone-First heard it and said nothing. Stone-First's own: on the line each stone is brought, in somebody's hands, and Stone-First has gone up before every working time without knowing whether the light would be there. It can be every time in the middle of the warm and this time on the line.* **New.** S11E03C01's `canon_facts` have the not knowing for three working times. This extends it to all of them, in Stone-First's block, and no page has said otherwise. Choice 7.
21. *At the next working time the knock came and was answered at once.* The treatment's last sentence for the episode, in Season 14's words.

**What no assertion says.** What the light was, or that it was kind, or that it will or will not come again. What the newest one made of being told, or what it looks at the wall for. How long a newest one is awake, or how fast it grows. That the up-people's stone is manners. That anything on the line has changed, or is going to. Why the up-people knock. What Stone-First made of Went-Round's reading beyond the one paragraph that is Stone-First's own. A name.

## The four tests

- **Derived from what is on the page.** Every item above cites its chapter. The one step the treatment did not have, item 16, is derived from a published fact about a named person.
- **Adds without moving anything.** No `canon_facts` line of any earlier chapter is changed. S11E03C01's reading stands as said, and Went-Round says so: *"That will not change, however many stones."*
- **Closes no shadow and gives no remedy.** The bright one is told with no middle and used for nothing. The sleeping question, the name and the reading all stay open.
- **A reader would recognise it as already true.** That a teller never hears their own piece, and that a small one born into a thing has it before the telling of its beginning, are both true of the people as Seasons 11 to 14 wrote them.

## Style-critic read

Register: Season 14's below side. Short paragraphs, plain words, one viewpoint to a scene, *"this is told the way things are told below the roof"* varied at each opening, intervals in tellings and names, no day. Measured against S14E03C01, S14E05C01 and S14E07C02: negations run at 32 and 39 per thousand words, where those three run 29, 33 and 39. No dashes and no exclamation marks. One semicolon.

*What lands:* *"That is when there is the most awake left in it."* · *"It is easier to tell a wall going thin when you are looking at it not doing so."* · *"That is three by you. Three by you is one."* · *"You told it. It lay there."* · *"I only wanted to see whether anyone had found it."* · *"It did not come. It was brought."*

*Settled and applied:* a first draft had Keeps-A-Hand-On-It stand up while an old one was still telling the long dark, which is an interruption; the long dark now ends first. Carried-It-Sleeping's reason for refusing *Every-Time* as a name was first *"the newest one was asleep"*, which leans on the finale's name; it is now *"it is not a thing the newest one did"*. *Eyes* was cut from the last scene of C01, since no chapter gives the people's looking an organ.

*Flagged and kept, by the drafter's judgement under the 28 September ruling:* *"The one who tells a piece is the one person who cannot be told it."* (Carried-It-Sleeping, C01) and *"Up here it had been this one."* (Stone-First, C02). Each is close to a theme said aloud. Each is one person's reading in their own block. Choices 7 and 9.

## Choices for him
