4. **The order at the junction.** Heard from the line: the loud settles; one voice by itself says a thing and stops; the unit hums; then one of them comes on down the third passage. That is S16E02C02's order (Nakagawa tells the unit the channel is off and the time; the unit hums once; Aravena picks a stone).
5. **Coming through.** Aravena comes out of the squeeze onto the silt with the light held low, the stone held to the front, the handheld on the chest, and breath not back.
6. **The light.** It finds the feet of the one who answers first, as always. Then it goes an arm's length to the side along the line, as it has at every knock since the shift with two shapes. This time it does not find silt. It finds feet, and goes up from them *"slowly, the way a person looks"*, and onto the second one as it goes onto the first. It does **not** go round. E03 does not say how far up the light goes, and says nothing of a shadow on the silt or of the thermal return: those are E04's to state, and the treatment gives two upright shapes and two returns.
7. **Where the second one stands.** On the line, not past it and not below it. An arm's length along the line from the one who answers, on the side where the row of stones does not run. That is the place where the second shape stood in the third year (S12E02C03). Facing the squeeze. Upright. Still, with the whole body, from before Aravena comes through until after Aravena has gone. It does not move at any point, does not turn toward Aravena or toward the one who answers, moves no stone, and makes no sound.
8. **What E03 does not fix about the second one.** Its size, its coat, or anything by which it could be told from another of the people. E04 must leave the survey unable to say whose the second return is, and E03 gives it nothing to say so with.
9. **The looking.** Long. Longer than the few seconds the look to the side has taken on every shift since the third year (*"about as long as it takes to say a name"*), and shorter than *"a short telling"*. Uncounted below; E04 may give it a figure from the handheld's marks if it wants one, within that. During it Aravena stands with the stone held to the front, the handheld comes round with the light, so the second one is in its frame, and Aravena's breath stops for a while and then comes. Nothing is said and nobody on the line does anything.
10. **Then the knock, unchanged.** The light comes back, low, to the feet of the one who answers. Aravena kneels, and sets the stone at the end of the row, a hand from the feet of the one who answers, where it is set on every shift. Not by the second one's feet. Aravena steps back and says the thing that goes up at the end, with the face turned to the one who answers. The one who answers bends, puts a hand on the rock and moves it at once. The second one does not answer. Aravena waits the interval, the usual one and not a longer one, and goes back through the squeeze.
11. **No second look.** After the long look the light does not go to the side again. E03 does not say what the low light shows of the second one's feet while Aravena waits out the interval; it says only that the light is not taken there.
12. **Nothing said from below.** Neither of the two on the line says anything to Aravena. No hand is kept on the rock. No telling is told. Nobody crosses the line in either direction, nothing goes up the squeeze but Aravena, and both of the two are still on the line when the light has gone.
13. **What neither side knows.** Below, nobody knows what was said, or that the old asking of the dark was for the one who carried the humming stone. E04 must not have the party learn, guess on the record, or be told who the second one was; and nothing in E04 may say what Aravena said, since E03 has Carried-It-Sleeping unable to tell whether it was *"the same thing said again"*.
14. **What E03 leaves entirely to E04.** Whether Aravena wants to look again, what Aravena thinks the second one is, what the handheld's frames show, the gravimeter, the entry, and Egede's wish to lay the second return against shift 41.

## Choices for him

**RULED 28 November 2026**, his words: *"Yes, as recommended"*; all 13 settled on (a), as drafted.

Each has a recommendation and its basis. None is settled here.

1. **Where Carried-It-Sleeping stands.**
   - (a) *An arm's length along the line, on the side where the row does not run, which is where the one not asked stood. Each of the two knows it and neither says so: "Somebody has to stand somewhere."* **Recommended, and drafted.** Basis: it is the only place on the line beside Stone-First that is neither Stone-First's place nor among stones that are not the people's to move, so it is derived and not chosen. It is also where the up-people's light has gone at every knock since, which is how the second one is found without the knock being changed. And it gives E04 the treatment's *"inverse of the shift with two shapes and one"* exactly. Nothing is said about the shape.
   - (b) *The same place, with the two paragraphs that notice it cut.* The reader of Season 12 will still notice.
   - (c) *A step behind Stone-First, on the deep side.* It avoids the place. It also puts Carried-It-Sleeping off the line, and the treatment has two on it.
2. **How the light finds the second one.**
   - (a) *By the look to the side it still makes at every knock.* **Recommended, and drafted.** Basis: S12E07C01 and S14E02C01. It extends the habit into the fifth year, which no page contradicts. The flagged line on *chosen* sits here and can be cut without loss to the structure.
   - (b) *The light finds the second one because two are in front of it, with no habit stated.* It commits the fifth year to nothing.

   - (b) *The first ground only.* Plainer, and the scolding turned over has less under it.
