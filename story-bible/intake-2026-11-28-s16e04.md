**The reading drafted.** One chapter, five scenes, two shifts.

- *Two Returns* (C01, Aravena, Nakagawa and Ferrant). Shift 347. Aravena comes through the squeeze looking at the light, which is on the feet of the one who answers. The hand takes the light to the side, as at every knock since the shift there were two, and it finds feet. The light goes onto them and up as far as a light held low goes, a shadow lies behind, and it stays, because the hand has only ever known what to do about silt. Aravena thinks *who are you*, and of the smallest profile in the file, and then that this is holding a light on a person. The light comes back and the knock is a knock. At the junction the rise is most of a minute late. Aravena logs *knock answered* and adds *two on the line, one answered*. Egede asks how big, which one answered, and what was said and to whom. Nakagawa writes the entry with *the second* in it and no noun, and does not add to the count of shifts with one on the line. At the station Ferrant reads the gravimeter before the frames and says before she is asked that there is nothing at that minute. She gives the look a figure from the handheld's marks and declines to give the second one a size. Egede lays the second return beside the profile of shift 41, failure modes first, and has nothing but that both are warm. Nakagawa writes nothing under Individual Four, refuses a lifted lamp, and waits for Aravena to ask for anything at all. Aravena does not. Shift 348: silt, one, and a count Aravena has never asked about.

**How the question is left, and that nobody knows.** E03 paid it from below without either of the Told knowing. E04 leaves it from above in so many words: Aravena's last scene sets out that it might have been Four, in which case the asking was once in Four's hearing, or it might have been anybody, and that from the roof the two look the same. The chapter does not say that the thing said at the line is the old question. It says what S11E07C02 already says, that the asking *"went down in the mornings"*, and no more. Egede says *"it might have been"* aloud and is refused as a guess. Nobody on the roof learns who the second one was, and no record has it.

**The ending test.** The episode is the second half of a pair, so the test is run on what it changes behind it. S12E07C02 ended on *"One?" "One." "Good,"* and a count that *"doesn't stop and start again"*. E04 ends on the same exchange with the *Good* gone, and with the count's own rule doing what Nakagawa said it would the day it was begun. Behind that, S11E07C02's *"it was not quite where it had been sent"* reads differently: the place it was sent may have come to it, once, and the survey lead's best instrument for knowing is a person who chose not to look twice.

**Self-containment.** Scene 1 restates the knock, the look to the side and the shift with the shape the light went round. Scene 3 restates what Ferrant's instrument did on that shift, and scene 4 what shift 41 is. Scene 5 restates the question asked of the dark. A reader who starts and stops here has a whole short story: a party that learned to count how many were there finds two, and cannot say who.

## Choices for him

1. **The shift.**
