## What the rulings forced

- **Not the Cerebraun again.** Their skin is already a fast, chosen,
  high-bandwidth display (`src/lore/cerebraun.md`; `species-design.md`, *The
  skin is a channel*). A second people with the same channel would be a copy.
  So the change here is slow where it is honest and chosen only where it is
  emphasis.
- **Not on the human frame.** *A singular exception stays singular*: the Krenyi
  are the only other people on it. "More pronounced features" is therefore an
  outline that rises, not a face that sharpens.
- **No mimicry.** His exclusion of shapeshifters and doppelgängers, with the
  trope default, rules out any change that copies another being or hides the
  body. Nothing in either trait alters shape or lets the body pass for anything.

## What was written

`src/lore/planets/solsta.md`, on the `people-body-design` skill's first step,
the world before the body (the Ilenne pattern). One tilt of about fifty degrees
gives both traits: the summer that does not set kills by heat, hence crests that
fill to shed it, which can be taken in hand like a breath and raised for
emphasis; the winter that does not rise kills by dark, hence a reflecting layer
behind the eye that turns from gold to deep blue over weeks, as in Earth's
reindeer, and cannot be willed. One honest channel and one chosen.

Every number is proposed and worked plainly on the page: a star of about 1.2
solar masses and about twice the Sun's light; an orbit of about 1.4 AU; a year of
about 1.51 standard (the square root of 1.4 cubed over 1.2); polar circles at
the fortieth parallels (ninety less the tilt); about thirty times Earth's carbon
dioxide, which holds heat through the dark and keeps the world short of
compatible with an unmodified human (Tír na nÓg's exception untouched).
