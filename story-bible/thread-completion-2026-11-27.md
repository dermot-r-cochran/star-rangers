# Thread completion: a map and a plan — 27 November 2026

**His words, 27 November 2026:** *"Yes Option 1 - completion of each saga"*.

He had asked in what sense the story is complete and how it could be extended. The answer given was that it is complete as a record, and that the one real incompleteness is the arc's own end: the last beats of *the shape of the saga* sit after the published end, the approved terminus farewell has no slot, and *You're Up Early* waits on it. Option 1 was to complete it.

**Reading taken.** *Each saga* is read as each of the eight storyline threads in `lib/storyline-threads.js`, with `tissadelle-arc` first. This note says *thread* throughout. *Saga* is already the name of Tissadelle Shepherd's spine (*the shape of the saga*), and he is separately deciding whether the general unit keeps the name *thread*.

**What this note is.** A map of where each thread ends, and a plan for the one piece that would close it. It is not prose, and nothing here is canon until a chapter carries it. Nothing under `src/` changes. Under the 20 October ruling the story has no last page, so *complete* is asked of a thread's arc, never of the work.

**Counted from `origin/main` at `49481de5`.** 206 chapter files in fifteen seasons (0 to 14): Season 0 twelve, 1 thirty-four, 2 sixteen, 3 fifteen, 4 eleven, 5 eleven, 6 ten, 7 eleven, 8 eight, 9 twelve, 10 fourteen, 11 fourteen, 12 fifteen, 13 ten, 14 thirteen. The gaps checklist's 23 November line says 205 and gives Season 8 seven.

**Sources.** Focus lines are quoted from `focus.md` and not restated. *Owed* is from `critic-read-2026-11-26-loose-ends.md`, § *Owed to later episodes*, with each item traced to its chapter, from `narrative-gaps-checklist.md`, and from the treatments.

**The test used for every piece proposed.** The ending test of 20 September: the end must change how the beginning reads. So each piece below goes back to something its thread opened and has not paid. No piece closes a marked absence, gives a remedy, or settles what a thread's line says nobody may settle.

### What the season must not do

- **State a mechanism for the terminus.** It is deliberately unwritten. The approved scenes do not state one and the outline below does not need one. Choice 5.
- **Number the interval.** No timestamp in the season carries a year for the terminus. Choice 2 covers a count the approved prose already holds.
- **Say which members crossed, or how many.** Several members are named on her page. Which of them survived Dock Seven is undecided, and the farewell is written so that it stays so.
- **Rescue her, return her, or let anyone else decide the end.**
- **Tell the martyrdom story.** The act is shown, and the narrative of sacrifice stays the arc's antagonist.
- **Explain the arrays.** Rook-7's arrays carry the final phrasing and the cadence under it. Their origin stays unconfirmed.
- **Settle what Aoife met**, or let the avatar's identification into the general tier's text.
- **Say on the page what the shadow is.** Tobble's *consistent with* is all he can know.
- **Depict her.** No brief exists for how she looks after Season 5 (`open-questions.md`, 7 October), so no chapter image shows her until he describes her.

- **The end changes how the beginning reads, three times.**

## Choices for him

Each has a recommendation and its basis. **RULED 27 November 2026**, his words: *"Yes, as recommended"*. Every choice below is settled on its recommended option, (a) throughout. The proposed focus line for Season 15 is not among them and stays *proposed*.

1. **The slot for the Tissadelle completion.**
   - (a) A new Season 15 on `tissadelle-arc`, seven episodes, as outlined. **Recommended.**
   - (b) Season 7 continued, from S07E08.
   - *Basis:* Season 7 ends on a finale whose line, *"leave the end of her story hers"*, is paid as it stands, and (b) would turn that finale into a middle. A season number is an index and not a date, so the unnumbered interval can sit between two seasons, where no chapter has to cross it.

2. **Years in the completion season.** The interval is deliberately unnumbered. The approved prose nonetheless counts *"six years"* from Dock Seven three times in the farewell and once in *You're Up Early*, and Tobble's *"thirty-eight years"*.
   - (a) No timestamp in the season carries a year. Each gives a season of the year and no more, on the precedent of S08E02C01, *"the house keeps no year for it"*. The counts in the approved prose stand as the characters' own. **Recommended.**
   - (b) Date the outside chapters by year, which fixes the terminus at about 2837.
   - *Basis:* (a) keeps the approved words untouched and the record's own dating silent. Both drafts list the interval among the things they *deliberately do not fix*.

3. **The tier of the completion season.**
   - (a) The general tier, by registration on `tissadelle-arc`, which `lib/editions.js` already lists there. Only the avatar's block is `tier=contemplative`. **Recommended.**
   - (b) Gate the season above the general tier.
   - *Basis:* the 21 September reading that the founding is the one reward the general tier can carry in the record's own voice; the thread's existing registration; and his rule that tiers are reading level and depth, never protection. Option (b) is a tier gate, which nobody moves without him.

4. **When *the shape of the saga* goes public.** Ruled on 21 September: story bible now, public once the terminus chapters are published.
   - (a) With the season's last episode, on the thread's intro and the README synopsis. **Recommended.**
   - (b) With E05, the terminus chapter itself.
   - (c) Story bible only, after all.
   - *Basis:* the line's last four beats are spread from E01 to E06. Fronting the work with it at E05 would pre-tell the founding episode by one publication.

5. **The terminus mechanism.** Flagged, not decided. The outline works under either.
   - (a) It stays unwritten for good, and the approved scenes carry the event. **Recommended.**
   - (b) A chapter states one, authored by him.
   - *Basis:* the 27 August ruling fixed the register and expressly not the mechanism. The approved coda lists *"any instrument-side mechanism beyond the founding signature's existence"* among what it does not fix. Nothing drafted since has needed one.

6. **The farewell on the page.**
   - (a) One chapter in two scenes, exactly as the draft stands, with a title of his that is not the closing line. **Recommended.**
   - (b) Two chapters, the Wender coda as its own.
   - *Basis:* (a) is the form he approved. A title quoting the closing line would print her last words on every index page.

7. **The siege in this season.**
   - (a) E04 before the founding and E06C02 after it, as outlined. The member Asteria speaks for the first time, under a new point-of-view id of her own, distinct from Asteria Wessex's. **Recommended.**
   - (b) Hold the siege for a later season, and give E04 and E06C02 to the holding alone.
   - *Basis:* the 23 September ruling put part of it on the page and fixed its three points of view, and the treatment ends it at the founding. Option (a) does change one line of 27 November, that the member Asteria *"has no line in any chapter"*.

8. **Seasons 1 and 3's owed items.**
   - (a) They stay owed to those casts' own later episodes, outside the completion. **Recommended.**
   - (b) A further Season 1 episode is planned beside Season 15.
   - *Basis:* none of them bears on the terminus, and the survey team's open items are a different cast in a different year. They are deliberate setups, and nothing in them is a loose end that confuses.

9. **Below the Roof: does the survey leave?**
   - (a) Yes: a fourth season, the last shift. **Recommended**, and last in order.
   - (b) No: the thread rests on S14E07, and a coda episode pays what is owed.
   - *Basis:* it is the only end the treatments name, and S14E06C01 has put *return with the run* on the page. It is still a fact about the world, so it is his.

10. **A coda episode after a finale.** Four of the pieces above are single episodes added after a season's last one (S02E13, S08E08, S09E08, S13E08).
    - (a) Allow it, the episode marked as a coda in its intro. **Recommended.**
    - (b) Each gets a short season of its own.
    - *Basis:* his 23 November rule gives every season at least seven episodes, so (b) costs six episodes each to carry one. The cost of (a) is that Episode 7 is no longer the last page of those seasons.

11. **The order of the other seven.**
    - (a) After the Tissadelle completion, the single episodes in order of how long each debt has stood, then the one season: Orbital Five-O (opened late autumn 2826, in S02E01), Young Star Rangers, Church Space, Five Islands, Undercover Pets, Below the Roof. **Recommended.**
    - (b) By readership: the children's doors first (Undercover Pets, Below the Roof), then the rest.
    - *Basis:* oldest debt first; the largest piece, and the one that waits on choice 9, last. Lev's chapter sits late because it is set furthest ahead.
